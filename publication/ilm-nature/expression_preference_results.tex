\paragraph{Training the reader and writer together improves independent completion.}
Local counts supplied a complementary source of supervision. On the 9,135 earlier confirmation passages, the local baseline solved 323 that the published guided writer missed, whereas the writer solved 80 that local counts missed. Training the writer on aligned local hints gave 143 exact completions on 8,759 previously unused passages, compared with 104 after shuffling the same hints and 139 for the original guided writer. Its four-answer gain over the guided writer had a paired 95\% source-group interval of $-0.14$ to $+0.23$ percentage points.

We then tested whether the guided writer assigned the observed expressions to their corresponding passages. In 3,056 pairs with identical immediate neighbors, the model preferred the correct joint assignment 1,956 times, but independently preferred both recorded answers over their competitors in only 270 pairs. Updating the visual reader and the recurrent writer together on the original reference trajectories produced 548 exact development completions, up from 378. Adding a whole-trajectory preference term yielded 333. On a further 8,790 gaps these counts were 207, 131 and 132 for the joint reference, guided and preference writers, respectively. Preference supervision increased the count of pairs with both supplied answers preferred from 336 to 585 relative to joint reference training. This gain in supplied choice accompanied a reduction in free completion (Fig.~\ref{fig:expression-preference}).

The reference-only fit was the control in that comparison. We froze it for a separately specified confirmation. Across 17,555 gaps from 2,605 further source groups it generated 433 recorded expressions, versus 276 for the guided writer. The gain was 0.89 percentage points (paired source-group 95\% interval, 0.68--1.10), with 255 gains and 98 losses. Of 12,981 cases missing all four answer glyphs, 309 were exactly correct. It solved both answers on four of 6,646 matched pairs and none of 21 unseen whole expressions. All 70,220 emitted cells had a training-known glyph bitmap. Local counts still solved 789 passages. The historical source reader preserved every original intact and relocated stream on the 600 development plates after integration.
