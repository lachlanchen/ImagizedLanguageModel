\subsection*{Reading the headword and field requests}
We excluded visually duplicate head entries and unavailable renderings before building a 6,971-record collection. Each training pair specified a printed question and the locations of the relevant headword and field mentions. The reader pooled each 32-pixel cell, located the focal glyph and classified the mentions. Identical source ink admitted a record, and the compositor copied the requested fields in question order. A fixed origin request returned the supplied gloss and available historical stages. Four matched fits used 2,400 batches of 96 questions drawn from 64,000 authored examples per wording condition. Held questions varied the focal character and field order. A deletion test removed the correct entry before lookup. Empty fields, missed questions and incorrect records remained in the denominator.
