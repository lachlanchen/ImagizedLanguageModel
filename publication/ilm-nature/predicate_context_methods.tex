\paragraph{Disambiguation of a pictured predicate.}
Both neural arms took 582 AdamW steps at a learning rate of 0.0001, including one pass over the full 55,872-pair training set. Each ordinary minibatch received eight additional examples balanced across four senses. We excluded 24 of 138 candidate training clauses after reading the source and gloss. The two fits shared a schedule with 1,811,103 target cells per arm. The extra contrast was $\log(1+\exp(\ell_{-}-\ell_{+}))$, where each $\ell$ is the log of the summed copying and exemplar probabilities for one of the two distinct pictured initials, given the same target prefix. The frozen exemplar collection contained all four initials. Replay took 13.4 seconds and the contrast fit 20.5 seconds on one RTX 4090 D. Both peaked below 378 MiB of allocated tensors, excluding parent training, data preparation and scoring.

The first test excluded 5,076 prior paired chapters, previously seen complete source or target images, and centred three-cell predicate neighbourhoods present in the curated fit. We matched contrasting senses by source length, permitted one clause per test chapter and excluded eight of 23 proposed pairs before fitting. After free generation, a separate CPU calculation changed the source while holding the archive answer prefix constant. The contrast writer preferred the intended picture in sixteen of thirty cases with the recorded source and fifteen with the opposite-sense source. The archive-prefix calculation supplies language unavailable during free writing.
