\paragraph{Additional passages and rare expressions.}
We doubled the number of distinct training passages while holding the reader, writer and number of training examples seen fixed (Fig.~\ref{fig:passage-diversity}). On 17,862 new gaps the starting writer exactly recovered 410 expressions. Repeating the existing passages yielded 516 and including new passages yielded 550. The diverse-data fit gained 166 and lost 132 relative to repetition, a difference of 0.19 percentage points (paired source-group 95\% interval, 0.00--0.39). Its ten most frequent answers occupied 41.1\% of outputs, compared with 35.6\% after repetition. All 550 correct expressions had occurred at least 21 times in its training set. A local count baseline trained on the same combined corpus recovered 972. The frozen renderer could reproduce every target glyph under canonical input in 17,858 of the 17,862 evaluation windows.

Giving each training example weight inversely proportional to the square root of its expression count recovered two of 2,161 rare cases on a further 17,962 gaps, up from zero with uniform weights. Overall exact completions fell from 521 to 427, with 69 gains and 163 losses (difference $-0.52$ percentage points, paired 95\% interval $-0.68$ to $-0.35$). The number of distinct outputs fell from 371 to 316. Across 6,772 context-matched pairs the weighted fit recovered both answers seven times, versus twelve for the uniform fit. Reweighting the recorded answers changed the errors and increased concentration in common predictions.
