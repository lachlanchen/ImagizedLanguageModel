\subsection*{Dictionary adaptation and physical attribution}
We initialized both 628,104-parameter writers from the same passage-contrast checkpoint and rendered each entry as a sequence of visible 32-by-32 cells. Each two-entry card contained the gloss, pronunciation and two attested historical rasters for each headword. We used 8,920 standard training questions and 44,600 additional phrasings on 1,115 cards. The control reassigned questions within each card, preserving the card, target, wording frequencies and replay schedule. Both arms received five complete passes with 12 DRCD and 12 DuReader rehearsal examples per update. Matched and control training took 115.3 and 102.3 seconds, respectively, with peak allocated memory below 0.70\,GiB each on one RTX 4090 D. The initialization cost is shared. All 568 held-out historical forms were present in their pictured card and absent from the finite writing memory and entry training. We scored the full stopped image against the rendered reference and identified the source of its penultimate glyph by physical equality. Bootstrap intervals resample complete cards.
