\paragraph{Finding the paragraph that supports a pictured reply.}
Holding the date writer fixed, we supplied it with the whole page, one of the two annotated paragraphs, or the paragraph selected from the pictured question (Fig.~\ref{fig:paragraph-evidence}). Learned selection increased complete twelve-reply comparisons from 8 to 13 of 48 with distinct subjects and from 2 to 8 of 34 when the two paragraphs named the same subject in different roles. Longest-run glyph matching also passed 13 comparisons with distinct subjects but only two with the same subject. The learned selector chose the right source on 394/408 shared-subject questions, compared with 306 for literal matching. On competing quotations it recovered 28/64 complete comparisons, below the literal rule's 34. Changing only the competing paragraph's year preserved the selected reply on 526/540 pairs.

The first corrected response kept the actor and changed the year from 2011 to 2005. Finding the paragraph still left 123 of the 576 distinct-subject questions unanswered correctly with either source. In the first such error the writer supplied the author and stopped before the year. With 302 human questions whose accepted answer occurred in exactly one of two sections, learned selection identified the answer-bearing section on 279. The date writer answered zero from the annotated section. A separate visual span reader recovered 198 from that section and 202 from the full page.
