\paragraph{Fixed-position action exchanges.}
We exchanged the actions in two adjacent clauses while holding both names at identical pixel locations. The 32 original excerpts came from \textit{Romance of the Three Kingdoms} and \textit{Water Margin}. In the original clause order, the assigned-name optical reader answered every Chinese question before and after the action exchange on 11 of 16 excerpts in the former and 9 of 16 in the latter (Fig.~\ref{fig:fixed-action}). Equal-label training completed two and five. Paired 95\% intervals for these differences were 31--81 and 6--50 percentage points, respectively. The frozen parent also completed 11 and nine, so the assigned update largely preserved a capability present before this adaptation. None of the visual readers completed an English contrast. With the input reversed as an additional requirement, the assigned reader succeeded on ten and nine Chinese excerpts. Text Qwen3-8B completed 13 and 16 in Chinese and 13 and 15 in English in the original order.
