\paragraph{The same printed verb can require different answers.}
The classical verb \emph{qu} can describe leaving a place or the distance between places. Likewise, \emph{fa} can describe felling trees or attacking a state. The isolated glyph provides identical evidence in the two senses. We compared two writers trained to translate complete clauses. Both saw the same 55,872 parent passages and the same 4,656 additional exposures to 114 curated clauses. At the first glyph distinguishing the two modern glosses, the contrast writer also learned to prefer the recorded picture over its rival. The additional binary training loss fell from 0.440 to 0.00044. Yet on thirty previously unused clauses from thirty chapters, the contrast writer produced three faithful translations and the replay writer produced four. Neither translated both members of any of the fifteen same-length, opposite-sense pairs faithfully (Fig.~\ref{fig:predicate-context}). Even with the recorded answer prefix, the candidate preferred the intended glyph to its rival on only sixteen clauses. The two candidate pictures together received a median probability mass of 8.8\% through copy and exemplar routes. Learning a two-way training preference left the writer free to emit many other pictures.

A second writer learned to emit a contiguous modern phrase for a contextual reading. Three of twelve fresh archive translations and three of eight authored translations were faithful as whole answers. The intended sense was recognizable in ten and seven, respectively. The writer distinguished the distance to Shouchun from the act of leaving Zhongbao, using the same printed source verb in both cases. It also chose the distance sense when a passage said that Duan Jun departed. On ten pairs the learned writer conveyed both senses in seven but gave two fully faithful modern answers in none. The explicit cue rule scored nineteen of twenty senses and one complete pair. The whole-pair criterion therefore failed. On 1,024 diverse passages the learned writer made 15,561 edits against the modern references, three fewer than the unmodified phrase composer. Neither produced a faithful translation in the twelve reviewed passages. Other words and their boundaries still determine whether a complete translation can be read.
