\paragraph{Training against physical first-image errors.}
For a boundary state $b$, pooled classical source $s$ and modern prefix state $h$, the corrected first drawing uses $\tanh(b+h+\alpha W_2\,\mathrm{GELU}(W_1[b;s;h]+a_1)+\alpha a_2)$. The hidden layer has width 256 and the output layer starts at zero. For each 48-pixel signature $v$, $S(v)$ sums the twelve conditional four-pixel log probabilities given its own preceding pattern groups. Both residuals minimize the mean of $-S(v_{\rm ref})$. The preference arm adds the mean of $\log(1+\exp[S(v_{\rm old})-S(v_{\rm ref})])$ over examples with distinct signatures. This loss directly compares two drawings. It imposes no ordering on a third image. The frozen drawing mechanism fills the remaining pixels. The evaluation compares all 1,024 pixels of every emitted glyph.

We trained 131,456 additional parameters per arm for eight epochs using identical shuffled batches of 512 and AdamW at a learning rate of 0.0006. Of 241,256 training positions, 184,447 had different old and reference signatures. A separate set of 13,440 calibration positions selected residual strength by exact physical first images from five scales between zero and one. The likelihood and preference arms selected 1 and 0.25 respectively. Mining the first images took 32.1 seconds. Preparing fixed observations, optimizing both arms and calibrating took another 63.4 seconds on one RTX 4090 D and peaked at 1.02 GiB allocated device memory. These incremental figures exclude training the parent and building its local count tables. We measured complete free phrases, first-image changes and likelihood under alternative classical sources on the same five enrolled cohorts. Historical teacher-forced exchange scores for Figures~\ref{fig:conditional-ink} and~\ref{fig:sentence-choice} were corrected after finding that a loop variable selected the final position in a batch for multiple target steps. Replaying every historical matched pair preserved the original generated-answer counts.

For autonomous translation, the existing controller chooses copy, replace, insert, drop or stop from its current source cursor and generated history. An insertion delegates drawing to the phrase model. After a predicted phrase end, the next action must consume a source glyph or stop. Every emitted image, including an exact source copy, advances the recurrent history. We retained the 32-glyph phrase and 192-glyph sentence caps and recorded cap exits separately from learned stops. Distance is the Levenshtein distance between full bitmap sequences.

In the teacher diagnostic we sampled 32 calibration pairs with different first glyphs, at least two glyphs per insertion and a nonempty authentic modern prefix. The teacher saw the classical source, marked boundary, prefix and two phrase alternatives, with the following translation withheld. We crossed source assignments and reversed option order. Only success under both orders received credit. The secondary pass retained all previously identified choices. We ran the locally quantized Qwen3 4B Q4\_K\_M with greedy decoding on CPU. Its outputs were used solely for evaluation. The two visual writers scored each entire candidate's conditional pixel patterns and end decisions at their own drawing positions. The suffix repetition count checks identical blocks of one to twelve bitmaps at the end of each answer.

For the final calibration audit we counted full physical bitmaps and adjacent ordered bitmap pairs in each source and translation. We assigned the two translations to the two sources by the greater sum of cosine similarities. Differences within $10^{-8}$ counted as ties. Several insertion pairs used the same two complete sentences, yielding 6,716 distinct sentence pairs across the 6,720 records.
