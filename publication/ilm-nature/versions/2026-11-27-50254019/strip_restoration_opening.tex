\noindent We trained two visual writers on 268,342 English--Chinese passage pairs. When asked which of two pictured answers assigned the same participants to the correct roles, one writer chose correctly on both directions of reversal in 83 of 84 pairs. The same reader failed all 84 passive paraphrases in a separate English construction challenge. On 192 requests from four unseen books, masking-only training preserved \maskBook{} complete meanings, training on displaced strips preserved \restoreBook{}, and a quantized 8B text model preserved \qwenBook{}. The model learned regularities in printed word order. Its decisions broke when grammatical voice changed the relation between order and meaning.\par
\begin{multicols}{2}
\section*{Reading and writing pictured language}
A page can preserve handwriting and historical forms that a transcription discards. Our independent writers read ordered $32\!\times\!32$ glyph images and write through a finite repertoire of pictured strips. Both predict answer length from the source. Adapting \href{https://aclanthology.org/D19-1633/}{parallel masked translation}, we start with an empty canvas and make six refinement passes. During training the second writer also sees strips displaced within a sentence. Both update 3.25 million parameters on the same 19.68 million selected positions. Their four additional epochs took 289 and 293 seconds on one RTX 4090 D.
\end{multicols}

\section*{How much does the source contribute?}
With target context on either side of a missing interval, the masking-only writer recovered 60 of 962 hidden book strips. Exchanging the source reduced that to 33. With an entire unordered answer supplied and an initially blank canvas, maximum-weight position assignment reconstructed 128/336 controlled sentences exactly. Restricting the choice to two complete orders raised individual accuracy to 167/168. The choices carry at least 0.947 bits about orientation on this balanced panel. But on 42 affirmative English pairs the reader followed the first name throughout, with 84/84 active sentences correct and 0/84 passives correct.
\section*{Where free writing breaks down}
On the separate OPUS panel the masked writer earned \maskOpus{}/256 whole-answer credits and the displaced writer \restoreOpus{}/256. Displacement increased the number of book outputs repeating a four-glyph sequence from 109 to 170 of 192. The causal parent repeated one in 73. A reference-hidden model judge scored meaning twice in different orders.
