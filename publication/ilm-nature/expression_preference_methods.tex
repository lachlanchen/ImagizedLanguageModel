\paragraph{Cross-fitted hints and whole-trajectory scores.}
Eight source-group folds supplied out-of-fold local predictions on training passages. We accepted the most frequent expression for the first observed neighborhood in the existing backoff order when it occurred at least twice and accounted for at least 10\% of that neighborhood. The rule covered 10,113 of 71,439 passages and was correct on 1,343. At 121,226 randomly selected visits we substituted the hint into the existing guided target schedule. The shuffled control permuted the same hints within the same fold and epoch. The two fits used the same initial state, minibatches and 24 epochs. Their fit times were 40.1 and 38.8 seconds for aligned and shuffled assignments, respectively.

For a four-glyph candidate $a$ and a pictured context $x$, let $L(x,a)$ sum the log probabilities of 48 physical four-bit patterns and one stop. At each glyph boundary the scorer reads the landmark pixels of the actual rendered glyph. This scores one specified physical production path. For answers $a,b$ recorded in contexts $x,y$, the difference $L(x,a)-L(x,b)+L(y,b)-L(y,a)$ cancels additive answer-only scores. We verified batched scoring against a stepwise recurrent replay. The preference fit added $0.08\,\mathrm{softplus}((L(x,b)-L(x,a))/2)$ at paired rows to the mean per-step reference loss. Training used 30,048 nonoverlapping pairs from different groups and ordinary likelihood on all 71,439 training windows. Both joint fits began at the published guided state and updated the same 700,291 reader and writer parameters for eight epochs. The landmarks and physical renderer were fixed. The fits took 26.7 and 39.8 seconds on one RTX 4090 D. Prior reader, renderer and guided-writer training are additional costs.

The independent confirmation selected the next eight prespecified source hash buckets. We discarded 97 duplicated or overlapping windows and 24 unsupported renderings. The same confirmation beam and learned stop were used for both writers. In the historical regression all four intact or relocated conditions retained the same output pixels. Both erasures still gave zero exact answers. Erasing the plate selected the modern writer and changed some wrong outputs. The sealed test partition was unused throughout.
