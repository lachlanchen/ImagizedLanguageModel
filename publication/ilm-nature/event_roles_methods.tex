\paragraph{Participant exchange and reversible assignment of ink.}
We scanned 7,304 chapters of the classical--modern NiuTrans archive and excluded 4,682 chapters previously used in paired experiments from the new tests. The targeted fit used 203 original clauses from 132 chapters. Of 42 held-out original clauses from 32 chapters, two had a match within a place or personal name and were excluded from the geographic analysis before generation. The forty retained pairs included one example each of rescue and flight, predicates reserved from the new fit. Counterfactuals exchange two names in both pictured streams. A separate test covered 27,309 modern reference cells in 1,024 passages from 305 chapters. Both arms shared their parent, glyph likelihood, batches, step order and decoder. Each completed sixty AdamW steps over twelve passes of 3,914 target cells each. Incremental fitting took 1.92 and 1.40 seconds for replay and exchange on one RTX 4090 D. These times exclude the common parent, data preparation and evaluation.

For an unfamiliar input bitmap, a table estimated the probability of occupying a participant position from neighbouring cells in the 203 training originals. The centre cell was excluded. We used the longest available neighbourhoods observed at least twice. If participant counts exceeded other counts, we reserved a familiar, absent participant bitmap, using contextual counts or a global-frequency control. The resulting disjoint exchanges define an input-specific involution $P_x$. If $q$ denotes the trained writer, the wrapped distribution at a visible output $y_t$ is
\[
 q(P_x y_t\mid P_x x, P_x y_{<t}).
\]
The writer emits the inverse-mapped image and reads its actual pixels into the following step. The 3,106 observed neighbourhoods and 7,282 familiar bitmaps are frozen at inference. Exact bitmap matching assumes aligned 32-by-32 cells. The rule can preserve an unfamiliar name without identifying its referent.

We selected historical exemplars by stage preference and catalogue order from the available Hanziyuan splits. Independent rasterization of all ten selected SVG files reproduced their cached luminance masks. The first run had used white-paper bits as ink. We retained its outputs and repeated inference with the same ten exemplars and weights after converting luminance to foreground. A preliminary export that changed exemplars was excluded from this matched comparison. Under the original polarity the unwrapped and contextual writers preserved both names and rewrote both directions for one and twelve pairs, respectively. Masked reading used canonical modern cell identities and bracketed labels for authenticated historical images. On repeating the reading, 72 identical source--answer wordings retained their prior grades and 33 new alternatives were graded with method assignments hidden. The reader was study-involved. The mixed-period clauses and the fixed Qin--Chu probe were constructed for this evaluation.
