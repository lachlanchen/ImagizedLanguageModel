\paragraph{Learning to revise observed writing.}
Four chapter-disjoint models generated a fallible training draft for each classical source; the emitting composer excluded the entire source chapter and any identical complete source. The test composer used all 55,872 training pairs. For each draft glyph we aligned the recorded modern gloss to the draft by minimum bitmap edit distance. The revision actions comprised identity and up to eleven alternative fragments seen at least twice in training, each zero to four images long. Unsupported training edits received identity supervision. The resulting inventory included 18,102 alternatives distributed across draft glyphs and 7,280 distinct training bitmaps. Unknown draft ink could be preserved. For each position two bidirectional recurrent networks read the source and draft. A distance-biased attention distribution aggregates the source for that draft position. A contextual vector scores the ordered visual features of candidate fragments with a frequency prior and a change-versus-keep term. Each answer ends after the final draft position. The model does not infer an open-ended stopping time.

We trained the final 255,046-parameter editor for eight passes and 4,736 updates on a single RTX 4090 D. Training took 69.1 seconds and reached 182 MiB of allocated tensors, excluding the preparation and training of its phrase composer. The composer and revision checkpoint together occupy 6,391,309 bytes. We selected the change threshold by complete edit distance on 512 development passages from 96 held-out chapters, with ties favoring fewer changes. The untouched 1,024-passage sample represented 623 further chapters and excluded earlier evaluation sources, duplicates and training chapters. The source exchange preserved the length and used a different chapter for each case. Comparisons used the complete emitted image sequence and 2,000 paired chapter resamples.

The changed-draft reading included the first changed example in each of eighteen different chapters. The second reading retained the six-per-marker scheme for all eligible outputs, with a different chapter per example. The deduplicated union comprised 32 passages. A local Q8 Qwen3 8B generated modern text from their literal classical sources using greedy decoding, thinking disabled and a 160-token limit. Randomized choices combined its untouched outputs with the visual answers and corpus glosses. One study-involved model reader scored complete faithfulness before method assignments were revealed. The source-capability comparison used one Q8 Qwen3-VL 8B Instruct checkpoint. It received either the literal source or its original pictured strip enlarged twofold by nearest-neighbour resampling. No transcription or reference was supplied to the image arm. Both translation conditions used the same instruction. A separate request transcribed the pictured passage. Whitespace-normalized transcription incurred two character edits over 294 characters. The separate modality-blinded translation reading reused the same reader already familiar with the passages. The foundations' pretraining and text decoding preclude a matched-resource claim for the pictured editor.
