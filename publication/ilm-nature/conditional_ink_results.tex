\paragraph{Correlations within a glyph and meaning across a sentence.}
We fitted 50,072 parameters to predict how the preceding four measured pixels should change the probabilities of the next four (Fig.~\ref{fig:conditional-ink}). Across four chapter-held-out cohorts the number of exact stopped insertions increased from 2,027 to 2,117 of 23,211. Training counts of whole phrases recovered 2,997. One of 8,331 instances of previously unseen insertions was written and stopped exactly. In that sentence an eldest son explains his departure after hearing of a pardon. The model generated the three-glyph causal phrase meaning “therefore came” before the supplied translation continued “to say farewell.” These three glyphs occur consecutively in six modern training sentences, including once within a longer insertion. The accompanying gallery shows the original output strips from all 48 contextual readings. On the newest 6,435 sites the model wrote 455 exact phrases, against 440 for its parent and 670 for whole-phrase counts. The chapter-bootstrap 95\% interval for the gain over its parent was 0.05--0.43 percentage points. Both neural writers yielded 13 acceptable fillings in 48 blinded readings at these new sites, compared with 20 for the count rule and 43 for the recorded translations. The updated writer matched 35,132 of 35,138 emitted cells to a known single-font image. It recovered zero of 2,617 unseen insertion instances on this cohort and stopped on 5,679 sites.

To test wider context, we exchanged the full classical sentences in 786 natural pairs from different chapters with identical local visual measurements and conflicting target phrases. The updated model gave the authentic assignment higher complete-phrase probability on 460 pairs and the exchanged assignment on 326 pairs. Free generation reproduced 158 of 1,572 recorded insertions under the authentic assignment and 151 with the exchanged source. On the newest cohort the corresponding counts were 32 and 27 of 464. Sensitivity to the broader source thus produced only a small aggregate change in independently correct insertions. These exchanges can also change sentence length, register and boundary position.
