\subsection*{A restricted learnability result and its visual implementation}
Let a factual answer have a subject $s$, relation $r$ and object $o$. For each relation, observe three possibly empty sequences of glyphs $(a_r,b_r,c_r)$ surrounding the aligned subject and object in a training answer. Define $W(s,r,o)=a_r\,s\,b_r\,o\,c_r$ by concatenation. A constructive learner stores the first observed triple of sequences for each relation. If examples for a given relation exist and all specify its true pattern, this learner identifies that pattern. The identity then holds for fresh participant strings of arbitrary glyph type. On any finite sample, full-answer errors under the common writer are bounded by the sum of errors in the three fields. If two questions have the same encoded state but distinct correct answers, a deterministic decoder cannot be right on both. Generalization from a representative across a name permutation requires both truth and the learned predictor to commute with that permutation. These results were checked with the kernel of Lean 4.12.0 (\texttt{RoleBinding.lean}). They establish neither acquisition of the fields by gradient descent nor general syntax. The substitution and transfer experiments test assumptions on actual model outputs.

We fitted a 202,768-parameter head to frozen contextual features of a previously calibrated visual Chinese QA reader. A classifier scores twelve relations. Four position scores identify the two endpoint pairs. Object scores include the pretrained span prior. Decoding maximizes each endpoint sum over valid spans of up to 48 cells. The whole-statement writer copies both spans as physical bitmaps. Training used 19,984 pictured DuIE questions, 6,796 participant exchanges and 110 weakly labelled human training questions, comprising 23,209 distinct passages. We excluded normalized subjects and full passages shared with the primary evaluation. Six passes over cached features took 40.1 seconds on one RTX 4090 D, following 40.2 seconds for visual feature extraction. This excludes upstream training of the 85,951,202-parameter reader and its visual calibration. The control randomly reassigned relation targets and exchanged subject and object supervision on a random half of its training examples.

For independent sentence realization, a source-pointing UER Chinese GPT-2 image writer received the selected subject, a pictured relation label and the selected object. The writing instruction was supplied separately in pixels. Two passes used 8,000 pairs of ordered sentences and 4,096 naturally occurring short clauses from training sources. An initial fit included physically unreachable auxiliary targets and its likelihood became numerically uninformative. In a fresh fit we checked every target cell against the printed bank or source fields and skipped 63 auxiliary candidates to fill the same quota. The fresh fit took 54.1 seconds and used 1.83 GiB peak device allocation. Comparisons used greedy generation from predicted and reference fields, both requested orders, the unchanged starting writer and the original full sequences. The natural-clause objective can reward incidental details even when several authentic continuations are consistent with the same three fields.
