\subsection*{Conditional retrieval of previously written words}
We reserved the next 147 stories in the previously fixed CoQA hash ordering before fitting. We ranked distinct fitting-answer word images by frequency and broke ties by first occurrence. The top 2,048 were stored as sequences of observed glyph images. Each prototype has a learned retrieval key. The passage, question and visible answer words produce the frozen writer's hidden state $h$. We trained a query projection and a scalar gate $g(h)$ with the prototype keys and biases. For answer-word image $w$ the physical likelihood is
\[
P(w\mid h)=(1-g)\bigl[\pi_c C(w\mid h)+\pi_s S(w\mid h)\bigr]+g R(w\mid h).
\]
Here $C$ sums over copy locations and trims that produce exactly the target image, $S$ multiplies spelling and stop probabilities, and $R$ is a softmax over fitting images. Minimizing the negative log of this sum trains the retrieval component in proportion to its contribution to the observed word. Gradients update only the new retrieval parameters. The size of the memory and all inherited weights count towards deployment cost.

Both arms used 3,200 updates of 32 turns on identical batches (44,417 distinct turns, one seed). With 785,190 parameters including 211,169 fitted here, the visual checkpoint occupies 4,264,184 bytes. On one RTX 4090~D the visual fit used 24.4 seconds, 2.86 kJ and 347 MB peak allocated GPU memory, after 5.9 seconds and 0.49 kJ for preparation. The byte fit used 24.6 seconds and 2.88 kJ, after 4.8 seconds and 0.40 kJ for preparation. These are incremental device measurements; the inherited models have separate training costs.

Decoding combines exact copy and retrieval masses by output image and adds the mass of the greedy completed spelling. Other spellings can contribute at most the unused spelling probability $u$ to any rival. If the largest resolved mass exceeds the second largest by more than $u$, its image must maximize the full mixture. We checked this sufficient condition and its worst-case tightness in Lean on an arbitrary common denominator. The floating-point implementation uses a $10^{-6}$ safety margin. The condition held at 678 of 1,308 supplied-neighbour visual positions with retrieval. This measures agreement with the model's own mode; a certified mode can be the wrong word. At deployment the writer predicts its own length and previous commitments. The disabled-retrieval control uses this same decoder with $g=0$; the unchanged parent retains its original route-first decoder.
