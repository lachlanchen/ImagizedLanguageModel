\subsection*{Causal writing from a retained book prefix}
We reconstructed volume order in the Kyoto classical Chinese treebank v2.16, omitted titles, and split a volume at characters unavailable in the fixed font. The fitting corpus contained 379,533 glyph positions. All 35,347 positions in \emph{Mencius} were reserved for development. The two test works supplied 30,282 positions before windowing. A $32\times32$ binary cell was averaged over $2\times2$ squares. Two causal attention blocks of width 128 read either these 256 features or four fixed 64-dimensional byte vectors. A learned gate combined a softmax over 5,501 physical training shapes, attention to observed cells and a smoothed suffix distribution of depth at most six. Pointer generation has previously been used for textual summarization (See et al., \url{https://aclanthology.org/P17-1099/}). Here copies and probability sums use bitmap identity. The suffix branch used another three volume folds during fitting and the complete fitting corpus at deployment. With regional counts $c_h$, total $n_h$ and smoothed unigram $u$, its probability was $(c_h(g)+2u(g))/(n_h+2)$.

Each writer received 3,200 AdamW updates of 24 windows with 59 scored predictions per window. The visual mixture had 1,032,841 parameters and required 20.16 seconds and 2.19 kJ on one RTX 4090 D during fitting. The byte mixture used the same sampled windows, 19.08 seconds and 2.10 kJ. Device counters exclude host preparation and data acquisition. We evaluated disjoint 16-glyph target blocks after 32-glyph prefixes and resampled volumes within works 5,000 times. Held outcomes share overlapping histories. Under self-feedback a finite palette and a copy pointer can reach exactly the shapes in the training palette or the initial visible prefix while it remains in view. Induction over the emitted stream formalizes this property in Lean. Of 663 target positions outside the palette, 131 were present in the prefix. Only nine of these were recovered. Of 28,348 reachable positions, 27,509 were missed. Most errors therefore persisted even when the required bitmap could be emitted.
