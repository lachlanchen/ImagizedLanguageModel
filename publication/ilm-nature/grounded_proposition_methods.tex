\subsection*{Exchanging source facts and planning two separate writing calls}
We built 850 two-relation boards from individual passages in the \href{https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleNLP/issues/447}{Baidu DuIE 2.0 release} and another 850 by combining excerpts describing different subjects with a common relation. We interchanged two object strings in each source and trained on both questions about both versions. The resulting 6,800 examples were mixed with 1,536 earlier tasks for the single-fact fit. The 224 development boards were drawn from the official development split and share neither a source nor a subject with the added fit. Their 1,792 single questions cover familiar and rephrased requests. The 448 compound questions cover both orders and assignments on the 112 two-relation boards. We withheld two combinations from all two-clause supervision while retaining their constituent relations separately. The target sentence was generated from the annotated tuple and could not be copied as a single contiguous span. The assessments allow a fixed set of equivalent formulations. Correctness on this designed language need not transfer to freely phrased statements.

Let $P$ be the pictured source and $q$ the pictured question. A bidirectional recurrent network applied to the projected glyphs of $q$ predicts the number of clauses and three pairs of endpoints, selecting a shared subject $s$ and two ordered descriptions $r_1,r_2$. For a two-clause request the emission probability conditional on these selections factorizes as
\[
  p(y_1,y_2\mid P,s,r_1,r_2)=
    p_\theta(y_1\mid P,s,r_1)\,p_\theta(y_2\mid P,s,r_2).
\]
Both calls use the same source-pointing autoregressive image writer. The next cell depends on earlier emitted cells within its clause. Each call must stop and the joined output must fit within 48 cells. Single requests keep the original question. The title and role slices consist of physical input glyphs. Only training and the explicitly identified oracle receive annotated endpoints. The planner has 138,471 parameters and was fitted on 12,272 requests. Its training took 19.8 seconds on one RTX 4090 D, following a 52.8-second clause-writer adaptation. Earlier writer and language pretraining are excluded from these times. The deployed planner and writer contain 86,726,729 parameter elements and 25,404,434 buffer elements. An isolated CPU process reproduced the pixels and stop decisions of eight archived examples. We also evaluated 48 separately selected human questions whose complete reference name was visible in the first 416 cells. Exposure during upstream language pretraining was not audited.

For evidence conditioning, the visual span reader first scored the original passage and unaltered question. A separate writer received the predicted span, one blank cell and the original passage, with the original question. The genuine and control fits saw 7,824 records for two passes and used identical targets. In the control, the supplied span belonged to the other fact on the same training board. In the reference-assisted evaluation the supplied span was the annotated name. The original human questions did not explicitly require the full statement imposed by our evaluation. Name containment and complete accepted statements are therefore reported separately.
