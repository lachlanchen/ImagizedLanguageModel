\subsection*{Selecting the source of a written answer}
We used clauses with a one-cell root verb and exactly one direct object of one cell. The two roles had distinct glyphs and English reference glosses. We removed conflicting or repeated printed clauses and excluded a held clause if either target window had previously been used to fit the gloss writer. The six training works supplied 19,067 clauses. The four reserved works supplied 522 clauses in the development and test portions of Kyoto UD 2.16. An external reading set drew on 18 selected clauses or clause excerpts in the \textit{Dao De Jing}. We fixed source locations and accepted one-word translations with reference to Legge's translation before model evaluation. One excerpt shared a writer-training window, leaving 17. We applied the fixed lists even where a related English word may be defensible. The independent book is small and was scored by the authors.

For each source position $i$, the frozen writer supplies a visual context and a distribution $p_W(y\mid x,i)$ over complete sequences of pictured glyphs, including the stop. A second bidirectional recurrence reads the page contexts. It assigns focus probabilities $a_\theta(i\mid x,q)$ from the pictured question $q$. The additional training objective was
\[
 -\log\sum_i a_\theta(i\mid x,q)\,p_W(y\mid x,i).
\]
The answer-supervised reader uses the reference answer image during fitting. Gold source positions enter only the location-supervised comparator. An exchanged control permutes the two answers within about half of the training pages at each epoch. We cached the frozen training contexts and likelihoods once. A Lean proof verifies the local condition for moving probability mass between two competing locations to improve the marginal likelihood.

To test question order, we composed pairs with the identical multiset of all three-cell windows, including boundaries. The two questions request opposite roles. Mean and maximum pooling of pointwise features of these windows cannot distinguish their order in exact arithmetic. A Lean permutation proof establishes this invariance for folds whose adjacent contributions can be interchanged. The measured probability differences for the matched pooled comparator were below $3.0\times10^{-7}$. We replaced its question convolution and pooling features with a bidirectional recurrent question encoding followed by maximum pooling. Both architectures received the same 16 training question pairs and 1,788 updates. Four contrastive pairs were reserved. To test everyday language, we subsequently froze four additional pairs that avoided the two grammatical role names.

The final deployment uses 1,345,457 learned parameters, including the frozen image writer. Fitting the 164,480-parameter question and focus module took 24.97~s and 2.35~kJ on one RTX~4090~D. GPU allocation peaked at 234,447,360 bytes. The shared emission cache cost a further 5.19~s, and the original writer was fitted separately. Energy telemetry was unavailable for the cache and the initial three fits. For optional lexical decoding, we retained a freely drawn stopped gloss if it belonged to the 2,193 distinct physical gloss sequences from writer training. Otherwise we scored all these sequences, stop included, and drew the most probable under $p_W$. This computation does not change the predicted location.
