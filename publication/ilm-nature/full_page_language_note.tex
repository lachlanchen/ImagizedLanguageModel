\section*{Supplementary Note 51. Full-page execution and a failed unoptimized QA baseline}

We next check the native interface on complete natural-question pages rather
than extrapolating from copying strips. The existing CMRC-derived preparation
has 256 training and 32 article-disjoint held-out questions, paired by passage
with distinct accepted answers. All 288 normal question pages reproduce their
source pixels and geometry exactly. Question-erased controls preserve every
passage pixel and fixed labels; their answer-label height is normalized within
the pair so both erased inputs and seeds are identical. This also changes
layout relative to a shorter original question. Passage-erased controls
preserve the complete original question pixels and position. These interventions
do not establish that the generator reads the remaining content.

The fixed preflight selects the first future-training pair and first held-out
pair in manifest order. Each complete input is $1024\times1024$ at the existing
32-pixel font; no answer-aware crop or text transcript enters generation.
Its earlier balanced checkpoint is specified before observing any QA output,
regardless of the subsequent ink-mass comparison. Native inference reloads
that fixed checkpoint, which has no QA optimizer exposure. Partition labels
identify the prepared future data splits, not prior QA exposure of this model.

The four full input pages encode to $1\times128\times64\times64$ native feature
fields. Posterior mode, patchification and normalization remain unchanged.
Complete reconstructions have thresholded foreground F1 of 97.834--98.180\%
and mean RGB absolute error of 1.039--1.071\%. All four decoded pages are
visually inspected and remain readable. This checks physical representation
on four pages, not perfect preservation of every character, learned reading
by the generator or general script invariance. Codec execution takes
5.401\,s at 3.574\,GiB peak allocation (Fig.~\ref{fig:s28-full-page}).

The real gradient probe uses only the first future-training question pair.
It restores the fixed adapters, starts fresh AdamW at rate $10^{-5}$ and decay
.01, clips at one and executes two actual updates under Note 48's unchanged
objective. Model seed is 20268801; independent trajectory seeds are 20268802
and 20268803. The first differentiable forward is pixel-exact with native
inference before its target is loaded. Prediction-gradient norms at sampling
steps 0, 24 and 49 are .78508, 1.44972 and .12166. Pre-clipping adapter norms
are 2,026.184 and 123.281. All are finite; all 206 adapter tensors change and
optimizer states reach step two. Frozen parameters have no gradients. This
takes 228.879\,s at 12.203\,GiB, and the resulting weights are explicitly
discarded. It verifies actual execution, not semantic improvement.

Separate input-only processes then reload the prospectively fixed earlier
checkpoint, never the discarded weights. They use 50 native steps, guidance
one, one generic condition and $512\times256$ output, without a training
manifest, reference answer, per-question text embedding or recognition reader.
Four normal calls and four held-out control calls are completed. The two
question-erased inputs share pixels and seeds; their actual output tensors
and PNGs are bit-exact. Normal inference takes 94.888\,s, question-erased
inference 48.952\,s and passage-erased inference 48.753\,s, each at
9.536\,GiB allocated peak. Acquisition, earlier training and CPU OCR are excluded.

None of the four normal answers is correct by independent OCR or raw inspection;
all four controls also fail. Outputs copy and corrupt portions of the input
instead of producing the requested answer. The first pair requests the manga
series installment and story description; the second requests a fish's
distribution and type locality. All eight outputs are retained. This is a
small unoptimized baseline, not a complete QA benchmark or a before/after
language-learning comparison. Reference OCR on the broader first-32-training
and 32-held-out canonical answers is only 23/32 and 26/32. Recognizer failures
must not be confused with model errors; raw pages remain necessary.

The completion worker waits for the exact comparison owner to exit before
exporting that complete result and launching these probes sequentially.
All eight generated PNGs and four codec PNGs are authenticated against actual
neural tensors. Raw review follows the immutable producer assessment. No
output is repaired, rerolled or selected from intermediate checkpoints. All
owned processes exit and GPU0 is released. The deployed generator still
retains its externally pretrained 3.900B transformer and 84.046M VAE.
Meaningful QA training, complete held-out paired-question assessment, reliable
writing, historical forms and efficiency at matched language quality remain
unachieved by this writer.
