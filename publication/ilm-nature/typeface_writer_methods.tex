\paragraph{Reading across typefaces while preserving source ink.}
The two writing conditions started from the same interval-distilled checkpoint. Each received 32,457 examples, including 6,076 additional renderings of paired human training questions in Noto Bold, UKai and Noto Serif. A further 34 renderings lacked a required glyph. Both one-epoch fits made 1,638 optimizer updates at 400,724 answer positions. We used the same order and the same learning rates. Only the mapped writer received an additional visual correspondence learned from 23,812 available renderings of 5,958 canonical bitmap identities in the training questions and passages. Its convolutional metric and prototypes were trained for 1,200 batches of 192 images with small translations. We kept the HanaMin family outside adaptation and model selection.

For an incoming bitmap absent from the fixed inventory, the correspondence model computes cosine scores against the prototypes. It uses the winning regular-print embedding if the highest score exceeds or equals 0.65 and exceeds the second score by at least 0.06. Otherwise it uses the inherited visual embedding. The original bitmap remains addressable by the pointer in both cases. For a confidence threshold $\tau$ and required gap $\delta$, a winning score above $\tau+\epsilon$ and a gap above $\delta+2\epsilon$ suffice to preserve acceptance under score perturbations of at most $\epsilon$. Lean checks the finite-score inequalities in common integer units. The perturbation magnitude for a previously unseen font is an empirical quantity.

Each reply had to stop and match one entire reference, allowing each cell to take its canonical or incoming printed form. Separate scores retained the strict typeface requirements. We examined the original paired questions and selected two further human questions per eligible passage from the public CMRC trial file. We excluded training and development fingerprints, repeated question IDs, reserved traditional-script sources and answers outside the visible passage. The local catalogue pairs an attested headword with a distractor supporting both requested stages, records their source labels and presents their original ink to the visual writer. The supplied headword is a retrieval key. The language model sees the retrieved page and the request as images.
