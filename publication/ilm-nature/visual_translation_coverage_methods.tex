\subsection*{Matched supervision across chapters}
We selected one source per training chapter, with 256 candidates in each of four source-length ranges. A local Qwen3-8B model drafted a modern Chinese translation from each classical source. A model editor retained 480, revised 361 and excluded 183. Both writers started from the same 2.33-million-parameter checkpoint. At each of 1,164 AdamW updates, 48 common archive pairs were accompanied by four reviewed targets from each length range. Each arm received 18,624 supplemental exposures. Matched padding gave identical tensor extents per update; target exposure still differed by 10,932 real glyphs. The narrow and broad continuations took 31.5 and 28.4 seconds on a single RTX 4090 D. These durations cover the two fits and exclude previous training, curation and teacher inference.

The fresh archive comprised 192 passages from 161 chapters. No revised target matched an entire held-out input or target in rendered pixels. Six whole readings per available length range and eight numeral substitutions were fixed before fitting. These substitutions used sentence frames absent from training after erasing numerals. One model reader graded deduplicated, shuffled outputs with method identities withheld. We allowed alternative wording, required a modern rendering faithful to the fragment, and scored the specified quantity with its unit or referent separately. Each writer generated up to 192 glyph images; across the 200 inputs, the narrow and broad writers reached this limit before stopping on fourteen and eleven, respectively. Source images, answer streams, reader decisions, checksums and both checkpoints accompany the experiment.
