\section*{Supplementary Note 45. Task memory and contextual image-answer learning}

The component in Note 44 separates physical source-pixel reproduction from
selection of an appropriate answer. Two subsequent observations examine the
conditioning path: a fixed-head task-memory substitution, followed by a
matched comparison of frozen and adapted contextual features. The former
uses the earlier head, whose normal development score is 9/64. It is not a
third arm of the later comparison.

\subsection*{Task-memory substitution with the earlier head}
Let $H_\phi(V,M,G)$ denote the learned distribution over source spans, where
$V$ contains contextual visual descriptors, $M$ contains task-frame memory,
and $G$ contains physical geometry. For two questions $i,j$ about one
passage, the intervention is
\[
 H_\phi(V_i,M_i,G_i)\ \longrightarrow\ H_\phi(V_i,M_j,G_i).
\]
Weights, source pixels, unit boundaries and $V_i,G_i$ remain fixed. This
constructs hybrid features rather than a native encoding of a new image.
Moreover, $V_i$ can contain question information, so the substitution does
not isolate one internal cause of the original errors.

Paired reference-image sets are disjoint and both remain available in each
owning source. Mean span-index total variation is 0.8423 on training and
0.8556 on development. The log odds of the owning versus partner reference
move toward the partner in every one of 32 training and 64 development
substitutions. Original owning references outrank their partners in 45/64
development cases, but only 9/64 selections beat all competing spans.
Two-reference preference therefore cannot replace complete-answer scoring.

The diagnostic replays all 96 original selected images and boundaries and
retains all 192 original/substituted images. A separate NumPy/PIL check
reconstructs them from source columns and recomputes probabilities, paired
counts and total variation. Cached CPU probabilities differ from the native
GPU values by at most $4.293\times10^{-6}$; they are not bit-identical.
The audit makes 192 head calls, no source-encoder calls and no optimizer
updates. Its 39.644-second invocation includes 1.631 seconds setup and peaks
at 0.551 GiB RSS, excluding the earlier 7.531B source encoding and the
separate verification duration. It rejects exact insensitivity of this
memory branch on these cases without repairing the reader.

\subsection*{Matched contextual adaptation}
The later comparison fixes the source-pixel motor, admitted data, draw
schedule, initial head and final-checkpoint rule. Both arms optimize the
image-level likelihood from Note 44, summing probability over all admissible
source crops that equal a target under the declared exterior-white
normalization. Control learns the 2,378,370 head parameters from authenticated
frozen features. Adaptation also learns rank-eight query, value and output
adapters in all 36 contextual layers:
\[
 W_\ell^{\mathrm{eff}}
   =W_\ell+\frac{\alpha}{r}U_\ell D_\ell,
 \qquad r=8,\quad \alpha=16 .
\]
The up matrices initially vanish. The 6,193,152 additional parameters give
8,571,522 optimized parameters in adaptation. Both complete runtimes contain
7,531,277,171 parameters and external Qwen3-VL pretraining. Vision and base
weights remain fixed. Contextual base storage is BF16, with FP32 contextual
computation and learned parameters; the original BF16 feature boundary is
preserved.

Each arm sees the same four shuffled passes over 3,193 admitted Chinese QA
images and 64 training copies: 13,028 draws, batch four by sequential
accumulation, and 3,257 updates. Eighteen QA records are excluded before model
calls. Head/adapter peak learning rates are $10^{-4}/10^{-5}$, with 100
warm-up updates and cosine decay to one tenth of peak. AdamW uses
$(0.9,0.95)$, decay 0.01 and joint norm clipping at one. The intervention is
this complete regimen, including the changed clipping parameter set.
Development does not select a checkpoint.

Of the 64 matched normal questions, 20 are correct in both arms, six only
after adaptation, three only in control, and 35 in neither. Both pass the
30/32 copy floor and 25-percentage-point question-erasure gain floor, but
fail the 48/64 natural-answer and 20/32 paragraph-pair floors. Gains of
three answers, zero pairs and zero copies miss the separate improvement
rule of at least eight extra answers, four extra pairs and no more than
two lost copies. These floors are decision rules, not significance tests.
Normal unordered-column F1 is 61.622\% and 67.444\%; it does not measure
semantic correctness or readability.

Question erasure preserves the correct ink but moves other ink in 11/64
inputs. Passage erasure removes every correct source crop, so its zero
score is forced by this motor's support. Public development is repeatedly
reused and is not a sealed test.

\subsection*{Execution, costs and scope}
Independent verification authenticates all schedules, four draws per
training ID, finite final Adam moments at step 3,257, and both final
checkpoints. Fifteen head tensors change in each arm. Control changes no
contextual tensor; adaptation changes all 216 adapter tensors. No optimizer
update is replayed. All 512 native arm/input calls retain original PNGs.
Independent scoring reconstructs every output from declared source columns
and reproduces the aggregate and paired decisions. Eight additional
standalone calls reproduce pixels and events exactly without opening
training manifests, a feature cache or the original head checkpoint.

Original setup takes 93.118 seconds. Feature preparation takes 3,387.907
seconds, with 2,937 new encodings and 320 authenticated reused payloads.
Control/adapted optimization takes 126.665/30,856.570 seconds, at
15.265/18.075 GiB peak allocated and 15.504/22.082 GiB reserved memory.
Control retains the full resident body during its cached-head optimization.
For four standalone calls, setup plus inference is $77.695+5.022$ seconds
for control and $76.854+5.056$ seconds for adaptation; both peak at 17.948
GiB allocated. These local costs exclude external pretraining.

Assessment is interrupted four times across five controller invocations.
The prescribed calls accumulate as 218 control, then 38 control and 72
adapted, then 184 adapted. All 328 prior indexed outputs survive the final
recovery unchanged. Complete assessment and additional reload duration are
unmeasured. The retained 45.634-second control interval covers only 38 calls;
223.855 seconds covers only the last 184 adapted calls. A completed-control
resume records almost no current-invocation work, not the cost of 256 calls.
The public execution notes retain these timing boundaries. The termination
sender remains unknown. All model stages use one RTX 4090 D.

The source-copy law assigns zero probability to answers absent from its
admissible crops. Neither arm qualifies a general reader or independent free
image-language generator, and this regimen does not qualify for scale-up.
Historical writing and handwriting remain untested. CMRC2018 retains
Cui et al.\cite{cmrc}, CC-BY-SA-4.0 attribution; Qwen3-VL retains Alibaba,
Apache-2.0 attribution.
