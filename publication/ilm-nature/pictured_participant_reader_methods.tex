\subsection*{Question-conditioned participant scores}
The reader retained the calibrated action decision and 782,171 inherited network parameters. An additional 103,426 parameters scored candidate spans. The residual network aligns projected source states to question states, joins the source, attended question and their elementwise product, and applies three width-five convolutions with dilations 1, 3 and 9. For a span from $i$ to $j$, the new score is the inherited score plus learned start, end and start-conditioned length terms. For a role target $t$ with competing participant $r$, we minimized $-\log p(t\mid P,Q)+\log(1+\exp(s_r-s_t))$ over every legal source span, retaining the original greedy image decoder. DRCD rehearsal used marginal likelihood over its accepted intervals. The 1,080 updates per arm covered 17,440 grammatical questions, 69,120 constructed-event questions and 25,919 rehearsal questions. The matched fit took 44.2 seconds and peaked at 178 MiB allocated on one RTX 4090 D, excluding prior learning and data preparation.

Chinese GSD r2.16 supplied the original sentences and grammatical relations (\url{https://universaldependencies.org/treebanks/zh_gsd/}, CC BY-SA 4.0). Its annotations were converted from an earlier manually annotated scheme. Training, development and test followed the official partitions. Constructed training events crossed 24 verbs, six syntactic frames, alternate names and simplified and traditional renderings. The control exchanged the two targets with independent probability one half per training pair. We carried out 5,000 bootstrap resamples grouped by sentence or independently written situation. Chinese XWinograd (\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/Muennighoff/xwinograd}, CC BY 4.0) served as a subsequent external test. The pictured passage retained its printed blank and the question asked for its referent. All gold referents occurred as a contiguous sequence of input glyphs. Seven items gave identical option images, and we retained all 504 reference responses. No options were shown to the model.
