\paragraph{Matched full-corpus training and a controlled speech construction.}
Both arms started at the common causal writer trained on 55,872 archive pairs. For each target cell, the single-path objective scored its aligned source position when copying, its indexed exemplar when retrieving ink, or its supervised drawing descriptor. The all-path objective scored the sum of the probabilities of all source positions and exemplars with exactly matching pixels. When no such path existed it supervised the shared drawing descriptor. This is a lower bound for matching known ink because it omits any contribution from the drawing distribution. Descriptor likelihood for residual drawings is a separate approximation to physical bitmap likelihood. Both arms retained the same END, alignment and progress losses. Each took 2,368 AdamW steps, four passes over 1,762,187 target cells per pass, a learning rate of 0.0003 and the identical minibatch schedule. The runs took 50.9 and 50.4 seconds, with allocated tensor peaks of 444 and 441 MiB on one RTX 4090 D. These measurements exclude common parent training, raster preparation and evaluation.

The test excluded the earlier 519 evaluation chapters as well as prior training and evaluation chapters and exact source-image duplicates. All 1,024 images were decoded freely with identical certified pooling and a 96-cell ceiling. We preserved whole output streams and resampled chapters in 2,000 paired draws. One study-involved model reader graded randomized complete answers, including the reference, before seeing method assignments. Every displayed character in this masked reading was subsequently rendered and matched against the original output cells. The eight supplementary speech prompts were selected after examining the list of exact translations. A second masked reading separately scored speaker, permission or refusal, and the complete modern utterance. A literal speaker designation could preserve identity yet remain ambiguous in modern prose. One initially prepared transcription packet for these eight cases paired the wrong corpus field with image labels. Before grading, we discarded its transcriptions, retained the original model images and verified every corrected character by rendering it back to the exact saved pixels.
