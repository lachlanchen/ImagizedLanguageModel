\subsection*{Paired likelihoods on shared passage images}
We retained every DRCD question pair for which the whole set of recorded answers met the shared-glyph overlap constraint. Both arms began with the same 628,104-parameter writer. For each question, $s_c$ and $s_f$ denote mean log probabilities of the stopped canonical response and its sibling under their own physical histories. The additional training term was $0.5\log(1+\exp((s_f-s_c)/0.35))$, averaged over both questions. We computed the same scores in the control with zero ranking weight. Both arms used eight passes and 2,376 updates. On one RTX 4090 D, the two fits took 79.8 and 69.6 seconds, respectively, with peak PyTorch allocations of 1.36\,GiB. From the shared parent's training set we found no exact held-out question and no exact match to 7,507 aligned 32-glyph anchors sampled from the 521 development passages. Shorter shared phrases and paraphrases can survive this screen.
