\subsection*{Matched training with traditional Chinese passages}
We retained 21,934 DRCD training questions on 6,579 passages and 3,505 development questions on 994 passages. The original ink was preserved. For overlap screening we normalized case and typography, converted writing to simplified Chinese and removed non-alphanumeric characters. We rejected shared article titles, duplicate normalized passages and candidate passages containing a 32-character window sampled at stride 32 in a reserved text. The training exclusions covered the DRCD development and test releases and both CMRC releases. The DRCD development exclusions covered CMRC. Unrenderable source glyphs excluded 136 training and 19 development questions. Overlap screening removed another 4,866 training questions. Independent preparation checks verified every source image, every question image and all 42,339 training and 7,687 development answer intervals.

Both fits used the preceding visual representation and fixed ordered planner. The reader maximized marginal likelihood over every accepted physical span, including alternatives of different lengths. Each training epoch had 21,934 common CMRC presentations and 21,934 substitution positions. Replacements in the replay arm were matched by passage-length rank. Both arms received the same batch membership schedule, 175,472 presentations and 5,504 AdamW updates over four epochs. The learning rate was 0.00020 and the gradient norm limit was 2. Mixed training used 81.80 million source-cell presentations and 87.40 million after batch padding, versus 86.31 and 95.01 million under replay. Fit times were 43.0 and 45.1 seconds respectively on one RTX 4090 D. The complete serial preparation, fits and scoring took 138.2 seconds. Mixed-arm peak allocated PyTorch memory, including evaluation, was 277.3 MiB. These times exclude the inherited reader's training and the original corpus rendering.

We evaluated final weights and reread the 214,503 saved streams across the three arms. The uncertainty calculation drew 379 DRCD development articles with replacement 10,000 times and retained every question belonging to each sampled article. Containment required a whole contiguous accepted bitmap at cell boundaries and a stop. It is a measure of visible reference inclusion; a longer response may be accurate, incomplete or contradictory. Standalone CPU inference reproduced all three models' archived images for the first mixed-over-replay gain and the first remaining error. The unit of evaluation is a reference-matched span or an ordered pair, and the model emits only available source ink.
