\subsection*{Answer spans in pictured passages}
We used SQuAD 1.1 (Rajpurkar et al., 2016, doi:10.18653/v1/D16-1264), preserving the official development set and reserving training titles whose SHA-256 integer was divisible by 17. We retained printable ASCII passages of up to 192 whitespace-delimited words and questions of up to 32. Whitespace was standardized before rendering. The training target was the first annotated interval of at most 16 words with fewer than 24 cells trimmed from each boundary word. All recorded answers were retained for evaluation. After screening, 53,867 training questions from 420 articles were eligible. Twenty reserved and 48 development articles remained. Held paragraphs identical to training paragraphs were excluded.

A compact text teacher (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) was tested before fitting. It ranked the correct supplied English action names on all questions for eight of 21 previously unseen-question Kyoto clauses and 35 of 118 dictionary-description clauses. The preceding optical encoder scored four and 48. The registered rule required an improvement on both sets, so we fitted the passage reader with direct interval supervision.

Visible blank cells delimit English words. The inherited 1,900,594-parameter encoder maps word pixels to 50 features. A fixed projection of four boundary glyphs and word length supplies another 65. Local convolutions and dot-product attention combine each passage position with aligned and pooled question features. A start score and end score define a joint interval score $s_i+e_j$, with $0\leq j-i<16$. Two further heads score within-word trims subject to a nonempty interval. Training minimizes the sum of the two endpoint cross-entropies and 0.35 times each trim cross-entropy at the true endpoints. We fitted 235,587 parameters in 4,000 AdamW steps of 48 examples. On one RTX 4090 D the additional optimization took 31.2 seconds (3.88 kJ); preparing the training image features took 5.49 seconds (0.47 kJ). These device-counter measurements exclude the earlier word-encoder fit, teacher qualification and evaluation. The output consists of source pixels between the selected boundaries. Eight fixed-order outputs were independently reproduced from the saved page and question PNGs on CPU, including failed cases.
