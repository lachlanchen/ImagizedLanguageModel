\paragraph{Rereading a fallible draft.}
A visual editor read both the classical source and a first draft in modern Chinese. We trained it on 55,872 drafts generated outside their own chapters. It could preserve any draft cell or replace it with a short observed image sequence. On 1,024 new passages it changed 189 drafts. Glyph edits fell from 13,356 to 13,307 over 23,903 reference cells, a reduction of 0.205 percentage points (chapter-bootstrap interval 0.088--0.326). Exchanging the source for a different passage of equal length while keeping the draft fixed raised the count to 13,488 and changed 399 answers. The source thus affected physical decisions beneficially on average. The editor gained one exact reference, a biographical statement identifying a man as Mongolian, and lost two (Fig.~\ref{fig:pictured-revision}).

The small physical improvement did not extend to complete meaning. Among eighteen changed passages reviewed from distinct chapters, three initial drafts and one revision were acceptable. The editor lost both a terse date and a flood report. Five changes introduced a temporal error and one introduced a grammatical error. On a separate eighteen-passage sample chosen by source markers, neither the drafts nor their revisions produced a fully faithful modern gloss, compared with eleven of the references. Four passages occurred in both readings. On the same marker-selected passages a pretrained Qwen3 8B text translator produced ten faithful answers. The declared promotion criterion failed.

We separately presented the same eighteen passages as text and as pictures to a single pretrained Qwen3-VL 8B model. The reader accepted twelve translations from text and eight from the images. In a separate transcription request the model reproduced sixteen complete pictured sources exactly after whitespace removal. In one case the image was transcribed accurately, yet the translation interpreted a classical metaphor for the law as a prohibition on computer networks. These measurements distinguish visual recognition from interpretation even within the same pretrained system. Both translation conditions retained the model's pretrained text decoder.
