\subsection*{Question-conditioned scoring of physical answer locations}
Each frozen reader ranks valid start and end positions, with spans limited to 48 glyphs. We collect up to its first eight spans, deduplicate locations and retain separate occurrences of identical ink. Each location receives the four readers' log posterior scores and the question-conditioned page summary, start, end and mean contextual states. A fixed signed projection reduces each contextual state from 768 to 48 coordinates. A learned gate mixes the four posteriors and a residual network scores each location. Together they have 21,395 trainable parameters. For a set of physical positions $A$ and reference strips $Y$, the loss is
\[
-\log \frac{\sum_{a\in A:\,\mathrm{ink}(a)\in Y}\exp s(a)}{\sum_{a\in A}\exp s(a)} .
\]
Uncovered questions remain in evaluation denominators and supply no training gradient. Inference sums probability over positions yielding the same strip. Lean verifies that adding candidates preserves availability, that splitting a weight among identical images preserves their mass, and that an added competitor can change the winner. A second proof establishes that a deterministic writer with equal encoded inputs cannot satisfy mutually exclusive output obligations on two pages. These results apply to the decoding and input constraints.

After screening, training contained 6,453 original human questions and 9,728 paired role questions. We removed 547 training and 48 validation records by source ID, official split, reserved article title or normalized passage overlap. Epoch selection gave equal weight to 464 natural validation questions and 96 complete four-question validation boards. The validation board subjects were absent from the earlier local specialist training. The depth-one fit restricted the same scorer to the first proposal from each reader. The shuffled fit randomized valid target positions within questions while retaining target counts. Preparing the frozen features took 82.2 seconds on one RTX 4090 D and fitting all three heads for 16 epochs took 20.1 seconds, excluding inherited pretraining.

The replacement development questions require an annotated answer within the first 416 rendered source cells. We used one question per paragraph, excluding previous local selector samples and the reserved IDs, titles and long matching segments before rendering. Upstream pretraining exposure remains unknown. We did not repeat the archived human score on its 19 protected DRCD inputs. For the free-writing comparison we took the first 64 eligible DuIE records with an authored clause containing both names. For the date test we took the first 32 eligible passages with a unique four-digit year and incremented it by one. A response must include the requested four digits and exclude the competing four digits. The writer received the correct invariant factual triple; the direct reader received each complete page and the same pictured year question. The direct reader extracts a bounded strip and has no learned stopping decision.
