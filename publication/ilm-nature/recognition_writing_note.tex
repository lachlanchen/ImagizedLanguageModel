\section*{Supplementary Note 47. Recognition supervision and autonomous writing loss}

The paired-image writer in Note 40 uses the externally pretrained undistilled
FLUX.2-klein-base-4B and its native VAE. A fixed generic condition is compiled
offline; changing question content enters only through the image branch.
The next experiment adds a frozen training-only reader, a pinned GOT-OCR
conversion\cite{gotocr}. Its measured parameter count is 560,528,640; it is
absent from native inference. Its vocabulary and teacher-forced labels are
supervision tools, not the student's deployed output interface.

For normalized native target features $y$, image-reference features $x$ and
independent $\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$, the assisted objective is
\begin{align}
 z_s&=(1-s)y+s\epsilon,\qquad s\sim U[0,1],\\
 \widehat y_s&=z_s-sv_\theta(z_s,x,s),\\
 \mathcal L_{\mathrm{assisted}}
 &=\operatorname{mean}\bigl[(v_\theta-(\epsilon-y))^2\bigr]\nonumber\\
 &\quad+0.05\,\mathbf1_{s\leq0.5}
 \operatorname{CE}\!\left(R(D_{\rm disp}(\widehat y_s)),t+\mathrm{EOS}\right).
\end{align}
Here $D_{\rm disp}$ uses native unpatchification, inverse batch normalization,
frozen VAE decoding, RGB normalization and clipping. $R$ is the frozen reader,
and $t$ is the training reference
string. CE scores only answer positions, including EOS. Differentiable image
preprocessing permits input gradients without updating reader weights.

Both arms start with fresh rank-16 adapters, use the same first 32 copying
pairs for 512 updates, and retain batch one, seed 20268321, AdamW learning
rate $10^{-4}$, decay .01 and clipping one. All 512 sampled IDs and noise
times match the earlier flow-only control. A preflight reproduces its native
flow term exactly before adding recognition. Complete independent CPU OCR
scores are 6/32 seen and 9/32 held out, versus 6/32 and 10/32 for flow only
(Fig.~\ref{fig:s24-recognition}). Clean references score 27/32 and 28/32.
Visible omissions and repetitions independently fail the 30/32 writing floor.
One run per arm does not establish that all recognition supervision is
ineffective. The treatment takes 374.748\,s and 12.268\,GiB peak allocated
memory, versus 287.182\,s and 8.437\,GiB for control; acquisition and separate
assessment are excluded.

Complete clean-reader calibration reads 62/64 targets exactly, Chinese 32/32
and English 30/32. Mean blank-minus-clean target NLL is 9.785251 nats. The
first calibration fails preprocessing agreement. A recorded correction
matches native Pillow's intermediate clipping with a continuous separable
resize surrogate, preserving the original thresholds. Both calibration
reports remain available. Frozen-reader pixel gradients and the student's
recognition-to-velocity gradients are finite and nonzero.

The added recognition term is active on 255/512 updates and averages
.031519 nats. A separate complete diagnostic scores actual autonomous
decoder tensors without changing any writer weights. Mean target NLL is
1.041635 on seen outputs and .574959 on held-out outputs, against .033706
and .043822 on the clean references. Assisted updates have noisy correct
answers and changing checkpoint states; free outputs use the final state
and independent noise. These measurements do not isolate one causal factor.

The reader is not an independent judge. Its greedy transcripts are exact
on 14/32 seen and 19/32 held-out outputs, exceeding the separate CPU counts
without making the pages correct. In held-out case 026 it returns the
expected string despite a visibly duplicated line; target NLL is .042427
versus .000192 for its clean reference. It similarly overlooks a repeated
character in case 002. These blind spots motivate retaining direct physical
image supervision rather than treating reader reward as writing accuracy.
All 64 raw outputs, all transcripts and hashes of actual neural pixels are
retained. No natural QA is optimized or assessed in this copying experiment.
