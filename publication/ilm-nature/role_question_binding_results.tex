\paragraph{Reading classical role questions.}
We reserved 28 excerpts from Liu Yiqing's \textit{A New Account of Tales of the World}. Each had two people, two questions and an edited version in which the names exchanged places. A complete result required four correct image strips for one language. We fine-tuned the previous small reader on 2,277 naturally paired bilingual questions and 349 pairs made from separate supporting excerpts. The additional objective matched the pictured English question to the corresponding Chinese question among the two about that page. The aligned reader gave 34 of 112 correct Chinese answers and 26 English answers. Training on the same human answers without the alignment loss gave 34 and 22. The previous reader gave 36 and 33. All three readers completed zero classical contrasts in either language. Text Qwen3-8B completed 25 of 28 in Chinese and 17 in English (Fig.~\ref{fig:role-question-binding}). A generic English question elicited 26 correct answers, the same total as the question-dependent student. Aligning the questions improved the training objective but did not establish transferable role assignment.
