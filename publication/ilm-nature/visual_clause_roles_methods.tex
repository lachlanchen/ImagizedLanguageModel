\subsection*{A joint visual reader of predicates and objects}
We used 27,776 distinct clauses from the same six training works. Each supplied the visual positions of a root verb and its direct object. The training set contained 248 fronted objects. For contextual cell states $h_i$, the score for a possible verb at $i$ and object at $j$ was the sum of two learned endpoint scores and a scaled inner product between learned projections of $h_i$ and $h_j$. Padded cells and equal positions were masked. Cross-entropy supervised the complete ordered pair. The pictured question independently selected one endpoint of the highest-scoring pair; the preceding English writer used the visual context at that location. Identical role decisions for two questions cannot recover distinct targets on a single page. We checked this implication and the fixed-order impossibility result in Lean~4.

Uniform and balanced training each used 333,312 clause presentations and 2,604 updates. The balanced schedule included 83,328 fronted presentations. A further control randomly swapped the two endpoints within groups sharing both physical target glyphs. With the balanced passage network frozen, 160 question updates mixed the original questions with reviewed training-only questions generated by a local Qwen3 8B teacher. Duplicate screening removed 25 of 96 generated pairs and editorial review removed 17 more. The full released image reader and writer have 1,401,973 parameters. The balanced arc fit took 16.6 seconds on one RTX 4090 D; the teacher reported 42.9 seconds across twelve preparation calls. These measurements omit earlier training and do not provide a matched-quality efficiency comparison.

We reserved opposite-order excerpts from the \textit{Mozi}, \textit{Zuo zhuan}, \textit{Guoyu} and \textit{Huainanzi}. One of 19 pairs overlapped prior training. Separately, 5,495 treebank clauses remained after visual overlap exclusions. For the changing-role test we matched equal printed target glyphs across distinct pages using content-hash order and disjoint registered glosses. The 429 pairs span 124 distinct glyph bitmaps and 817 different passages. A passage may contribute under another glyph. A context-free baseline counts grammatical uses of each central bitmap in the training set and chooses two different endpoints. It receives the question role directly. We replayed 1,944 free replies from the released reader and writer on CPU and independently recounted the primary scores from the emitted pixels.
