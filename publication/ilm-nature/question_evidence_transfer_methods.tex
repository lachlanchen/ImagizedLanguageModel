\paragraph{A frozen question vector for evidence selection.}
Let $q\in\mathbb{R}^{128}$ be the unit vector learned from parallel question images and $h_i\in\mathbb{R}^{768}$ the frozen reader state at page position $i$. For endpoint $k$ (start or end) we added $(A_k h_i+b_k)^\top B_k q$ to the original endpoint score. The two projections use 96 dimensions per endpoint. Initializing $A_k$ and $b_k$ to zero preserved the original logits. Four passes of the bounded interval likelihood fitted the 172,224 parameters on 22,876 pictured requests per pass. The exchanged condition retained the original question in the page reader and supplied the vector of a different question from the same page to the residual head. At inference all three systems received the genuine request.

We joined English and Chinese questions by identifier on the Chinese-context development split of MLQA (Lewis et al., \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.07475}). Of 504 parallel questions, 68 exceeded the full-context limit, eight failed the font check and 16 lacked a bounded target. The remaining 412 questions span 374 pages. No retained page shared a sequence of at least 47 aligned glyph cells with any of 16,529 known adaptation pages in the overlap screen. This screen does not cover the inherited encoder's pretraining. Complete four-request scoring used the first two questions with distinct answers on each eligible page, giving 32 pairs. Exact bitmap matching differs from MLQA's official normalized scoring.
