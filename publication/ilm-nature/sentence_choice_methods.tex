\paragraph{Matched sentence assignments.}
From 240,000 physically distinct classical Chinese sources and their modern translations we obtained 1,234,724 aligned insertion sites. We matched sites across chapters on the two surrounding classical glyph signatures, two preceding modern signatures and insertion length, requiring different answers. Training used 120,628 nonoverlapping pairs and calibration used 6,720 pairs from separate training chapters. Each site entered at most one pair. The enlarged source pool excludes the chapters of all four preceding phrase evaluations. For an additional holdout we excluded chapters used anywhere in the earlier translation training and test rosters. After excluding 7,221 chapters, 84 eligible source sentences remained in 75 chapters. All were rendered; 73 chapters contributed insertions. We retained every eligible sentence.

Let $s_{ij}$ be the log probability of the recorded phrase for site $i$ under the classical sentence and insertion boundary of site $j$, using the authentic modern prefix of $i$. This probability includes the twelve successive four-pixel patterns at each glyph and every continue or end decision. A 49,408-parameter residual reads the frozen boundary and pooled source states. For a pair of common length $L$, both models minimize $-(s_{11}+s_{22})/(2L)$. The paired model also minimizes $\log(1+\exp[-(s_{11}+s_{22}-s_{12}-s_{21})/(2L)])$. We initialized both residual outputs to zero and trained for three epochs with identical pair order. Calibration selected full residual strength for both. Preparing the frozen trajectories, fitting both residuals and calibrating took 62.4 seconds on one RTX 4090 D, with 1.89 GiB peak allocated device memory. These are incremental costs. The inference model contains 2,100,046 neural parameters, requires 29.8 MB of serialized weights and tables, and expands the count tables to 76.5 MB before inference. The ending-path control holds weights and decision thresholds fixed and applies the source residual independently at the draw and stop computations. All generations feed their own rendered pixels back to the recurrent writer.

The fresh contextual sample includes 24 single-glyph and 24 multi-glyph targets from 48 distinct chapters. A study-involved model reader graded deduplicated, anonymously ordered alternatives with the classical text and preceding and following modern text available. The mapping was joined after the judgments were frozen. Each neural writer's acceptability count includes four answers that differed from the recorded insertion. Independent human adjudication and complete-sentence language quality remain open.

For the first-glyph comparison each of the two recorded first signatures and the previously emitted first signature was scored at the same recipient prefix. The twelve conditional pattern probabilities used each candidate's own within-glyph history. We excluded pairs with identical recorded first signatures. The comparison concerns the writer's 48 measured pixels; exact output correctness was independently recounted on the complete 32-by-32 image.
