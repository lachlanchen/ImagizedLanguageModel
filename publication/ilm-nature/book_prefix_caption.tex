\textbf{A longer pictured prefix influences autonomous writing in two classical Chinese books.} \textbf{a,} The same 28,880 glyphs were predicted with the source history supplied at every step or with each predicted bitmap fed back to the writer. Each neural writer included a learned pointer to previous cells. \textbf{b,} First test passage in each work, followed by its reference and sixteen physical outputs from the visual mixture. Both examples enter short cycles. \textbf{c,} Correct positions after exchanging the first 30 cells with a passage from the other work or restricting the writer to two observed cells at every step. The intervals resample volumes within each work.
