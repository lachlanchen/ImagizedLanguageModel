\spread{One source state across several glyphs}
\noindent A reader can represent a five-letter word at five image locations. If the decoder gives every copy the same attention score, their combined weight becomes five times the original. We tested a correction with two pretrained bilingual translation models as measuring instruments.

Each direction has 472 comparisons from 118 families. The visual writer uses its own bitmap likelihood. The pretrained teacher uses its own piece likelihood.}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\section*{Conserving attention mass}
For source state $j$ with key $k_j$ and value $v_j$, the unnormalized weight for query $q$ is $w_j=\exp(q^\top k_j/\sqrt{d})$. If $r_j$ locations carry that state, adding $-\log r_j$ to the logit of each copy gives $r_j(w_j/r_j)=w_j$. Both $\sum_j w_jv_j$ and $\sum_j w_j$ are preserved. This is the degeneracy correction for a discrete partition sum. A Lean proof verifies the finite numerator and denominator identities for rational masses and signed values.
\section*{Testing the interface}
With exact teacher states and corrected weights, every one of the 480 free outputs matched its original, including one native English output that reached the length limit. All 6,388 supplied-prefix top choices matched. The largest observed reference log-probability difference was $1.53\times10^{-5}$. Unweighted copies changed 334 complete outputs. These tests use teacher-provided state identities and boundaries.
\section*{Testing the teacher}
Both teachers recovered all 1,334 natural passage assignments and all 472 polarity changes. In the English-output direction the teacher recovered only 320/472 participant exchanges, against 451 for the visual writer. The comparison cancels preferences depending on either endpoint alone by taking the difference of the two complete assignment scores. The result failed our 90\% criterion for using this teacher as a general sentence-pair judge. The models are the public \href{https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-zh-en}{Chinese--English} and \href{https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-zh}{English--Chinese} OPUS-MT checkpoints.
\end{multicols}

\spread{What survives a learned image representation?}
\noindent We trained a separate 3.22-million-parameter reader in each direction to predict a 512-dimensional source state and a log attention weight from each glyph's pixels. At inference the reader receives the image and its occupied cell count. A fixed translation decoder provides a readout of the resulting field.

Errors are normalized by training-state variance and weighted so that each original source piece contributes equally. \textbf{b}, Complete meanings on the first 12 pairs from each of the four previously assessed works. Exchanged, learned and native source representations use the same pretrained piece decoder. ILM denotes the independent role-trained bitmap writer. Quantized Qwen receives the source text.}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\section*{Testing meaning}
The learned readers received 3/96 source-only credits, against zero for exchanged images, 35 for the intact teaching models, two for the independent ILM and 76 for Qwen. All stopped. The three credited readouts were \emph{Tom asked.}, \emph{I think so too.} and a Chinese rendering of \emph{What way?} The work-resampled 95\% interval for the gain over exchanged input was 0--6.25 percentage points. Each optical reader is independently executable. Its diagnostic readout uses a pretrained text-piece decoder with 58.77 million parameters per direction. This dependency and the teachers' prior training matter for any efficiency comparison.
\section*{Separating two errors}
We exchanged predicted and original states and weights independently on the two development books. With original states, learning only the weights cost 0.0094 and 0.0089 nats in forward KL divergence per target decision for English and Chinese output. With exact weights, learned states cost 0.7355 and 1.0015 nats. The field errors were largest on source pieces absent from the training books. These interventions locate the larger loss in the content of the visual state. They motivate measuring state error by the change in the decoder's predictions at real bilingual prefixes.
\end{multicols}
