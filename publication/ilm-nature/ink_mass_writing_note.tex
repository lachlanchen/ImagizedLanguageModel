\section*{Supplementary Note 50. Fixed target ink mass and matched writing improvement}

The balanced continuation fails its Chinese and seen-retention requirements.
We next examine a specific weakness of its physical objective. For a binary
unit error, balanced L1 assigns missing ink weight $1/(2N_{\rm ink})$ and extra
ink weight $1/(2N_{\rm background})$. A large blank page therefore weakens the
relative cost of extra writing. This objective still has the exact reference
as a minimum; it does not mathematically require a duplicate line. A frozen
recognition reader can also ignore extra writing (Note 47).

A CPU-only audit constructs a nonoverlapping duplicate of the complete target
line on each of 64 reference pages, and compares its physical cost with deleting
the answer. These are explicit reference-derived corruptions, not generated
pages. Duplicate-to-deletion cost under balanced L1 has median 1.790\%, range
.837--3.447\%. This diagnoses relative weighting, not a unique cause of the
model's errors. The diagnostic uses clipped, quantized PNGs; actual physical
supervision uses unclipped decoded RGB.

Let $p_j$ be an unclipped predicted RGB sample and $y_j$ its reference, with
$j$ indexing spatial positions and colour channels. Define
\begin{align}
 a(v)&=(1/2-v)/\tau,\qquad t_j=\operatorname{sigmoid}(a(y_j)),\quad \tau=.05,\\
 K(p,y)&=\frac{\sum_j\left[
 \operatorname{BCELogits}(a(p_j),t_j)
 -\operatorname{BCELogits}(a(y_j),t_j)\right]}{\sum_j t_j},\\
 \frac{\partial K}{\partial p_j}
 &=\frac{t_j-\operatorname{sigmoid}(a(p_j))}{\tau\sum_k t_k}.
\end{align}
This is summed Bernoulli KL normalized by fixed target ink mass. The same
invertible transformation is applied to prediction and reference; the
reference remains the unique optimum in real arithmetic. No predicted mass
enters the denominator, and unclipped errors retain gradients. Finite differences
agree with autograd, with zero loss and gradient at the reference. These are
standard loss calculations, not a new learning theorem. The new diagnostic
duplicate/deletion ratio is 85.857\% at the median, range 82.079--91.566\%.
This does not prove that optimization will correct writing.

We test the complete replacement in two matched 32-update arms. Both restore
the balanced checkpoint's weights and step-160 AdamW states, then execute the
same full permutation of 32 inputs: sixteen Chinese and sixteen English.
Model seed is 20268601; CPU order and noise streams use 20268602 and 20268603.
Every optimizer state ends at step 192. Source weights, frozen VAE and reader,
rank-16 adapters, rate $10^{-5}$, decay .01, clipping one, native input encoding,
all 50 differentiable steps, guidance one and $512\times256$ output remain
common. Targets enter only the final losses after independent-noise generation.
No natural QA, extra font or new writing string is optimized.

Control retains reader CE plus $10B(p,y)$ from Note 48. Treatment replaces only
the physical term with $\lambda K(p,y)$, keeping reader CE unchanged.
The coefficient $\lambda=.4035679875746343$ is fixed before optimization as the
median training-reference ratio $10B(\mathrm{blank},y)/K(\mathrm{blank},y)$.
No held-out score selects it. This calibration matches a deletion-cost scale,
not parameter-gradient norms. The comparison isolates this replacement bundle;
normalization, curvature and interaction with clipping are not separated.

All prospective exploratory conditions pass: at least 6/16 held-out Chinese
and two above control; at least 13/32 seen strings without control regression;
and at least 13/16 held-out English without control regression
(Fig.~\ref{fig:s27-ink-mass}). Seen English nevertheless decreases by one.
Seen paired changes are four treatment-only and two control-only exact strings;
held-out changes are six and three. This is one deterministic schedule on
reused development data without independent-seed replication. OCR and font
recognition can contribute to measured changes; clean-reference calibration
remains 27/32 seen and 28/32 held out.

All 64 target/starting/control/treatment panels are inspected. Treatment removes
the control's extra instruction line in seen case 012 and quotation mark in
held-out case 000. Errors remain: seen 004 omits characters, 013 and 027 retain
quotation marks; held-out 002 repeats a character, 005 corrupts English letters,
015 loses \texttt{morning} and corrupts \texttt{evening}, 016 retains a quotation
mark, 026 adds a character and 030 copies instructions. More than two obvious
failures per split independently reject the unchanged 30/32 writing floor.
The inspection is not a quantitative manual annotation of every glyph. A
passing exploratory rule permits bounded investigation, not reliable writing
or a semantic-language claim.

Both initial native replay checks pass. The first actual pre-update page is
bit-identical across arms at the same input and seed, with recognition loss
.07570605. Pre-clipping gradient norm is 8.79523 in control and 15.20039 in
treatment. Finite nonzero gradients traverse early, middle and final sampling
steps. All 206 adapter tensors change; frozen parameters have no gradients.
Export reproduces both schedules and authenticates all 64 training PNGs and
128 final PNGs against retained neural tensors. This is artifact verification,
not a second independent neural replay.

Control and treatment training invocations take 1,146.423 and 1,145.598\,s,
at 12.032 and 12.033\,GiB allocated peaks. Separate 64-case inference takes
513.746 and 514.150\,s, at 8.031\,GiB each. Earlier learning, acquisition and
CPU OCR are excluded. The inherited 3.900B transformer, 84.046M VAE, 24.359M
trained parameters and 560.529M training-only reader remain unchanged. Neither
foundation training nor efficiency at matched language quality is established.
