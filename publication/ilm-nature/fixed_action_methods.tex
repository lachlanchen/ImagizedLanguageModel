\paragraph{Fixed-position action exchanges.}
We selected contiguous clauses with distinct named subjects and equal-width action phrases. The pictured questions paraphrased each action. Both name regions were bitwise identical between original and exchanged worlds. We also reversed clause order, giving eight requests per excerpt and language. The case order, source hashes, rendered image hashes, weights and outcomes were specified before evaluation. The assigned reader and its controls selected freely among all source strips of up to eight cells. A separate calculation compared the two annotated name scores. In Chinese these were positive at 115/128 and 117/128 positions. In English they were positive at 64/128 and 62/128. All 2,048 physical outputs were independently checked and rescored. CPU inference reproduced 511 of 512 assigned-reader bitmaps. One English output changed on the reversed page and both responses scored zero. Confidence intervals used 10,000 paired resamples of whole original excerpts within each book.
