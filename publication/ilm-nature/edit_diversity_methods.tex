\paragraph{Sentence diversity and the reach of the decoder.}
We hashed rows from the original training chapters into four blocks of 48,000 new classical--modern pairs. We excluded previous source strings and raster sequences, and prohibited duplicate physical sources across blocks. The two additional cohorts comprise fresh sentences from disjoint chapter sets outside training. Of their 388 and 401 chapters, 381 and 398 appeared in earlier evaluations. An initial attempt to exclude all previously evaluated chapters yielded only 85 discovery rows. We revised the split before fitting or scoring. We retained five and six unrenderable requests in the exact-answer denominators.

The two runs started from identical weights. Each included 192,000 translation and 48,000 quotation presentations over 1,920 AdamW updates. The first 480 updates yielded byte-identical tensor values. We held all encoder, renderer and insertion-count parameters fixed. The two local count rules saw the common 48,000 parent-training pairs and the distinct additional pairs available in each arm. We calculated paired edit-rate intervals by resampling chapters 2,000 times and recounted complete translations from emitted bytes and stop traces. The experiment used one seed per condition.

For source length $n$ and permitted insertions $b_i$ at each cursor, including end of source, the largest possible output length is bounded by $U=\min(192,n+\sum_{i=0}^{n} b_i)$. Copy or replacement consumes one source position and adds one output. An insertion consumes one allowance and a deletion adds no output. Thus $[|y|-U]_+$ is a lower bound on edit distance to reference $y$ for every choice of drawing. We also counted physical source multisets with distinct reference sequences. An elementwise sum of ink across a stack maps all permutations of that stack to the same input. The multiset count gives a lower bound on collisions under this projection.

In the follow-up we set $b_i=\min\{k:F_i(k)\geq0.90\}$ using the unchanged learned count distribution $F_i$. This is the minimizer of asymmetric absolute count error at an under-allocation to over-allocation cost ratio of nine to one. The causal editor still chooses whether to use each permitted insertion. We verified individual generated lengths against their bounds and recomputed edit distances from the saved pixels. The threshold was fixed before generation.
