# Attribution for pictured treebank examples

The natural participant-reading and localization archives contain UD release 2.15 sentence images, head-word crops and associated sentence IDs. The images are typeset versions of the upstream texts. Model predictions select parts of those images. The source-specific terms and underlying text credits continue to apply to these examples and their presentation in the manuscript. The full CoNLL-U files used to prepare them are available from the original publishers.
