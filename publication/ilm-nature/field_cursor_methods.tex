\subsection*{Latent field positions and sequential evidence copying}
We used the original dialogue splits of \href{https://aclanthology.org/2020.tacl-1.19/}{CrossWOZ}. Eligible examples contained one supplied venue and eight possible scalar properties. The natural fitting set had 5,097 single- and 368 two-field turns. We also joined pairs of independently worded training questions and their human answers about the same venue, yielding 45,202 ordered combinations before sampling. The two training tasks each used 2,400 batches of 48 on a fixed schedule, comprising one half single-field, one quarter natural two-field and one quarter joined presentations. These fitting examples recur. The strict test had 515 single- and 37 two-field turns. Times and energy refer to model fitting, with downstream exploratory controls and grading metered separately.

A local convolution maps pooled visual cells to field scores $z_{ti}$. Over $n$ nonblank query positions a singleton receives $\log\sum_t \exp z_{ti}-\log n$. For distinct visible fields $i,j$ the ordered score is
\[
 s_{ij}=b+\log\sum_{u<v}\exp(z_{ui}+z_{vj})-\log {n\choose2}.
\]
Cumulative log sums over the first position evaluate the marginal without enumerating position pairs. We normalize scores across legal singleton and ordered-pair plans. Fitting uses the construction order for joined examples and the printed order for natural questions that explicitly name each field once. For other double questions we sum the probabilities of both permitted orders. This use of latent unsegmented positions is related to \href{https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~graves/icml_2006.pdf}{Graves et al.}, with global normalization over a finite field schema here.

The compact writer reads the visible domain, copied field, current field and next field as pictured labels. A recurrent state also sees the preceding emitted glyph action. Each source-value action emits the current span and advances one position. Ordinary glyphs are chosen from a training-derived bitmap bank. The name action copies the venue name. The writer learns from the exact surrounding human wording and chooses its own lexical material. A legal stop consumes the entire inferred plan. At a 120-action ceiling we reserve sufficient steps to emit any outstanding source spans and stop. The earlier transformer comparison retained separate beams by completed pointer count, related to \href{https://aclanthology.org/P17-1141/}{grid beam search}. A Lean proof characterizes the accepted pointer sequences, including arbitrary interleaved drawings. It does not guarantee that the printed predicate agrees with the copied field.

For the explicit attachment check we masked exact source values and localized their nearest governing field cue within the clause. This denies credit to valid implicit paraphrases and can overlook other linguistic errors. We preserved an anonymous local Qwen review of the earlier arms. It accepted several visibly incorrect value attachments, including outputs from independent clause generation, so it cannot supply human adjudication. Each held card was replayed through the public CPU PNG interface. All 552 field decisions and full packed image streams matched the GPU evaluation.
