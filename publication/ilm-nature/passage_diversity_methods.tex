\paragraph{Training exposure and target frequency.}
Both further fits started from the released joint reader and writer. Hash-selected source groups supplied 90,300 eligible new windows after overlap and rendering checks. We uniformly sampled 71,439 without replacement. Each arm saw four passes over 142,878 positions in the same shuffled order. The second half of the training rows contained either the original 71,439 passages or the additional sample. Each fit comprised 2,980 AdamW updates and 571,512 examples. Evaluation used learned stopping and the same four-path beam. The two fits together took 58.0 seconds including setup, with 280 MiB peak allocated device memory on one RTX 4090 D. These measurements exclude prior training, data preparation and evaluation.

For the subsequent frequency intervention, the loss for each complete pictured training answer with count $c$ received weight proportional to $c^{-1/2}$, normalized to unit mean over all training rows. We retained the matched uniform-weight checkpoint. The weighted fit took 29.8 seconds. Two disjoint eight-bucket source partitions were reserved before their respective experiments, and repeated or previously seen pictured windows were excluded. Rare answers occurred one to twenty times in the combined training data. All reported complete-answer scores require exact pixels, exact length and a stop.
