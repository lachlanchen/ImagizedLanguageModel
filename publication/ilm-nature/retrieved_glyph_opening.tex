\section*{A memory chosen from source ink}
From 497,775 bilingual training pairs we retrieved 32 passages by the cosine similarity of their pictured source strips, weighted by inverse document frequency. The writer saw their paired target strips. At each step, occurrences of its last three emitted glyphs cast votes for the next glyph, including stop. If $n$ occurrences matched, the vote distribution received weight $0.35n/(n+2)$ and the neural prediction received the remaining weight. Both directions selected 0.35 on 72 development pairs. We held all neural weights fixed.

\section*{Complete answers on four further books}
Both credited answers were into Chinese and came from the same economics book. One, concerning wasted resources, was identical with and without memory. The single additional credit for \emph{It runs like this} moved the score from 1/96 to 2/96. The paired interval under resampling of four works was 0.00--3.13 percentage points. The text comparator earned 83 credits and supplied translations earned 61. These are automated judgments. The related writer changed 64 answers, preserved both polarities on 223/236 pairs and all roles on 105/118 sets. The original and random-memory writers preserved 225 and 107. All four visual conditions stopped on every request.
\section*{Correct glyphs along a supplied sentence}
With the true prefix supplied, related memory repaired 65 wrong decisions and spoiled 26 correct ones among 3,619 positions including stop. Random memory repaired 25 and spoiled 26. The added correct decisions yielded one additional credited sentence under free generation. Searching and preparing related supports took 15.20 seconds for 568 requests, compared with 0.99 seconds for batched neural decoding. The two corpus arrays occupy 191.15 MB on disk. CPU replay reproduced 24/24 sampled output images and stops; all 568 source-exchange outputs reproduced their donor's answer. \repo{references/retrieved_glyph_writer_result.md}{Corpus, results and scoring} and \repo{publication/ilm-nature/evidence/retrieved_glyph_writer.zip}{all original outputs}.
