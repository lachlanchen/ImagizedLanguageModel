\subsection*{English supervision of the visual question encoder}
We used the same 8,920 training requests per pass in both arms and varied the question frame and synonyms over twelve passes. In each group of four questions the headword and candidate fields and scripts were fixed. The control shuffled the four questions within the group. The convolutional question encoder, its key projection and pooling were trainable. We froze the passage and output pathways. Each fit used 1,680 updates, 107,040 English examples and matched Chinese dictionary and natural-question rehearsal. The fits took 59.4 and 57.6 seconds on one RTX 4090 D and each allocated less than 0.45\,GiB at peak. A separate Qwen3-8B generated 96 English test requests from a headword, a fact and a script. It saw no answer values or ILM outputs. The 24 test headwords and family split matched the earlier independent Chinese assay. We preserved all requests including one in which the author changed the headword. The primary estimate resampled whole cards.
