\paragraph{Locating an answer with a cross-script question vector.}
We tested whether the correspondence learned between pictured questions could help a reader find the requested fact on a page. A residual endpoint model conditioned on the frozen question vector selected 41 exact excerpts on 412 English MLQA requests, compared with 17 for the frozen parent and 13 for the exchanged-vector control. Relative to the parent, 28 previously missed questions became correct and four became incorrect. The gain was 5.8 percentage points (95\% passage-bootstrap interval 3.3--8.4). On the parallel Chinese requests, both the matched model and parent gave 124 exact excerpts. None of the three readers answered all four requests on any of the 32 MLQA passages with a selected pair of distinct answers. On BiPaR, English exact selections increased from 92 to 151 of 748; Chinese exact selections changed from 329 to 327 (Fig.~\ref{fig:question-evidence-transfer}a).

The independent causal writer gave 75 exact stopped replies in Chinese and 36 in English when supplied with the matched reader's excerpt. With the full page it gave 64 and 26. With excerpts from the frozen parent it gave 75 and 16 (Fig.~\ref{fig:question-evidence-transfer}b). Of the 165 correct matched selections across both languages, the writer lost 67; among the remaining requests it repaired 13. On the classical packet the reader selected both facts under both languages on one passage for the first time. In that passage the Chinese requests yielded two correct written names. One English request produced a wrong stopped reply and the other reached the 48-cell limit (Fig.~\ref{fig:question-evidence-transfer}c).

The residual fit took 24.1 seconds on one RTX 4090 D after 85.4 seconds of frozen feature preparation. Its cached contextual states occupied 10.05 GB of host memory. The two-stage reading and writing system contains approximately 174 million learned parameters across its components. Training the inherited bodies and their upstream text models is outside this adaptation cost. The results demonstrate transfer to visual evidence selection and some complete written answers; unrestricted reasoning and composition still require separate evidence.
