\paragraph{Local physical probabilities and contextual reading.}
For an insertion, the local context contains the flanking source cells and the last two available modern cells. It uses 48-bit measured cell signatures and a separate boundary marker. For each group $r$ of four measured pixels we count its sixteen possible patterns in single, paired, triple and four-cell contexts. Contexts with fewer than two observations back off. Each observed distribution receives four pseudocounts from the preceding level; we average the available estimates at equal specificity. Global frequencies have half a pseudocount per pattern. For pattern $v$ the corrected logit is
\[
  \ell_r(v) + w_r\log\!\left[p_r(v\mid C)/p_r(v)\right].
\]
The neural logit $\ell_r$ depends on the full earlier within-glyph drawing history. Each added count vector pools only one group. We selected each $w_r$ by reference log loss on 500 training chapters excluded from the provisional counts, over thirteen quarter steps from zero to three, then refitted counts on all 296,837 insertion sites. The inherited weights had seen these training chapters. The weights and four-way within-glyph beam were frozen. The stream variant repeats the correction for 330,601 subsequent glyphs and adjusts binary stop log odds from 627,438 post-cell observations. Its context uses the actual newly emitted image. Counts and calibration of the two conditioned stages took 6.8 and 10.5 seconds, excluding shared preparation and prior neural fitting. Lossless packing reduced the three conditioned table arrays from 286.6 to 76.5 MB (decimal), with 18.95 MB on disk. These costs are additional to the visual editor and do not establish a matched-quality saving over text models.

Six of the 1,024 fresh sentence pairs were unrenderable. The 1,018 renderable pairs contained 5,987 aligned insertion sites. Although all fresh sources were deduplicated, 543 of the 549 chapters had contributed other passages to older translation evaluations. The perfect-length counterfactual replays each draw along the true prefix, requiring agreement of complete bitmaps at every step and retaining the 32-cell cap. Reinstating learned endings exactly reproduces the freely generated counts on all cohorts. For the contextual check, we sampled 24 single-glyph and 24 multi-glyph sites from distinct fresh chapters. A study-involved model reader saw classical text, the supplied surrounding modern translation and deduplicated, anonymously ordered fillings. Decisions (acceptable, incompatible or uncertain) were frozen before linking them to systems. The sample is stratified, the surrounding text is supplied and independent human adjudication remains open.
