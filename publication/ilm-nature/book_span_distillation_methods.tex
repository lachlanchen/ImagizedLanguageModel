\paragraph{Distilling source intervals.}
We selected complete renderable passages with two distinct human answer spans: 1,646 passages from the four training novels, 697 from CMRC and 4,096 from DRCD. This yielded 7,070 pairs of Chinese questions, including 2,277 pairs with English versions. The pictured Chinese question and passage fit in the teacher's 512-position window. Reserved book passages, work identifiers and explicit title matches were excluded from student training. The optical teacher inherited a pretrained Chinese QA encoder and the preceding font-invariance adaptation. Exposure during its original pretraining is unknown. The teacher scores a source interval by adding its start and end logits. Each Chinese distribution supervises the corresponding English view when available.

Let $z_i$ index the student's valid source intervals and $p_T(i)\propto\exp(z_i/T)$. The teacher retains its 32 highest-probability intervals at $T=2$. The map $C$ keeps those bins separately and sums all remaining probabilities. For a human answer image $a$, with $I(a)$ the set of intervals reproducing that image, the loss is
\[
  -\tfrac12\log\!\sum_{i\in I(a)}p_1(i)
  +\tfrac{T^2}{2}\,D_{\rm KL}\!\left(Cq_T\,\Vert\,Cp_T\right).
\]
Here $q_T$ is the teacher distribution. The released Lean proof shows that regrouping preserves mass and that any loss observing only a kept mass and a residual is invariant to redistributions within the residual. The human term still distinguishes exact source images within that residual. In the control, both the teacher probabilities and their selected indices come from the other question about the same page. Fresh students used identical initialization, sample order, 4,720 AdamW updates and 149,552 question presentations over eight passes. We selected the final weights. For inference, we summed the probabilities of all occurrences of each distinct bitmap and returned the highest-mass strip. Paired intervals use 10,000 bootstrap samples of passages within each work.

After inspecting the registered two-arm outcome, we specified a third, human-only fit with the same initialization, sample order and updates. Its objective used unit weight on the human image log likelihood. It saw the same pages and questions and required no teacher predictions. The independent authored check retained both versions of each of twelve edited narratives, two questions per version and both languages.
