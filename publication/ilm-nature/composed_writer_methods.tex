\subsection*{Answer composition with a span and raster decoder}
From 40,000 training and 10,000 development DuReader records, we retained 2,565 training questions and 571 held-out questions with short human references and a retrieved excerpt. Retrieval compared the pictured source text and question during offline preparation using normalized textual overlap. The model received binary images on a fixed 32-pixel glyph grid. The training and development splits shared no complete passage or question image. Two distinct answer images occurred in both splits. Counts of attainable references with unrestricted reuse of visible cells, training-answer cells plus visible cells, or any training cells plus visible cells were 271, 560 and 567, respectively. These are physical upper bounds for reproducing one selected human reference; other phrasings may be valid.

The writer used 731,384 trained parameters and a frozen 101,144-parameter reader for passage-boundary features. Supervision assigned the longest available input run to a copy action and requested one image when the required cell was absent. Copied positions trained a start distribution summed over matching intervals and an end distribution conditional on the first such start. A recurrent state received the first, mean and last shape features of each emitted segment. For a writing action, a shared encoder scored 2,692 bitmaps from training answers. The open renderer added learned logits to the selected bitmap and thresholded each pixel. We jointly optimized action, boundary, whole-image classification and binary pixel losses. Eighteen epochs gave 1,458 AdamW updates. The completed fit took 41.1 seconds on one RTX 4090 D and peaked at 120.6\,MiB of PyTorch allocation. An earlier complete fit exited before checkpoint serialization and was repeated; the reported time covers the successful fit only.

We archived all 2,855 autonomous answers from five conditions. The regrader checked termination, exact images, source attribution and missing reference glyphs. Models received their own emitted segment history until stopping or reaching the 64-cell limit. Source copying, training-shape emission and continuous pixel correction were decoded from identical final weights. A cyclic question rotation held each passage fixed. A single model-based reviewer, shown the source, question and outputs with hidden decoder labels, assessed four fixed cases per group for responsiveness and support. This reading measures agreement with the supplied excerpt. It supplies neither an independent human rating nor external fact verification.
