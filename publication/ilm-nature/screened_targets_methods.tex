\paragraph{Paired supervision and one-cell interventions.}
A local quantized Qwen3-8B drafted modern translations from classical sources alone. An editorial model retained 50 of 128 new drafts, revised 51 and excluded 27. Twelve earlier screened targets brought the total to 113. Of these, 112 differ from the paired book in their physical glyph sequence. Starting from the same 2.33-million-parameter ordered-copy writer, each fit replayed 55,872 paired passages once and oversampled the same 113 source rows 18,624 times. The 1,164 matched AdamW updates used 48 replay and 16 additional rows per batch, learning rate 0.0002, identical padding and 910,550 trainable parameters. The two continuations took 29.9 and 30.5 seconds on one RTX 4090 D, excluding teacher inference, preparation, prior fitting and evaluation.

Each deployment received only the pictured source. The fixed assessment contained 118 natural passages from 108 reserved chapters and twelve authored minimal pairs. Four independently selected passages per length band and every pair received a complete reading. A model reader saw randomized answer options without writer names. Frozen earlier readings were reused for identical teacher answers. The reader had prior experience with the system. The teacher saw the same source strings directly and rendered its answers for the glyph-edit metric; training histories and input modalities differ. A source-preserving answer may satisfy the changed detail while failing modern translation. Book references received nine whole-answer credits on the sixteen archival passages under this fragment-only rubric.

For the exploratory numeral probe, we required a uniquely aligned source and target glyph followed by the same unit, rotated the digit in a fixed order and excluded any modified input already present in training. We required the entire revised target bitmap sequence and a learned stop. The displacement probe changes one nonempty aligned glyph without clipping ink and accepts the original or shifted form at its target position. Its image must be absent from the training glyph bank and its prompt absent from the training sequences. Both probes reuse the rest of a supervised sentence. The archive and paired-prompt assessments were fixed before fitting.

The analogy control accepts a unique same-length training example at glyph-edit distance one. The replaced bitmap must occur exactly once in both the source and its stored translation. It abstains on zero or multiple eligible examples. It uses only pictured sequences.
