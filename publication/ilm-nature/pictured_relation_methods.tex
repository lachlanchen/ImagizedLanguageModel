\subsection*{A question-side estimate of the relation}
We masked any occurrence of the training subject in each pictured question and hashed binary sequences of one to three adjacent glyph images into 32,768 indicator features. In inference the mask used the predicted subject. A regularized multinomial logistic regression estimated one of twelve relations. We fitted it to the original 26,890 questions plus 200 explicit questions per relation using training subjects. The same examples, solver, regularization and iteration limit were used with permuted labels. At an estimated probability of at least 0.8, the question reader supplied its relation to the original writer. Otherwise the contextual prediction was retained. The threshold was fixed before evaluation. At least one fit reached the 160-iteration limit. Predictions were evaluated at the returned coefficients. Exact bitmap features restrict transfer to different fonts. We reran both word orders on all 1,280 pictured inputs and scored the generated image and stop signal. The song credit that motivated this study was reported separately from a second song evaluated before and after the two names were exchanged.
