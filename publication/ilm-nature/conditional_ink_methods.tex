\paragraph{Adjacent physical pattern transitions.}
The additional model reads four 48-pixel signatures, four boundary flags and one phrase-onset flag. A 96-unit hidden layer produces eight coefficients for each of eleven transitions. For the previous pattern $a$ and proposed pattern $v$, the addition to the existing next-pattern logit at group $r$ is $\sum_{m=1}^{8} h_{rm}(C) B_{rm}(a,v)$. The $16\times16$ basis matrices are centered along both pattern axes. The first group, physical renderer, sentence reader and learned stop are fixed. At inference each beam branch indexes the matrix with its own preceding group. We trained the residual using reference histories from 558,955 insertion glyphs in three passes and chose one overall strength on 68,483 glyphs in 500 other training chapters. The inherited model had seen those chapters. On a single RTX 4090 D, preparation and fitting together took 23.0 seconds, including 18.7 seconds extracting fixed logits. Peak allocated GPU memory was 543.5 MiB. The 200,892-byte update is additional to the two-million-parameter parent and its local count tables.

The fourth roster contains 1,024 additional sentences from 561 chapters outside training and the three phrase-test rosters. Five pairs lacked a complete rendering; 520 chapters contained other sentences from older translation evaluations. The model reader graded anonymously ordered, deduplicated fillings at 24 single-glyph and 24 multi-glyph reference sites from distinct chapters, before seeing the system mapping. For the separate source exchange we matched sites on all four visual signatures, fixed the recipient's modern prefix and independently changed only the source sentence and its cursor. We scored every reference draw and ending and generated both versions freely. One of 1,572 authentic outputs changed when regrouped into exchange batches; its exact-answer grade was unchanged. No authentic output changed in the other three cohorts. Exact assignment counts use consistent batching on both sides.
