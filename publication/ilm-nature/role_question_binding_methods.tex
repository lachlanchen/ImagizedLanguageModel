\paragraph{Parallel pictured questions.}
The prospective classical excerpts are contiguous selections from the supplied traditional-script edition; each was located once in the source before any test answer was computed. Questions were newly written in both languages. The edited pages exchange the two complete reference names simultaneously. Exact answers can exclude a longer title or a valid alias. These selections are a targeted reading test from one work. Among modern training pages, we collected isolated human who-questions with one source occurrence, retained its sentence and immediate neighbours where available, and paired excerpts with different names and different questions. Neither excerpt or Chinese question could contain the rival name. Both fits began at the previous distilled student's weights. Both trained on the same 126,048 question presentations in 2,628 updates. The existing attention over pictured question glyphs produced one pooled vector per question. Within each passage, the two normalized Chinese and two English vectors formed a two-by-two matrix of cosine similarities at temperature 0.1. We added the mean row-wise and column-wise translation cross-entropy with weight 0.5 to the unrestricted answer-image loss. The control computed the same auxiliary scores with zero weight. Original and edited sheets, all questions and both controls were fixed before fitting. Paired uncertainty was estimated by resampling original excerpts.
