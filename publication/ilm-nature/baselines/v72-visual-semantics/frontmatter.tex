\newcommand{\PaperTitle}{Learning conditional language\\in continuous image space}
\newcommand{\PaperSummary}{Written language includes historical and handwritten forms that fixed character inventories cannot faithfully represent. We investigate language modelling with writing images as input and output on one consumer GPU. The deployed actors use physical rasters without a character vocabulary. A frozen continuous writing codec reconstructs 126 of 128 development blocks exactly, at 99.998\% foreground F1. An inherited 7.539B-parameter actor adapts 9.352M parameters for 10,000 updates on one RTX 4090 D, with 16.965 GiB peak allocated memory. Its autonomous Chinese answers reach only \CodecLanguageFOne\% F1 and zero exact matches out of 128, despite a \CodecLanguageQuestionGain-point effect of question pixels. A fixed diagnostic finds poor predictions even with exact reference history. A subsequent vocabulary-free image interface retains bounded reading: a separate diagnostic text projection yields four of eight exact answers, versus zero with questions erased. This helper result does not establish image writing. We implement a successor that conditions a causal continuous writing distribution on frozen visual semantics and rereads its emitted pixels. Its execution checks pass; full language training and autonomous evaluation remain prospective. The evidence separates rendering, reading and conditional image generation without claiming a general image-language assistant.}
