\newcommand{\PaperTitle}{Learning conditional language\\in continuous image space}
\newcommand{\PaperSummary}{Written language includes forms that cannot be faithfully represented by a fixed character inventory. We study language modelling with writing images as input and output on one consumer GPU. Joint reader--density learning improves exact target likelihood to \JointNll\ versus \ControlNll\ nats per image for matched frozen-reader training, while retaining exact supplied-image copying. Raw-pixel search raises next-image match to \BeamAccuracy\% but produces repetitive streams. We then add a normalized copy distribution over observed prompt images and a learned stopping rule. The 19.01M-parameter model answers 10 of 256 held-out Chinese reading questions exactly, versus one with question pixels blanked and zero for a matched no-copy model. Its raster F1 is \QaFone\%, with a paired question-dependent gain of \QaQuestionGain\ points [\QaQuestionLow, \QaQuestionHigh]. Both questions are answered correctly in only one of 393 shared-paragraph pairs. These results demonstrate limited question-dependent use of writing images, while the registered reading and generation criteria remain unmet. Faithful forms, conditional likelihood and meaningful complete answers require separate evidence; a usable general image-based language model remains unresolved.}
