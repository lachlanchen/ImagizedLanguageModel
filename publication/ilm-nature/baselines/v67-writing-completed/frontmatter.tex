\newcommand{\PaperTitle}{Learning conditional language\\in continuous image space}
\newcommand{\PaperSummary}{Written language includes historical, handwritten and transitional forms that a fixed character inventory cannot faithfully represent. We investigate independent language modelling with writing images as input and output on one consumer GPU. The deployed actors consume and generate physical rasters without a character vocabulary. Complete observed answer blocks enable limited Chinese reading: 25 of 256 development answers are exact, but only four of 393 shared-paragraph pairs have both answers correct. A matched assignment objective subsequently weakens free writing despite measurable question use. We then separate faithful rendering from conditional answer selection. Direct reconstruction fits 63 of 64 training blocks exactly, yet reaches only 56.563\% foreground F1 on 128 broader development blocks. An existing frozen continuous writing codec reconstructs the same development cases at 99.998\% F1, with 126 exact blocks. A complete inherited 7.539B-parameter actor connects contextual states to this separate output space, trains 9.352M parameters and passes 24 execution checks at 16.953 GiB peak allocated memory. These reconstruction and execution results do not establish autonomous language. They identify a usable writing interface and a concrete conditional-learning experiment, while useful general image-language generation and historical-form reasoning remain open objectives.}
