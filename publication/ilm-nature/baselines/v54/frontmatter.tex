\newcommand{\PaperTitle}{Learning conditional language\\in continuous image space}
\newcommand{\PaperSummary}{Written language includes forms that cannot be faithfully represented by a fixed character inventory. We study language modelling with writing images as both input and output, continuous internal states and one consumer GPU. A causal Chinese raster reader ranks the next glyph at 19.97\% top-1 on 2,048 development windows, compared with 12.26\% for a bigram control and 18.36\% after shuffling earlier history. Converting that state into readable continuation remains difficult: a directly conditional four-glyph diffusion model reaches only \BlockIdentity\% block identity. An exact autoregressive raster density subsequently preserves all 256 supplied images under greedy copying and reaches stochastic-copy F1 \DensityCopyFone. This 18.71M-parameter runtime trains 2.43M density parameters for 8,000 updates in \DensitySeconds\ seconds. Natural-image likelihood is \DensityNll\ nats per glyph under ordered history and \DensityShuffledNll\ after shuffling, with no resolved earlier-history advantage. Autonomous first-sample identity is only 4/256. The results establish reliable visual copying and tractable image likelihood, while exposing the unresolved coupling between contextual prediction and generated language. They do not establish a broadly capable language model.}
