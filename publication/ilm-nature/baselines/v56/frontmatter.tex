\newcommand{\PaperTitle}{Learning conditional language\\in continuous image space}
\newcommand{\PaperSummary}{Written language includes forms that cannot be faithfully represented by a fixed character inventory. We study language modelling with writing images as input and output, continuous internal states and one consumer GPU. Joint reader--density training improves exact target likelihood over a matched frozen-reader continuation: \JointNll\ versus \ControlNll\ nats per image, a paired gain of \JointNllGain\ with 95\% source-record interval [\JointNllLow, \JointNllHigh]. Greedy supplied-image copying remains exact on 256 targets. Without changing weights, raw-pixel beam search then raises exact next-image match from \GreedyAccuracy\% to \BeamAccuracy\% on 2,048 development prefixes. Earlier-history shuffling reduces it to \BeamShuffled\%, and every primary beam output exactly matches a known canonical raster. These gains coexist with a sequence failure: eight-step visible feedback repeats adjacent images at \BeamRepeat\%, against \TargetRepeat\% in the observed futures. The 18.71M-parameter model therefore learns conditional writing distributions and emits clear forms on one GPU, while coherent extended language and general instruction following remain unresolved. Search quality, copying and image likelihood must be distinguished from a usable language model.}
