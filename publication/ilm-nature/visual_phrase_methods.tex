\paragraph{Conditional phrase likelihood.}
We extracted 296,837 contiguous insertions comprising 627,438 modern glyphs from 64,384 training pairs. Both fits started from the summed-path editor and retained its 48 measured ink pixels, four-way within-glyph search and frozen renderer. We summed the recurrent modern-prefix state, the source state immediately right of the boundary, and a learned projection of the state on its left. A missing left neighbor contributes zero. Given a nonempty site, the model draws the first glyph unconditionally on length. After each emission its new state predicts a Bernoulli end probability. For a reference of length $m$, the negative log likelihood sums the twelve physical group losses per glyph and $m$ binary losses, with an end target only at $m$. During generation the measured pixels of each actual output update the recurrent state; each site permits up to 32 glyphs and a stop after glyph 32. The ablated fit receives one constant source cell and a fixed boundary throughout training and evaluation. The two fits each updated 584,241 parameters of a 2,000,566-parameter network in 2,016 updates over three epochs. These additional fits took 19.9 and 18.3 seconds with peak allocated GPU memory below 368 MiB on one RTX 4090 D. Costs exclude inherited training, preparation and evaluation.

Both cohorts use the established chapter-held-out sentence rosters. Two discovery and seven replication requests lack complete rendered pairs and contribute no aligned sites. The local control returns the most frequent whole training insertion for the most specific available combination of flanking source glyphs and the last two modern glyphs, backing off through shorter contexts with at least two training observations. All free-answer scores compare the full emitted bitmaps including termination. For exchanged phrases we sum the log likelihood of each assignment up to and including its end, conditioning on the supplied preceding modern pixels. We retain all 293 original pairs. A mirrored boundary accompanies source reversal, so the reversal changes both sequence order and which neighbor lies on either side of the site.
