\subsection*{Autoregressive writing from illustrated entries}
Each pictured card contained two Hanziyuan entries with independently shuffled fields. The question specified a headword, a lexical field and a historical stage. Each answer combined the requested source gloss or pronunciation and the original historical bitmap in an authored modern Chinese sentence. We gathered 8,920 training requests on 1,115 cards and 1,136 development requests on 142 cards. Families, entry identifiers and exact historical rasters were disjoint. A separate recount authenticated the source fact and historical ink of every complete target. These records preserve the lexicographical source's interpretation.

Four fixed delimiter images identify a header, field labels and their values. Each such span is a possible physical emission. Additional actions emit one of the 64 most frequent training answer tiles or stop. Candidate features include pooled ink, span length, span type, and the presence and location of the heading and field label in the pictured question. A 64-dimensional recurrent state reads the visual appearance of the preceding emission. After choosing a shortest physical segmentation of a training answer, we minimise the sum of $-\log\sum_{a:E(a)=y_t}p(a\mid x,h_t)$ over steps, where $E(a)$ is the image emitted by action $a$. This credits all actions that emit the same supervised span. Greedy inference starts from the learned initial state and chooses its own spans and stopping point. The within-card control uses identical initial weights, batches, questions and answer-image frequencies. It replaces the correct assignment with a fixed random derangement on every training card. Each fit ran for eight epochs with AdamW on one RTX~4090~D. The matched and deranged fits took 3.85 and 4.71 seconds, respectively, excluding corpus construction, feature extraction and alignment. We assessed complete answer identity and an actual stop, and separately checked the emitted fact and terminal historical form. Presentation and interpretation outside the specified grid and record structure remain unmeasured.
