\section*{Supplementary Note 49. Additional-training control and balanced exposure}

Statements about missing control in Note 48 and Fig.~S25 describe the initial
pilot assessment. The final-image pilot is next compared with additional optimization of the
old assisted objective. Both continuations start from the same recognition
checkpoint, use fresh AdamW with learning rate $10^{-5}$, decay .01 and
gradient clipping one, and reproduce the same 32 example IDs and noise seeds.
Native input encoding under BF16 autocast is held common. The control retains
the old velocity-MSE objective and its recognition term, active only when
$\sigma\leq .5$. It does not differentiate through final noise-to-image sampling.
All 206 adapter tensors change; base, VAE and reader parameters stay frozen.
The first Chinese and English starting trajectories reproduce native pixels.

The control gives 5/32 seen and 10/32 held-out OCR-exact strings, compared
with 15/32 and 13/32 in the final-image pilot. Held-out Chinese remains 3/16
in both. On held-out examples, four become exact only in the pilot and one
only in control; all five are English. The comparison meets its exploratory
rule of at least three additional held-out exact strings without aggregate
seen regression. That rule was specified after observing the pilot and before
observing control. This is a retrospective comparison of objective bundles,
not an independently replicated or compute-matched causal estimate. It does
not isolate recognition, physical error or trajectory differentiation.

The control training invocation takes 56.962\,s at 12.251\,GiB peak allocation,
including two initial native replays taking 16.192\,s. Its optimization loop
takes 33.790\,s. Separate native inference takes 513.381\,s at 8.031\,GiB.
An initial inference invocation was interrupted after 19 outputs when a
legacy whole-repository identity check detected an unrelated source commit.
All interrupted artifacts are preserved; the replacement invocation uses
identical inputs and seeds and reproduces all 19 PNGs and retained tensors
exactly. The complete 64-case cohort is assessed without quality selection.

We then continue the final-image pilot for 128 additional updates under the
unchanged objective in Note 48. Unlike the pilot's sampling with replacement,
the registered schedule makes four complete permutations of its 32 training
inputs: four updates per input, 64 Chinese and 64 English draws. CPU seeds
20268502 and 20268503 fix order and noise seeds; model seed is 20268501.
The pilot's weights and AdamW states are retained. Every optimizer state
reaches step 160. No resume is used. All updates differentiate through all
50 native steps; independent noise, guidance one and the $512\times256$
output canvas are unchanged. No QA example is optimized.

The balanced continuation's prospective rule requires all three conditions:
at least 6/16 held-out Chinese, 15/32 seen strings and 10/16 held-out English.
The Chinese and seen-retention conditions fail; only English retention passes
(Fig.~\ref{fig:s26-balanced}). More exposure under this recipe is therefore
not admitted by that rule. Seen case 002 becomes OCR exact, while 003, 025
and 027 cease to be exact. Held-out cases 007, 015, 022, 023 and 028 become
exact, while 010 ceases to be exact. These are reused development inputs;
font recognition can contribute to the observations. Clean-reference OCR
remains 27/32 seen and 28/32 held out, rather than perfect accuracy.

Complete original-order target/pilot/final galleries are inspected. Seen
case 004 still omits characters; 012 copies instructions and 025 repeats a
letter in \texttt{small}. Held-out case 000 adds a quotation mark, 002 repeats
a character, 026 duplicates its line and 030 copies instructions. More than
two obvious failures in each split independently reject the unchanged 30/32
requirement for correct, legible requested strings alone. This is not a
quantitative manual annotation of every glyph. No PNG is repaired or rerolled.

All 128 updates have finite nonzero gradients and change all 206 adapter
tensors, with frozen parameters without gradients. Initial native replays
and the first actual differentiable forward reproduce pilot pixels exactly.
The first checked prediction-gradient norms at steps 0, 24 and 49 are .020289,
.024817 and .145401. Export authenticates all 128 pre-update PNGs and all
64 final PNGs against retained neural tensors. This verifies artifact bindings;
it is not a second independent neural replay. Training takes 4,500.117\,s at
12.032\,GiB peak allocation; separate native inference takes 512.959\,s at
8.031\,GiB. Acquisition, preceding training and separate CPU OCR are excluded.
The inherited transformer, VAE, reader and trainable counts remain those in
Note 48. No lightweight foundation training, semantic learning, historical
writing or efficiency at matched language quality is established.
