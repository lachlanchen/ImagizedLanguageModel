\paragraph{Writing with the pictured source available.}
We initialized all three adaptations from the same factual glyph writer. The raw-page models read a pictured question and passage. The control received the same question with images of the annotated subject, relation and object. At each output step the decoder mixed its distribution over stored bitmaps with a distribution over physical source positions. Equal bitmaps share probability before greedy emission. The writer could stop after each glyph, up to a limit of 48. We updated the last four transformer blocks, final normalization and pointer for two epochs. The 28,599,938 trainable parameters are a subset of 86,588,258 network parameters. The count excludes fixed image and projection buffers. Adaptation times exclude prior language pretraining.

From screened DuIE training sources we prepared 9,600 questions requiring one of two factual word orders, 2,015 requests for an attested clause, 3,600 one-paragraph detail questions and 4,608 two-paragraph detail questions. Each detail reply must identify the participant and separately state the year appearing in the same paragraph. A year mentioned in a paragraph need not be the date of the work or of the named relationship. We changed years outside the annotated fields and excluded ambiguous four-digit expressions. We removed training passages that shared a normalized subject, passage or 64-character passage window with the factual evaluation material. The two controls had the same reachable training targets, with 33 clause candidates omitted. The previously inspected 32 date sources were excluded from both date evaluations.

One adaptation used the same wording for all detail requests. The second used 12 wordings across the same 19,823 answers, with identical batch membership, order and optimizer settings. The additional DuIE development comparison screened the original factual train and evaluation records, earlier paired-passage sources, and the DRCD reservation. We asked three further questions on every new board. A single paragraph had to elicit two exact, stopped replies per wording. A two-paragraph board had to elicit four, one for each combination of queried subject and year assignment. For transfer we also retained the original 359 human questions and the historical-image cards, without additional adaptation to their prompts. Exact authored clauses measure extractive reproduction. An alternative sentence can be faithful while missing the exact criterion.
