# What happens when a second translation teaches a pictured writer?

The two trained students each produced zero faithful translations in an eighteen-passage masked reading. Across 1,024 unseen pictured passages, the model taught with both archived and Qwen3-VL translations incurred 22,338 glyph edits against the archive. A matched student taught with two copies of the archive incurred 21,751, copying the classical source incurred 14,560, and the phrase composer incurred 13,649. The pretrained Qwen3-VL teacher read transcribed sources and produced fourteen faithful answers. One study-involved model reader performed the assessments. [Original masked readings, assignments and physical counts](evidence/paired_student_20261119)

The parent had already learned on 55,872 pictured sentence pairs. The matched fine-tuning comparisons added twelve passes through 511 eligible classical passages. Both students had 2,251,925 parameters, learned to stop and fed their own visible answers back. They shared the starting weights, shuffled order and optimizer. An independent output comparison used 1,023 complete teacher glosses, with one capped teacher answer excluded. The teacher was used offline and consumed 216 seconds to generate training targets and 411 seconds to produce evaluation glosses. These costs exclude foundation pretraining. ## Why the first glyph sometimes goes wrong

If the same visible glyph appears twice in a passage and also in the writer's memory, three different internal decisions can give the reader exactly the same result. The original decoder compared the routes before comparing what they would print. Adding their probabilities instead produces a lower bound for each physical image. The unassigned drawing probability bounds how much any competing image can gain. Only an unambiguous winner overrides the original decoder. Pooling copy and generation mass has a precedent in word-based pointer generators. Here the equivalence classes are identical bitmaps, and the drawing route can emit ink outside the inventory. Autoregressive text-image generation itself has also been demonstrated previously. [See et al., ACL 2017](https://aclanthology.org/P17-1099/) · [Tai et al., Findings of ACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.874/)

The CPU replay agreed on every original first mark. Pooling certified 997 decisions and changed 222 of them. Archive first-mark matches rose from 393 to 451 out of 1,024. Teacher first-mark matches rose from 530 to 629 out of 1,023. Using the same rule for the whole answer reduced archive edits by 2,545 and teacher edits by 2,508. The model gave the wrong meaning even when the corrected output moved closer to the recorded wording. For 高祖亦嘉之, the recorded gloss describes Gaozu praising someone's talent. The model wrote that Gaozu had died. The two figure examples are the first compact gain and loss under the declared physical comparison. Four CPU image-input executions reproduced their published outputs byte for byte.
