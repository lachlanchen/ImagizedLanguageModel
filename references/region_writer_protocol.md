# Writing complete answers from selected visual evidence

The previous whole-story writer gave zero exact answers on 116 source-absent multiword questions even when supplied the reference evidence. This experiment compares a full-page writer with a writer trained and deployed on a region selected by a separate optical reader. Both writers use the same new recurrent decoder, bitmap bank, initialization, ordered human answer presentations and optimizer schedule. An output decision can spell a training bitmap, copy from the pictured inputs or stop. Train and score complete answers up to 256 decisions on one RTX 4090 D. Register the protocol and source before evaluating the new checkpoints.

The training tasks include human QA2D statements paired with SQuAD questions and paragraphs, complete English E2E descriptions with a competing restaurant, and human CrossWOZ questions and replies about Chinese database cards. Restaurant questions are constructed from entity names. The other two sources provide real questions. Input regions are the card fields, the two entity records or sentence-sized strips from the paragraph. The restriction to aligned glyphs and supplied region boundaries is part of the interface. The answer evidence supervises the common region selector during training. At inference, only the question, region boundaries and their pixels are supplied. Run the frozen selector on every training input, then use its actual predictions for the region writer throughout training. The full writer sees the complete source in the original order. Neither writer gets a human region label. Both see all eligible complete target formulations for four shuffled passes.

Reserve 96 families per source from the official development partitions in a fixed hash order. Exclude their complete primary pages or catalogue entities, exact normalized reference strings and held natural statement questions from new fitting. Give credit to complete stopped normalized matches to any recorded reference. Score the occurrence of the requested short fact where an independent value exists, and all slots in E2E with its supplied semantic error scorer. Report exact and fact presence separately, including lengths, whole-prefix copying and correct stopping. Exchange questions or change a source entity/value, score both versions and report identical replies. For natural CoQA test previously defined strict noncopy questions as out-of-domain transfer. Inspect original generated output in fixed held order, including failures. Keep normal passage input, empirical hard selection and supplied correct region separate. Preserve both fits even if the shorter context improves throughput. The single-trial primary decision requires better autonomous exact and grounded factual responses on held natural questions, retained bilingual performance and generalization beyond restaurant records. Compare against previous applicable checkpoints and do not treat SER, copying or lower loss as sufficient. A corpus-level fact-match count with a failed semantic judge is not a measure of paraphrase accuracy.

The Lean identity expresses the effect of replacing the selected image with a human region as recovered complete answers minus lost complete answers. The selected image can in principle lead to another acceptable phrasing even if it differs from the annotation. Check the identity and the deterministic-image implication on actual pixel outputs. Record selection accuracy and how often each intervention reverses answer credit. At inference bound the common selector to 24 regions, the page to 1,536 cells before boundary padding, and the question to 192. Require a full stop within the 256 decisions. Use a maximum of 18 GiB allocated and three hours per fit, retaining model, optimizer, data identity and schedule for resumption. The held outputs are reserved for final evaluation.
