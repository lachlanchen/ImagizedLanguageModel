# Does aligning whole pictured sentences improve their free translations?

The conversational and editorial writers reproduce local glyph sequences while changing or dropping the event. Compare two continuations of the same likelihood-trained proposition writer on a broader human parallel corpus. Both learn to read and translate each pair in both directions. The second also aligns the pooled optical representations of each English sentence and its human Mandarin translation, distinguishing other sentences in the same batch. Both translations already require encoding both sides. At deployment discard the projection used for this training loss. Both released writers have the same parameters, physical palette and single-cell decoding interface. No teacher, tokenizer or transcribed source is available to the writers in PNG inference.

Use OPUS-100 English–Chinese at a pinned revision, leaving raw examples in the private data directory. Screen the complete official evaluation splits and all preceding conversational, news and proposition holdouts for normalized overlaps in either language. Use the first 240,000 eligible unique official training pairs in archive order, with the existing 96-cell limit and fixed ink inventories. Mix in 12,000 conversational, 12,000 editorial and up to 6,000 previously supervised proposition pairs per pass. Run four matched passes, same source/target presentations and batches, with final weights fixed in advance. Reserve 32 additional official test sentences and up to 128 unique sentences for held cross-script retrieval. Report all filters, learning curves, actual energy and memory. Use a single owned GPU, at most 18 GiB allocated and three hours per arm.

Test free semantic adequacy on the existing paired novel wording, conversational and article cohorts and on the extra official test set in both directions. Grade anonymized answers with opposite candidate orders, real references, paired or unrelated foils, local Qwen and stopped outputs. Evaluate exact glyphs and both-correct pairs separately. A benefit of alignment requires gains over equal-exposure likelihood on complete meanings; improved matching of supplied sentences alone cannot establish this. A broadly capable translator must also close the gap to the local comparator. Preserve failures and independent weights. Additional corpus coverage and objective effects have separate controls.
