# Choosing a word from a pictured book passage

A visual predictor recovered 590 of 3,949 published LAMBADA endings on the same panel where the earlier neural ranker recovered 109. A rule using passage frequency and inverse training-book document frequency recovered 662. The new model improved when given the correct distant passage on two additional book cohorts. It recovered one exact two-word prefix and zero three-word prefixes under self-feedback. ## What the model reads and writes

Training used 245,760 distinct prediction windows from 768 fiction books and 96 PG-19 books. Entire works were reserved for each new test cohort. Half the windows target ordinary positions and half target the last alphabetic run before sentence-ending punctuation. Task type is supplied. The output unit is a case-sensitive run of English letters up to length 23. Apostrophes split contractions. The scoring procedure compares the complete emitted bitmaps with the independently rendered reference. [Source manifests and split hashes](evidence/passage_selector_v1/data.json) · [LAMBADA](https://aclanthology.org/P16-1144/) · [PG-19](https://github.com/google-deepmind/pg19)

Aligned print is matched to 52 canonical letter shapes. The network reads six spatial summaries of each word's ink plus its length, separator class and relative position. No word type has a trainable embedding row. A three-layer attention encoder predicts copy-location weights and a mixture weight. A recurrent spelling head conditions on the encoder and previously chosen glyphs. Locations bearing the same visible spelling contribute jointly to the output probability. Every distinct eligible word on the page and the best completed width-four spelling proposal are scored under both routes. Clean glyphs come from a fixed print motor. This architecture depends on alphabetic segmentation, its fixed face and a 96-book word-frequency table. The underlying copy–generation principle has an established precedent. [Implementation](../ilm/visual_lm/passage_selector.py) · [Pointer-generator networks](https://aclanthology.org/P17-1099/)

Both 759,351-parameter models started from identical weights and received four passes with identical batch schedules and target words. Every epoch used 1,920 optimizer updates and contained 61,571 targets present on the page and 184,189 absent. The loss balances these two groups within each batch. Intact and shuffled training used 100.8 and 99.4 seconds on one RTX 4090 D. Peak PyTorch allocation was 0.5445 GiB per fit. These measurements cover fitting after dataset and descriptor preparation. A text LLM comparison at matched quality remains open. [Intact fit](../models/optical_passage_selector/intact/fit.json) · [Shuffled fit](../models/optical_passage_selector/shuffled/fit.json)

## What fails after the first word

On four fixed ordinary prefixes per fresh book, 40/480 first words, 1/441 eligible two-word prefixes and 0/402 eligible three-word prefixes matched the published continuation. In the one two-word success, the author wrote **her to talk** and the model emitted **her to her**. No predicted sentence stop matched a reference stop within three words. A different sensible continuation can also miss exact match. This experiment did not obtain human judgments of alternative continuations. [Original generated pixels](../publication/ilm-nature/evidence/learned_passage_words.zip) · [Editable figure](../publication/ilm-nature/figures/Learned_passage_words.pptx)

Across all 13,872 missed words, the frozen model assigned the published word less probability than the word it actually wrote. We scored the complete spelling including its stop and summed every matching copy location. The tolerance was 0.0001 nats and no comparison fell inside it. Widening the same maximum-probability search cannot make these lower-scoring references win. The deficit is largest on absent words in PG-19, where the median gap is 7.75 nats. The Lean derivation states the required difference in copy evidence when the spelling branch prefers the rival and gives a sufficient global search certificate conditional on a bound for omitted spellings. The experiment measured reference and emitted probabilities directly. [Likelihood audit](evidence/passage_selector_v1/masses.json) · [Lean 4 derivation](../formal/span_list/VisualPassageSelection.lean)

The frequency comparator uses no neural network. The additional local count comparator uses the same training targets and task labels, choosing the most frequent continuation of a suffix of up to three words supported at least three times. Exchanging pages can delete the target. In 994 fiction positions where both passages contained it, intact and exchanged answers were correct 377 and 352 times. Most of the apparent LAMBADA donor effect coincides with changes in access to the target. These controls limit what the comparison says about semantic understanding. [Scoring code](../scripts/score_passage_selector.py)

On CPU, both saved models reproduced every original pixel and stop on 48 cases each. Both typed and image CLI inputs reproduced the complete three-word output from the published figure. The separate bilingual writer reproduced 22/22 sampled outputs and its stop decisions. The historical dictionary reader reproduced 24/24 including changed values and headwords. The new English predictor does not itself translate Chinese or produce historical glyphs. ## Supervision available in the same passages

The training windows already contained 15,002,169 visible word occurrences. After allowing at least 16 words of left context, 11,070,002 interior occurrences meet the current output-alphabet and length rules. Alongside the existing 245,760 endpoints, that gives 46.04 times as many candidate supervision positions in the same input windows. This counts occurrences in overlapping text. A causal learner must exclude future pixels and protect held surfaces before training on them. The ratio measures available labels, with no established speed or accuracy multiplier.
