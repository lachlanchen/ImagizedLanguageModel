# Can a retrieved writing state complete a whole lexical span?

Single-glyph recall improved development cross entropy by 0.00191 nats and gave the same three whole-answer credits as the ordinary writer and its shuffled control. Before fitting a persistent phrase-recall mechanism, inspect the existing 480 development requests. Under the true causal answer history reconstruct the actual 16 selected training states among the same 32 nearby training sentences. Measure whether any selected state can continue with the entire next alphabetic English word of at least three letters (at word starts), or four consecutive Chinese Han glyphs (at any eligible position). These are separate operational criteria with different denominators. Also measure availability in any position of the retrieved sentences and in 32 uniformly drawn training sentences. Stop at the end of each recorded training trajectory. No evaluation wording is added to memory. Report exact counts by direction, and whether all lexical positions in a single request could be covered. This supervised-history oracle can reject a purely contiguous reuse mechanism when the needed runs are absent. It cannot establish free language quality even when a run is available.
