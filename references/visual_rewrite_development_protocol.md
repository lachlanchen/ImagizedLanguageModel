# Can a recurring visual edit transfer to a new factual statement?

Both new recurrent writers lost complete statements on the fifth cohort. The earlier system still sometimes states a wrong subject or drops a date. As a separate development experiment, fit a conservative writer of reusable edits from the original pictured question, human fact and full answer. On sufficiently repeated visual question boundaries it can splice the visible variable into language learned from real answers. Else retain the preceding unrestricted writer. Keep the recurrent writer study and its test untouched. Train on the authenticated original writer split, including unexplainable wordings in the confidence denominator. Evaluate only on the previously observed fourth cohort. Do not claim an independently confirmed gain from this development set.

Induce one variable question interval from the longest contiguous overlap of question and reference after the unique human fact has been marked. The surrounding literal strokes and the fact address come from the reference. Also permit answers consisting of the visible fact alone. Require a rule to recur across four or more source families and a question-boundary group with at least eight observed examples and four positive families. Use empirical precision of at least 90% in the group and retain whole-answer fallback on all unqualified questions. Use fixed prefix lengths 0, 3, 6, 10, 16, 24 and suffix lengths 0, 2, 5, 9. Break ties by estimated precision after adding one error and one success, then support and stable rule order. Account for single examples with multiple human wordings. No Unicode text, word tokenizer, reference answer or retrieved training sentence may be used at inference. Separate a correct reusable operation from the reader's ability to identify the value. Score every question, including those with no applicable rewrite, and count gains, losses and coverage against the same fixed writer. Keep book, historical and long prose qualification open.
