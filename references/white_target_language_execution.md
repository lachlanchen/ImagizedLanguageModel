## Four physical conditions

Admission requires every original readability group at least 90%, ink in every
credited candidate, at most 11 false-ink outputs among exactly 252 source-white
positions, and at most 37 false blanks across all 12,597 target positions.
The white-target-specific gate must contain each explicit safeguard. Top-level
success, another trial's gate or a missing new safeguard is insufficient.

The evaluator reproduces the registered physical-review source binding without
importing its neural metadata. It authenticates the session, first freeze,
boards, owner mapping, complete pixel archives and every original reviewed
image. It recounts all seven readable groups, verifies blank flags and credited
ink from original opaque pixels, and independently recomputes the complete
white/nonempty partition and both error counts from binary arrays. Every case
and total must agree with the frozen gate. It preserves contradictory readings
and rejects them without repair. Scope changes to shared review modules are
restored after normal return and exceptions.

These checks concern physical writing. A comma can be recognizable yet be the
wrong continuation. Canonical bitmap agreement, semantic correctness, complete
answer quality, natural stopping and coherent book continuation remain separate.
