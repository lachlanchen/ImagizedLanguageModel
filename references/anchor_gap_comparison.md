# Ordered copy and generation between preserved source glyphs

The preceding writer can replace one local span. In the full paired corpus, 356,806 intervening regions require different source and target pictures. This comparison fits a contextual binary copy decision at each source position and an autoregressive image writer between successive preserved positions. A binary decision at each output step closes the gap, causing a physical copy of its right anchor or termination at the source boundary. At other steps, the writer selects a source image, an exemplar or a generated drawing. Copy and exemplar probabilities for identical physical images are added before choosing an output. Drawing uses a four-path beam. A factored output distribution preserves a sharp picture when several different continuations are plausible.

Fit all 55,872 paired passages for six complete shuffled passes with seed 194207, batches of up to 96, AdamW at 0.0006, gradient clipping at 1, BF16 operations and the existing marginal writer as initialization. Use the final state. Reserve 24 GiB available host memory and limit peak allocated GPU memory to 10 GiB and fitting time to one hour. Training uses the uniquely ordered physical correspondences and true preceding target ink. At deployment the writer sees its own previous pictures. Its 2,048 exemplars are pictured crops. The task uses a single rendered script style and explicitly segmented glyphs.

Evaluate on the frozen 512 passages and 24 whole-passage readings. Compare the learned source-only policy, keeping every source position with identical writer weights, the existing phrase composer, and literal copy. A separate diagnostic supplies only the source indices of the reference-derived correspondences. It supplies neither the target images nor their positions to the writer. Use a threshold of zero on binary logits, 192 generated images maximum, and count every truncation. Record edit totals, stopping, output lengths, planner precision and recall, and complete meaning with the methods hidden during reading. Credit an intelligible modern Chinese answer that conveys the full passage without material invention or omission. Leave genuinely uncertain outputs uncredited. Advance the writer only if both its edit total and its complete-meaning count strictly improve on the fixed composer and all-position policies. A failed comparison calls for isolating the failure at selection or generation before another fit. [Training correspondences and frozen controls](ordered_copy_anchor_protocol.md)

Prior pixel-language experiments report readable autoregressive outputs and measure the effect of the training objective on legibility. Here the endpoint is the meaning of a complete translation from an unseen chapter. [Tai et al., PIXAR](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.874/)
