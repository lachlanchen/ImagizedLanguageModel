# What the visual writer learns between copied glyphs

A 2.33-million-parameter reader and writer translated pictured classical Chinese into pictured modern Chinese on one RTX 4090 D. The source selection rule recovered 8,512 of 10,085 reference-aligned positions (84.4% recall), with 8,512 correct among 12,247 selected (69.5% precision). On 24 passages from unseen chapters, two entire translations preserved the meaning. Both differ from their source. Neither appears even as a contiguous image sequence anywhere in the 55,872 training targets. One gives the neighbouring state's refusal, and the other identifies the ruler of Jin as speaker. The latter contains a repeated 是. The same writer turns an evening meal into an act of writing. The 512 inputs come from 390 chapters. Six passages per source-length range formed the fixed reading roster. One model reader graded complete translations, including a hidden reference control that received 19/24. Several references contain material absent from the source. The finite writer was developed after seeing the neural results and evaluated on the same cohort. Eight whole outputs inherited byte-identical previous judgments. The sixteen new readings were method-identified. A perfect selector among all generated answers and the literal source could receive at most four credits on this roster under the frozen grades. ## Learning objective and the observed failure

Let an image sequence $x$ have ordered source copy positions $a$ and output gaps $g_0,\ldots,g_{|a|}$. Training estimates a source policy $p(a\mid x)$ and conditional gap distributions $p(g_j\mid x,a,y_{<j})$. Boundary copies preserve their original pixels. The recurrent writer assigns separate probabilities to closing a gap and to emitting another picture. Its likelihood is trained using the correct preceding images. A low closing probability on the writer's own repetitions can sustain a loop even when every supplied source boundary is correct. The observed increase to 147 capped passages under supplied positions localizes a defect in free gap generation. It does not identify a unique cause of the recurrent errors. Learning finite gap sequences removed this particular route to repetition and reduced reference edits by 12,011. It left many classical expressions untranslated. [Generation records](evidence/anchor_gap_20261119/generation.json) · [Finite outputs](evidence/anchor_gap_20261119/finite_scores.json)

Rendered-language research shows that a visual objective can yield readable language generation and that document-image training can support some language understanding tasks. The evidence here concerns source-conditioned *meaning*. A book translator can insert facts available to a reader of the previous page. If the training target includes such a fact, the isolated source image may be compatible with multiple answers. For a discrete target $Y$ and source $X$, the minimum expected logarithmic loss over all conditional predictors is $H(Y\mid X)$. Giving a model additional context $C$ can lower this optimum by $I(Y;C\mid X)$. Neither quantity has been measured here. This gives an experimentally checkable reason to distinguish missing evidence, insufficient supervision and an inadequate generator. [Tai et al., PIXAR](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.874/) · [Chai et al., autoregressive pre-training on pixels and texts](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.182/)

Six full passes used 3,552 AdamW updates and 910,550 trainable parameters. The measured fitting loop took 82.8 seconds and peaked at 355.3 MiB of PyTorch-allocated memory on one RTX 4090 D. This starts with fitted visual weights, an existing offline ink motor, prepared paired data, segmented cells and a shared bitmap inventory. No OCR, teacher call or text rendering is required to generate answers from strips. These costs and scores establish no matched-quality advantage over a text LLM. ## Training data with a self-contained meaning

In a uniform sample of 32 training rows, two modern references prepend material from a different sentence. Two others convert a sexagenary date to a numerical day of the month. A pictured short clause alone does not generally identify that conversion. We asked a local four-bit Qwen3-8B for a source-only modern rendering of each training row, withholding the book reference. A single model-based review retained 12 of 32 drafts as suitable targets. For example, the teacher translated Xiao Ziyun's age and title without importing Xiao Zixian's bibliography. It also misread a celestial enclosure as a palace and an expression of inferior ability as a farewell. The approved training artifact contains 201 source and 274 target glyph images with row offsets. These drafts have never been used to fit or score the reported ILM. The next comparison should hold the source set, writer initialization and update count fixed while replacing flawed or extraneous targets with verified source-supported translations. Include a separate, chapter-held-out set of complete passages and minimal meaning changes involving agent, negation and quantity. Keep the original corpus as a matched control and freeze the new readings before revealing the model identities. Advance only with an improvement in complete independent meanings and preserved source details. This pilot provides twelve reviewed replacements and a replicable data-preparation method.
