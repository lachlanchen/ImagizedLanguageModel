# What a learned cross-script question contributes to a visual reader

The reader takes a pictured passage and a pictured question and returns an image of a source excerpt. A second mode gives this excerpt and the same question to a separately trained image writer, which chooses its glyphs and stop. Both modes run without a tokenizer, an OCR service or a text teacher. The base reader and writer inherit weights from text models. The experiment measures the cost of adapting these components on one RTX 4090 D.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Frozen interval reader | 124 | 17 | 0 |
| Interval reader with exchanged question vectors during training | 121 | 13 | 0 |
| Interval reader with matched question vectors | 124 | 41 | 0 |
| Causal writer with full pages | 64 | 26 | 0 |
| Causal writer with frozen reader excerpts | 75 | 16 | 0 |
| Causal writer with matched reader excerpts | 75 | 36 | 0 |

A 128-dimensional vector learned from question pixels supplied a correction to each possible answer's start and end scores. Relative to the frozen reader, 28 English requests gained an exact answer and four lost one. The difference was 5.83 percentage points (paired 95% passage-bootstrap interval 3.27–8.44). Chinese exact counts were equal. This met the prespecified MLQA development criterion. On BiPaR the new head raised English exact answers from 92 to 151 of 748, while Chinese changed from 329 to 327. On the earlier classical packet it completed one of 12 passages. These rates measure exact physical excerpts. The selection requires the answer to appear on the page. The 172,224-parameter residual head took 24.1 seconds of optimization for 1,908 steps using the fixed models' pictured features. Preparing the features took 85.4 seconds and retained 10.05 GB of contextual states in host memory. The reader combines about 87 million parameters and the writer adds about 87 million. The fresh-weight question encoder accounts for 848,961 of these. Inherited training and previous adaptation are excluded from those times. In the public-domain Xunzi passage, the reader selected 伯牙 for the musician whose playing made six horses raise their heads and 瓠巴 for the musician whose playing attracted fish. Both selections were correct for both question languages. The writer returned both names correctly under the Chinese requests. Under the English requests it gave one wrong stopped reply and reached the 48-cell limit on the other. [Original pictured inputs, excerpts and written replies](evidence/question_evidence_transfer_20261124/examples/) · [Case readings](evidence/question_evidence_transfer_20261124/figure.json)

Across all 824 MLQA requests the writer converted 111 to exact stopped answers from matched excerpts, compared with 90 from full pages. It lost 67 of the reader's 165 exact excerpts and repaired 13 incorrect ones. Among the lost cases, 28 continued past the full correct excerpt and 25 reached the generation limit. A post-hoc calculation stopped each saved writer stream when it first completed an exact copy of the supplied excerpt. This gave 115 Chinese and 45 English exact replies (160 total), compared with 165 when returning the excerpt directly. The calculation diagnoses unnecessary continuation in explicit quotation tasks. Its 160 include externally imposed stops. On the five-work literary packet the same writer gave 20/92 exact replies and completed zero of 23 passages. The earlier full-page writer gave 19/92, and text-input Qwen3-8B Q8 gave 85/92. The model therefore remains far behind the text reference on these works. [Complete literary writer recount](evidence/question_evidence_transfer_20261124/book_writer.json)

Use the excerpt mode for a request to quote a source. Retain independent writing for requests requiring additional language. The next writer experiment should preserve the full page alongside predicted evidence on two-fact questions. Compare matched and within-page exchanged evidence under identical supervision, and score replies that must express both facts with language absent from the selected spans. Require a gain over the full-page writer, preserve explicit-quotation accuracy and measure free stopping. Keep the MLQA development observations distinct from prospective confirmation.
