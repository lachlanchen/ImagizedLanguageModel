# Does additional traditional Chinese improve the selection of evidence?

The released pictured reader recovered 999 of 3,170 individual answers on official CMRC passages. Its planner can locate questions under several instructions, but its passage reader frequently selects the wrong evidence. Test additional human questions and attested image spans from traditional Chinese DRCD. Hold the architecture, visual features, learned planner, ordered decoder, initialization and writing actions fixed.

Compare the released checkpoint, a reader given repeated CMRC training and a reader given additional DRCD training. Each fit receives four passes of 43,868 examples. At 21,934 shared positions per pass use balanced permutations of the original 7,235 CMRC records. At the other positions use every training DRCD question once or an equal number of CMRC replays. Match replacements by passage-length rank and keep batch membership and step count fixed. Counts of glyphs, supervised occurrences, wall time and floating-point operations can differ. Optimize the marginal probability of every cell-aligned accepted answer span, including alternatives of different lengths, using AdamW, learning rate 0.00020 and a gradient norm limit of 2. Use only the final weights. Run serially on GPU 0 with at most 86% of the device and a 30-minute timeout.

Primary outcome is complete stopped single-question answer images on all 3,505 prepared DRCD development questions. Also score all eligible exchanged question pairs with two pictured instructions, original internal CMRC requests and all official CMRC requests across four wrappers. Credit any accepted exact physical answer. Compare the mixed model with both controls. Resample DRCD by article to report paired uncertainty against replay. To adopt the new reader as the next passage baseline require at least five percentage points more DRCD exact singles than each control, more correct DRCD pairs in both orders with the traditional wrapper, and no decline of more than one percentage point against the released reader on official CMRC singles and both orders of the short Chinese instruction. Require at least 430 internal ordered requests and 420 internal singles. Other outcomes are reported irrespective of this decision. The first DRCD exact answer gained over replay and the first remaining error supply the illustrated output images.

On success, retain the checkpoint for source-grounded writing and next test whether it can express selected facts in a newly composed answer. On failure, preserve the released checkpoint and locate the discrepancy between answer selection and transfer. The comparisons measure extraction with a known cell grid. A gain cannot establish free writing, linguistic reasoning absent from the passage, or a compute advantage at equal language quality. [Corpora and overlap checks](traditional_passage_preparation.md)
