# What the continuous writer produced after training

The final eight-component writer produced zero readable answers on 32 pictured questions and zero coherent continuations on 12 long book prompts. Both questions were correct in zero of 16 pairs. All twelve long outputs reached the 640-cell limit. The single-component control had the same scores. Across both arms, no intermediate checkpoint gave a readable complete answer. The failed comparison is terminal. [Training account](evidence/continuous_image_training_completed_20261118.json) · [Frozen-review measurements](evidence/native_continuous_review_20261118.json)

The review covered all 792 scheduled streams. The original 526 were preserved and the final 266 were generated at their registered settings. The 197 anonymized boards included 144 visible book prefixes. One model reviewer with prior development familiarity inspected board overviews, individual stream contact sheets and the complete catalogue of 1,687 distinct physical output cells before reference scoring. Fifty-two output pages were white. The other 740 had ink, usually a repeated dotted frame or a tapered stack of horizontal marks. No confident transcript was recorded. These judgments have no independent human adjudication. Variation in individual pixels did not amount to a readable sentence.
