# Does broader parallel reading improve what a visual writer says?

The source-order experiment produced no complete translations on 192 natural requests. Compare two continuations of the released varied-source writer on public TED2020 alignments and locally prepared bilingual literary units. Both continuations retain the 32-pixel input and output geometry, 1,688,480 weights, visual inventories and bidirectional conditional likelihood. Include complete aligned units and successive pairs of units when they fit. Keep the source order, punctuation and stop. Local literary translations can be machine-assisted. The private lowercase page library has separate page-level supervision. Count its eligible pages locally and keep their text and images private.

Use the same minibatches of length pairs and domains for the two arms. In the narrow arm replace every training pair by cyclic sampling from a fixed one-eighth subset within the exact same length pair and domain, rounding up in small strata. The broad arm sees each eligible unique pair once per pass. This matches update count and target and source glyph exposures exactly. Train for six complete passes on one available card, with AdamW, clipped gradients and a shared declining learning rate. At each pass also evaluate the unchanged initial checkpoint and use the lowest mean development negative log likelihood across the three domains for checkpoint selection, including the initial state as a candidate. Measure open generation separately on held data.

Partition talks by source document, literary units by whole work, and FLORES-101 development and devtest by article URL. Within the supported font and length range, screen held assessment strings against prior translation data and current training. Report any residual uncertainty about upstream document overlap. Keep 24 translated units per domain and split when available, giving 48 directed held requests per domain. For books distribute them across reserved works. Use purposefully reversed grammatical-role requests as a separate retention check. Evaluate free answer images without supplied target history. Record whole-meaning credits in two masked grading orders, reference and foil calibration, the quantized Qwen text comparator, exact bitmaps, stop and paired uncertainty grouped by source document. Report the actual examples in fixed order. Treat a gain on this restricted test as evidence about its population. Preserve both controls and test unseen fonts and longer pages separately.

If the broad writer improves held complete meanings and holds up on book families, extend generative training. If the intervention lowers development loss alone, test decoder capacity or teacher transfer next. The Lean calculation should predict which physical and training quantities are exactly matched and state the conditions under which the comparison says anything about meaning.
