# Transferring a visual reader's uncertainty about source spans

The small reader recovered 50 Chinese and 34 English answers using automatically proposed names; a reader trained with independent labels recovered 51 and 48 with the same proposals. Test whether the question-dependent distribution of a pretrained visual reader, together with additional natural questions, transfers to the small convolutional reader. Its output remains a source image. The larger reader is used only during target preparation and as a measured reference.

Use the original optically adapted Chinese QA reader from the font-invariant study, before any fine-tuning on BiPaR. Collect paired human questions on the same training passage with different exact answers of one to eight renderable cells. Use all eligible passages from the four training works, up to two disjoint question pairs per passage. Add up to 4,096 eligible CMRC and 4,096 DRCD training passages, with one pair per passage. Retain complete passages and exclude book passages duplicated across the work boundary or containing the held book titles. Enforce the visual teacher's context limit for its Chinese input. Keep the first 32 most probable source intervals under the teacher and retain the probability mass of the remainder as a single category. The Chinese distribution supervises both views of each bilingual question. Measure the teacher independently on the 226 novel passages for both input languages.

Train two fresh readers of the same 64,666-parameter architecture. Use the original human answer images for half the objective and a temperature-two categorical distillation loss for the other half. Both readers see the same human supervision. Exchange only the teacher distributions for the two questions on the same page in the control. Keep the same initialization and page order. Eight passes, 12 pages per batch, AdamW and cosine decay from 0.001, one GPU, under 12 GiB allocated and under 20 minutes per fit. Evaluate all 226 pages under the same restricted, completely unrestricted and automatically proposed-span conditions. A positive paired lower 95% bound for exact unrestricted answers over exchanged supervision and over the earlier small reader is required to attribute a practically useful transfer to the teacher in a language. Report the freer comparison even if the two-candidate task improves. This reuses a known diagnostic set. A prospective evaluation would require other works. If the teacher itself fails on Chinese, reassess the source signal before another distillation fit.

### Follow-up after inspecting the two registered students

The matched student produced 104 Chinese and 45 English exact answers. The exchange control produced 88 and 59. Both paired uncertainty intervals against the exchange control included zero. Fit one additional student on exactly the same 7,070 question pairs, random initialization, 4,720 updates and hard targets, using only the human image negative log likelihood. Use unit weight on this loss, and retain the same clipping rule and optimizer. This unregistered-at-outset ablation measures whether the complete teaching objective improves on the simpler fit with the enlarged data. Report it separately from the original two-arm decision, on all 904 inputs. Choose neither a checkpoint nor a release based on its score. If no advantage is resolved, prioritize language-dependent role binding and a prospective independent-work comparison.
