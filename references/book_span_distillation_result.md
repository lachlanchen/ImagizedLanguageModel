# What a small visual reader learned from 6,439 passages

A 64,666-parameter model can read a question and a passage as two glyph images and return an exact short answer from the passage. On Chinese questions about people in two reserved novels, it found the source answer 104 times in 452 attempts. Eight of 226 passages received two correct answers to questions about different people. The same reader found 45 of 452 answers with the questions in English; no passage received both correct answers. The output characters are legible because the reader copies the selected ink. Answer selection remains the larger error. On the same passages, supplying two candidate names raised the matched student's score to 300/452 Chinese answers and 230/452 English answers. The Chinese result included 83 fully correct pairs. Automatically proposing up to 16 people gave 103 Chinese answers and 49 English answers. ## What transferred

The matched and exchanged students saw identical human answers, initial weights, sample order and optimizer schedules. The exchanged student received the teacher's probabilities for the other question about the same page. The Chinese advantage for matched teaching was 3.5 percentage points (95% interval −0.4 to 7.5); the English difference was −3.1 points (−6.4 to 0.2). Neither met the prespecified requirement for attributing improvement to question-specific teacher targets. The matched fit improved over the earlier fit by 17.9 points in Chinese (14.2 to 21.5) and 8.4 in English (5.8 to 11.1). Both the training population and objective changed in that comparison. Estimates use 10,000 paired passage resamples stratified by work. Two novels and one initialization constrain generalization. [Protocol and follow-up chronology](book_span_distillation_protocol.md) · [Paired estimates](evidence/book_span_distillation_20261124/audit.json)

After observing the registered comparison, a third student learned from the human answers alone on the same expanded data. It recovered 81 Chinese and 48 English answers. Matched teaching improved on this simpler objective by 5.1 points in Chinese (1.8 to 8.6) and changed English accuracy by −0.7 points (−3.5 to 2.2). This comparison supports some benefit from the full teaching objective on the Chinese diagnostic set. The exchange comparison leaves the question-specific mechanism unresolved. These 226 passages have informed earlier design decisions. ## Objective, data and cost

The training data comprise 1,646 passages from four novels, 697 CMRC passages and 4,096 DRCD passages. Distinct human answers on the same page produce 7,070 question pairs; 2,277 include the English questions. No reserved passage occurs in the small readers' training data. The optical teacher uses a Chinese text-pretrained encoder adapted to read visual glyphs. Overlap in its original pretraining is unknown. The 177 Harry Potter and 49 Three-Body diagnostic passages are distinct from each other and from the student training pages. [Corpus receipt](evidence/book_span_distillation_20261124/corpus.json) · [BiPaR](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1249/)

For each question the teacher supplies its 32 highest-probability source intervals, plus a single probability for the remaining intervals. The 32 carried a mean 88.2% of the mass. The Lean derivation establishes conservation of arbitrary lists of integer masses and proves that redistributing mass inside the residual leaves every loss of the observed bins unchanged. The model adds a human answer loss over all occurrences of the answer image. The proof specifies an information limit of distillation. The evidence about language comes from physically generated answers and matched comparisons. [Lean source](../formal/span_list/CoarseSpanTeacher.lean) · [Teacher targets](evidence/book_span_distillation_20261124/teacher_learn.json)

The matched student took 4,720 optimization steps on 149,552 pictured questions in 66.7 seconds on one RTX 4090 D, with 163.3 MiB peak PyTorch allocation during fitting. Its device energy increment was 7.50 kJ. Computing the Chinese teacher targets took 53.9 seconds and 16.03 kJ. These measurements exclude source data preparation, earlier optical adaptation, original pretraining and whole-machine energy. Replaying 132 held-out responses with the standalone CPU reader reproduced all 132 GPU bitmaps exactly. The pixel audit checked 7,232 response images. The released student requires no teacher or tokenizer at inference. Comparable-quality general generation and a thousandfold compute saving remain open. ## Where the reader loses the answer

The two pictured Chinese questions elicited different output images on 189/226 passages. Across 452 replies, 104 were the requested name, 32 were the competing name and 316 were neither. For English requests the reader repeated exactly the same output on 164/226 passages. It returned the requested name 45 times, the competing name 46 times and neither name 361 times. This pattern points to two different limitations: finding a participant among arbitrary strips in Chinese and making the answer depend on the English question. The Lean file also proves a lower bound for two opposite margin targets when the scores ignore the change of question. The bound motivates a question-dependent loss; it does not predict the result of training with that loss.
