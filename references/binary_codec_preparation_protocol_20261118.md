refinement work of its 64-bit codec against a matched 64-dimensional continuous
control. This preparation admits no neural call, optimization or language score.

Reuse all 68,948 packed cells from the original training-only prepared support.

when its digest's first eight bytes, interpreted big-endian, are zero modulo ten.

every original cell, byte and source label; do not repair or render anything.

language source or a repeated Unicode/image-alias audit.

physical metrics. These groups are not classifier targets or an inference

not open development answers, reserved DRCD data, old frozen reviews or teacher
