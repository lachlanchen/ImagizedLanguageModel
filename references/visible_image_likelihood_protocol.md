# Train the probability of a visible glyph

The route-first writer can assign probability to the correct bitmap through several paths. Pooling probabilities at inference reduced 2,545 archive edits in 1,024 passages while both tested decoders still produced zero faithful translations in eighteen. Compare a single supervised route against giving credit to all source positions and exemplars matching the target image. Retain the same pointer alignment, progress loss, open drawing, feedback, learned stop and certified pooled decoder. For targets absent from both source and memory, retain the shared drawing-descriptor likelihood. This is a lower bound on complete ink likelihood where several latent paths exist.

Both arms start at the original shared 55,872-pair parent, use all 55,872 real training passages (1,762,187 target cells per pass), four fixed shuffled passes, batches of at most 96, AdamW at 0.0003, gradient clipping at one and the final weights. The single-path control additionally charges the probability of the aligned source position when that route supplies the target. Both retain the common auxiliary alignment loss. No sequence teacher or external language head is used. Limit each fit to ten GiB allocated on GPU 0 and retain 24 GiB host reserve.

Score 1,024 further pictured passages from chapters excluded from both training and previous evaluation, including the recent 519-chapter treatment study. Compare the shared parent, both new models, copying, the phrase composer and same-length wrong-chapter input for the marginal model. Record whole-image edit counts, complete and changed exact answers, stopping and a paired chapter-bootstrap interval against the single-path arm. Read eighteen new source-marker passages (six per stratum, distinct chapters), including the actual archive gloss in the masked packet. Only improved free answers over both matched and phrase baselines plus improved edit counts warrant a candidate for independent replication. If the two outcomes diverge, retain the stronger deployed baseline and locate the semantic error before adding compute.
