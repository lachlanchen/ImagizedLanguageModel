# Testing whether a visual reader follows the action

A name exchange changes the image at each possible answer location. The Guoyu reader passed four of 14 such exchanges in Chinese. Even a correct four-answer result can come from selecting a location associated with the question. The next comparison keeps the two names at identical glyph coordinates and changes which person carried out each action. The same question must receive different answer images on the original and altered pages. Lean verifies that a reader insensitive to this edit must fail on at least one of the two pages. Reserve the passages, pictures, response aliases and evaluation order before model calls. Use two actions of equal rendered width, two distinct name images, and two orders of introduction. Verify bitwise equality of both name regions across the edit. Have each question expressed in Chinese and English, with at least one paraphrase whose wording differs from the passage. Label edited pages as counterfactual. Track natural originals and constructed clauses separately. Preserve the book, predicate and source IDs so that repeated passages or predicate-pair templates cannot cross training and qualification.

First evaluate the released optical reader, its equal-label control and its frozen parent with one serial pass on GPU 0, up to 12 GiB allocated and 20 minutes per reader. Give a generic question on the same pages as a question-insensitivity control. Report exact individual images and the proportion with all four answers correct for each work and language. Bootstrap entire original excerpts. If the assigned reader cannot outperform the equal-label control, retain the current release and test the marginal probability of the correct answer versus the rival specifically at the two fixed positions. If the effect transfers to a second work and the paired interval excludes zero, use the experimentally supported page evidence to condition the separate generative writer. Qualify that writer on new questions requiring two ordered source fragments and an absent connective, scoring all emitted pixels and stopping. Neither a chosen source span nor a readable wrong name establishes that capability.
