# Read a Chinese question, locate an answer on a complete card and write it in Chinese

Use the original human Chinese CrossWOZ questions and agent replies, with the original attraction, restaurant and hotel database. Pin the upstream commit and artifact hashes. Select one requested and one informed attribute, the same verified establishment, an actual catalogue value occurring in the human answer, a finite input width and at least two distinct available source values. Exclude duplicate attributes, unresolved discourse references, phone numbers and non-unique answers. Use the complete card including the distractor properties. Preserve the original response wording surrounding each supervised name/value span.

Train a cold shared visual encoder and causal answer writer. Given a question image, a domain image and all visible labelled values, estimate the requested relation without its annotation. Generate Chinese glyphs plus optional copy actions for the chosen value and establishment name, with learned stop. Fit both image and aligned-byte question readers, on identical records and updates. Cap each fit at 8,000 updates, 48 records per batch, 15 minutes and 8 GiB VRAM. No LLM at inference. A fixed renderer makes one 32-pixel cell per character; all catalogue values may contain imagery outside the fitted answer palette.

Reserve the official test conversations. Report the numbers whose establishments and complete question strings were absent in training, overall selected relation, emitted exact value, extraneous competing values, exact human wording and sentences after masking copied spans. Compare a most common available relation, question exchange on the same physical page, and removal of the question. Inspect generated Chinese for grammar and unwarranted additions. Treat value correctness and semantic adequacy separately. No new fit or checkpoint selection on the test scores. If free prose is limited, publish both fluent and incomplete replies. Add this evidence to the reading manuscript alongside the new variable-ink result.

As a stronger test of reading beyond isolated keywords, keep the two property names, sentence characters and complete page constant, reversing only which field is asked for and which is mentioned as a distractor. Before observing results, use three authored constructions (an excluded field before the request, an exclusion after the request, and a previously discussed field before the request). Use each supported domain and unordered matched property pair once with one actual held card. Also test the two questions asking for a single field alone as a positive control. Preserve the human-data checkpoint. Report pairs of correct routes and complete emitted values. These probes have authored wording and repeated forms; they do not measure the frequency of such requests in natural dialogue.
