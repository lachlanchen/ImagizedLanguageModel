# Full-response likelihood as supervision for pictured evidence

Use 48 previously unexamined FairytaleQA training-story families as a new confirmation sample, with two different questions and distinct answers about exactly the same full section of each story. Exclude every other question in those 48 stories from fitting. Exclude all 64 diagnostic story families from confirmation selection. Use all retained questions from the remaining training stories and 2,048 English statement plus 2,048 Chinese exam questions and their human wordings from the original supervised writer pool. Retain the previously trained signed candidate generator, sentence writer and span comparator as frozen shared components. The new selector sees pixels at deployment. The full-response target is privileged training data.

For each actual candidate score all distinct complete reference images, including stop, by the exact spell/copy mixture. Compare the log-sum over candidate locations with the best single joint path. Both train the same independent residual head initialized to reproduce the signed parent, with the same candidate lists, references, eight example orders and optimizer. Count unsupported rows explicitly. Neither receives the annotated location as a target. Keep an 18 GiB and three-hour ceiling for each stage. Evaluate all three systems on the new story pairs, the prior held story and Chinese cohorts and ordinary book/historical transfer. Judge the first sixteen fresh story replies and prior bilingual controls with the same two-order protocol. No general bilingual promotion is possible from new story questions alone. On a failure check whether likelihood training changed evidence, whether its advantage reaches stopped semantic replies, and whether the unchanged writer can express the required propositions.
