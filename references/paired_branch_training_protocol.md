# Training a writer to choose a changed proposition

An independent writer often scores the aligned complete sentence above the translation of a nearby source, yet emits a third glyph at the first position where the two translations differ. On the completed corpus-derived training audit this occurred on 112/308 first differing positions, including 83 where the aligned branch already beat the crossed branch. Only 186/308 autonomous paths reached the common reference prefix. This experiment asks whether a teacher-verified local source interaction transfers to independent writing. The separate corpus qualification remains a prerequisite. No qualification may use scores from this student comparison.

Start both arms from the fixed independent broad writer. Use only individually certified OPUS training families outside the sealed second-judge reserve, and require the previous two-model confirmation. For each pair and translation direction, find the longest common target prefix ending before the first different printed glyph or stop. On each source, compute the log probability of both alternative next glyphs under that same prefix. Both arms learn ordinary stopped likelihood on all aligned whole sentences and an additional positive categorical loss over the full writing repertoire at the two branching positions. The paired arm alone also learns the mean difference between the two aligned-versus-crossed log odds across sources. This interaction cancels a target-only baseline. Positive full-repertoire likelihood also supplies a gradient against a third glyph that beats both alternatives.

Use six passes, the same 16 families per update where available, shuffle by a fixed epoch seed, both target languages per update, AdamW with rates 0.00010, 0.00008, 0.00006, 0.00005, 0.00004 and 0.00003, gradient norm limit one. Take 48 additional pictures from the broad writer's earlier training pool for each update. Sample 32 corpus-mixture rows and 16 controlled rows using a fixed stream shared by both arms. The ordinary loss is mean gold negative log probability across supervised cells from the paired and rehearsal rows. Add 0.25 times the mean branching-glyph negative log probability to both arms. Add 0.25 times softplus(2(1−D))/2 in the paired arm, where D is half the sum of the two correctly oriented log odds. Use the same ordered source-derived drafts, frozen palette, source marker, stopping rule and greedy image writer. Bind data, initial weights and every schedule. Limit fitted GPU memory to 16 GiB and time to 30 minutes per arm on one card.

Reserve four new book editions and the certified teacher reserve before fitting. Evaluate all naturally stopped answers from actual emitted pixels. Require the paired arm to improve complete altered meanings on the sealed corpus families and free book meanings over the matched positive arm, with positive lower 95% paired bootstrap intervals for both, and preserve prior controlled polarity and participant roles. Publish original individual successes and failures. A lower loss or better ranking between supplied alternatives alone cannot qualify the model.
