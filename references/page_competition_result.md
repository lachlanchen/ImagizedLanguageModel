# Competing with every legal answer on the pictured page

The fresh Chinese plural comparison gave zero complete answers on 112 questions for all three readers. Both final fits completed the same two English lists on 48 pages. Chinese singleton exact fell from seven of 32 for the matched likelihood control to four for whole-page competition. The new fit gave zero complete Chinese original/changed-answer pairs on 45 edited pages. The qualification criterion failed. Both fits saw all 51,498 Chinese and 4,671 English training questions, each in the same schedule of 73,728 presentations. Control and competition training took 62.32 and 87.86 seconds, consuming 7.86 and 9.83 kJ of device energy. Each writer has 308,422 deployed parameters and uses aligned physical cells. The pretrained initialization, corpus preparation, host and evaluation costs are additional.

The additional objective compares the freely decoded path with the best supported complete human list, maximizing over supplied alternative lists and all occurrences of each retained literal spelling. The exact search bounds a partially assigned list by the sum of independent maxima for its remaining members. It returns zero added loss when the emitted list already matches an annotation. On an error, the loss is one plus the score gap. Lean checks the per-page error bound and pruning inequality. It is a mistake-driven update to a neural score with latent placements. Collins describes mistake-driven structured learning; Yu and Joachims formulate structural learning with latent positive completions. Neither result guarantees that these trained neural scores generalize. [Collins, EMNLP](https://aclanthology.org/W02-1001/) · [Yu and Joachims, ICML](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~cnyu/papers/icml09_latentssvm.pdf)

On 63 Chinese plural development pages with at least one legal reference placement, median free-minus-best-reference score fell from 14.013 to 4.481. On thirteen of those pages the annotated list rose strictly above the original reader's fixed wrong path; on every page an unrestricted answer still lost exact credit. On a separate sample of 112 Chinese plural training questions the competition reader wrote one correct answer. Smaller gaps can reflect smaller score magnitudes. The perfect unrestricted decoder and the availability of genuine training positives are insufficient to establish a useful language reader.

Against the same fixed first annotations, the control wrote 27 correct parts among 112 outputs and competition wrote 50 among 254, out of 729 requested parts. Both wrote the genuine compound 喷薄 when asked to give compounds illustrating the readings of 喷. It occurs in one human list, which also requests sixteen other compounds. The earlier parent wrote ten fragments, including broken page markup. No part of that markup was repaired for evaluation. On the separate 256 paired passages the parent, control and competition readers gave both correct answers on four, two and one pages. The three list readers gave zero exact responses on 96 source-absent writing questions per language. The separate English sentence writer also gave zero complete exact targets; single-reference scores may miss paraphrases. All three failed the 128 historical shape requests in each language.

A constructive audit found 55,674 distinct physical training pages for 56,169 question–page examples. A deterministic lookup using the pictured page alone could satisfy at least 56,002 of the training examples. This is an existence result for memorizing the observed training pages. The models' measured training accuracies were much lower. Sibling questions with distinct targets, open writing and passage-change tests provide an empirical way to distinguish reading from this shortcut.
