# Decision after complete-sequence continuation adaptation

The [complete comparison](visual_continuation_learning_result.md) rejects the
registered three-epoch recipe. Its final proportional result is 7/32 exact,
0/16 both-correct pairs and 14/32 readable answers, with zero coherent complete
long-book continuations. All five fixed snapshots and every prescribed control
are measured. Further unchanged training, selecting the three-quarter snapshot
as the final result, or relaxing whole-answer rules would not address this failure.

The result narrows the explanation. Adapting all 36 answer layers over complete
QA and book targets improves some short answers relative to the actual initial
checkpoint, but does not produce sustained visual language. The earlier
correct-image-history comparison already found conditional writing errors with
clean history; a feedback-only explanation is insufficient. That diagnosis used
the earlier actor and must not be presented as a measurement of this new final
checkpoint. The new trial does not isolate whether reading access, alignment
between language state and writing, uncertain conditional targets, generated
history or termination dominates the remaining error.

The next model intervention is selected in the
[joint-writer decision](continuation_joint_writer_decision.md), with an
implemented, untrained 2,642,368-parameter physical writer. Its full fit is not
yet registered. Preserve earlier native-reader, copying and history controls
as constraints rather than repeating completed measurements.

The [saved-trajectory analysis](visual_continuation_trajectory_result.md) is now
complete. Final long-book writing is predominantly dense and close to the
context-independent balanced-loss template, while first states and images still
change across all available question/erasure contrasts. This narrows the observed
failure without isolating its cause. The
[clean-history control](continuation_clean_history_result.md) is also complete
at the actual initial/final checkpoints, with both long cache checks passing.
Later proportional QA improves under correct history, while sampled training
books still fail conditional writing. These results motivate the selected
joint physical writer; complete autonomous language remains the acceptance unit.

A candidate ordered-block representation remains a hypothesis. Preserving order
in a shorter visual volume is established only for the invertible CPU operator;
it has not demonstrated compression, faster language learning or meaningful
generation. A proposed block model must predict future visible spans from
earlier spans, exclude the target block from its own context, decode coherent
images and reread actual emitted pixels. Reconstruction on supplied answers
cannot qualify that proposal.

Select one bounded change from the observed failure, retain an unchanged
control, and register the complete-language comparison before a new fit. Keep
the current paired QA, all erasures, long-book criteria and earlier successes.
A development pass must survive untouched source-group confirmation. Account
for the entire deployed body and inherited preparation costs before making a
lightweight or efficiency claim. Historical forms and English/Chinese etymology
remain part of the full objective after useful modern-Chinese language is shown.

The Nature-style manuscript and native PowerPoint figures must incorporate the
actual checkpoint table, failures and resource scope. Existing reading/review/
redline PDFs predate this completed result and must not be described as current.
Preserve their baselines and original images during the next bounded revision.
