# Writing around a selected piece of evidence

The two-question writer received both exact facts in its excerpt banks on 168 of 384 novel requests. It returned a stopped answer containing each fact once in the requested order on four of those 168. The first new page shows one failure directly. The writer repeated the person who lifted the pages and omitted the binding material.

We factor the answer into two clauses. For each pictured question the writer sees the original page and one marked excerpt. Its supervised output is the natural clause with the excerpt replaced by one physical insertion mark. At inference, a stopped frame with exactly one insertion mark expands around the selected source pixels. A malformed frame remains visible and fails structural scoring. The two clauses follow the supplied question order, with ordinary punctuation between them. The system supplies ordering and the excerpt pixels by construction. The neural test concerns the words generated around those pixels and the accuracy of the evidence selection.

Fit three passes over the original 671 accepted pages with the same writer initialization. The teacher and human answers are available only for supervision. Test predicted top-one excerpts against bare joined excerpts, the direct writer and explicitly labelled oracle excerpts. Use the original 96 novel and 12 authored pages for diagnosis and reserve the next 64 eligible BiPaR validation pages in the existing deterministic order for a further test. Record empty frames, valid stops, exact facts, both orders, newly drawn context, and the quality of statements. Claim progress in grounded writing only when the candidate improves both-order scores over the direct writer in the two original collections and independent reading verifies complete, supported statements on new pages. Exact inclusion under oracle excerpts is solely a diagnostic. Preserve failures, original pixels and partial outputs.
