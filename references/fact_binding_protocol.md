# Can a single latent choice bind a set of visual facts?

The additive reader improved individual Chinese matches but completed zero of 96 fresh plural answers. An audit found concrete pairs of accepted DuReader lists whose same-cardinality exchanges match no annotated alternative. For disjoint placements the sum of the two original path scores equals the sum of the two exchanged scores. This blocks simultaneous strict preference for both originals. A system can still win exact QA by choosing just one original. Test the finite-score constraint and free writing separately.

Freeze the complete 308,422-parameter contrast reader. Fit the same contextual two-logit projection in two arms on genuine Chinese questions with an exchange witness. The additive arm adds the sum of learned start and interior gains of selected runs. The two-reading arm adds the absolute value of that same sum. Each list independently chooses its better sign. Exact decoding for the latter is the best of two ordinary dynamic programs. Use 2,000 batches of 12 witnesses, reshuffling without replacement and retaining complete source context. Train on the two original lists against their one-member exchanges, with a unit-margin softplus, a squared correction penalty and weak regularization for absolute correction on real annotated lists. Evaluate only final weights. Use integer scores at thousandth precision for both supplied-list and unconstrained decoding. Keep source and cardinality rewards unchanged. Limit each fit to 12 GiB allocated and 30 minutes on GPU0.

Before fitting screen new official development witnesses against both original training pools, previously tested pages/questions and one another. Keep up to 64 in hash order. Exclude original training witnesses matching previously evaluated questions or excerpts. Archive all identities. For each new witness test whether both originals independently beat their respective swaps, ties separately, and repeat with an unrelated pictured question. Also let the decoder select any admissible spans on these pages without access to the candidates, counts, or source annotations. Score complete answers and all fixed 224 previous questions, including Chinese singletons. The matched additive arm and frozen parent are controls. Qualification for autonomous use requires a strict gain over both in free exact on fresh witness pages, a positive paired binding gain, and no exact loss on the 224-page comparison. A binding gain alone qualifies only the conditional representation. If discrimination survives unrelated questions, test dependence on the text of the question explicitly before attributing that gain to comprehension.
