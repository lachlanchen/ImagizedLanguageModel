# Who does a marked expression refer to?

A known venue name resolved many explicit Chinese price comparisons, but the writer charged both the former and the latter 12 yuan in a passage giving two different admission fees. The same sentence can contain adequate physical evidence and still attach it to the wrong participant. This comparison tests the intervening skill of connecting a different-looking, marked expression to an earlier written name.

Use the original Chinese and English human coreference clusters in GOLEMcoref with its published whole-work train, development and test splits. Use the printed source sentences and exact character offsets. Exclude empty nodes and split-group referents. Restrict queries to printed personal pronoun expressions and potential antecedents to short annotated mentions containing a proper noun or foreign proper-name candidate. The POS filter uses upstream automatic tagging. A deliberately limited 288-cell passage can omit a character's name. Include passages with at least two candidate entities whether or not the requested name is present. The operational null answer means that no eligible earlier mention of the referent is visible. It cannot establish that the character is unnamed elsewhere.

Fit a lightweight glyph encoder and interval selector on GPU0. At inference it receives only the passage ink and a visible underline at the requested expression. It may copy any contiguous earlier span up to 24 cells or abstain. The start, end and underline come from the image. Upstream tokenization, POS, entity clusters, name candidates, language identity and gold answers are supervision or evaluation only. Minimize the negative log probability of all acceptable source locations or null, with an auxiliary loss for finding annotated candidate names. Train a matched byte observation model. Use 2,400 updates per fit, equal languages per batch of 32, fixed draws, final checkpoint, at most twelve minutes and 4 GiB allocated per fit. A snapshot of device energy covers each optimization loop. Do not tune on either reserved split.

Score both languages and every held work separately. Measure exact source interval, copied output matching any acceptable name, visible-answer coverage, false abstentions and false positive answers on missing evidence. Cross distinct pronoun queries on the very same image, including pairs sharing the nearest prior name. Compare with the nearest annotated name, the most frequent visible entity and an oracle candidate list scored by the learned reader. Test erasing context ink with query geometry preserved. A gain against geometry alone cannot establish a gain against linguistic baselines. Read results at the level of works as well as individual correlated questions. Preserve failures and report the limits of marked questions, the supplied font and naming labels. Failure to beat a candidate baseline directs the next test to name recognition or referent attachment, according to which error persists when candidates are supplied. [Human annotations and supplied splits](https://github.com/GOLEM-lab/GOLEMcoref/tree/4f038383971cc45fe5901dcb18b5b0ab83a67066)

Alongside the maximum-score interval, compare a decoder that sums probability over all intervals spelling the same physical strip before choosing an output. Keep null as its own action. Summation credits the repeated printing of one answer without changing any weights. Preserve the maximum-score interval as the primary result. For deployment keep that primary rule unless aggregation improves development in both languages. A separate transfer probe reuses the known garden and tower failure and an English translation. Neither language's fitting queries include “former” or “latter”. Keep these authored questions separate from natural coreference.
