# Writing an omitted Chinese expression from pictured context

The article-title model reproduced 629 visible spans but no title requiring an absent glyph. Only 490 training positions required independent drawing. Evaluate missing content on the [ChID idiom cloze corpus](https://aclanthology.org/P19-1075/) using the [CLUE distribution](https://huggingface.co/datasets/clue/clue/tree/28178267a609dd08bdc703dd6c931dfc2c2f4431/chid). The standard benchmark supplies choices. This experiment omits them and uses the answer annotation only as the supervised target. Multiple expressions can make sense in a passage. Strict agreement measures recovery of the author's wording.

Keep training groups whose SHA-256 of the ASCII decimal group index begins with three zero bits. Use the complete official validation split, two sides of up to 24 pictured cells around each gap, and an identical neutral placeholder for any other gap. Reject unsupported renderings and duplicate pictured windows. Exclude from validation any window in the selected training set. Retain source group identity for resampling and never access the official test split. All model inputs and targets are binary Noto Sans images. Physical tile indices in the preparation files compress repeated bitmaps and are never model features.

Separate the task into obtaining a physically writable representation and selecting its successive measurements from context. Select 48 distinct pixel coordinates using only the distinct nonempty training target masks. At each step choose a coordinate that separates the most pairs of still indistinguishable shapes. If all remaining pairs are distinguished, use coordinates with high binary entropy. Record collisions. Train a dense network to reconstruct the full image from these 48 literal ink measurements. Test exact reconstruction independently with training-known and unseen development shapes. The measured bits can identify familiar characters; the trained renderer can learn their shapes in its weights. No complete-glyph table, answer-choice inventory or font is permitted in answer generation.

The sequence model reads the ordered visible ink on both sides through a trainable visual encoder. Predict successive groups of four measured pixels and a stop at a glyph boundary. Feed the previous completed picture back into the recurrent state. The 16 possible values of a four-pixel observation are the same at every position. Use seed 419, 12 epochs, AdamW and the final checkpoint, with beam width four and an eight-glyph safety limit. Evaluate intact input and a cyclic exchange of contexts, measuring complete exact stopped images, number of recovered idioms, absent Han cells and comparisons by source group. Include a training-frequency baseline. Score the actual emitted images and compare them with the best reconstruction allowed by the true measurements. The stage criterion is at least 20 distinct correct reference expressions containing an absent Han glyph, a positive lower 95% group-bootstrap bound over exchanged input, and at least 99% exact rendering on training-known reference shapes under true measurements. A positive result supports natural visual cloze completion. Prompt switching, explanations, handwritten and historical writing, book continuation and matched-cost general language remain separate requirements.

Use one RTX 4090 D, two CPU threads, at most 8 GiB allocated VRAM, 6 GiB host RSS and 35 minutes for fitting and evaluation. Preserve a failed fit, its original output and fixed criteria. Inspect resulting source balance, free images and reference-conditioned drawing before selecting a further intervention.
