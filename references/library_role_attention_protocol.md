# Where the translator attends while writing a grammatical role

The literary writer often draws recognizable words while losing who did what to whom. Before adding role supervision, measure how much existing last-layer cross-attention lands on the matching grammatical role. Use both frozen writers, the two development books and all eligible English and Chinese writing positions. Supply the correct answer prefix. The scoring annotations are absent from the neural forward pass.

For each attention head, normalize the query–key dot products over all valid source windows. Split each window's probability uniformly among its occupied glyph cells. Punctuation and unlabelled cells retain their share. Average the heads and sum the probability on source cells with the target's role. The physical windows span four cells and overlap at stride one. An encoded window can contain context from the entire sentence. These weights therefore measure attention to labelled locations. Establishing that the model uses a specific word to generate a meaningful answer requires separate interventions and free writing.

The control keeps the source sentence, attention, all tagged locations and each role's count fixed. Randomly permute the source role labels among the tagged locations. If a role occurs on (k) of the (K) tagged cells and the total attention reaching those cells is (m), its exact expected attention after reassignment is (mk/K). Linearity of expectation applies even with overlapping windows: every tagged cell has probability (k/K) of receiving the role. Skip a target role absent from its source. Report observed and expected mass by work, direction and role, including signed differences. Each reported mean weights eligible target glyphs equally.

Use one frozen CPU forward pass per minibatch, under four CPU threads and 4 GiB of model working memory. Check probability conservation, the exact 5,960 eligible English positions and 2,289 Chinese positions, and a small exhaustive permutation example. The outcome establishes the baseline for a matched training comparison regardless of the sign. The subsequent comparison still needs an independent free-meaning assessment.
