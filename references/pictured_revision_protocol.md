# Source-grounded revision of a pictured first draft

A classical-to-modern writer trained on its own mistaken glyphs lengthened answers and lost the subject of short utterances. A learned phrase composer preserves more of each sentence but cannot express most recorded translations. Give the revision model the complete pictured classical source and a fallible modern draft, and let it revise bounded spans. Positions outside accepted edits retain their original ink. At deployment the drafting and revising systems must both be included in cost and independence measurements.

Prepare four chapter-disjoint folds from the existing 55,872 aligned training pairs. For each held fold train a composer only on the other three folds, additionally removing identical complete source ink. Feed each held source through its corresponding composer. Keep the resulting training drafts, including empty and erroneous ones, with their original sources and modern targets. Use the full 55,872-pair composer at evaluation. A measured draft corpus is preparation for the comparison, without giving a revision model any quality credit.

Train the next candidate to estimate a bounded edit from the **source and draft together**, with the unchanged draft as a legal decision. Supervise acceptance on held training chapters. Freeze the acceptance threshold before any fresh book evaluation. Compare with the draft untouched and with the same trained reviser given mismatched source context. Require a paired improvement over the draft on complete physical edit distance and faithful complete translations, and report every loss among previously acceptable drafts. Review the reference alongside all anonymized alternatives. Count new grammatical errors, changed identities, polarity reversals, stop failures and limitations of corpus alignment. If meaningful acceptance fails to increase, preserve the baseline. Cap the initial comparison at one RTX 4090 D with 10 GiB of allocated tensors and preserve a 24 GiB host reserve.

The preparation produced all 55,872 training drafts from 1,200 chapters, with 1,226,175 draft cells and 1,762,187 target cells. Each held fold contained 13,145–14,312 aligned rows. No complete source bitmap appeared across the prospective fold boundary. The stored array includes the canonical images, per-row offsets and fold assignment. No revision model is fitted in this preparation. [Measured fold counts and archive hash](evidence/pictured_revision_drafts_20261118.json)

### Fixed comparison

Use all four prepared folds to train a visual editor. Its actions keep one draft cell or replace it with zero to four observed target images. For each draft cell retain up to eleven nonidentity alternatives seen at least twice during alignment. The physical descriptors of candidate strips are shared across contexts. Two bidirectional encoders read source and draft, with position-biased cross-attention. Fit eight passes and keep the final checkpoint. Calibrate a common change-versus-keep log-score offset on 512 previously held-out development passages with drafts from the full composer. Using these development chapters preserves the entire draft corpus for optimization. Choose the smallest complete edit count, breaking ties toward fewer changes. Include the unchanged draft as a calibration option.

Exclude every fitting, calibration, coverage, causal-writer and previous recovery-test chapter from a fresh 1,024-passage test. For the source intervention rotate sources within equal-length groups when possible, retain the same actual draft, and count distinct substituted sources. Report counts and chapter-paired bootstrap intervals. Grade the first 18 changes from distinct chapters blind, and a separate all-comers stratified sample of 18, because the acceptance decision may leave most drafts untouched. The changed sample measures the actual edits. The all-comers sample measures the delivered system. The two cannot be pooled as independent random samples. For the changed packet record semantic losses among accepted drafts and new errors of grammar, identity and polarity.

Before reading any new passages or method assignments, add an inference-only text baseline to the two reading packets. Use local Qwen3 8B Q8 with the classical source rendered back to its original text, greedy sampling, thinking disabled and a 160-output-token ceiling. Ask for the entire modern gloss only. Include its verbatim response among the anonymized choices and retain truncations. Report this comparison separately from the 1,024 physical predictions. This additional control uses a pretrained language model, supplies the transcription to it, and does not participate in editor training or inference.
