# Preserving a proposition in optical translation

The stride-one translator failed all sixteen relation, negation and number challenges. The news continuation translated none of 32 editorial requests faithfully. Test whether distinguishing nearby propositions improves whole translations. Keep all earlier outputs, weights and judgments. Begin from the released news continuation, train two copies in the same order on identical pictured sources, targets and ordinary bilingual replay. Fit six passes. Include bilingual minimal edits of real conversational and editorial sentences with alignable names, modal polarity or small quantities, and compositional examples with explicit paired events. The second loss adds a contrast at the first physical target cell where the two correct translations diverge, comparing the correct glyph with the other translation's glyph. The first fit uses ordinary complete-sequence likelihood. Never provide a candidate answer or reference during free generation.

Reserve before optimization (1) unordered combinations of participants and number contexts in the authored grammar, (2) separately worded sentences about all three phenomena, (3) real conversation families and editorial article groups outside both preceding semantic evaluations and (4) the original 16 failed contrasts as a regression cohort. Keep identical normalized English or Chinese sentences out of the intervention training when they enter evaluation. Measure complete exact output, conditional discrimination with the shared correct prefix supplied, paired autonomous success, blinded semantic adequacy with reference and foil calibration, and general-domain retention. Treat a result limited to familiar constructions as such. A general improvement needs gains over the starting writer in both human domains and evidence of transfer to independent wording. A new loss must beat matched likelihood to merit attribution. Use the fixed final checkpoint, one GPU under the project lock, two CPU threads, at most 18 GiB allocated VRAM and three hours per arm. If generation still fails despite successful conditional discrimination, inspect complete free answers and test a compositional writing intervention.
