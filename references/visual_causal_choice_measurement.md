# Causal meaning under a changed premise

Evaluate the frozen dense and endpoint readers on the original COPA test set and all original–mirror pairs of Balanced COPA. This supplementary measurement is separate from the registered free-writing decision. No training, tuning of templates or alternate checkpoint selection uses COPA. Render the premise followed by “because” for a requested cause or “, so” for an effect. Lowercase the initial determiner or pronoun of each supplied alternative when joining the clauses, retaining first-person I. The primary score is mean log probability per alphabetic run of the entire alternative, minus its mean log probability after the same connector alone. Retain direct and neutral scores separately and archive scores before grading. Evaluate strict alternative order, both members correct after a premise reversal and the direction of the change in preference. Report ties. Both members use identical alternatives, so any prior that scores them without the premise can select at most one correct member. Use bootstrap intervals over questions or complete mirror pairs. These candidate rankings test premise sensitivity, not free generation. [Original COPA](https://asgordon.github.io/copa.html) · [Balanced COPA](https://aclanthology.org/D19-6004/)
