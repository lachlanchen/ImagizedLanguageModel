# Passage prediction with separate language and drawing memories

A frozen writer given target images copied 1,450 of 3,845 unfamiliar combinations after drawing history was reset at each glyph. It copied zero with the same within-glyph search and retained drawing history. Both arms were given the output length. This experiment removes both sources of answer information. The same pictured ChID passage alone must determine the next 48 ink measurements, when to stop, and the whole returned answer.

Use the released passage encoder, physical pattern recurrence and frozen 32-by-32 renderer as initialization. A new outer GRU retains its own language state across glyphs and reads 48 measurements from each actual drawing together with the original passage state. The inner GRU starts at the current language state for each glyph. It sees the current glyph's previous four-bit pattern and already drawn landmark prefix. It models twelve physical groups of four bits each. A separate learned stop decision precedes each glyph, including the initial one. Commit the best of four within-glyph paths; permit eight glyphs plus a final stop decision at that boundary. No answer candidates, target images, reference length, lexicon or font reach free inference.

Fit two arms from identical weights seeded at 720, four passes over the same 142,878 pictured passages, minibatches of 192, AdamW at 0.0002 and gradient clip 1. Pair each passage with its own target in one arm and derange entire passages in the other by rotating the epoch permutation halfway around. Train on the total negative log likelihood of the supervised trajectory divided by its 48 pattern and five stop/continue decisions. Supply measurements of the rendered reference bits as past glyphs during teacher forcing. Keep final weights only. Before fitting verify that the batched teacher score agrees with a stepwise reference for a real minibatch and that generation can stop immediately and reach its cap.

Reserve previously unused source buckets 124–131 and exclude earlier pictured windows. On this partition measure whole answers including stop, known emitted glyphs, per-position exactness, correct prefixes, novel expressions, targets absent from context, hard pairs and output concentration. Compare the aligned fit with shuffled training, swapped passages, the released writer, the existing uniform-exposure writer and training-only local counts. For deployment require positive lower bounds of paired 95% source-group intervals against the shuffled, released, uniform and count baselines, at least 99% known emitted cells, and no decline from the uniform writer in both-correct pairs. Run the existing historical regression only if these conditions hold. Otherwise keep the released checkpoint and locate the first loss in free generation. Because registered answers in this corpus contain four glyphs, correct stopping here cannot establish variable-length control.

Use one RTX 4090 D and two host threads. Each phase must complete within 35 minutes, 8 GiB of allocated device memory and 6 GiB host RSS, with the existing machine admission checks. Report setup and training cost separately from prior training and evaluation. Results on this diagnostic task cannot establish general book writing or savings at matched language quality.
