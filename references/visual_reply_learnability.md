# A finite learnability result and its limit on the measured replies

Consider a finite family of page-image predictors. Each returns one physical spelling and a stop. For a stream of labelled requests with one accepted spelling, predict the most frequent reply among the still-consistent predictors. Upon observing the answer, retain the predictors that gave it. On an incorrect prediction the group supporting the incorrect reply is disjoint from the group that survives and is at least as large. Consequently, after $m$ mistakes the initial number of predictors is at least $2^m$ times the number remaining. If one predictor is correct throughout, at most $\lfloor\log_2 H\rfloor$ mistakes are possible from $H$ initial predictors. Lean verifies the finite trace argument over natural numbers, including tied plurality decisions. The result assumes access to labels after prediction. It does not establish realizability in books or convergence of neural-network training. [Checked theorem](../formal/span_list/VisualExpertLearning.lean)

We checked the premise on the 824 pictured MLQA requests. Each has a single accepted bitmap spelling. The four existing methods return the selected excerpt, the full-page writer's output, the excerpt-conditioned writer's output, or its saved stream stopped when it first completes the selected excerpt. Even a selector given the answer could recover only 149/412 Chinese requests and 65/412 English requests from those outputs. On the other 610 requests all four miss. A better selector over these same four outputs therefore has an exact-answer ceiling of 214/824 on this packet. New answer hypotheses or changed writing behavior are necessary to move beyond it. We then sorted the frozen reader's physical intervals and counted each distinct bitmap spelling once. All 824 first choices reproduced the saved excerpts. Among the 610 requests missed by all four outputs, the correct spelling already occurred in the first eight candidates on 171 requests and in the first 32 on 326. Across the entire packet, the top-32 lists contained 324/412 Chinese and 209/412 English answers. Access to the answer was used only to measure coverage. Supplying these alternative spans alongside the original page is a testable change to the writer's inputs. Compare against the same number of within-page distractors, and require autonomously written two-fact answers on additional works before claiming composition.
