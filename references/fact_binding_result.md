# Can an image reader learn which facts belong together?

A paired-list score can learn a relation that the additive reader cannot express. On 64 previously unseen Chinese development pages, the two-reading head ranked both supplied annotated lists above their respective one-item exchanges on 20 pages. Both additive baselines scored zero. Each original list has disjoint physical placements and each exchange keeps its count. Lean proves the additive scores of the original pair and the exchanged pair have identical sums. A maximum over two signs breaks the restriction and still admits exact whole-page decoding in two passes. No inference-time word dictionary, external model or answer candidates are required to run the free reader.

**What the reader could write.** The frozen reader answered one of those 64 pages exactly. Both trained heads answered zero. On the separate 224 confirmation pages the frozen, fitted additive and two-reading readers wrote 17, three and four complete answers, respectively. A source-sensitive whole-answer gain was the precondition for adopting a head. Neither head qualified, and the deployment default stays at its earlier checkpoint.

**What the reader could recognize.** The two-reading head retained simultaneous preference on nine pages under an unrelated question, lost it on eleven, and gained it on seven. The two-sided paired exact test gave P = 0.481. In all 64 original conditions its free optimum scored strictly above both supplied annotated placements, by a median 17.048 score units (range 1.757–84.636). The parent median was 4.786. Other accepted placements can exist. This comparison identifies an omission in the training objective: it controls those two swaps but leaves other incorrect whole-page selections free to win. The protocol therefore supports conditional list discrimination and supplies no evidence of improved question-dependent writing.

**Data and execution.** We found 7,713 exchange witnesses among 67,405 Chinese entity training questions and 219 among 2,675 development questions. Each one is a lower bound from a conservative search for a joint disjoint placement. The matched fits used all 7,587 screened training witnesses in 24,000 presentations each. Both start with the same frozen 308,422 parameters, add 10,434 learned parameters, use final weights, and run on the same RTX 4090 D. Fitting took 19.31/19.57 seconds and 2.38/2.41 kJ of incremental device energy; earlier fits, corpus construction, evaluation and host costs are additional. Independent repeated fits reproduced the two released weight files byte for byte. The primary experiment used the registered in-place implementation; the reproduced code also accepts empty paths in supplied-candidate scoring. Training witnesses always have at least two members. The empty-output state was available to the decoder in both executions.

**Which training pages can supply a complete target?** We separately screened the raw training partitions using the same 1,200-cell excerpts and sixteen-part cap. For each annotated list we sought a non-overlapping placement of every distinct literal member, in any order. Choosing the earliest eligible finish for a fixed next member preserves feasibility, as checked in Lean. The bounded search exhausted no budget on these partitions. At least one annotation fits on 52,795 of 67,405 Chinese and 4,813 of 5,230 English training pages. Considering alternatives recovers 8,701 Chinese pages that the first reference alone excluded. On 34,508 Chinese pages with a valid answer, at least one member of a valid list occurs more than once. These counts precede font, question and source-overlap screening. The score of one chosen placement can underestimate an acceptable answer's score. **Next acceptance test.** Train against the highest-scoring unrestricted answer on the entire pictured training page while retaining the original reader's likelihood and count supervision. Evaluate on fresh question/page families, two different questions about the same page and a corpus requiring prose absent from the source. Keep the image-only student at inference. Evaluate historic-form fidelity and meaningful English and Chinese prose separately. A decrease in the free-optimum score gap on held placements measures the mechanism; a gain in autonomous answers over both controls is the adoption criterion.
