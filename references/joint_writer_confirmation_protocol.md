# Independent confirmation of joint reference training

The joint reference-only writer completed 548 of 22,907 development gaps and 207 of 8,790 newly examined gaps, compared with 378 and 131 for the published guided writer. Adding the competing-trajectory term gave 333 and 132. The reference-only fit was a control and was not selected for release on that comparison. Freeze its final checkpoint and the published writer. Evaluate both on an additional source sample, hash buckets 44 through 51 inclusive. Exclude all previous contexts and original training groups. Reuse the candidate-free, fixed beam and learned stop. No further training, checkpoint selection or threshold adjustment. Require a positive lower paired 95% source-group bootstrap bound for complete-answer improvement over the published writer, at least 99% known emitted shapes, and at least as many both-correct matching pairs. Recount every exact answer and include the count baseline, unseen expressions and the number of complete answers with all four glyphs absent.

If these conditions pass, test integrated historical use before publication.
