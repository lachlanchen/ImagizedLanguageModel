# Saved-output dependence after the rejected full-context comparison

The full comparison has zero readable complete QA in both arms. The completed
gradient diagnostic does not meet its opposing-direction criterion. Its next
bounded analysis concerns the visibly recurring output patterns: measure exact
pixel repetition and variation across different questions and erased prompts
using only the retained autonomous streams. No neural call, new generation,
training, OCR, reference answer or transcription repair enters this analysis.

Read every cohort in the original generation manifest: all 792 streams, all five
prescribed checkpoints in both arms, all erasures and final long books. Verify
the manifest's original registration hash, cohort request/report hashes, frozen
score cohort identities and each raw-array hash. Read the original binary arrays
without repainting them. Empty streams remain separate; two empty responses do
not count as identical nonempty writing.

For each arm/checkpoint/profile, report the number of streams and nonempty
streams, distinct complete raw streams and distinct first tiles. Record the
largest exact first-tile and complete-stream multiplicities as descriptive
counts. Do not infer semantics or assign readability from those fingerprints.

Compare every normal QA question with each available question-erased and
passage-erased version. Compare both questions of each normal QA passage pair.
Compare every normal book with its available prefix-erased version. Retain all
prescribed pairings and every empty/missing-first-span case. Report complete
nonempty equality, first-tile equality/Hamming distance, common-prefix equality
over up to sixteen tiles, and first-span equality/distance when both first-span
lengths match. Keep stopping/cap differences visible.

The fixed generation seed, eight flow steps and matched first-span lengths
provide a common initial noise shape. Different first-span lengths change that
shape; do not pool those comparisons as controlled noise-matched effects.
Different pixels demonstrate visible dependence, not correct question handling.
Equal pixels for these deterministic draws do not prove that the entire
conditional distribution ignores the prompt. Recurring damaged patterns may
coexist with small prompt-dependent changes. A stopped empty stream is not a
meaningful response. Original language scores and frozen readings never change.

This is a descriptive analysis of completed outputs, not a new language test or
an opportunity to select an intermediate model. Use two CPU threads, no GPU,
a sixty-second deadline and at most one GiB process peak RSS. Bind this protocol,
analysis source, original manifest, original scores and every accessed raw array
and cohort report in the result. Preserve failures and do not drop cases to fit
the budget. No new fit is admitted by either high repetition or visible variation.

If exact repetition dominates, consider a separately registered conditioning
intervention rather than another unchanged epoch. If variation is present,
reject a claim of literal prompt independence and investigate the quality of
conditional image selection. Neither finding establishes semantic learning or
selects an output-law change without its own bounded rationale and preflight.
