# Writing the pixels of a chosen glyph

The literary writer always returns a stored bitmap. A clean contour therefore gives no evidence of learning to draw that contour. Freeze its entire language model and replace the table supplying the drawing with a shared neural map from each chosen optical embedding to 1,024 binary decisions. Retain the original next-choice scores and stop, then feed the mean ink of each emitted two-by-two square back to the language model. The deployed bundle may contain the learned visual choice vectors and scores. Exclude bitmap tables, fonts, text decoders, character labels and training examples from the inference path. This design retains a finite internal choice inventory.

Train on the union of the existing English and Chinese output inventories. Include the genuine space, exclude the stop. Use one fixed seed, 4,000 AdamW updates in batches of 256 with binary cross entropy, a cosine learning rate, and one terminal checkpoint. Require all deployed tiles to be correct or explicitly report their errors. Require bit-identical complete answers and stopping for at least 99% of the 192 already reserved book requests to establish faithful transfer of this language system's behaviour. Evaluate every request, including empty, wrong and repeated answers. Source text, reference images and the stored repertoire remain unavailable to the deployed writer. A mismatch in a generated answer requires a new reading before assigning it the old meaning credit. Equality demonstrates reproduction of existing language behaviour. Open-form generation, semantic improvement and compute advantage require their own tests. Cap the fit at 12 GiB and 30 minutes.
