# Reading a local pictured phrase inside a full passage

Among 194 previously held-out passages whose eight glyphs nearest the gap occurred together in training, exact completions numbered 16 for the uniform-weight writer and 144 for local counts. To test whether those glyphs can directly condition the independent writer, add a residual from the four ordered cells on each side of the gap. Concatenate their existing 112-dimensional visual measurements, apply layer normalization, a 512-unit GELU hidden layer and a linear projection to the 192-dimensional context. Initialize the final projection to zero. The original full-passage reader and physical writer remain trainable and the renderer remains frozen.

Start two arms at the same released joint reader/writer and identical new parameters. Both use 142,878 windows, four passes, seed 720, the previous physical likelihood, optimizer, minibatches, decoder and stopping rule. The aligned arm receives its own neighborhood. In the control, rotate the ordered row permutation by half its length each epoch to supply a different passage's neighborhood. Both use the ordinary passage and gold answer for the original reading path. At evaluation give both writers a single real input with its actual neighborhood. Compare also with the earlier uniform-weight writer trained from the same initialization and budget without the additional branch. The published starting model is a separate reference.

Reserve training-file source-group hash buckets 112–123 before training. Exclude every earlier scored or training window and the full additional-training pool. Report source-group paired intervals on exact autonomous writing, separate eight-cell matches and new neighborhoods, count-baseline successes and failures, both answers correct in same-neighbor pairs, rare and unseen expressions, stops and emitted glyph fidelity. On the preceding 104–111 cohort measure retention of the 286 earlier answers missed by local counts. A candidate merits integrated and standalone release checks if the lower 95% limits against both shuffled and original uniform fits are positive on the new cohort, with more exact answers both on repeated and on new neighborhoods than the uniform fit, at least as many correct answers among local-count misses, and at least 99% of emitted cells matching a training target bitmap. If qualification fails, retain the released checkpoint and distinguish ineffective conditioning from lost whole-passage evidence before another fit.
