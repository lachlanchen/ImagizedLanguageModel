# How much meaning is recoverable from a freely written draft?

The independent phrase writer covered more target content but received zero whole-translation credits. Test whether grammatical editing alone recovers the missing meaning before training an independent editor. Freeze all 96 book requests and their previously emitted answer images. Send lossless optical readbacks of the phrase draft or neural draft to the same local quantized Qwen3-8B editor. In those two conditions withhold the original sentence and reference. A third condition supplies the original sentence and the phrase draft. Retain the earlier source-only teacher, both raw drafts, the paired reference and the unrelated foil. Grade all eight modes together with the previous source-only judge in two opposite orders. Use one GPU and preserve all responses. Treat results as a teacher diagnostic on an already evaluated panel. A high source-assisted score cannot count as independent ILM performance.
