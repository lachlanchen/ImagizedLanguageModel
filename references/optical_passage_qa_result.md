### Where copying stops

The reader can emit any admissible interval of a supplied page. Changing the question can change the selected fact. A proof shows that a question-independent answer cannot complete both members of a pair with disjoint acceptable answers. It also proves that an answer outside the set of page slices is unreachable for any setting of this decoder. These are statements about the model interface. The held measurements establish how often the learned parameters succeed. [Lean derivation](../formal/span_list/OpticalPassageQA.lean)

In CoQA's 7,199 first-turn training answers, 1,258 answer strings occur nowhere in the corresponding passage after lowercasing and normalizing whitespace; 765 of them contain multiple words. This count concerns the recorded first reference. It permits meaningful alternative answers. The next experiment should give a glyph writer access to the pictured question, evidence and earlier dialogue, and compare it with interval copying on held passages. Report absent multiword answers separately from binary replies, with a question-exchange control and tests of source dependence. Unconstrained conversation, historical handwriting and source-free etymology remain open.
