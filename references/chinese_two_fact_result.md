# What makes a price a price in a generated sentence?

A Chinese guest asked for a hotel's price and address. One image writer included both values but drew “地址是1354元，价格是北京西城区广安门内大街217号。” Its successor drew “价格是1354元。地址是在北京西城区广安门内大街217号。” Both models saw the same question and full venue record as pixels. Both had the same inferred order. Giving the writer access to the field currently being expressed corrected the relation in this example. ## Learning to select evidence and express its role

CrossWOZ provides human dialogues, dialogue acts and venue catalogues. We used the official conversation splits. Inclusion in the original two-fact set required one resolved venue and two different catalogue values that both appeared in the human answer. The training split yielded 5,097 single-field and 368 double-field turns. We constructed 45,202 additional question–answer pairs by joining two training turns about different fields of the same venue. Their wording comes from humans and their juxtaposition is synthetic. The development split contained 517 single-field and 37 double-field turns. The test split contained 515 and 37. We examined the original 37 during method development. The later 14-turn check was frozen before evaluating those turns. [Dataset paper](https://aclanthology.org/2020.tacl-1.19/) · [Inclusion and training protocol](chinese_two_fact_protocol.md)

The reader assigns a score to each possible field at each ink-bearing position of the pictured question. The score of an ordered field pair averages its evidence over all strictly increasing pairs of positions. A prefix sum evaluates the mass over possible first positions for each second position. This makes the computation linear in question length at fixed field count. Training marginalizes over both field orders when their order lacks reliable supervision. Lean proves the prefix identity under the stated algebraic assumptions. Exhaustive short sequences check the implementation and its gradients. A second proof identifies the exact copy sequence an ordered decoder can accept. These results establish computational properties. The held questions test whether the learned scores attach linguistic roles correctly. [Mathematics](../formal/span_list/VisualFieldAlignment.lean) · [Ordered-copy theorem](../formal/span_list/OrderedSourcePlan.lean) · [Numerical tests](../tests/test_two_fact_writer.py) · [Earlier latent-alignment work](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~graves/icml_2006.pdf)

The 90,345-parameter optical reader chooses one or two ordered properties. At each output step a 243,505-parameter GRU sees the domain, previous, current and upcoming field, and the preceding output action. The current-value action copies the original image strip and advances the field cursor. This gives the recurrent writer the same property identity when it writes the cue and when it inserts the evidence. Either a generated ordinary glyph or a name copy can occur between value copies. Stop becomes legal when the fields have been expressed. An earlier constrained-search comparison applied ordering only to emitted pointers. The surrounding words could then contradict a correctly ordered pair of copied values. ## Natural questions and counterexamples

A clean answer contains the requested values and no competing catalogue value or unexpected number under the literal scorer. The attachment check additionally requires an appropriate property cue in each value's local clause. Neither criterion covers all acceptable paraphrases or unrelated statements.

| Writer on the 37 two-field turns | Clean values | Clean values with local property cues |
|---|---:|---:|
| Visual joint writer, original training turns | 14 | 11 |
| Visual joint writer, joined training pairs added | 24 | 18 |
| Same weights, ordered copies required | 30 | 22 |
| Visual position alignment, ordered copies | 36 | 24 |
| Two clauses independently written under the same aligned plan | 36 | 23 |
| Visual position alignment, current-field writer | 36 | 36 |
| Matched byte reader, same current-field writer | 37 | 37 |
| Local Qwen3-8B using ordinary text | 35 | 35 |

The final image model gave clean values on all 515 single-field test turns and 513/517 development turns. On the 37 development pairs, all 37 had clean values. With both values masked, the final test replies showed 19 surface patterns for 19 domain and field-order combinations. Only four of the 37 answers reproduced the full human sentence verbatim. All 552 public test PNGs, replayed on CPU, reproduced the output pixels of the batched GPU evaluation. Values outside the generated alphabet can pass through the physical copy path. Reading the question exposed a limitation of the labels. One guest explicitly requested rating, cost per person and opening hours. Only cost and hours were annotated. The image writer supplied rating and hours. The byte reader supplied the annotated pair. Qwen supplied all three. A second literal Qwen miss expanded 北京顺义区 to 北京市顺义区. These examples explain why its count cannot rank general answer quality. A blind Qwen judge also accepted visibly swapped field names from an earlier comparison. That judge did not evaluate the final current-field writer. The source-aware reading and automatic labels in this experiment are available for inspection. Independent human adjudication remains open. [Actual Qwen replies](evidence/chinese_two_fact_v1/qwen-answer.json) · [Earlier judging outputs](evidence/chinese_two_fact_v1/qwen-judge.json)

After freezing the networks we admitted additional official held turns without requiring the human reply to repeat the annotated values. All 14 question wordings were new. Both visual and byte models passed on 12/14. One annotation requested an address that the visible user never asked for. One genuine miss asked how long a visitor could stay at 三里屯酒吧街 and where it was. The visual answer omitted the duration. Only one venue was unseen in training and that example failed. This extension shares the original corpus and selection of single resolved venues. For 18 property pairs presented in both directions, the visual model followed a novel list-order wording on 18/18 and a first-then wording on 12/18. Asking it to report the first-mentioned property *second* reduced this to 0/18. Each wording is a single construction reused across the pairs. The visible order of evidence still dominates some instructions. A test of general visual reading must include natural passages without fixed field labels and requests where evidence order and answer order disagree.

## Computation on one RTX 4090 D

The two scratch fits used 2,400 batches of 48 examples each. The writer saw 1,240,255 target actions. Optical reader plus writer took 27.2 seconds and 2.58 kJ of device energy. The byte reader plus the same writer took 26.3 seconds and 2.51 kJ. These intervals count model fitting and exclude dataset preparation. At warm inference, one serial sweep over 37 questions took the image model 0.393–0.492 seconds and 35.3–44.5 J, across three 32-sweep blocks. Qwen3-8B Q8 took 18.66–18.71 seconds and 4.17–4.23 kJ in two warm sweeps on the same GPU. Its path includes loopback HTTP. Page preparation, host energy and model loading are excluded. The specialist has a known layout, eight possible fields and short answers. A general-language efficiency comparison requires matched capability on broader held material.
