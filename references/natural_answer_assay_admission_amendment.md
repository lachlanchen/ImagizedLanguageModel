# Admission to the complete-answer comparison

Adopt this amendment on 11 November 2026 while the real prompt-state cache is
running and before the candidate fit or qualification has produced a report.
The frozen [cohort registration](natural_answer_assay_protocol.md) originally
required a passing first-image score to start the complete-answer comparison.
Replace that requirement with completed first-image measurements and inspection
of the ten fixed boards. Bind the same final 36,000-update checkpoint to both
reports and record the original threshold result in the binding receipt.

The balanced writer improved reconstruction while the new-context complete
answer count changed from 19/32 to 18/32. Foreground overlap measures agreement
with a canonical raster and can penalize a readable form. Measuring complete
answers after either physical result lets this experiment test the relationship
directly. Report both results and any physical failures. See the
[balanced writer comparison](balanced_glyph_writer_result.md).

The reserved inputs, comparison parent, final step, full physical measurements,
stop and cap rules, output review, reference scoring and descriptive pilot
thresholds follow the original protocol. The amendment authorizes measurement
with a failed first-image score. Deployment still requires a separate decision
using the complete evidence and the standalone replay.
