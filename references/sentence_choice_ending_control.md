# Where the extra exact insertions come from

The paired writer recovered 2,529 of 24,048 complete insertions against 2,143 for its frozen parent, while multi-glyph successes fell from 138 to 60. The source residual affects both the pattern logits that draw the next glyph and the ending logit. To measure the contribution of each path, hold the trained weights fixed and independently enable the residual at drawing only or at ending only. For both controls use the same decoder, original full sentence, location, modern prefix, feedback from the model’s own emitted pixels, original ending threshold and all five existing rosters. Include every enrolled site. Recount exact stopped answers, first-glyph matches, multi-glyph successes and complete stops from the saved images. Compare with the already scored parents. These hybrids are diagnostic. Neither can replace the registered candidate. If first-glyph errors dominate, the next training decision should test the ability to choose the intended first glyph at matched source contexts before another end-to-end fit. If end-only produces most of the difference in exact answers, separate ending from content in future training.
