# Returning to a page while writing an answer

On a Chinese fossil-discovery question the recurrent writer gave 尼日, where the interval reader returned 与隐. On a second Chinese page it gave a fish's distribution when asked for its classification. Across the 256 held paired passages the recurrent model passed both questions on 38, compared with 44 for the earlier optical interval reader. Its complete individual answers fell from 195 to 178 of 512. The byte counterpart to the recurrent model passed both questions on 37 passages. [Original ink](../models/recurrent_evidence/examples/dynamic/paired/cmrc) · [Measured scores](evidence/recurrent_evidence_v1/dynamic-scores.json)

The new head can readdress the passage based on its written history. It mixes copying a complete interval, starting a visible run of length one, two, four or eight, and writing individual glyphs. At a run boundary, different actions can spell the same output. An inside recurrence sums their mass, and the reverse recurrence yields posterior edge visits for backpropagation. The exact finite weighted-path theorem was checked with Lean 4.12; it assumes finite paths and integer weights. Separate numerical tests check the floating-point estimator, its gradients and the physical decoder including a censored prefix. None of these identities asserts that an answer is true. [Proof](../formal/span_list/RecurrentEvidencePaths.lean) · [Executable checks](../tests/test_recurrent_evidence.py)

Each arm received 3,200 updates, 64,000 draws and 3,529,972 target cells. The new training sources contributed 38,500 Chinese and 3,289 English examples alongside the prior book, instruction and question data. The 344,879-parameter optical head with dynamic attention took 81.45 s and 10.65 kJ of device energy. Its fixed-attention control took 84.75 s and 10.85 kJ. The matched byte arm took 84.04 s and 10.89 kJ. These are incremental measurements on one RTX 4090 D. The standalone optical writer includes the 2,117,313-parameter book model. [Protocol](recurrent_evidence_protocol.md) · [Sources and reserved IDs](evidence/recurrent_evidence_v1/corpus.json)

Exchanging the source increased the cost of supplied answer glyphs from 1.55 to 3.13 bits in English and from 5.67 to 7.61 in Chinese. With self-generated histories, the writer had zero exact matches on 96 English and 96 Chinese questions whose recorded answers were absent as contiguous spans. In a masked, project-model reading of eight questions per language, two responses conveyed the full answer, compared with five for the source-sentence heuristic. Removing the whole-interval route still gave zero exact source-absent answers and only 14 English stops. On the museum prompt the trained writer supplied only one of two requested bronze counts. It gave neither historical form correctly in the 言 example. It solved no external Winograd pair.
