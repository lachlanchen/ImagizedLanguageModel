Test whether seeing additional contexts helps the same visual reader and physical writer recover the source expression. Keep prepared source indices, final weights, complete output pixels and stops. Use GPU 0, at most eight GiB allocated device memory, six GiB process RSS, two CPU threads and 35 minutes per phase. Retain the shared machine's 24 GiB available RAM and 48 GiB disk reserves.

Compare both fits against each other, the starting writer, and local counts estimated from each training corpus. Use paired source-group bootstrap intervals. Count distinct emitted streams, aggregate top-ten frequency, cases where all expected glyphs are absent, both members of same-neighbor pairs, and accuracy on unseen and rare expressions. Use original training-frequency bins for a common comparison and also give counts under each arm's own corpus. Qualification requires a positive lower 95% paired bound on fresh complete answers against both repetition and the starting writer, at least as many both-correct pairs as each, at least 99% emitted cells matching training shapes, a lower top-ten share than repetition, and a positive point gain over repetition on the preceding set.
