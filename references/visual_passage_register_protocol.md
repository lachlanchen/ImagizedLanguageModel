# Training on modern passage ink at a matched exposure budget

Of the native reader's 2,571,477 prediction targets, 2,400,020 come from fourteen Chinese book titles and 171,457 from short answers. The modern QA prompts contain 4,678,984 input positions. A prompt image can affect the answer through gradients to the reader, but its successor receives no direct writing target inside the prompt.

For a diagnostic of this difference, index every distinct training QA passage once. Use the authenticated source group and training question identifier only during preparation. Match all packed cells to the training rendering of the complete passage and record the exact slice of the original training shard. Exclude question, answer and wrapper. The index yields 2,127 passages, 1,049,411 glyph positions and 1,047,284 next-image transitions. Training can read the original pixels through `VisualPassageCorpus` with a zero instruction prefix. The index copies no image or model weight. It contains training groups only.

## Controlled corpus comparison

Fit two identical smoothed visual bigrams, one on all 1,047,284 unique-passage transitions, the other on exactly that many historical-book transitions. For the book control, visit original training records in SHA-256 order of the string `book/<corpus index>`, ending at the fixed budget. Keep every transition inside a source record. Both models use the same alphabet of physical training bitmaps and one unknown type, with Witten--Bell interpolation and add-one unigram smoothing. Each complete sentence score conditions on the shared first cell and supplied length. Exclude stop factors for both models. Training sees only ordered bitmaps. Neither arm receives benchmark examples, grammatical labels or a tokenizer.

Use every eligible, physically distinct ZhoBLiMP order pair outside the earlier 552-pair diagnostic, including all 23 eligible paradigms. Exclude pairs with either physical sentence present anywhere in a complete original training record. Deduplicate in either orientation, including reuse of the earlier pairs, and count every exclusion. Traverse archive members alphabetically and rows by original file order. Test sentences share the first image, length and full multiset of rendered cells. The benchmark uses the existing pinned archive and font. This is a fresh pair cohort within the same known constructions. [Benchmark source](https://github.com/sjtu-compling/ZhoBLiMP/tree/e8402762a563c13def19080a31e2cbb8d89e4ef3)

Use the same 1e−6 tie tolerance, half tie credit and equally weighted paradigms. Record micro counts, macro accuracy and 10,000 paired whole-paradigm bootstrap intervals (seed 20261119). A modern advantage requires the lower endpoint for modern minus books to exceed zero. Evidence above chance requires a lower endpoint above 50%. If both hold, modern-passage prediction merits a controlled neural comparison after the autonomous evaluation. Otherwise retain the passages as available data and leave the intervention unqualified. This comparison tests corpus register with a local predictor. It cannot award the neural model credit for grammar, free writing or training efficiency.

Use CPU only, two threads, at most 4 GiB RSS and ten minutes, with 48 GiB host memory at entry and 24 GiB retained. Keep original scores and checksums. The ongoing neural evaluation uses its original single GPU owner.
