# Writing complete insertions in classical Chinese translation

A previous model often assigned the higher likelihood to the appropriate modern phrase when two phrases were supplied. Its independent sentence decoder reproduced zero complete changed references on two chapter-held-out rosters. This comparison tests whether the same visual network can generate a phrase with the site supplied. The full task still requires choosing the site and writing the preceding translation independently.

Use every contiguous insertion in the recorded alignments of the original 48,000 training sentences and the further 16,384 short training pairs. Keep the two established 1,024-sentence held-out rosters and their original unsupported requests. No evaluation target is used to fit weights or select epochs. All arms at evaluation receive a known nonempty site and the actual preceding modern glyph images. The conditioned writer also receives the full pictured source and its boundary. It draws at least one glyph, feeds its emitted pixels back to its recurrent history, and chooses an end after every glyph. The 32-cell evaluation limit permits an end after the final cell. Count overflow as failure, including for references longer than the limit.

Start both fits from the same marginal sentence editor. Use the 48 physical ink measurements and frozen neural renderer, an added projection for the left boundary and one learned end decision. Minimize the sum of the conditional negative log likelihoods of the twelve four-pixel groups of every drawn glyph and the binary decisions after it, normalized by target glyph count. Fit three epochs with AdamW, learning rate 0.0006, batch size 96 sentences, seed 3129 and identical minibatches. The ablated fit receives the same constant one-cell source and fixed boundary on every request, retaining the entire available modern prefix. Use the final checkpoints. One serial owner on GPU 0, two CPU threads, 8 GiB allocated CUDA memory, 6 GiB process RSS and 35 minutes per phase. Retain all raw output pixels.

Compare against the prior marginal writer forced to initiate at the known site, using its own emitted history and insertion action to continue, and a training-frequency baseline with ordered local source neighbors and modern prefix. Score exact stopped whole images, multi-glyph phrases, phrases absent as a whole insertion from training, and the established 293 natural phrase-exchange pairs. Evaluate both offered assignments and both independently written answers. Reverse source order with its boundary mirrored. Pair uncertainty by chapter for comparisons on the full rosters. Proceed to full sentences at predicted sites only if the lower 95% bounds for exact whole-phrase improvement over each of the three controls exceed zero on both cohorts, the conditioned fit completes at least one unseen insertion type per cohort, and at least 99% of generated phrases stop on each cohort. Otherwise keep the public default and use the new physical errors to identify what blocks independent writing.
