# Learning which pictured facts belong in a complete answer

On 116 previously evaluated Chinese plural questions, the independent reader wrote one fact on every page. Giving it the number of requested facts recovered only four complete lists. Test whether training on scores of competing full answers improves reading. Start both arms from the same published conditional reader. Preserve the existing image encoder, 16-run decoder, source length and learned count reward. Use a deterministic shuffle without replacement, with two full passes over Chinese training rows and four English and twelve Chinese examples per step. Pad only the final schedule with the next permutation. Report actual training, energy and unique counts. Use 7,182 updates per arm on one RTX 4090 D, a 30-minute update budget per arm and a 12 GiB allocation ceiling. Save recoverable optimizer state. Fit and evaluate the final weights. Keep the original reader as a third comparator.

The control continues the conditional path and count objectives. The treatment also ranks the complete training path above the highest-scoring strict subset of its marked runs. For a sampled gold run length it ranks the full path above replacing that run by the strongest disjoint non-reference run of equal length, and above appending that distractor when within the output cap. Mask all exact pictured occurrences of every annotated gold part when mining alternatives. Other plausible answers can remain unannotated. Use a one-nat margin, softplus and coefficient 0.20 averaged over the available contrast families and pages. Formalize the cancellation of local scores and the subset bound used for the hardest deletion. Check differentiable scores against full path enumeration, including touching runs, duplicates and padding.

Reserve fresh official development excerpts by hash before inspecting predictions, screening against the previous 544 tested questions/pages and the original English/Chinese training sources. Use 64 English plural, 96 Chinese plural and 64 Chinese singleton questions. Keep examples with missing evidence. Compare all-parts exact, part precision/recall, quantity, question exchange and complete answer pairs after replacing one fact with unfamiliar ink. Evaluate distinct real questions on the same page using the earlier bilingual paired corpus as a separate transfer test. Compare the output pixels on the earlier Western Xia case and write English sentences from the selected facts. For adoption require more fresh Chinese plural exact answers than both the control and original reader, no loss of English plural or Chinese singleton exact versus control, and at least one correct Chinese altered-ink pair. If selection improves while stopping or article-quality prose still fails, report these capabilities separately. If the gate fails, retain the original and identify the dominant remaining failure. Report all conditions in the manuscript with actual model ink.
