# Selecting a complete span improves Chinese short answers

A question-conditioned scorer of complete visual spans improved extraction and short-answer writing over the original reader on a fresh Chinese test. It recovered additional English facts while producing exactly the same five correct English statements. The bilingual adoption criterion failed. Complete statement formation with the correct fact already supplied is a separate bottleneck. [Study protocol](fact_span_reranking_protocol.md) · [Runnable weights and original images](../models/fact_span_ranking/README.md)

## Matched reader comparison

The original reader proposes 32 legal visual spans. The two new heads add an equally sized residual score to each position. One sums learned nonlinear scores for the left boundary, right boundary and length. The other scores their joint representation and the average question-conditioned state inside the span. Each head has 70,144 parameters, identical initialization and the same schedule of 156,228 presentations of 39,057 distinct training questions. The writer is frozen. Both fits optimize the conditional mass of all annotated positions in the available list. A correct target occurs on 29,299 lists. Among 1,249,824 candidate positions, 57,247 repeat an earlier physical strip on the same list. The stored endpoint and interior features occupy half precision in training and inference. Candidate preparation took 15.74 s and 1.82 kJ on one RTX 4090 D. The heads' separate fit costs are recorded per epoch. The fourth cohort was reserved before fitting. English uses human QA2D statements linked to QAMR question and passage annotations. The earlier SQuAD-derived English source had no eligible passage after all exclusions. Chinese uses CMRC. There are 96 distinct passage families with two questions in each language. The three selectors received identical candidate positions and base scores on all 384 questions. The release code reproduced the original reader's generated answer, selected region, offsets and stopping on every page. The whole-span gain over the original Chinese result was 6.25 percentage points, with a passage-paired 95% bootstrap interval of 1.56–10.94 points. Against equally exposed endpoints the gain was 3.13 points, with an interval of −0.52–7.29. This trial used one seed. In Chinese the improved hit count matches the number of correct selected spans. In English, two corrected choices and no lost first choices gave zero additional complete statements. Among accepted English answers absent from the visible input under both the normalized-text and bitmap tests, all three systems gave five normalized exact replies out of 181. ## From the training loss to a drawn answer

When several accepted positions repeat the same fact, their total likelihood can exceed that of an individual competitor even though the wrong position wins. If an incorrect position wins, the sum of the accepted weights is bounded by the number of accepted positions times the combined rival weight. We proved the bound in Lean for nonnegative integer weights. The audit uses exponentiated relative scores rounded to parts per billion. The sufficient condition certified only two English and eighteen Chinese winners for the whole-span model. All certified predictions selected an accepted fact. The audit tested all 384 emitted strips against their actual selected physical location. Independent exact enumeration of the conditional loss agreed with all eight parameter-tensor gradients for each head on real training rows with zero, one and two positive candidates. [Lean proof](../formal/span_list/FactRankMass.lean) · [Gradients and independent CPU PNG replay](evidence/fact_span_ranking_v1/verification.json)

The first English question on which whole-span scoring recovered the fact but the writer still failed asks “Who did he beat?” The reader recovered Terre'Blanche. The writer used the name as both subject and object. The first recovered Chinese fact giving an exact reply was the date 2009年1月5日. Six release manifests, including four for 言, independently reproduced their answer bits on CPU. Supplying the human fact yielded 44 exact English statements and 187 exact Chinese short answers on the 192 questions in each language. Of 143 English references containing the value exactly once, 113 failed. On 69 failed replies the right value appeared once, on 15 it was repeated and on 29 it was missing. Even with access to the reference and freedom to replace any single contiguous output interval with the fact, only 19 of these 113 could recover an accepted once-framed reference. In the other 94 the writer would also have to change the surrounding text. The automatic judge accepted only six of sixteen edited English human references and one of sixteen edited Chinese human references in both presentation orders. The study therefore leaves paraphrase quality ungraded. ## Transfer and efficiency

Historical-form requests yielded three of 130 exact Chinese specimens and zero of 130 English specimens for whole-span scoring. All four period requests for 言 still failed. All three pipelines failed the first eight glyphs on every one of 152 book continuations and all 68 in-window source-absent conversational replies. The new pipeline also lost two of the six exact earlier Chinese catalogue replies. On the image-matched 32-question Q4 Qwen3-VL-8B comparison, ILM and Qwen contained the requested English fact on 1 and 12 of 16 trials and the Chinese fact on 5 and 15 of 16. Measured device energy was 180 and 9,240 J under the respective implementations. The quality difference precludes a matched-capability efficiency factor.
