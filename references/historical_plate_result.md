# Reading an illustrated historical entry

A Chinese or English question and four pictured records enter a 2.65-million-parameter model. In the historical task the model locates the requested character and stages, returns the pixels of the selected forms in order and stops. A separate learned route writes missing expressions in modern prose. A fresh CPU process answered “Show 言: oracle, bronze” with Hanziyuan J04919 and B02976. Asking for bronze then oracle in Chinese reversed every output bitstream in the expected order. This 言 plate belongs to training. The first held-out two-stage example returned the bronze and seal forms of 马. ## Selecting a record

Hanziyuan provided the historical images. The experiment crossed two headwords with two attested stages on each plate, shuffled row order and asked sixteen questions. Training used 6,000 plates, 96,000 requests and 1,985 character families. Development used 600 plates, 249 other families and 1,372 distinct historical images. Source labels, family identifiers, entries and exact historical rasters had zero overlap between these splits. Rephrasings reuse those 600 plates. The sealed partition was unused. The corpus receipt predates the separate export of training plate 302 for the 言 demonstration. ## What the calculation buys

For a plate with four distinct images and a specified length of one, selecting the picture requires at most two bits to identify its row given the plate. Copying that row transports its 1,024 recorded bits. A writer without the plate must obtain the shape information from elsewhere. Relocating pictures tests this distinction because their identity changes independently of the printed headings. The design extends the use of attention to select an input position. The pointer and the final name matcher took 38.19 and 3.21 seconds of fitting on one RTX 4090 D. The 3.21 seconds exclude the earlier reader, the modern writer, preparation and evaluation. [Pointer Networks](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/hash/29921001f2f04bd3baee84a12e98098f-Abstract.html) · [Training protocol](source_plate_span_protocol.md)

Order must survive any compression of a stream of glyphs. Adding two image layers commutes, so the same fused layer represents both AB and BA. If both orders occur equally often, even an optimal decoder receiving only this fused layer can recover at most half of them. A compact two-dimensional representation can preserve order through position-dependent encoding. That addresses recoverability. Learning which answer belongs after a passage still requires predicting context-dependent distinctions. In the modern validation trial, 91,235 of 91,622 emitted positions had an exact training-known glyph shape, while only 378 of 22,907 expressions matched the recorded answer. This gap makes independent choice of words the higher-priority problem. Existing work on autoregressive pixel text and multilingual visual pretraining provides relevant comparisons. [Writer results](visual_writer_transfer_result.md) · [PIXAR](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.874/) · [Multilingual pretraining for pixel language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21265)

The historical reader uses four fixed headings, eight explicit Chinese and English stage names, and a four-row layout. Each row contains one modern headword, three slots for a stage heading, and a historical bitmap. The experiment establishes instructed extraction of a visible exemplar. General language, open-page reading, lexical-role disambiguation, independent factual explanations and matched-quality compute savings remain open.
