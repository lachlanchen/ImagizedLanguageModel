# Training a pictured writer against its own wrong answers

The previous editor favoured its own answer over the supplied translation by mean glyph log probability on 95 of 96 fresh requests. Keep the trained glyph editor, original draft rule, optical repertoire, marker and autonomous decoder. Reserve four further complete works before mining or fitting. Use twelve pairs per work in both directions and keep the 472 earlier controlled prompts. Exclude both sides of the training corpus, used works, earlier panels and shared printed passages.

Use a seeded sample of 2,048 natural training pairs in both directions. Freeze original free emissions with their actual stop. Two local bilingual models independently judge both candidate translations against the source. Reverse the order. Admit a preference only when both accept the reference as complete and reject the actual free answer as materially wrong. Preserve ties, rejected references and disagreements. Teachers have no inference role. Retain unfiltered parallel examples and earlier event rehearsal.

Start both arms at the same released draft-aware weights. Train on the same 39,008 examples, drafts, verified preference pairs, two epoch schedules and optical positions. Both arms compute the positive and negative sequence scores. The control continues supervised likelihood. The treatment adds a smooth pairwise margin loss on mean log probability per target cell including stop. For each ordinary batch add a separate sample of up to 48 verified preferences, using identical positive supervision in both arms. Use AdamW at 0.00008 then 0.00004, one RTX 4090 D, a 15 GiB allocation ceiling and 30 minutes per arm. Cap teacher verification at two GPU hours; keep independently completed batches resumable. Record wall time, device energy where available and upstream dependencies separately.

Test the parent, likelihood control, preference writer, and the same preference writer with exchanged source and its corresponding draft. Grade free translations in both candidate orders together with quantized Qwen, references and unrelated foils. Preserve original raw image streams, stops, constituent scores and a preregistered figure selection (first preference-only improvement over matched training, otherwise first differing answer; first subsequent failure in the opposite direction). Require more book meanings than both original and control, positive paired work-resampled lower bound over control, retention of parent polarity-pair and crossed-role performance, and improvement over matched training on mean reference-versus-own-output margin. If the comparison fails, locate which of training margin, held margin, free generation and controlled meaning failed. Keep the terminal original outputs.
