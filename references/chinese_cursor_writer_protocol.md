# Preserving the relation between the clause and its evidence

The aligned visual reader plus ordered-copy decoder returned both correct values on 36/37 human two-fact test questions. Several sentences assigned a price to an address or an address to a rating. Both value presence and the permissive blind local judge overstate sentence correctness. The same-plan independent-clause comparison also printed an address as a price. Preserve both sets of original outputs.

Keep the 90,345-parameter learned visual field planner fixed. Replace sentence realization by a compact recurrent writer trained from scratch on the same one- and two-field programme. At each step it sees the pictured domain, the current field, the most recently copied field, the remaining field if present, and the preceding generated image action. The source-pointer action transfers the next planned field and advances the cursor. Only stop after the plan is exhausted. The writer has to learn ordinary surrounding Chinese wording. It receives no reference answer, furnished sentence frame, or text question at inference. Training uses the same 2,400 updates and sampling schedule as the joined controls. At most 300 seconds and 1 GiB allocated. Release the original writer and failures as controls.

Compare both source values, their order and their linguistic attachment. Before scoring the new outputs, specify a strict automatic attachment check. For each requested value, isolate its local clause and require an appropriate nearby property cue. Reject a conflicting field label, except generic price wording when it denotes restaurant per-person expenditure or admission price. Include an explicit source-visible reading of all new replies and of the earlier 37 replies for contradictions, omissions and gratuitous claims. Preserve the original numerical tests. Include authentic references and Qwen in the same diagnostic to show which valid paraphrases it misses. A repeated surface pattern can be adequate for the requested facts but does not demonstrate broad Chinese conversation. Report independent regular-language bounds and whole-system cost.
