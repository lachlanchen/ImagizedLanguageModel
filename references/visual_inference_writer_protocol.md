# When the claim changes what the same passage means

The DuReader stance writer often responded to the passage alone. It retained its position across all 24 changes of subject that reversed the expected answer. A follow-up uses original Chinese claim–evidence pairs from OCNLI. Human annotations distinguish a claim supported by the premise, contradicted by it or left unresolved. The same premise can support different relations across claims. A complementary writing task removes a real, contiguous one- to three-glyph phrase from the claim and asks for the missing text. The three fixed decision sentences measure conditional interpretation. Missing-span generation measures exact writing under local context, including content physically absent from the two inputs. Multiple completions can be plausible. [Hu et al., Findings of EMNLP](https://aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.314/) · [Original data under CC BY-NC 2.0](https://github.com/CLUEbenchmark/OCNLI)

Render each original sentence and each full target in the existing 32-pixel font. The full-image audit checks every retained premise, claim and response against the upstream record. Use 33,441 training pairs, 3,348 internal validation pairs on 512 reserved premises and 2,947 official development pairs. Normalized full premises and full claims each have zero same-field cross-partition overlap. The 512 validation families and 35 official families that include all three outcomes provide fixed triplets requiring all three responses. The internal validation cohort intentionally contains richer premise families than official development.

Use the just-measured 694,152-parameter matched stance writer as initialization. Keep its ink embedding and writing keys fixed. The sole paired-versus-mispaired training change is which pictured premise each claim receives in both tasks. Reassign within genre, preserving exact premise counts, changing the normalized premise for every training row and varying the permutation each epoch. Retain every human claim, label and missing phrase. Draw one eligible target per claim and epoch, uniformly among available lengths and then positions. Keep at least one visible claim glyph, exclude punctuation from targets, and use a constant physical mask independent of hidden length. All mask targets have a route through the fixed writing memory even under premise reassignment. At evaluation fix all mask draws before generation with seeds 73121 and 73122. Evaluate actual matched, passage-erased and, for decisions, claim-erased inputs.

Optimize eight epochs in batches of 64. For decisions weight the first-cell negative log likelihood and the sequence mean including stop equally. Add the missing-span mean including stop and 0.15 times the mean likelihood loss on 12 mixed English and Chinese dictionary rehearsal rows. Use AdamW, 75-step warmup and cosine decay from 0.0004 to 0.00008. Use the final weights only. Limit each fit to 25 minutes and eight GiB allocated on GPU 0 with two CPU threads. Report model and inherited costs separately.

Require the paired model to exceed both mispaired training and its own erased-premise inference on exact stopped decision sentences with premise-clustered 95% bootstrap intervals above zero on official and internal validation. Disclose complete triplets and blank-claim results. For missing natural text, apply the same two comparisons separately to all spans and to those requiring a glyph absent from the inputs. Report scores by span length and spontaneous answers on the unchanged real question sets. A positive decision result, exact short spans or a fixed response inventory each leaves general open-ended language, historical fidelity and matched-cost efficiency to be tested. If pairing gives no separable advantage, inspect reference visibility, genre and single-input shortcuts before any scaling.
