# Generated-form validity: post-result diagnostic

Date: 2026-11-05. V54/V55 primary audits and decisions are already known.
This is a read-only diagnostic; it does not amend their gates or decode new
samples. The previous goal turn made progress by training and auditing V55,
verifying an independent export, publishing measured figures, and checking an
external baseline. The next unresolved question is the validity of the actual
generated forms, separately from matching one observed next glyph.

Use every saved primary single-image output and four-step feedback output
from V54, V55 frozen and V55 joint. Reconstruct their unchanged, evaluator-only
canonical bank from training forms plus audit targets. Check 9,932 unique
rasters, full target coverage and identical bank fingerprints across runs.
No bank image enters generation or replaces any output.

Measure exact binary-raster bank membership, nonblank membership, distinct
exact forms, blank rate, and maximum ink F1 to any bank raster. Maximum F1 is
a descriptive approximation measure, not proof of human readability. Count
exact target-image matches separately. Record all per-example values and
paired source-record bootstrap intervals for V55 joint versus matched frozen
exact-form membership and target-image match. Use 2,000 bootstrap draws with
the existing audit grouping/seed. No optimized threshold is introduced.

Canonical membership is specific to the tested font and inventory. A readable
variant or genuinely novel form may fall outside this bank, while a valid
isolated form can still be an irrelevant continuation. The result must not
be promoted to general legibility, language fluency, historical provenance,
instruction following or a changed primary qualification decision.

Retain reports and a measured figure. If the diagnosis changes the next
learning intervention, record that implication explicitly before training.
