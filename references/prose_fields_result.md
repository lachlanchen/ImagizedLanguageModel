# Reading the right value for the right venue

A human travel assistant put two ratings in one paragraph. With the first restaurant named in the prompt, the visual model wrote **评分是4.7分。** With only the name changed, it wrote **评分是4.8分。** This was the one natural CrossWOZ test utterance that contained two distinct catalogue names and a different literal value for each under our screen. A larger controlled experiment used unedited human replies joined in pairs. ## Removing the row labels

The previous optical writer recognized fixed property rows. Here the evidence is a continuous human utterance from a different conversation about the same venue. Source and reference reply must differ. The reader learns a null option and a contiguous interval for each of eight properties. The query planner and clause writer stay frozen. A missing requested interval triggers an application-level abstention. Training used 11,605 human utterances, including 9,782 unique normalized texts, and 10,722 within-venue combinations of utterances. The evaluation excludes passages duplicating the training material. Only two of the original 184 answerable pairs concern venues absent from the locator's fitting set. Its literal references concern the values as recorded in the catalogue. ## Changing the referent on the same page

A locator that receives only paragraph and domain must return the same location for the same field whichever venue is requested. Its input contains insufficient information to distinguish two owners. The new locator receives three features at each cell from the unique exact pictured occurrence of the target name. They represent membership in the name, the side of the name and decaying distance. Its trainable convolution can assign source evidence before or after the name. Duplicate and absent names receive zero features. Across all 54,307 original and competitive fitting presentations, exact bitmap matching reproduced every training name position, including 12,725 presentations without a unique name. [Position audit](evidence/prose_fields_v1/anchor-equivalence.json) This intervention still assumes a supplied venue identity and a fixed domain schema. [Formal obstruction and scan identity](../formal/span_list/ProseInterval.lean) · [Matched comparison](prose_entity_protocol.md)

The information loss can be counted exactly. Erasing the name row leaves 1,078 distinct pictured inputs from 2,156 questions. Each remaining image has two equally frequent, different correct intervals. Any deterministic reader returning one interval per visible page can answer at most 1,078 correctly, even with unlimited capacity and training. The erased reader recovered 1,075. Restoring the name gave 2,156 distinct inputs and the fitted optical reader recovered 2,061 requested intervals; the byte reader recovered 2,081. For two answers with frequencies $a$ and $b$ on one observation, the unavoidable number of errors is $\min(a,b)$. The frequency-majority rule attains that bound on the observed sample. Lean verifies its optimality summed over observation groups. The corresponding zero floor on distinct full images expresses identifiability; unseen-page generalization still requires a trained model and a held evaluation. ## How far does an admission-price answer travel?

In the official DuReader development sets, ten questions containing 门票 asked about named attractions. A question-only excerpt ranker selected from upstream marked documents. Eight selected excerpts contained at least one compatible base price or the free-admission statement. The original model returned a compatible core on four, and the name-conditioned model returned one on six. One of the six has contradictory references. Only one of two designated single-fee probes was correct. On the Yuantouzhu question the model returned 35 yuan for the boat while the reference gave admission starting at 90. A seasonal price, discount or compulsory reservation can be lost when an answer consists of one number. These are historical reading examples. ## Learning objective and cost

Each legal interval receives the sum of opening, interior and closing scores. The interior contribution is a difference of two cumulative sums. A cumulative maximum over earlier starts computes the best interval per end. A cumulative log sum computes the exact normalization against all intervals and the null alternative. In both cases work is linear in passage length per field. Exhaustive enumeration independently checks values, gradients and decoded endpoints. The Lean proof supplies the corresponding exact mass identity under the distributive laws. It makes no statistical assumption on unseen language and predicts no held accuracy. [Interval reader](../ilm/visual_lm/prose_fields.py) · [Numerical and interface tests](../tests/test_prose_fields.py)

The original locator has 152,352 parameters and its optical fit took 17.50 seconds and 1.79 kJ. Adding the target-name features brings it to 152,592 parameters; the extra fit took 13.15 seconds and 1.35 kJ from those weights. The frozen planner and writer add 333,850 parameters and 27.20 seconds of original fitting. The deployed total is 486,442 parameters, 57.9 seconds and 5.72 kJ for its four required fits on GPU0, an RTX 4090 D. The original and additional byte-locator fits took 17.45 and 13.33 seconds and 1.80 and 1.38 kJ. Corpus preparation, comparison fits, evaluation, loading and host energy are additional costs. A compute advantage over a comparably capable general language model remains unmeasured. ## What to learn after exact name matching

When a paragraph introduces two people and later substitutes “the former”, the ownership relation must persist after the name disappears. One testable change is to score candidate mention spans jointly with the evidence span. Given a pictured request and paragraph, learn a distribution over the mention that denotes the requested entity, then marginalize the evidence score over those mentions. Supervise the link on passages with human coreference annotations and supervise the final source interval where evidence is available. Preserve the clause writer across the comparison. Chinese and English can use the same cell encoder, but each language needs its own held test of pronouns and word order.

Whole stories with human annotations of recurring fictional characters are available in multiple languages, including Chinese and English, in GOLEMcoref. Such annotations supervise which mentions designate the same character. A price, action or other property must still be paired independently with its evidence to test a full answer. Split by work before rendering and compare against the nearest compatible mention. Hold out entire constructions as well as passages. The distinction follows COGS's separation of familiar examples from unseen combinations. PIXEL provides a precedent for learning representations of rendered text; the additional question here is whether those representations support selecting the referent and emitting a correct answer image.
