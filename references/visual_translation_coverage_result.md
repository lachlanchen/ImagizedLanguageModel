# What broader supervision changed in a visual Chinese writer

A writer trained on 841 reviewed translations learned to modernise the pictured biography 吕乂字季阳，南阳人也。 Its answer 吕乂字季阳，是南阳人。 preserves the name, courtesy name and birthplace. The starting checkpoint and the matched writer trained on 108 reviewed passages both missed the exact translation. On eighteen passages from untouched chapters, all three neural writers received zero whole-meaning credits. Figure 68 shows the biography beside two unseen inputs with the same astronomical meaning and different counts. Both new outputs preserve the number and add 岁, changing the count into an age. Independent CPU image-file inference reproduced the saved biography and the original astronomical error byte for byte. ## Repetition and retention

Each fit continued the same 2.33-million-parameter parent on one RTX 4090 D. Both writers replayed the 55,872 original book pairs in the same order, received 18,624 reviewed exposures, and took 1,164 updates with matched padding. Across the 108 sources common to both reviewed sets, the narrow writer reproduced 49 exact translations and the broad writer two. Across the additional 733, their counts were four and seventeen. Only the broad arm received the reviewed wording of those additional sources. Both saw their original book pairs. Five reviewed translations were already identical to their original targets. [Fit registration](evidence/visual_translation_coverage_20261119/registration.json) · [Training-source replay](evidence/visual_translation_coverage_20261119/replay.json)

The narrow and broad fits took 31.5 and 28.4 seconds respectively, with peak Torch allocations of 278 and 277 MiB. These incremental costs exclude the parent, corpus, editor and teacher. Timed generation over 200 inputs took 5.6 and 4.6 seconds; the local text teacher took 93.6 seconds in its separate collection workflow. Different runtimes and different answer quality preclude a matched-efficiency claim. ## Where the age unit comes from

An exploratory audit counted maximal, multi-glyph Chinese numerals that appeared exactly once on each side of a real training pair and preserved their image sequence. Among 4,663 such matches in the common book replay, 289 were followed by an age glyph in the target. When the source number came after 年 and before a comma, 75 of 87 targets supplied 岁 or 歲. For source numbers containing 百, ending in 十 and followed by a comma, zero of seven targets did so. These are corpus frequencies. The seven-case subgroup is small, and the count cannot establish what caused an individual neural output. It does identify a usable contextual cue, while rewarding the visible numeral alone gives full credit to literal copying. [Bitmap-based corpus audit](evidence/visual_translation_coverage_20261119/age_unit_audit.json)

A context-sensitive objective can compare two candidate completions of the same visible span under two different pictured contexts. If their scores are \(s(x,y)\), the interaction \(s(x_1,y_1)+s(x_2,y_2)-s(x_1,y_2)-s(x_2,y_1)\) cancels any additive preference that depends only on the source or only on the answer. A positive interaction can coexist with poor free writing. The next test therefore needs independently scored free responses to both contexts and passages from new chapters. Construct contrasts for participant roles and polarity as well as units. Audit the two continuations for meaning and reserve entire sentence frames before training. If a context-trained writer wins the contrast and still fails the passages, retain the local result and do not extrapolate to general understanding. Pixel-level language generation has also been demonstrated by PIXAR and MIXAR. Their reported generation tasks and larger pretraining budgets differ from this test.
