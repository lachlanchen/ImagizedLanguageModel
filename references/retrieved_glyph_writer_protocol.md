# Autonomous writing with related visual passages

On the earlier held panel, a fixed three-cell suffix followed by voting in target images of 32 related training passages gave a 10.4-point advantage over matched random passages at supplied-history errors. Keep the original draft editor, learned stop, source, draft mechanism, print inventory and the exact 497,775-pair corpus. Use TF–IDF cosine over pictured source strips to choose 32 supports. At each free step, mix the neural distribution with votes from those target passages at the exact three-cell history actually emitted (start padded). For n matching target positions set the mixture fraction to strength times n/(n+2); an absent suffix gives zero weight. Train the scalar strength per target direction on the earlier 72 development pairs by mean 1–4-gram glyph F2 of autonomous outputs, choosing from 0, .35, .70, 1 in that order and disclosing any zero selection. No test answers may enter support retrieval or calibration.

Reserve twelve pairs per work from four additional editions before model selection. Use the full 472 existing control questions. Compare the unchanged neural parent, related memory and identically weighted random memory matched by retrieved source length bins, plus a repeat of related memory on exchanged source and its draft. Record physical outputs, cold index and deployment size, search and generation times, stop, copy of any complete neighbor, control retention, per-work uncertainty, all grader votes, and an independent CPU replay. The primary adoption rule requires greater complete book meaning than the parent and random support, a positive lower bound over the parent under whole-work resampling, and at least 225/236 controlled polarity pairs and 107/118 role sets. A conditional next-cell or development improvement alone gives no free-writing credit.
