# Answering a live headword request from the local inscription catalogue

Build a local index of the previously screened source plates. The user chooses the headword and optionally the two historical stages. The index supplies an evidence card, including a second headword with the same two stages, and preserves entry IDs, original glyph labels and source hashes. The writer receives that image and the actual request. Return its unedited answer. Resolve both stage orders to the identical source card. A missing headword or stage has an explicit catalogue error.

Evaluate up to 100 distinct development headwords in fixed seeded order. Exclude headwords used in the training cards or in the 48 previously scored development cards. Require a second eligible entry from the development split. Generate two opposed orders with familiar wording, two with a changed Chinese instruction, two with the question in HanaMin, and two in English asking for a Chinese response. Score the full stopped output, both historical bitmaps in the requested order, and question pairs. Evaluate the parent, alternate-typeface replay and mapped writer. Retain a correct or incorrect example by fixed order and save provenance. The English instructions are an exploratory cross-language test. A correct copied shape is evidence of reading an archive page. Historical descriptions beyond the supplied evidence remain a separate task.
