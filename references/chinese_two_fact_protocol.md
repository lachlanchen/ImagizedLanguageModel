# Writing a Chinese answer from two requested facts

The previous human Chinese writer binds one value on each page. When two different values are requested, its only value pointer cannot bind both. A second independently addressable source span is the bounded model change. Learn an ordered plan with one required field and an optional different second field. Supervise it from the order of the exact evidence spans in the human reply. Let the existing visual decoder address the name, first or second value, generate its own connecting glyphs and choose stop. Exact pictured row labels and the supplied entity remain assumptions.

Use official CrossWOZ dialogues and the pinned venue catalogues. Preserve all admissible single-field cases. Extract same-domain two-request/two-informed answers containing both distinct catalogue values verbatim. Maintain three separate controls: the frozen one-field writer, two standalone replies from that writer under the new reader's inferred plan, and a matched byte input trained under the same schedule. Train an optical natural-only arm and an optical/byte pair where half the double-field presentations come from joined fitting questions about the same venue. Disclose their synthetic answers (concatenated human replies), separate from the original dialogue evidence. Start each arm at its corresponding original 8,000-step checkpoint. Fix 2,400 additional batches of 48, half single and half double, final checkpoint only, one GPU, 1 GiB allocated and 10 minutes per additional fit.

Keep question, answer length, field count and values unmarked at inference. Compare all tenable natural turns on validation and test: recovery of both values without competing values or numbers, human reply order, exact whole human wording, both role labels where detectable and wrong/absent fields. Rows where the sequence of literal field mentions in the question is unambiguous provide an additional order check, with naturally flexible ordering interpreted separately. Inspect genuine outputs, including the first failed fact test, unsupported clauses and repetition. Include a question-erasure baseline and an order reversal on held cards with the same two values. Replaying schoolbook-style rendering on held PNGs checks the full interface. Performance claims about handwritten glyphs, unsupplied records, true open-domain language and general efficiency remain open.

Update the reading manuscript and original-output figure using the measured result, including costs of the inherited checkpoint. Preserve earlier original images, editions and failed results. Keep only the latest reading PDF and recent editions in the local delivery folder. If composition fails a control, record the failure and localize it to the plan, wording or bound values.
