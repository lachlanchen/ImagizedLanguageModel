# Writing an answer from a pictured conversation

The preceding span reader can express a single contiguous interval. CoQA supplies human conversational answers together with supporting passage intervals. Fit one conditional writer that can stop, point to a visible word, or spell a word one pictured glyph at a time. Allow a pointer to omit the first or last cell, or both, when producing a nonempty word. Condition each decision on the current pictured question, passage, preceding exchange and previously emitted ink. Use the inherited optical English word encoder and initialize the passage network from the reproduced SQuAD fit. The free writer and its stop decision start from fresh weights. The held writer receives only the input images. [Previous copy limit](optical_passage_qa_result.md) · [CoQA and source licenses](https://stanfordnlp.github.io/coqa/)

Use the pinned CoQA parquet release with one recorded answer per turn. Preserve the immediately preceding human exchange as supplied conversational context. Retain entire printable ASCII passages of up to 320 words, current questions up to 32 words and up to the last 48 history words. Reserve whole normalized training passages whose SHA-256 integer modulo 17 is zero, and independently evaluate official validation passages. Remove held stories duplicated in the training set. Fit on answers of at most 16 words with at most 24 cells per word. Count longer held answers in evaluation. Use visible word boundaries and a training-print glyph palette, with no vocabulary of output words. Learn to point from all matching physical source words and to spell when none match. Annotated evidence supervises the reader only during fitting. Missing evidence intervals have no auxiliary loss.

Use 8,000 AdamW updates of 32 turns, seed 710491, without intermediate selection. Cap fitting at 50 minutes and 12 GiB allocated VRAM on GPU0. Compare the frozen SQuAD extractor on the same complete page with the current question, and measure the free writer with each held question and history rotated to the next distinct turn on its page, and with its passage replaced by a different held passage. Keep the original question and history in the passage-exchange control. Report eligibility, physical stopping, exact pixels, normalized first-reference match and word F1. This single-reference evaluation differs from the official multi-annotator CoQA score. Score first-turn and binary answers separately. The primary composition subset requires a multiword reference absent as a case-insensitive substring from each normalized input field and absent as a complete normalized training answer. Credit only terminated streams. Compare the same subset under both exchanges, with a paired recount of successes lost and gained. A source-sensitive composition claim requires more exact successes than both exchanges and the extractor on both held splits, and actual matching output pixels for at least one such answer per split. If it fails, inspect conditional word writing with the correct previous answer words, the predicted channel and the copy positions before selecting a further change. Distinguish readable answers, unseen wording and general conversational competence.
