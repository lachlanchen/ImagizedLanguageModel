# A causal image writer for classical Chinese books

The English two-word writer collapsed 147 prefixes to 36 states after its second emission. Test a causal student trained from scratch with direct access to the original 32-glyph prefix throughout a sixteen-glyph rollout. Use the installed Universal Dependencies Classical Chinese Kyoto v2.16 treebank, credited to the Institute for Research in Humanities, Kyoto University (CC BY-SA 4.0). Reconstruct volumes in source order, omit title records and break at unavailable glyphs. Reserve all of the Analects (KR1h0004) and Chu Ci (KR4a0001) for testing and all of Mencius (KR1h0001) for validation. Combine the publisher's partitions before separating works. Fit on the other works only. The two test works define the scope of inference.

The model reads aligned physical glyphs. Compare canonical 32×32 ink with four UTF-8 byte slots per glyph at the same boundaries, backbone size and scheduled training windows. Compare separate neural-plus-copy and neural-plus-copy-plus-count fits in each modality, and a count-only comparator. Count probabilities use the longest observed suffix of up to six previous glyphs supported by at least two occurrences and smooth with fitting unigram probabilities. During optimization obtain count targets and count confidence from the other three volume folds. During deployment use all fitting volumes. The pointer can emit a physically observed prefix glyph absent from the learned output bank. A finite prototype head supplies other training images.

Fit 3,200 updates of 24 windows of 64 next-glyph positions per arm, seed 194671. Score positions 6 through 64 in each training window so that the count route cannot see earlier context than the network. Use the final weights, with a 25-minute and 12-GiB optimizer ceiling per arm on GPU 0. Record preparation, fitting, parameter count, checkpoint and count-memory size, presentations and held inference cost.

Starting at index 32 in each continuous held segment, evaluate successive disjoint sixteen-glyph reference blocks. Report exact physical top-one accuracy on every position, NLL on the common training-inventory targets, and eight- and sixteen-glyph exact continuation. Record free-running position accuracy and repetition alongside correct-history scores. In a source intervention replace the distant 30 glyphs with another work's prefix while retaining the most recent two images. Also run a two-glyph-history ablation. Report each work and paired volume-bootstrap intervals. Claim successful distant-prefix use only if original free-running glyph accuracy exceeds the exchanged-prefix accuracy with a positive lower interval. Require positive lower differences over both fitted neural-only and count-only comparators for a whole-model forward-accuracy advantage. A reference continuation is one historical reading. Autocompletion is a separate task from answering questions about the work or writing unseen scripts.
