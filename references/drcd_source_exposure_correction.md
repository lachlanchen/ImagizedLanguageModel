# Correction to the DRCD confirmation claim

The November 16 reservation contained 998 official test paragraphs and 3,485 questions. Later code used the official test file as a development source. Comparing IDs in the saved factual and span-reader records to the original reservation identifies at least **530 distinct questions on 529 paragraphs from 264 articles**. This count covers named records with recoverable question IDs. An older pointing-reader evaluation also selected 128 paragraphs from the official test file. Its exposure may overlap these totals. The original reservation no longer qualifies as a wholly unseen confirmation set.

| First 384-question selector sample | 192 of 192 traditional-script questions |
| Second 384-question list sample | 191 of 192 traditional-script questions |

The recorded pixel scores describe outputs produced at the time. Their DRCD components cannot establish untouched confirmation. Seven records in each recent selector training collection also matched a normalized 64-character window from the reservation. Question IDs alone missed them. A separate article-title screen excludes additional same-topic records. The wider-list refit excludes 547 training examples and 48 validation examples after those checks. Its traditional-script evaluation comes from eligible official development paragraphs. Earlier learned components remain inherited, and the overlap audit cannot establish independence of their pretraining.
