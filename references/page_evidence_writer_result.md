# A pictured year changes a generated Chinese answer

A causal writer trained with 12 ways to request a fact and a year produced all six exact replies on **34 of 64 new single-paragraph comparisons**. Each comparison used three new question formulations, before and after a one-year edit. On two-paragraph boards, where the requested person and year could come from either paragraph, it produced all twelve replies on **8 of 48**. The same writer trained with a single date instruction passed zero of either group across all three formulations. Both models received the actual passage and question as glyph images. [Fit and source fingerprints](evidence/page_evidence_writer_20261124/varied_corpus.json) · [Physical output scores](evidence/page_evidence_writer_20261124/varied_evaluation_results.json)

On the first new passage, the model wrote **《交往行动理论·第一卷》的作者是尤尔根·哈贝马斯。该段提到1993年。** After the source edit it kept the author and changed the year to **1994**. A failed two-paragraph answer named the correct actor in 《天外仙》 but gave **2011**, the year in the other paragraph, instead of **2005**. The date task asks which year appears in a passage. It makes no historical claim about when a book or film was created. [Original outputs and reference strips](../publication/ilm-nature/figures/Figure_98_page_evidence.png)

When we restored the familiar training wording on the very same pages, the one-instruction writer recovered 76/96 complete single-page pairs and 13/64 complete two-paragraph boards. On the additional fresh inputs, the varied writer passed 53, 54 and 37 of the 64 single-page pairs under each new formulation separately. The two-paragraph counts were 19, 19 and 8 of 48. The joint criterion requires every formulation on the same source to succeed. A second count from saved bitmaps and autonomous stop flags reproduced the joint totals. The predeclared half-of-boards criterion for two paragraphs and both ordinary factual retention thresholds failed. ### What the writer retained

Simply repeating the whole source page with normalized terminal punctuation would reproduce **125/128** attested-clause references. The varied writer recovered one of the three references that actually required selecting a shorter clause. When each question was presented with two competing paragraphs, the varied writer reproduced the requested clause on 6/256 requests and completed zero of 64 four-reply boards. All six successes occurred with the requested paragraph first. [Competing-clause experiment](evidence/page_evidence_writer_20261124/competing_clauses.json)

On the earlier 359 screened human questions, the initial writer gave 39 exact stopped answers from the raw page, the one-instruction writer 27 and the varied writer seven. The project’s frozen extractive reader gave 242 on this same sample. On the 192 historical-form prompts in familiar wording both adapted writers gave 18 exact answers, and neither returned an exact answer on the 192 rephrasings. The one-instruction writer did return both catalogued forms of **言** in both requested orders on the demonstration card. The varied writer missed both. That particular card belongs to the original plate-training split. It demonstrates execution on recorded forms. It cannot establish generalization to a new inscription. ### Training and scope

The raw page and image question enter a decoder adapted from a Chinese text-pretrained GPT-2. The decoder generates one bitmap from a fixed bank or copies a physical source cell, and learns when to stop. All three fits used the same 19,823 answers, two passes, 2,052 optimizer updates and 842,468 target-cell exposures each. The varied writer trained 28.6 million of 86.6 million network parameters in 82.6 seconds of optimization on one RTX 4090 D, with 2.64 GiB peak allocated during optimization. Fixed projection, interface and bitmap buffers also ship with the model. The timing excludes data preparation, initial training of the Chinese backbone and the preceding visual adaptations. Lean verifies that a target reply can factor through a visual observation when its value agrees on every group of inputs with the same observation, given a representative of each attained observation. It also verifies a two-of-four bound on exact answers when an exchange leaves each subject's input unchanged. These results characterize the information available to the writer. Whether a fitted network learns to use that information is measured by the held-out source edits. The model still needs to select the relevant paragraph consistently, retain answers to unrestricted questions and transfer to other written forms. General visual language acquisition and whole-system efficiency relative to a matched language model remain open. [Lean derivation](../formal/span_list/PageEvidence.lean)
