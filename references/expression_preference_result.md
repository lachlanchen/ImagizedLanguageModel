## What the two training changes teach

Local hints drawn exclusively from other training folds were eligible on 10,113 passages and correct on 1,343. With a fixed reader and renderer, correctly assigning the hints improved writing relative to shuffling the same hint images across training cases. On 8,759 new passages the gain against the already published writer was four answers (95% source-group interval −0.14 to +0.23 percentage points). Both hint fits are retained as experiments. [Training protocol](local_teacher_writing_protocol.md) · [Training assignment counts](evidence/expression_preference_20261118/local_hints.json)

Two passages that share the cells at the edge of their gaps can require different answers. For both contexts we calculated the probability of each of the two complete physical production paths, including the stop and feedback from the actual drawn glyphs. On 3,056 matched pairs the guided writer assigned the two answers to the appropriate contexts in 1,956 cases. It independently preferred each own answer to the competing answer in only 270 pairs. The first measure cancels any score attributable solely to how often a particular answer occurs. Neither measure gives the writer an autonomous completion. [Paired scoring protocol](paired_physical_answer_protocol.md) · [Scoring and sequential replay](evidence/expression_preference_20261118/paired_diagnosis.json)

We updated the same 700,291 context and writing parameters in two arms for eight epochs. One minimized the observed physical sequence loss. The other added an explicit preference for the recorded answer over a competing expression in 30,048 training pairs. The preference fit increased the number of pairs in which both supplied expressions were correctly preferred to 585 of 3,126, versus 336 for the reference-only fit. Yet it freely generated only 132 correct answers on those 8,790 passages, versus 207 with references alone. The two supplied candidates omit thousands of other possible outputs. Improving the comparison between them can leave a third, incorrect expression with the largest probability. The experiment measures this divergence without identifying which unenumerated outputs have globally maximal probability. ## Independent release confirmation

The reference-only fit was a control. Its checkpoint was frozen and its release conditions specified before opening another 2,605 source groups. It produced 433 of 17,555 exact expressions (2.47%), versus 276 (1.57%) for the preceding guided writer. The paired difference was +0.89 percentage points (95% source-group interval +0.68 to +1.10). There were 255 gains and 98 losses. The new writer recovered 309 of 12,981 cases in which all four expected glyphs were absent from the input and produced 133 distinct exact expressions. It solved both answers of 4/6,646 same-neighbor pairs and 0/21 unseen whole expressions. Every one of its 70,220 emitted glyphs exactly matched a training-known bitmap. Local counts recovered 789 answers (4.49%). [Independent confirmation design](joint_writer_confirmation_protocol.md) · [Confirmation source groups](evidence/expression_preference_20261118/confirmation_sources.json)

A subsequent recount of the same saved confirmation outputs measured how much of the answer distribution each writer used. The source supplied 3,250 distinct answers. The preceding writer emitted 443 distinct physical sequences and the joint writer emitted 373. Its ten most frequent outputs occupied 9,580 of 17,555 answers (54.6%), compared with 206 for the ten most frequent source answers (1.17%). It solved zero of 905 cases whose target occurred one to five times in training. The improved exact score therefore coexists with a narrower output repertoire. This recount follows the Figure 43 manuscript build. [Complete frequency counts](evidence/expression_output_concentration_20261118.json)

The historical source route retained the exact answer, length, stop and source decision for all 9,600 questions in each of the original, rephrased and relocated conditions. Its score remained 9,565 per condition. Both erasures yielded zero. With the plate erased, most wrong modern outputs changed. The optional historical reader correctly used the modern path on every new confirmation passage. Fresh CPU runs reproduced the two modern examples under both input modalities, plus the historical 言 and held-out 马 image streams. The new patch occupies 2,805,396 bytes and reuses the original physical renderer. [Model and CPU verification](evidence/expression_preference_20261118/release.json)

Both local-hint fits and their training preparation took 87.0 seconds in total. The subsequent joint comparison took 67.0 seconds on one RTX 4090 D with 308 MiB of peak allocated GPU memory. The joint reference fit within it took 26.7 seconds. Earlier aligned training took 57.2 seconds, reader-guided writer training took 36.6 seconds, and initial renderer preparation and all evaluations add further cost. These measurements concern the stated Chinese cloze task. They do not establish a 1,000-fold saving, general conversational competence or independent etymological explanation.
