# A short pictured fact can support sentence writing

A 116,547-parameter reader and an 865,605-parameter writer produced stopped answer images directly from pictured questions and pages. Training the writer on a question and a short human fact increased exact answers on held passages in both languages when a separate reader supplied the fact at inference. English sentence formation remained unreliable. Chinese gains on the examination set matched an extractive reader alone. The prespecified requirement for improvement on both natural question pairs and edited pages in both languages failed. ## Evidence from new pages

All three writers saw the same 101,364 human answer wordings in the same order across four passes, from identical weights. Training included English QA2D and restaurant descriptions, Chinese catalogue replies and reading questions. The reader saw a disjoint set of 39,057 extractive questions from English SQuAD, simplified Chinese CMRC and traditional Chinese DRCD. An independent comparison of passage families, complete pictured questions and ordered region images found zero overlap across the fitting partitions and the 192 new passage families. The evaluation asked two distinct questions about each passage. ## Intervening on what the writer reads

Under ordinary generation the reader found the exact English short fact in 22/192 questions. Ten yielded the complete human statement. The reader alone returned zero complete statements. Substituting the human fact raised the fact-only system to 41/192 English and 185/192 Chinese exact images. Exchanging evidence between sibling questions reduced autonomous exact matches from ten to one in English and from 29 to three in Chinese. The ordinary Chinese span itself matched an accepted answer on 29 questions and both answers on six pages. The English writer had ten normalized exact replies among 183 questions whose accepted wording was absent from the inputs. [Stage errors](evidence/fact_channel_v1/formulation.json) · [Swaps and withheld inputs](evidence/fact_channel_v1/crossed-evidence.json)

A checked Lean theorem gives an obstruction for a deterministic writer. Two changed pages with disjoint admissible answer sets cannot both be solved if the question and selected evidence are identical. The measured fact-only inputs had this collision on 38/64 English and 92/190 Chinese edits. Passing the wider region reduced collisions to 27 and 30 but did not improve paired correctness. An independent enumeration on three real pages verified the reader likelihood and all 31 gradients against 18,721 to 19,110 legal spans per page. This checks the computation that trains the reader. Empirical answers establish what those weights actually do. Creating the reader predictions used in training the region arm added 12.2 s and 1.89 kJ. The release entry point creates this cache before initializing the writer to preserve matched seeds. These costs cover fitted neural components. Data acquisition, rendering, evaluation and external pretraining of comparator models are distinct. The strongest pipeline has 982,152 learned parameters and uses 32 × 32 pre-aligned cells. It can copy an observed glyph outside the spelling bank. An independent reading pipeline for unsegmented scans and robust unseen typefaces remains an engineering and evaluation requirement. Six saved states on 64 development questions per language show the early improvement and subsequent plateau. On the first sixteen English and sixteen Chinese confirmation questions, local Q4 Qwen3-VL-8B saw the same question and page content with an additional answer-format instruction. ## Where to intervene

The frozen reader's first choice matched 22 English and 29 Chinese facts. A marked-region restriction alone raised these counts to 44 and 37. A known correct start gave 128 and 92. At rank 32 an accepted fact was available on 122 and 119 questions. Every one of the 384 original selections reproduced on CPU. Candidate availability alone cannot establish that a correct sentence will be written. Enumerate the frozen writer's actual reachable answer set before committing to a longer selector fit. [Localization](evidence/fact_channel_v1/span-localization.json) · [Capacity experiment](fact_channel_capacity_protocol.md)

We then passed all 12,288 ranked positions through the frozen fact-only pipeline, reusing identical physical evidence within a question. All 384 first-ranked outputs reproduced the original fact coordinates, answer pixels and stop decisions. For a fixed writer and candidate generator the number of failures decomposes exactly into cases with no accepted output anywhere in the list and cases with an accepted output that the selector missed. The Lean theorem checks the identity for arbitrary finite lists and selectors returning a listed choice. In English, a selector with access to the reference could obtain at most 35 exact statements. The observed ten leave 25 selection errors and 157 questions outside the writer's attainable set. In Chinese the corresponding counts are 115 attainable, 29 observed, 86 selection errors and 77 outside. Requiring the same candidate to supply an accepted fact and produce an accepted answer gives ceilings of 32 and 115. Only three English and 32 Chinese passage pairs could be solved in full by any choices. This satisfies the registered criterion for testing a learned selector in each language. English also requires better formulation to exceed 35 exact answers on these pages with this candidate set.
