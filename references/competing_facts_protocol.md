# Choosing between plausible facts printed on the same page

The released statement pipeline retrieved a recorded fact on 25 of 83 fresh pages containing the answer. Compare three continuations from the released interval readers. The first optical arm learns the complete visible answer's marginal probability across every matching interval. A second optical arm adds a logistic preference for that answer over a different human answer to another question on the same page. A third arm applies the same contrast to the prior byte reader. All receive identical question pairs, source windows, image targets, sampling order, update count and learning rate. Freeze the ink embedding, free glyph generator, gate parameters, book model and English statement writer. The updated visual reader still free-writes without answer candidates at inference.
