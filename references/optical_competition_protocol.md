# Can comparing the alternatives on a page improve a freely written answer?

The signed optical reader previously missed an available accepted strip on 88 English and 63 Chinese MLQA questions out of 192 each. Freeze that reader, its original ranker and the image writer. Every proposed answer has three query-conditioned visual states (start, end and mean interior). Give a learned query for each proposed span the same attention projections, values and number of trainable parameters in two arms. The control attends within the span to its own three states. The experimental arm can also attend to every valid rival's states. Neither scorer receives character identifiers, answer references or a pretrained language model at inference. Compare against the unchanged signed reader as well as the matched control.

Use the finite marginal likelihood of all annotated locations in the actual 32 candidates. Unsupported questions cannot provide a gradient to a frozen candidate list; record them by domain. Train for eight identical shuffled passes on supported lists from all 39,057 original training questions, AdamW at 0.001, clip norm one and a zero output projection. The limit per arm is three GPU-hours and 18 GiB. Deduplicate supervision by location; identical ink at multiple annotated locations remains admissible. Report marginal training cost and the cost of constructing the candidate bank. Record the full head, underlying weights and output images. A reference-dependent score can reverse a pairwise preference; an independent score cannot. Verify permutation equivariance and suppress rival attention at inference without removing any final candidate. Include a same-page question exchange.

Reserve up to 96 previously unused paired pages for each of English human statements, Chinese reading questions, English/Chinese MLQA and English DROP, excluding prior reservations, complete supervised questions, pages and writer targets. Keep source-absent answers in DROP. Compare actual evidence, complete stopped answers, both answers on a page and replies containing the fact. Check general-language transfer on prior long answers, books and period-selection tasks. Judge a fixed subset with blinded two-order semantic assessment and human/sibling calibration. Preserve the baseline unless complete bilingual free answers improve against both controls on new pages, with semantic and transfer evidence. Report intervals over paired documents. A training improvement alone does not qualify the model. Historical and book language are part of the independent model's goal.

Before any new fit, the source reservation yielded only one unused English QA2D sibling pair. Retain that pair as exploratory and extend the stated English human-answer test to educator-written FairytaleQA. Use the official held test and validation stories, one pair of distinct local questions per story with the same complete section and all provided answer phrasings. Use the unchanged image budget. Include literal and inferential answers. Log actual denominators and judge these story replies as a separate cohort. [Data source](https://github.com/uci-soe/FairytaleQAData)
