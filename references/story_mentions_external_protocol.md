# Transfer to independent marked-pronoun questions

Evaluate the frozen bilingual models on all eligible official GAP test rows and CLUEWSC2020 development rows. Neither corpus enters either fit. Require the marked expression and all source-provided candidate intervals to fit in the same 288-cell crop, to appear at their recorded offsets and to end before the pronoun. Use up to 32 right-context cells, sacrificing right context if needed to keep both earlier candidates. Exclude candidates longer than 24 cells, duplicate GAP spellings, invalid offsets and multi-label GAP rows. Record every exclusion. The GAP task supplies two candidate names, and the known options for the cast condition are their pictured spellings. Score exact returned name or neither. For the single-candidate Chinese verification, score a positive exactly when the predicted strip equals the specified candidate. Report positive recall, negative recall and the positive/negative denominators, alongside constant and recency baselines. A different emitted name can establish a negative under this binary metric without identifying the true referent. The free condition never receives candidate names. These screens differ from the full official benchmarks. Preserve both the original and masked visual results, bytes and geometry controls.
