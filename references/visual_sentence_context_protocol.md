# Learning to write the words around a pictured fact

On the fourth held cohort, 94 of 113 incorrect English statements containing one fact in the human reference cannot be repaired by replacing a single interval with the correct fact. An experiment on writing therefore freezes the released complete-span reader. Keep the old reader and writer as an external baseline. Fit two writers from the same released human-fact writer. Both receive the pictured question, a fact from the frozen reader and the original evidence. Both see full human answers even when they rephrase the evidence. The expanded, training-derived physical inventory is common to both. No reference answer or supplied fact enters autonomous inference.

**Hypothesis.** The value itself can dominate the recurrent state. Teaching the words that occur before and after a value, with its interior removed from the teacher's preceding output, will improve autonomous statements. For a uniquely occurring fact with at least one surrounding glyph, form an auxiliary stream with the fact collapsed to one neutral feedback cell. Score the surrounding glyphs and stop. Keep the original question and evidence. The matched control scores the same number of uniformly spread original targets, including stop, under ordinary history. All examples retain ordinary whole-answer likelihood. Match initialization, update order, position counts, learning rate, decoding and data. Test the accounting and equal loss on matched histories against the original implementation. The Lean statement concerns supervision accounting and the equivalence of the prefix before the fact. It does not predict that either model will generalize.

**Corpora.** Reserve up to 96 unused two-question QAMR/QA2D and CMRC passages per language in deterministic order. Block all families, question images, passage images and writer references from the released training splits, original development and four prior paired cohorts. Reject any fresh example overlapping a previous held question, passage or target. For human long response controls reserve up to 64 DuReader and 64 Dolly questions whose reference is absent from the pictured question and excerpt per language, targeting equal strata below and above 255 cells. Keep whole reference answers, including over-budget responses. Dolly controls come from the official project-local sealed split and the held partition of the prior inventory study. Keep all available eligible paired families when fewer than 96 exist. Record the administered count. Exclude new checks from added training by physical question, passage and answer, and by data source family where available. Retain the original regression set for development. In addition to the previous writer data, admit up to 8,192 complete DuReader and Dolly training pairs per corpus and 4,096 training-side Alpaca examples per language with an answer shorter than 1,024 cells. Explicitly count coverage and exclusions. Human and teacher-provided supervision are separate sources.

**Comparison.** Two epochs per arm, AdamW, fixed initialization from the released writer, a 1,024-decision cap including stop and greedy image emission. At most 18 GiB allocated GPU memory and six device-hours per fit. Reproduce both assessment settings with and without supplying human facts for paired short questions. Report complete bitmap equality, causally edited pairs, both questions on the same passage, and answers absent from the source. Score long human replies separately by length, including stopping and normalized edit similarity. Comparisons with the preceding report use the same old model on the new questions. A language improvement requires gains against both controls in autonomous English and Chinese complete answers, preservation on edited pairs, and independent composition. Inspect true answer images ordered by the fixed test manifest; check book and historical transfer independently. Keep the original deployment if the proposed intervention fails.
