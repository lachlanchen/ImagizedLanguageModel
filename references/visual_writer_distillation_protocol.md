# Can a pictured-expression reader teach an independent writer?

The archived reader selected the reference on 930 of 22,907 development gaps when supplied pictures of 3,805 training expressions. Its autonomous writer recovered 79 of those 930 and 55 additional answers. Test whether the reader's training distribution improves independent writing.

Freeze the published reader, landmarks and renderer. Both arms start from the identical final aligned checkpoint. Use the same optimizer, minibatches, 24 additional epochs, 71,439 training contexts, and one four-glyph-and-stop target per context per epoch. Use AdamW with learning rate 0.0006, batch size 192, gradient clipping at one and seed 613. In the control, every target is the original expression. In the treatment, independently replace it with probability 0.75 by a complete expression drawn from the frozen reader's distribution. Compute that distribution on training contexts only over all distinct training expression pictures, using cosine similarity at temperature 0.02. Sample the whole expression before using its successive physical pixel groups as targets. This retains order and within-expression dependence. Report the teacher's training accuracy, concentration, realized target changes and cost. Cache the frozen context states identically for both arms. Verify equivalence to the published teacher-forcing path and verify frozen tensor equality after optimization.

Evaluate final checkpoints only on the original official validation set, with the unchanged beam of four, eight-cell limit, physical feedback and learned stop. Record exact whole images, exchanged-context scores, transfer among the 930 reader successes and the 851 previously lost, newly acquired and lost cases, diversity, all-four-absent cases, unseen whole expressions and the existing 8,900 matched pairs. Move the treatment to the runnable model collection only if the lower 95% source-group bootstrap bounds versus both continuation control and original writer exceed zero, reader-correct cases increase above both controls, and at least 99% of emitted cells exactly match a training-known glyph. Preserve all original models regardless. If only ordinary continued supervision improves, keep that finding separate from the distillation claim. The target local baseline remains 1,012. Development has been used for earlier decisions. A fresh source-group holdout will be needed for a stronger generalization claim. Use one 4090 D, 8 GiB allocated device memory, 6 GiB peak host RSS and a 35-minute execution ceiling.
