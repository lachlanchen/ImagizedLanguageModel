# Does an exact pictured answer transfer when its meaning changes?

One of four exact nonidentity outputs on the 1,024 held-out passages translated 子曰： 不可。 as 孔子说： 不可以。 Check compositional transfer at the same modern writing task. Cross the ordered classical subjects 子, 王, 帝 and 臣 with 可 and 不可, keeping punctuation, space and artwork conventions unchanged. Include the observed success. For each of eight image inputs run the actual marginal writer and phrase composer. Check exact physical training source and complete target overlap. Save both complete outputs. Mask methods, retain ties, and grade whether (i) speaker identity survives, (ii) permission or its negation survives, and (iii) the entire modern utterance reads naturally. Count pairs where both positive and negative answers satisfy all three criteria. These are targeted development prompts chosen after inspecting one success. They are not a new population estimate. If either axis fails, use the substitutions to locate the next learning problem; exclude these eight prompts from evaluation of any future changes.
