# Can a teacher transmit a change of meaning between two pictured sentences?

The independent writer often favoured its own unfaithful sentence after correctly conditioning on the source. A source-independent preference for a frequent target can produce the same ordering on two different sources. Use two attested translations from the training pool whose English sentences differ by one word. For source rows A,B and targets a,b, compare both conditional margins, S(A,a) − S(A,b) and S(B,b) − S(B,a), where S is mean log probability per predicted target unit, including the end decision. Their sum removes any additive source-only and target-only bias. A positive sum alone can coexist with a wrong answer on one row. Require both individual margins to clear the threshold in both translation directions.

Mine the 147,456 natural training rows from the completed breadth experiment. Preserve the original index, printed spelling and target. Take English frames with at least four lexical words, retain punctuation and contraction structure, substitute one alphabetic word, require distinct Chinese content, and use each sentence surface and masked English frame at most once. Choose deterministically, sampling up to 4,096 disjoint contrasts. Reserve the first 128 shuffled qualifying families for independent inspection and exclude them from subsequent learning. Record the population and the edit vocabulary. The qualification measures coverage in this mining scheme. It does not measure general book performance.

Run the two pinned native OPUS-MT models on each of the four source–target combinations in each direction. Require all four within-source margins to exceed 0.10 nats per target unit. Recompute a seeded 64-family subset with both source and target order reversed to test positional and batching effects. Accept a maximum score drift of 0.001 and identical threshold decisions on the replay. Measure the same comparisons with the frozen independent broad writer reading the actual visual sources and using its own source-derived drafts. Include the stopped reference and counterfactual lengths. Report exact trained-ink replay for source encoding, the both-source rates and autonomous exact writing on the 128-family audit.

Independently assess the reserved 128 families with local quantized Qwen in both translation directions. Present one source at a time, both candidate orders, and allow both or neither answer to preserve the meaning. A family passes only if all eight calls accept the aligned translation alone. Preserve all decisions. For exploratory training with the screened OPUS labels require at least 512 further families, at least 100 distinct word-substitution pairs, and at least 112 of 128 complete confirmations. If any requirement fails, withhold bulk targets and diagnose the actual rejections before fitting. The independent inspection is automated. Passing would support a matched training comparison, not validate free writing.

Use one GPU owner, at most 18 GiB of allocated PyTorch memory, 16 GiB for the local judge and two hours per stage. Keep the book evaluation images and final reference grades outside teacher selection.
