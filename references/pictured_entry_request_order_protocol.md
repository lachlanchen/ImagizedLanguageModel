# Which part of a pictured question is being asked?

Four requests use the same pictured headword, the same two fields and the same two historical stages. In one request the reader already knows the meaning and asks for the pronunciation. In another, those roles are reversed. Independently exchanging the roles of the two stages gives four distinct complete answer images. The lengths and the multiset of all question glyphs are identical within each group of four. Both orders of the fields, both orders of the stages and both orders of the clauses occur in the corpus. All source records, splits, historical images and target sentences come from the preceding pictured-entry study.

The training corpus contains 35,680 questions in 8,920 groups. Development has 4,544 questions in 1,136 groups from 284 families absent from training. Each development request also has a version with reserved expressions and reversed clause order. The reserved wording evaluates lexical and structural transfer together. These are authored Chinese questions in one font. [Preparation and file hashes](evidence/pictured_entry_request_order_preparation_20261119.json)

## An exact bound before fitting

Let the four targets be equally frequent. Any representation invariant under permutation of the question glyphs supplies identical inputs on each group, including the same picture of the card. A deterministic writer can produce at most one of the four distinct answers, hence at most 25% complete accuracy and zero complete groups. Sampling independently of the intended answer preserves the 25% bound in expectation. A successful reader must preserve two bits per group to distinguish all four choices exactly. The same restriction applies to summing the ink layers into a single unweighted image. A lossless spatial tiling with a fixed reading order preserves the sequence. This is an elementary identifiability condition and does not imply that training will recover those two bits. Permutation-invariant representations have a formal treatment in Deep Sets. [Zaheer et al., 2017](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/f22e4747da1aa27e363d86d40ff442fe-Abstract.html)

The preceding model measures some positions. Direct comparison found **1,135 of 1,136** development groups identical even under its original features. Its deterministic upper bound is therefore 1,139/4,544 exact answers and one complete group, allowing all four answers in the remaining group. This bound is independent of learning rate, training duration and decoder size. Masked visual language modelling and autoregressive rendered-text generation already have published precedents. The specific question here is whether drawing the next answer from document evidence depends on the grammatical role of the pictured text. [Rust et al., 2023](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=FkSp8VW8RjH) · [PIXAR, 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.874/)

## Matched order comparison

Initialize two writers from the same pictured-entry checkpoint. Add an encoder that reads the full question through ordered glyph thumbnails. Condition the next writing action on the question as a whole and on the neighborhood of a matched field or headword. The control receives the same question glyphs in canonical bitmap order through this new encoder. Retain the exact same original label and position features in both arms. Use the same eight training passes, original simple-question replay, target images, optimizer and batches. Keep the final checkpoints and score independent generation through model stop. Each arm is limited to one GPU, 8 GiB allocated and 20 minutes of fitting. Report preprocessing separately.

Score complete raster-and-stop matches across all 4,544 requests, the count of groups in which all four changes are followed, the reserved wording, and retention across all 1,136 original questions. Require at least 80% exact and 65% complete groups on known frames in unseen families, at least a 50-percentage-point exact gain over the control, and at least 95% exact on the original task. Apply the same two absolute criteria separately to the reserved wording before claiming transfer to those expressions. If only the familiar frames pass, record learned role assignment within that grammar and test new language separately. Open-book answers and original measured translation fidelity retain their own evaluations.
