# Reading two human questions about the same pictured page

A 2,442,424-parameter optical writer answered 195 of 512 human questions on freshly reserved English, simplified Chinese and traditional Chinese passages. It wrote both answers on 44/256 passages. The previous optical writer, evaluated again on these same passages, answered both on zero. On a held article about a damselfish the new model wrote **厚壳仔** when asked its common name and **辐鳍鱼纲鲈形目隆头鱼亚目雀鲷科** when asked its classification. All sixteen archived correct and incorrect responses replay from PNG inputs with identical emitted pixels and stops on CPU. ## What the probability model learned

The writer scores whole source intervals up to 48 glyphs. The alternative path assigns probability to each successive glyph and to stopping. Exact summation over all intervals that emit the same pixels avoids having to label one occurrence as correct. The evidence loss asks an internal distribution to attend to the human-marked range. Greedy writing carries the posterior mass of every path still compatible with the glyphs already emitted. If a complete target outweighs all competitors combined, it wins every prefix vote through stopping. A Lean proof establishes the finite integer-weight statement. On 510 primary references scored post-fit, the evidence reader assigned probability above one half to 104 and wrote all 104 exactly. This calculation certifies a decoder property given weights; it does not prove that training will learn a language. Supplying two reference answers for diagnostic scoring, the model preferred the appropriate answer under each of the two pictured questions on 144/255 passages, including 105/128 Chinese passages. The autonomous score of 44/256 quantifies the additional difficulty of writing without answer options. One SQuAD pair could not be scored by the probability audit and remains included in the main endpoint.

## Where the model fails

In English dialogue, using all four official human references gave 33/128 individual and 4/64 paired successes. On the 32 questions for which all four normalized reference wordings were absent from the visible passage, both credited replies were **no**, including one question for which humans supplied both yes and no. Always answering **yes** scores 12/32 and always answering **no** scores 3/32 on this slice. These two matches supply no evidence of composing a new explanation. The writer gave both opposite correct answers on 0/120 external Winograd contrasts. In 152 free bilingual book continuations none began with the first eight reference glyphs. Other continuations can be valid, but the stored English and Chinese examples show persistent repetition. These endpoints remain separate from extractive question answering. In an exploratory transfer test after fitting, 64 local Hanziyuan development headwords each appeared on a newly typeset page containing two attested forms. Four additional questions about **言**, using oracle-bone J04919 and bronze B02976, also failed. Each physical output and target for 言 is archived separately. These questions distinguish returning attested forms from reproducing unfamiliar digit shapes. ## Data, compute and decision

Preparation retained 219,711 QA rows and 35,019 fitting passages. After grouping duplicate training answers, 34,551 passages had two different training answers. Eight in ten batches used human questions, the remainder replayed ordinary visual writing. Each arm consumed 2,295,177 target positions in 115,200 examples with identical fitting draws. The auxiliary locations were visible on 75,778 examples. The new head trained on one RTX 4090 D for 89.94 seconds, using 11.60 kJ and 269 MB of peak allocated device memory. Including its frozen writer's two fitting stages gives 272.82 seconds and 37.51 kJ. Corresponding byte stages took 280.48 seconds and 38.01 kJ. These device measurements exclude corpus preparation, controls, evaluation, host energy and other development work. The byte system has higher paired accuracy. The comparison supplies no evidence for a tenfold or thousandfold advantage. The next decision concerns composition. Test whether the reader can combine evidence from more than one location and express a human answer whose wording is absent from the passage. Retain the modern paired questions, unseen-ink interventions, historical forms and open prompts as regression measures. Score the complete written response, mark correctly grounded facts separately from wording, and compare matched bytes and a reference-copying baseline on fresh source families.
