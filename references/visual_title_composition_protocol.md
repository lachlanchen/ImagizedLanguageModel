# A pictured language target outside a selected passage span

The grounded writer answers by selecting a contiguous span. An unrestricted visual language model must also compose images that never occur together in its input. Prepare article title prediction from the official CMRC passage files as a separate generative challenge. Predict the source's recorded title after reading only a pictured paragraph and the fixed pictured instruction “请写出这篇文章的标题。” Keep the title out of the prompt. Titles can have several reasonable alternatives; exact title agreement is one measure of fidelity to the recorded title.

Use all original Chinese training and development passages and full titles that can be rendered in the trained Noto Sans font. Reject empty or unsupported pairs, repeated physical passage/title pairs, and development titles or passage pixels already present in training. Keep the original strings for source auditing outside model input. Hash all source files; record exclusion counts, original title groups, packed-pixel offsets and complete lengths. Classify each reference by the capability needed to compose it. A contiguous reference can be selected as one span. A distributed reference requires reordering or joining visible glyphs. A reference containing a glyph absent from the passage requires some independent writing. Compute these classes from physical bitmap equality; also count strict absence from the full pictured prompt, including the question. Report how often a target bitmap was unseen across all training inputs or titles. Group evaluation by original title. Preserve every hard case. Never train on the development partition or use the reserved DRCD pool. This stage measures dataset coverage and limits of extractive baselines; it does not measure a generative model.
