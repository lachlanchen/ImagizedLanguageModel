# The writer learned its corrections and missed the same distinction in a new book

In a passage from *Mencius*, King Wen's terrace is called 灵台 and his pond 灵沼. Asked in Chinese for the terrace's name, the preference-trained writer produced 灵台 and stopped. The equivalent English question elicited 民力, the labour used to build it. Asked in Chinese for the pond's name, it supplied the terrace again. These are legible source glyphs. The failure is selecting the fact requested by the pictured question. The two terrace replies reproduced pixel for pixel through the offline CPU interface. The two fits started from the same released checkpoint and replayed 49,283 examples for 2,496 updates each. The preference writer gained five and lost six individual exact replies relative to the positive-only control. Its one-answer deficit corresponds to −0.69 percentage points, with a paired passage-bootstrap 95% interval of −4.86 to +3.47 points within the six sampled works. The selection criteria retained the released model. This estimate concerns one training seed. Prior exposure of the text-pretrained bodies to these books is unknown. We mined 22,902 training questions. The released writer made 15,118 stopped mistakes. Among 10,701 eligible source-grounded alternatives, we sampled 768 across two languages and two rival types. Local Qwen3-8B Q8 accepted 646 uniquely in both presentation orders. Reading 48 examples against their passages exposed four uncertain labels, leaving 642 training pairs. The median complete-answer log-probability advantage of the annotation rose from −1.69 nats to +2.42 under positive-only replay and +5.21 under two-sided training. The wrong replies were supplied only during fitting and the diagnostic score calculation. At test time the causal writer emitted its own glyphs and stop. ## Where the pictured question influences the answer

Given the two annotated answer images, the preference writer scored their correct assignment to the questions higher than the exchanged assignment on 34/36 Chinese and 23/36 English passages. It individually preferred the correct supplied answer on both questions on 25/36 and 6/36 passages. Free generation permits many additional replies. On the four earlier works, subtracting the blank-question preference reversed 21/94 previously observed score losses. The requirement for a decoder trial was 47 reversals. All 30 originally exact replies retained the correct preference over the other question's answer. [Paired question scores](evidence/stopped_book_preference_20261124/question_interaction.json) · [Blank-question diagnostic](stopped_book_question_calibration.md) · [Results](evidence/stopped_book_preference_20261124/question_calibration.json)

A previously trained bidirectional visual reader examined the same six works and copied its predicted source interval. It answered 45/72 Chinese and 1/72 English requests correctly, with 2/72 correct when the question was erased. Local text Qwen3-8B Q8 gave 69/72 Chinese and 68/72 English exact responses and completed 32/36 passages. Warm serial image writing took 6.22 s for 144 questions and Qwen took 28.91 s at substantially higher accuracy. The 87.1M-parameter visual writer updated 28.6M parameters in 179 s on one RTX 4090 D, using 4.59 GiB peak tensor allocation. Mining cost another 330 s and teacher adjudication used 297 s of reported service time. The inherited text-model and visual pretraining costs are additional. Matched-quality speed, broad independent writing and a thousandfold efficiency gain remain unproved.
