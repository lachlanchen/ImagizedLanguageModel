# Reading actions and writing a reply in glyph images

The page reads 三藏上馬，行者引路. Two pictured questions ask who led the way and who mounted the horse, in that order. The model wrote **行者引路，而三藏上馬。** When the actions were exchanged on the page and the names stayed in their original cells, its answer changed with them. This was the first reserved excerpt of *Journey to the West*. ## Can the reader follow an action?

In two other novels, we froze the passages, questions and equal-width action exchanges before inference. The participants' name pixels stayed fixed when the actions were exchanged. For the original clause order, an excerpt earned credit only when the reader answered both questions under both assignments. The adapted reader completed 11/16 excerpts from *Three Kingdoms* and 9/16 from *Water Margin* in Chinese. Its frozen parent completed 11 and 9. The control trained with equal evidence for both names completed 2 and 5. Paired differences against that control were +56 percentage points (95% interval +31 to +81) and +25 (+6 to +50). The adapted reader completed 10 and 9 excerpts when also required to handle the reversed clause order. It completed none in English. One of 512 CPU replays produced different pixels from the GPU. Both versions of that English reply were wrong. ## Can the editor compose an answer?

The 220,035-parameter recurrent editor takes a pictured instruction, page ink and two predicted source locations. At each step it can copy a contiguous page span, generate a 32 × 32 bitmap or stop. We drew 115,200 two- to four-clause training pages from 7,412 annotated records (7,066 distinct strings). The records comprise 2,984 Kyoto classical Chinese examples and 4,428 BiPaR novel examples. Two additional novels supplied 12 reserved excerpts each. Every excerpt had two versions and two question orders. Exact credit required the correct source words, new connective, punctuation, order and voluntary ending. The sole experimental change in the matched control was randomizing evidence order on half the training presentations. ## Can it write historical forms?

On the previously studied 言 plate, the original optical reader located the oracle and bronze forms and the editor wrote both in either requested order. The adapted reader missed the bronze form. Two of four additional Chinese wording variants elicited both orders exactly with the original reader. We then reserved all 60 eligible historical development families using two entries per page, both page orders and both question orders. Their entries, families, catalogue labels and images were absent from the historical training plates. Here the instruction and layout stayed fixed. ## What the mathematics establishes

For a question-by-action score table, the difference of differences cancels additive biases from question identity and action assignment. Four correctly signed margins constrain this interaction. A single strong score can give a positive interaction despite three wrong choices, which motivates scoring all four actual answers. A second Lean theorem shows that copying and rearranging spans cannot produce a reply containing a bitmap absent everywhere on the page. Every successful book reply contains such a connector. This witnesses new ink and leaves open the harder question of generating new *content* words. A third theorem identifies a limitation of the editor: any exact, commutative pooling of instruction cells gives the same state under permutation. With page and source regions fixed, such a decoder cannot produce two different prescribed replies solely from reordered instruction glyphs. The editor averages per-cell instruction features. Floating-point accumulation can introduce rounding differences. It has no learned representation of instruction order in that branch. The evaluated question order is supplied separately as ordered source regions. ## Cost and next experiment

Fitting the aligned editor from random initialization took 2,400 AdamW steps, 37.5 s, 3.54 kJ of measured device energy and 43.46 MB peak PyTorch allocation on one RTX 4090 D. The full inference system uses 86.22 million parameters and inherits a pretrained contextual reader. Its pretraining cost and a matched-quality, end-to-end efficiency ratio are unknown. The release reproduced all 384 autonomous GPU book replies on CPU, including the wrong answers. [Fit receipt](evidence/grounded_ink_editor_20261124/aligned_fit.json) · [CPU replay](evidence/grounded_ink_editor_20261124/release_cpu.json)

The next writing test should require a content word absent from the page. Reserve paired passages with different meanings but the same answer format, and accept independently specified synonymous readings. Compare the frozen editor with a student trained on aligned and shuffled passage–gloss pairs. Score the new content word, clause meaning, historical-image identity and stopping separately. The prepared Hanziyuan entry metadata contains glosses and family identities. It can support this test without another harvest. The bronze-location result supplies a separate reading baseline for the same source plates.
