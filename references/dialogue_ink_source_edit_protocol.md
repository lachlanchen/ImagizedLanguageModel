# Changing a date while keeping the question fixed

As a secondary source-grounding test, retain every held dialogue whose first reference is exactly a four-digit year from 1000 through 2099, occurs exactly once as a whole word in the passage and occurs nowhere in the question or supplied history. Replace the passage year with the following year, skipping cases where that replacement already occurs in the inputs. Leave the question, history, surrounding letters and image geometry unchanged. On both final writers count exact, stopped original answers, exact changed answers and pairs with both correct. Include originally incorrect cases. This counterfactual measures reading a supplied date. It makes no claim about the historical truth of the modified passage or the ability to compose a new sentence.
