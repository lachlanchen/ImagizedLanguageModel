# Reading a lexical question from ink

The released contextual gloss writer requires a marked position. Here a pictured question requests the English gloss of the main verb or its direct object. Neither question supplies the target character. Success on a passage requires two independently stopped English image streams, each with the requested meaning and its correct source position.

Use Kyoto UD 2.16 root verbs with one direct `obj` dependent, both single visible Han cells with distinct English readings, in 3–32-cell clauses. Fit on the same six works as the earlier writer. Reserve the available dev/test excerpts of the *Analects* and *Chu ci* and the two Buddhist works absent from the actual pinned training file. Remove duplicate printed clauses, visually conflicting targets and any held clause with a target window already used to fit the writer. Report all exclusions. The independent *Dao De Jing* set has eighteen selected clauses or clause excerpts. The registered TSV gives target words and acceptable glosses based on the received text and Legge's translation. This is a small, author-scored external set. Apply the same overlap check and report any exclusions. [Treebank](https://universaldependencies.org/treebanks/lzh_kyoto/index.html) · [Chinese and Legge's 1891 translation](https://www.daodej.wiki/english) · [External readings](evidence/asked_gloss_v1/daodejing.tsv)

Freeze the 1,180,977-parameter visual writer. At each page position obtain its contextual image encoding. Train a bidirectional page reader and a pictured question encoder to assign probability to each location. Compare three identically initialized readers for twelve complete passes, batch size 128 and AdamW at 0.001. The primary reader learns solely from the marginal probability of the observed answer image, summing over possible locations; the supervised comparator sees locations. A control exchanges the two image answers on half of training pages, independently per epoch. At inference each system must select one position and draw its answer without a candidate list. Limit each fit to 15 minutes and 8 GiB allocated GPU memory. One GPU owner runs the comparisons serially.

Measure per-role location, exact stopped output, and both requested location–answer pairs on the same page. Report seen and withheld question wordings, fixed-question, supplied-focus, quoted-target bitmap matching and training-count controls. Inspect cases in which identical local ink occupies different grammatical roles in different clauses. Preserve correct and failed answers. Evidence for learning from answer images requires a paired-page advantage over exchanged answers on held works. Success restricted to adjacent words, seen prompt forms, a role or a single external work limits the interpretation. A gap to supplied focus localizes the next change to question-conditioned syntax; a gap at supplied focus localizes it to the gloss writer.
