# Reading an explicit relation from the pictured question

In a post-publication CPU example about the song 秋潮, the 26,890-example reader selected 沈河 correctly but called the composer a producer. This example triggered this intervention and cannot serve as independent confirmation. Keep the two learned page spans and both writers frozen. Fit a compact regularized relation classifier on local sequences of exact pictured question glyphs. Use the existing train split plus queries naming each of the twelve training relations explicitly and symmetrically. Mask the given subject's pixels in each question while learning, and the predicted subject's pixels during inference. At a fixed 0.8 probability, let the query classifier replace the contextual relation. Compare with the original reader and a label-permuted classifier on all 1,280 held examples and the eight programme exchanges. Accept as an optional update if the diagnostic is fixed and the complete counts on original human questions, programme notes and the larger DuIE set are at least as good as the original pipeline. Record failure or regression in any cohort. Test reversal of composer and lyricist on a separately specified song, with both names and facts on the page. Keep explicit-label and unfamiliar-phrasing cases visible.
