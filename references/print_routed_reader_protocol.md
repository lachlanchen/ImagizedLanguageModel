# Choosing a reader from the ink in the request

The stroke/scale reader improved the two trial families but lost one of 14 complete ordinary-print passages. Preserve its failed selection result. In a separate frozen comparison, choose the original correspondence whenever at least four fifths of nonblank glyph occurrences in each nonempty input strip match the fixed inventory exactly. Use the augmented correspondence otherwise. An all-white strip contributes no evidence for an unfamiliar font. Hold the threshold fixed and keep a single language writer and image inventory. A new request can be processed independently of other requests in its batch. Retain a runnable candidate only after actual output verification on the two original test families, regular print, both historical orders on at least 99 cards and the Chinese benchmark below.

Evaluate professionally translated XQuAD Chinese passages with their Chinese and English questions. Use one pair per passage in source order, require disjoint accepted Chinese answers visible on the page, a shared fixed prefix of at most 416 source glyphs, 1–48 answer cells and inputs within the writer's 1,024 positions in either language. Check overlap with the screened CMRC sources and the DRCD reservation. Report coverage by language and font, all exact answers, stopped paired accuracy, and overlap with local SQuAD development by original identifier. These are translated SQuAD documents and provenance of upstream pretraining is unknown. Do not use these questions or answers for fitting or threshold choice. Compare the original and routed writers on the same pixels. Require more complete HanaMin pairs for Chinese questions than original and no loss of regular-print Chinese pairs. English transfer is a separate reported outcome. This study follows inspection of the CMRC retention error. [Original XQuAD corpus and authors](https://github.com/google-deepmind/xquad)

For a same-device warm-serving comparison, use the first 64 XQuAD source-order pairs supported in HanaMin in both languages. Supply the same Chinese passage to the image writer in pixels and to local Qwen3-8B in text. Score Chinese answers for either language of question. Report serial latency, complete pairs, model bytes, GPU placement and difference from batched image generation. This comparison uses the first covered cases and makes no population-level efficiency claim.
