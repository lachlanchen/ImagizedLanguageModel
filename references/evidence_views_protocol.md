# Writing an answer with privileged evidence during training

Start both writers from the same pre-examination recurrent bilingual reader and frozen optical prior. Use two presentations of every eligible CoQA turn and Chinese C³ question. Block earlier reserved CoQA training stories, exact repeats and any shared 72-character normalized window with CoQA development or DROP validation. Use the complete story up to 4,096 glyphs, the previous question and answer when available, and the current question. Keep the current question intact within a total query budget of 320 cells. Preserve original highlight offsets and compare their text with the supplied source. Require the complete stopped answer to be physically writable from either training view. Record exclusions. Retain both successful and failed replications.

For each English training turn give both writers one full story. For the second presentation supply the human-marked evidence to the experimental writer and the full story to the control. Interleave both presentations and Chinese full-page replay with the same seeded draw order, target and learning rate. Only the visible English passage changes. Fit one model at a time on GPU0. Use AdamW, a peak rate of 0.0003, clip norm of one, batch size 16 and fixed final weights. Allow three hours and 16 GiB allocated per fit, with resumable optimizer checkpoints. Report actual training time and GPU energy, excluding inherited training and data preparation.

Primary evaluation uses whole stories with the first two distinct questions and distinct answers of each eligible official CoQA development story. Later turns receive the immediately preceding human exchange as dialogue context. Count both stopped, normalized any-reference answers and the subset for which all acceptable answers require multiword phrasing absent from the story and visible query. Report official multi-reference F1 and the earlier first-turn evidence intervention separately. Confirm transfer on the first 128 eligible new DROP validation passages in hash order with two different questions and two distinct unambiguous single answers. This subset tests whole-passage English reading and numerical reasoning. Keep answer choices, evidence, references and proposed phrases outside free inference. Compare with the pre-exam parent. Retain Chinese examination, changed-fact, bilingual QA, book continuation and crossed historical requests. Branch success requires more paired whole-story answers and more correct source-absent multiword replies than the equal-exposure control, with retained bilingual capabilities. Evaluate the final models once, with no outcome-dependent duration or checkpoint selection. The existing default remains in place if the comparison fails.

The finite-mass theorem bounds transfer between views at a target prefix using the measured drop in probability of the requested glyph. At tolerance 0.10, a cropped probability above 0.60 and a drop of at most 0.10 certify a full-page winner. Count certified positions on pictured questions by forced decoding and compare with actual greedy output along observed histories. Include stop.
