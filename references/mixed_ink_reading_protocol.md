# Choosing between a visible answer and a missing expression

The same pictured passage can support a request to quote visible words or to supply missing words. A whole-answer decision selects a span of source ink or a recurrent image writer. The source path can reproduce a shape absent from training. The generator must infer absent content. Pointer-generator models provide a precedent for learning a copy decision; here the copied objects and generated answers are physical bitmaps. [See, Liu & Manning (2017)](https://aclanthology.org/P17-1099/)

A sum projection of glyph layers cannot retain reading order. For any permutation of the layers, the sum is identical. Even a perfect decoder with that observation alone must give the same answer for two different orders. For two equiprobable orders requiring different answers its best exact accuracy is one half. Use ordered tiles. The analogous physical constraint in an inverse problem is identifiability. Increasing iteration count cannot recover evidence that the observation discarded.

Fit a new visual question encoder, whole-answer switch and conditional span boundaries. Hold the released idiom writer fixed. The source path considers the entire pictured passage. Train independent start and conditional end distributions from genuine answer lengths, retaining every legal end after a proposed start. Impose no eight-cell ceiling on the source path. Read the packed bitmaps directly. Storage handles can compare identical input bitmaps or gather observed pixel features, and have no learned entries.

Use all 7,235 source-disjoint training questions in the original CMRC split and 2,048 distinct original ChID training windows with eight nonblank central cells. Each window receives three equally long pictured instructions to fill its gap, quote the preceding four cells or quote the following four cells. The middle is fixed by the supplied gap layout. Use two phrasings per role during training and one further phrasing per role only at evaluation. Train twelve epochs with a fixed seed, AdamW and final weights. The new module receives no corpus or task identifier. The deployed gap generator reads the central 48 cells (zero padding for short inputs). Its own emitted ink and stop decision are retained.

Score all 1,788 natural questions from 425 previously held-out passages. Compare complete answers with the frozen extractive baseline, a blank question and a different question about the same passage when available. Report long answers separately. On the 17,686 previously held-out ChID windows score every gap, and score both directions on every window possessing eight nonblank central cells. Count the same-window trials with all three complete correct outputs. Score exact complete pixels and stopping at the predicted boundary. Evaluate both familiar and unseen instructions. These development groups have already appeared in earlier model comparisons.

To advance, require a positive lower endpoint of the paired passage-bootstrap 95% interval against the natural-QA baseline and exchanged questions, at least 99% retention of frozen generator streams, and at least 90% exact copying in each direction with both familiar and unseen phrasings. If a condition fails, retain all results and identify the largest measured loss before further training. If all pass, require independent source-group confirmation and historical-plate regression before deployment. Use one 4090-class GPU, two host threads, less than 8 GiB allocated GPU memory and 6 GiB host RSS with 24 GiB host reserve, 48 GiB disk reserve and 35 minutes per phase.
