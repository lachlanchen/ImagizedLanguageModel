# Training a visual translator on the information retained in a complete draft

The fresh four-work panel was reserved under the emitted-history protocol. An earlier prefix-recovery study had already tested its proposed edit-aligned suffix intervention. This amendment replaces the fit before any trajectories, optimization or held predictions. The panel, conditional comparison and whole-meaning decision remain fixed.

Select 4,096 distinct training rows without replacement with NumPy seed 61307. For each source and direction collect the frozen parent's greedy draft and one top-four draw at temperature 0.85 (torch seed 9128). Read the emitted 32-square cells. Precompute the writer's deterministic physical motor and its visual feedback once for each motor choice. Limit training drafts to 96 cells; only actual stops in both drafts and unequal images can receive preference weight. Given each draft, an offline reverse-direction OPUS-MT translator scores the original source by mean log probability per source token including stop. Both scores use exactly the same source target. Apply that same measurement to the human reference to check how often it exceeds both draft scores. Keep every row including ties and failed stops. The reverse likelihood is a training signal with no guarantee of semantic accuracy.

Both arms receive full bilingual human likelihood over the entire original library for four epochs. On the same 4,096 sampled rows in each direction both arms also score both drafts conditional on the original source. Compare length-normalized sequence log probabilities including end. If the teacher prefers A, minimize softplus of minus four times the student's A-minus-B score. Weight each eligible pair by its absolute teacher margin capped at one. Add 0.5 times this mean preference loss in the candidate and zero in the matched control. Share initialization, optimizer, order, schedule, positions and batch size. Training and scoring each have a 12 GiB allocated GPU ceiling and one hour. Only final checkpoints can be used for held evaluation. Inference receives source pictures, direction and cell count and generates its own ink. The reverse translator is absent.

On 192 untouched translations compare preference, matched likelihood, original parent, source-exchange and a local quantized Qwen3-8B text comparator. Reserve both orders of a blinded source-only judge, reference-informed votes, archive pixels and replay independently on CPU. Positive transfer requires a strictly positive lower paired work-bootstrap bound over matched likelihood, no declining direction and improvement over source exchange. If the comparison fails, measure whether higher reverse score actually identifies better complete meanings before scaling it.
