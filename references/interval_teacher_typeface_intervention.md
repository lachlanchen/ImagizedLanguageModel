# Which pictured input loses the answer after a change to Kai?

All four writers gave zero exact answers on the 96 complete CMRC passage pairs renderable in AR PL UKai. Use the final distilled checkpoint and exactly those 192 questions. Keep the writer weights, questions, references, decoding limit and font-coverage exclusions fixed. Render each pictured page and each pictured request independently in regular or Kai script. Compare the two crossed conditions with the archived all-regular and all-Kai streams on identical question IDs. Accept each answer cell in either typeface, and require a learned stop and a single complete accepted reference. Also report both-correct passages and how many originally correct answers each substitution recovers. Save every returned pixel. This is an intervention on the visual inputs of a fixed model. There is one GPU owner, at most five minutes of inference and no optimization. If correcting one field recovers over half of the all-regular successes while correcting the other recovers fewer than half, prioritize that field in the next training comparison. Otherwise test a shared representation that keeps the original bitmap available for copying.
