# What a bilingual visual memory changes in free writing

On four unseen English–Chinese books, a visual writer given 32 related training passages earned **2 of 96** complete-meaning credits. The same writer earned **1** with random passages and **1** without memory. Both credits came from a single book, both into Chinese. The difference over the parent was 1.04 percentage points, with a paired 95% interval of 0.00–3.13 points under resampling of four works. The related writer also lost two controlled polarity pairs and two complete role sets. The experiment failed the prespecified criteria for adoption. ## What the writer actually wrote

Given “More resources will be wasted by them.” both writers produced **更多的资源将被浪费。** The output conveys the waste of resources and omits the explicitly named agent. Given “It runs like this.” the parent wrote **这样的运作** and related memory produced **这样的。** Only the latter received credit in both grading orders. It is an elliptical response and the only new credit. In the opposite direction, a sentence saying that all consumers would benefit elicited repeated economic terms and lost the benefit claim. The supplied English reference for that example also contains extra material about oranges. The original inputs, held outputs and grading decisions remain available for direct inspection. ## From a pictured source to an answer image

The frozen 1.82-million-parameter editor reads aligned source glyphs and a phrase draft generated from the same source. A TF–IDF cosine search retrieves 32 source passages from 497,775 training pairs; it uses learned source strips and never queries the held translation. The memory expands the paired target strips into sequences of printed glyphs. At each decision it counts the continuations of the last three freely emitted glyphs among those sequences, treating stop as a possible continuation. Let P be the neural distribution, c(g) the count for glyph g, and n the sum of the counts. When n is positive the writer uses (1 − w)P(g) + w c(g)/n with w = αn/(2+n). At n = 0 it uses P. The output draws each selected glyph from the compiled learned print palette. New historical outlines fall outside this writer's vocabulary. [Source](../scripts/write_retrieved_glyphs.py) · [Formal comparison of candidate scores](../formal/span_list/RetrievedGlyphVote.lean)

We selected α separately by direction using mean glyph 1–4-gram F2 over freely generated development translations. Both directions selected 0.35 over 0, 0.70 and 1. Into English the score moved from 0.39428 to 0.39456, and into Chinese from 0.15259 to 0.15468. Both larger strengths lowered the score in each direction. The length-matched random control used the same calibrated strengths, number of passages and voting rule. A separate exchange control used a different pictured source and its corresponding draft. ## A conditional improvement and its limit

| Condition | Book meaning /96 | Controlled answers /472 | Both polarities /236 | All roles and polarities /118 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original editor | 1 | 461 | 225 | 107 |
| Related passages | 2 | 459 | 223 | 105 |
| Length-matched random passages | 1 | 461 | 225 | 107 |
| Exchanged source and related passages | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Twelve aligned passages from each of four reserved editions supplied 48 bilingual pairs and 96 directional requests. The related writer scored 2/24 in *Economics in One Lesson* and 0/24 in each of *Black Holes and Baby Universes*, *Gone with the Wind* and *The Sirens of Mars*. It changed 64/96 of the parent's book bitmaps. One of its 96 answers reproduced an entire retrieved target. All four visual conditions stopped on 568/568 requests. The quantized Qwen3-8B text comparator earned 83/96, the supplied references 61/96 and unrelated foils zero. An automated bilingual grader assessed deduplicated answers in two orders using the source. Human bilingual adjudication remains outstanding. [Per-work results, candidates and both orders](evidence/retrieved_glyph_writer_v1/assessment.json)

Under the correct preceding glyphs, the original writer chose the reference in 2,355 of 3,619 positions including stop. The related mixture had suffix evidence at 2,285 positions and changed 166 decisions. It repaired 65 errors and spoiled 26 correct predictions, a net gain of 39 cells. Random memory had evidence at 1,657 positions and changed 102 decisions, repairing 25 and spoiling 26. This measurement locates a real local advantage under a supplied history. It gives no mechanism for recovering the meaning of a clause after the writer has already drifted away. ## Time, storage and reproduction

The two corpus arrays each occupy 95,572,928 bytes. The directional sparse indices and auxiliary arrays occupy 53,124,600 and 67,786,096 bytes, in addition to the 25.37 MB numerical editor deployment. During evaluation the two index initializations together took 7.63 s. Preparing phrase drafts took 2.04 s. Across 568 requests, retrieval and vote preparation took 15.20 s and batched neural decoding took 0.99 s. The original writer took 1.06 s to decode. These times cover the specified stages on this workstation. They exclude data preparation, earlier fitting and grading. The study establishes no reduction in compute at matched language quality. [Timing record](evidence/retrieved_glyph_writer_v1/generation.json) · [Local inference](../models/retrieved_glyph_writer/README.md)

Every archived answer hash was checked. On CPU, all 24 sampled related and random answer images and stops matched the GPU outputs. All 568 exchanged outputs equalled the related output for the designated donor. The standalone CPU command reproduced the exact pixels for “It runs like this.” The Lean result identifies exactly when vote mass reverses the ordering of two candidates on a common integer probability scale. It makes no assumption about the correctness of a memory suggestion. [Recount and audit](evidence/retrieved_glyph_writer_v1/audit.json)

## Research decision

Retain the original editor as a control. The next intervention should test whether the editor learns complete propositions from a much broader sample of the parallel corpus. Compare the same visual architecture and initialization trained on repeated passages versus varied passages from the screened pool, matching supervised target glyphs, optimizer budget and controlled role examples. Reserve fresh works before training. Measure complete free answers, polarity and argument-role retention, and inspect raw errors and the quality of the bilingual pairs. A gain requires a positive lower bound on held-work improvement without a control regression. If both fits still fail, test source meaning in decoder states before spending more on decoding or retrieval. This scale comparison has yet to run.
