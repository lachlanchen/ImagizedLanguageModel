# Can a whole human answer teach a visual reader?

On 96 questions about 48 previously unseen stories, training the selector on the probability of a complete human reply reduced its mean negative log likelihood by 0.400 nats relative to the frozen parent (95% story-bootstrap interval 0.157 to 0.636). The gain over matched training on a single best path was 0.029 nats (−0.005 to 0.063). The marginal selector selected a different location from its parent on 78 of 96 questions and from the single-path selector on 22. All three writers drew zero verbatim answers. The marginal fit earned two of 16 story semantic credits, including a grammatically defective answer that mentioned the correct daughter. Keep the general baseline. ## Why these targets contain a training signal

For location probabilities p and frozen writer likelihoods L, the probability of a stopped reference image is their dot product. Where several distinct wordings are accepted, their complete probabilities add. The posterior on the evidence location is proportional to p × L. Differentiating the negative log probability gives the prior minus the posterior for each selector logit. A supported candidate receives increased relative weight when its complete-answer likelihood exceeds the prior average. Lean 4 verifies that comparison for finite nonnegative count masses and the effect of moving mass between two sites. The real-valued derivative was derived analytically and checked numerically. On 53 training questions without a verbatim candidate, all had nonzero gradients. Independently, Lean verifies an exact decomposition of the error count for a fixed writer and any admissible selector. On 92 earlier narrative questions every possible strip failed the exact-answer test. ## Training material and cost

Each fit received 5,988 English human questions from 184 FairytaleQA training stories, 2,048 human English statement questions and 2,048 Chinese exam questions. The separate 48-story reserve was chosen before optimization. It excludes all 64 stories used in the derivative check. Both members of each held pair concern the same section and have different answers. The fit and the 96 held questions shared no complete source section, document family or question image. Fourteen held questions had at least one reference wording also present somewhere in the fit. These are tests on new stories within the same collection. Under the released palette, all 96 held answers had positive probability for at least one candidate. ## Decision

The experiment establishes learning from complete visual answer supervision at the probability level. Performance on the new stories, the earlier Chinese cohorts and unrestricted writing gives no basis to adopt either head as a general model. The next controlled change is to train the writer itself on the complete narrative targets. Preserve the evidence interface and frozen-reader control initially. Compare the unchanged writer, the same writer tuned with its own selected evidence, and a matched writer that can attend to the full section when a single strip omits an argument. Measure answers drawn in full, question-pair consistency, Chinese retention and the response to changing one fact in a page. Exclude previously scored stories from the next confirmatory test.
