# Reading an action described in other words

Across 268 pairs of classical Chinese clauses, the image reader answered both object questions under both line orders and both question frames on 44 pairs. Its predecessor completed 11. Giving the same model English descriptions paired with exchanged source examples during training produced six. Both readers saw the same 16,632 training clauses and the same optical English word encoder during the matched aligned/exchanged comparison. The parent reader predates that encoder. A complete pair requires eight successful requests, including the correct object cell, the registered word in the emitted pixels and stopping. The dictionary and multiword rows share samples. In the aligned model, 78 of 268 dictionary pairs passed line selection. Supplying both correct lines raised complete writing to 138 pairs. Supplying the two object cells raised it to 201. These ceilings isolate different contributions to failure. [Scores and overlap audit](evidence/visual_action_meaning_v1/evaluation/audit.json)

The Tang result with *cup* and *child* reproduced Chinese lines present in the inherited reader's fit. None of the nine pairs passed a screen for unseen complete source images and target windows on both lines. We froze the reader, registered ten pairs from five Pu Songling stories and compared their complete pages and 17-cell verb and object windows against inherited training ink. All ten passed. On dictionary descriptions the parent selected both correct lines throughout on one pair, the aligned model on four and the exchanged control on zero. Only two pairs were writable even when the exact object cells were supplied. The reference readings are editorial selections informed by the original and Giles's translation. Exact scoring excludes unregistered alternatives, including *work* for 業 where *business*, *trade* and *occupation* were registered. The Pu set is small and selected after the Tang audit. The 1.90-million-parameter optical word network learned 50-dimensional targets for 29,447 English words. Its 1,024/1,024 nearest-target score was measured on previously trained terms. The 74,021-parameter alignment component received 7,423 WordNet aliases and definitions across 425 Kyoto action labels. A complete image-to-image predictor uses 3,526,604 parameters. On one RTX 4090 D, incremental word training took 19.0 seconds and fitting the aligned comparator took 14.2 seconds. The exchanged comparator took 13.2 seconds. Preparation, evaluation, public teacher training and inherited components are outside these timings. The numerical Tang gain passed the original count rule. The independent fresh-source requirement failed, so the aligned reader is released as an option. The current decoder fixes a single verb–object pair per line before reading the question. The Lean derivation proves that two queries requiring distinct object positions in that same line cannot both succeed under that decoder. The next test should reserve genuine multi-action clauses and let the question select among their event positions, with the current reader, exchanged bindings, and supplied-object writing as controls. General dialogue, free historical glyph generation and a matched-quality compute advantage remain open.
