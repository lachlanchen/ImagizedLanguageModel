# What source coverage buys a visual writer

A phrase writer translated every source strip exactly once on 96 new book requests. It reproduced **445 of 1,464 distinct reference strips**, compared with **280** for the preceding ordered model. The phrase writer repeated a content triple in four answers, compared with 28. Neither earned a complete-translation credit on this panel. The independent neural writer earned one, and text-input Qwen3-8B Q4 earned 90. ## Learning bilingual phrases from pixels

Each side of a parallel example consists of an ordered stream of physical printed cells. Longest matching optical strips are the basic units. We reused the 497,775 training pairs and the two previously estimated source-position HMMs. Viterbi decoding selected a path in each direction. We discarded links where the null emission was at least as likely as the selected source emission. The intersection of directional links supplied initial anchors. Adjacent links from their union could extend the alignment where at least one endpoint was unclaimed. A final pass paired still-unclaimed endpoints. We extracted consistent phrase spans of up to four strips on either side, allowing adjacent unaligned target strips. This implements a visual analogue of phrase-based statistical translation. [Registered protocol](covered_phrases_protocol.md) · [Statistical phrase-based translation](https://aclanthology.org/N03-1017/)

The 7,240,769 observations yielded 4,583,186 distinct bilingual pairs before pruning. Entries had to occur twice and rank in the top eight by count for their source. The English-output table retained 427,014 pairs and the Chinese-output table 456,539. The two compressed bundles, including target bigrams, occupy 10.43 MiB together. They require the earlier optical lexicon and fixed print inventory at inference. The directional GPU alignment loops took 4.33 and 2.30 seconds with less than 0.43 GiB allocated on one RTX 4090 D. Phrase counting and bigram fitting took a further 16.51 seconds on CPU, with a 1.68 GiB process peak. The timings exclude ancestor fits, data preparation, loading and development decoding. They do not establish a matched-quality compute advantage. [Direction-specific alignments](evidence/covered_phrases_v1/links-0.json) · [Reverse alignment](evidence/covered_phrases_v1/links-1.json) · [Fitted inventory](../models/covered_phrases/fit.json)

A decoding state records covered source positions, the preceding source endpoint and the last emitted target strip. A move translates an uncovered contiguous span. It may start at the first unconsumed position or up to two positions beyond it. Its log score combines directional relative phrase frequencies, target bigram fluency, output length, phrase count and source movement. Beam pruning retains twelve states per consumed-source count. A completed state covers the entire source. The two fluency and length coefficients were selected by mean glyph n-gram F2 on a separate development set of 72 pairs. The output is a concatenation of the actual target images associated with the selected strips. This is an independent, non-neural writer over discrete visual units in one aligned print repertoire. [Implementation](../ilm/visual_lm/covered_phrases.py) · [Development calibration](../models/covered_phrases/calibration.json)

Lean verifies that a span list whose concatenation is a permutation of the source indices consumes each index once and has the prescribed total size. It also proves that attaching arbitrary output values to those spans preserves this property. The runtime audit checked the premise on all 568 actual paths, reconstructed every phrase output from its selected physical strips, and independently reran twelve original image requests. The proof concerns coverage and places no condition on the meaning of the emitted words. ## Four books and the matched controls

The reservation selected twelve eligible aligned passages from each of four previously unused editions: *Berklee Music Theory*, *The Silk Roads*, *Love in the Time of Cholera* and *Until the End of Time*. Both directions were evaluated. The reservation excluded literal training-side matches and earlier held works. The grader received the source and deduplicated candidate texts. Credit required both reversed-order judgments and a completed output. The grader is automated and rejected 23 supplied translations. The 96 requests include both directions of 48 pairs. ## Can better search recover the missing reference?

For each new book request, we searched every admitted combination of phrase choices and source moves consistent with the reference prefix, without beam pruning. None of the 96 exact reference images was reachable. The median longest reachable prefix contained three glyphs. In 26 cases no choice emitted even the first reference glyph. The union of all target options for each source contained 953 of 1,464 distinct reference strips across the panel. Strip segmentation and paraphrase can affect overlap. The exact reachability result applies to the observed spelling; another wording may convey the full meaning. [Per-source reachability](evidence/covered_phrases_v1/audit.json)

A separate exploratory assessment tested whether the zero exact controlled score concealed usable paraphrases. Every fifth quartet supplied 96 pictured requests in 24 complete sets. The automated judge credited the phrase writer on 2/96, compared with 50 for the neural writer, 53 for unordered alignment, 51 for ordered alignment and 56 for the references. No swapped-role or flipped-negation foil earned credit. The judge's low acceptance of canonical answers limits the absolute interpretation. The phrase writer earned no paired-polarity credits in this sample. [Follow-up rule](covered_phrase_meaning_probe.md) · [All votes](evidence/covered_phrases_v1/role-meaning.json)

Could a more capable reader reconstruct the lost meaning from these drafts? In a separate diagnostic, Qwen3-8B edited each saved answer with the source withheld, or with both source and phrase draft visible. On the same 96 requests and two-order joint grading pass, the teacher earned four credits from phrase drafts alone, one from neural drafts alone, and 73 when it also received the original source alongside the phrase draft. The frozen source-only teacher answers earned 86, supplied references 71, raw neural answers one, and raw phrase answers and foils zero. The source contains information the saved draft rarely communicated to this reader. The small reference set and automated grading limit comparisons of absolute quality. [Editing protocol](covered_draft_editing_probe.md) · [Every edited answer and vote](evidence/covered_phrases_v1/editing.json)

The registered general-writing criterion failed. The phrase inventory supplies useful visual evidence for a writer but makes many target spellings inaccessible as hard output constraints. The next comparison will train a visual editor on the source and an automatically generated phrase draft. Allow it to copy, omit, reorder and generate glyphs freely. Compare it with the same trainable writer given the original source alone; hold out complete works and test autonomous meanings, names, role changes, stopping and compute. Retain the phrase writer as a reproducible control.
