# Recovering words and their order from a pictured source

A source-content predictor increased the number of free answers containing a requested predicate and one named participant. Whole book translation did not improve. A separate, independently executable reader improved its ability to choose between two supplied pictured translations. The results distinguish a learned affinity between source and target images from the ability to propose a faithful sentence. **Primary comparison.** Four unused books provided 24 paired passages each. One RTX 4090 D trained a visual multilabel predictor on both directions of 268,342 parallel pairs. Its 1,557,508 parameters score target-strip occurrence from local source features with maximum pooling. Prediction uses an independent frozen optical writer and an already trained image motor. Only the new predictor changes during fitting. Training the aligned and shuffled heads took 55.6 and 52.3 seconds. Both saw 2,146,736 directed examples over 11,592 updates, with identical target order. In 24 singleton batches a shuffled source could not be changed. Peak allocated tensor memory was 1.03 GiB per fit. These incremental costs exclude the parent training and drawing motor. The inspiration from target-set prediction in neural text translation predates this experiment. [Training protocol](source_supported_writing_protocol.md) · [Ma et al., ACL 2018](https://aclanthology.org/P18-2053/)

**What the writer produced.** The aligned writer rendered “But this was not true.” as “但这不是真的。” on book case 0127. The parent also preserved its meaning. On the pictured source 图24.2。, the parent produced “Figure 24.2” and the aligned writer shortened this to “F24.2.”, losing a credit. Across 192 requests the parent and shuffled fits each earned one English and one Chinese credit. The aligned fit earned one Chinese credit. Exchanging its source images earned zero. The aligned minus parent difference was −0.52 percentage points with a paired four-work bootstrap interval of −1.56 to 0.00, failing the registered rule. Qwen3-8B Q4 on the corresponding texts earned 166/192. The judge credited 147/192 corpus references and zero unrelated foils. All 768 GPU answers, including their stop decisions, reproduced on CPU. On the original OPUS-100 test sample, all 128 pairs survived both physical and normalized training-overlap checks. The guided writer’s only credited response translated “-We stay here tonight.” as “-我们晚上就在这里”. The source-only judge credited 176/256 provided OPUS translations. These are model judgments in two blinded presentation orders. **What the support predictor learned.** With the number of selected strips set to the true target cardinality for analysis, the aligned head recovered 735 of 3,595 distinct target-strip occurrences across the held book requests. The shuffled head recovered 505, and source-independent frequencies recovered 451. On supplied correct histories the aligned bonus repaired 103 action choices and lost 68, moving exact agreement from 800 to 835 of 3,910. With a 2.4-nat cap, 2,045 positions had an original score gap too large for any such first-emission bonus to close. The Lean proof bounds the number of compulsory errors; the empirical audit checks the implication at every held position. Literal name and predicate counts admit equivalent spellings or paraphrases as potential false negatives. **Can the correct unordered content be arranged?** A diagnostic supplied the actual target-strip multiset, including multiplicities and therefore length. The frozen writer greedily chose a remaining strip and stopped after consuming the inventory. Every output used exactly the prescribed bag. Only 7/192 book translations and 24/336 controlled sentences reproduced the original bitmap exactly. For 84 controlled pairs the two translations had an identical strip multiset. Only one pair reproduced both orders. Other permutations can convey meaning, so exact reconstruction is a narrower outcome than semantic adequacy. This experiment used privileged content. **Why joint matching can look much better than answering independently.** Let u be the true-minus-other log-score margin on source A and v the independently oriented margin on source B. A joint assignment wins when u + v > 0. Both individual readings are correct only if the smaller of u and v is positive. Equivalently, the source-dependent joint margin must exceed the absolute common target preference |u − v|. Subtracting one common target correction shifts the two individual margins in opposite directions and preserves their sum. Lean checks these statements in exact common score units. On the new books, the original strip body won 91/96 joint assignments yet chose both correct answers individually on 21/96 pairs. On 84 same-inventory role reversals, the corresponding results were 82/84 and 57/84. **Single-image candidate selection.** A portable reader estimates each proposed translation's baseline log score on 16 fixed training sources, chosen by the length of the individual input and target direction. It subtracts the mean from the score on the actual pictured source. Both supplied translations were chosen correctly on 77/96 book pairs, compared with 43/96 using per-action length normalization and 21/96 with raw likelihood. In the controlled role tests it reached 143/168 pairs and 76/84 identical-bag pairs. Each choice receives just one actual source and the alternative answer images. There is no competing test source. The hypotheses were developed using this panel, so transfer to new works remains to be demonstrated. The component requires pictured candidate answers from within the output repertoire. Its standalone command copies the selected PNG verbatim. Reconstructed canonical scores differed from independently tokenized batched scores by at most 0.000064 nats. On the archived freely generated proposals the corrected selector credited 2/192 book answers and 1/256 OPUS answers. Even a perfect selector could attain only two credits on each panel with those three candidate writers. **Decision.** Retain the single-query reader as a candidate scorer and keep its results separate from free writing. The next writer should learn to reconstruct the positions of missing and displaced target strips from source images plus incomplete target imagery. Include fully masked targets during training and begin the free test with no supplied answer content or length. Test the same body with true unordered content only as a diagnostic. Keep a matched training control, new books, legitimate stopping, wrong-name insertions, raw outputs and end-to-end cost. A stronger selector alone cannot recover missing proposals.
