# Where a pictured answer loses the question

The same passage says that Qin Che was lost and Zhuo Lin drew the map. Asked who drew it, the causal writer answered 路口那張地圖是卓霖替他畫的。 It also correctly changed the mapmaker when the two names were exchanged. Asked who needed directions, it repeated the mapmaking fact. The first response is a meaningful sentence. The second is legible and answers the wrong question. ## What the comparison establishes

We connected the causal blocks of Chinese GPT-2 to a learned visual input and output map. It feeds the visual encoding of each emitted bitmap back into its history. The inference program takes a pair of pictured lines and returns a PNG. It scores a finite bank of 7,990 printed bitmaps, so previously unseen inscriptions remain beyond this writer's output inventory. The earlier ordered decoder can still copy authentic historical forms from a page. [Inference guide](../models/causal_glyph_writer/README.md) · [Chinese GPT-2](https://huggingface.co/uer/gpt2-chinese-cluecorpussmall) · [Ordered historical writer](ordered_page_writer_result.md)

Two fits used identical answer batches and 322 updates on 2,565 human questions and answers. Correctly paired training yielded 16/571 exact stopped images, including two references assembled from dispersed source ink. Permuting passage and question pairs across training answers yielded zero. At inference, retaining the genuine question but replacing the passage also gave zero. For context, the previous ordered writer returned 37 exact answers, all copied as a single span. A second scoring pass reconstructed the official passages and evaluated each returned stream against all available renderable human answers, 1,366 distinct references within the 571 records. Best-reference glyph multiset F1 averaged 0.423 with genuine training, 0.170 with shuffled training and 0.144 with a different passage. Exact scores remained 16, zero and zero. Multiset overlap ignores order. In a descriptive reading of the first twelve absent-content records, two replies supplied the requested fact, three gave only part, and seven missed it or repeated text. ## The first missing glyph

All 1,525 target occurrences outside the pictured passage and question were available in the output bank. With the correct preceding answer supplied, the writer recovered 10,039/12,764 glyphs present in the prompt and 446/1,525 absent glyphs. At the **first** required absent glyph, it was correct in **18/300** cases, chose visible ink in **258/300**, and chose to stop in six. Median rank of the correct glyph at that first decision was 15.5. With autonomous generation it recovered zero complete absent-content references. One preliminary count included an unwritable glyph physically present on the page. The corrected calculation distinguishes input presence from output availability. [Corrected measurements](evidence/causal_glyph_writer_20261119/correct_history.json) · [Initial calculation](evidence/causal_glyph_writer_20261119/initial_candidate_coverage_history.json)

Giving that first departure eight times its usual training weight reduced the number of source selections from 238 to 226 relative to equal additional training. Both chose the correct glyph in 21/300 cases. The weighted fit completed seven open answers, further replay completed eight, and both remained at zero on absent-content answers. Reducing the tendency to copy did not tell the model which fact to express. ## Where retrieval matters

In 51/300 absent-content cases a different paragraph in the original documents contained the entire reference verbatim. A question-only lexical ranker exposed five of the 51 answers and the fixed reader returned zero complete responses. Centering the window on the known answer exposed all 51, of which the reader returned seven exactly. The reference-assisted result measures a recoverable evidence-selection error. Forty-four failures persisted even with that evidence in view. ## Cost and scope

One RTX 4090 D adapted the last four transformer blocks and final normalization in 15.2 seconds, peaking at 2.08 GiB of allocated GPU memory. Visual transfer took 10.2 seconds. The package uses 85,842,432 inherited causal parameters, 9,437,184 fitted regression coefficients, 498,912 learned glyph-metric parameters, 2,359,296 fixed projection coefficients and two 768-component boundary vectors. The adaptation timing excludes the earlier metric fit, upstream text pretraining, data preparation and evaluation. The matched original writer is the released research checkpoint. All four fixed map-question outputs reproduced pixel for pixel on CPU in an isolated, offline-capable package. Broad language learning from images alone and a matched-quality compute advantage remain open.
