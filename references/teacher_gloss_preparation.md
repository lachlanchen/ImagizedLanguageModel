# Paired complete targets for visual sequence distillation

The single Qwen3-VL checkpoint produced twelve acceptable translations from typed sources and eight from pictures on eighteen fixed passages. The compact draft editor produced none. The teacher can also make a confident error on a source it transcribes exactly. Its responses require measurement before they are used as training targets.

Prepare 512 complete modern glosses from 512 different **training** chapters. Use literal training sources of 5–24 pictured cells with 3–48 reference cells, selected by fixed hash of chapter and ordinal. Run the same local Qwen3-VL 8B Instruct Q8 model on their transcriptions with the measured translation prompt. Keep raw answers, their actual rendered glyph images, source and archive-target indices, generation stop reasons, unrenderable cases, cost and hashes. Exclude incomplete or unrenderable answers from any student fit. The original aligned archive target remains a matched control. None of the reported evaluation passages may serve as optimization targets.

This pilot creates data for sequence-level teaching. The proposed student reads source images and writes images from an empty answer using a learned order and stop. Measure complete changed answers and source-sensitive meaning against a model trained for the same time on original archive targets. Keep teacher preparation and any inherited foundation weights in the resource ledger. A higher score on a token teacher, accurate transcription or a large number of teacher labels does not itself qualify the visual student. [Sequence-level knowledge distillation](https://aclanthology.org/D16-1139/)

Read the first 24 entries in fixed training-roster order with anonymized archive and teacher outputs before considering any larger generation job. Report complete accepted counts, teachers fixing poor references and teachers degrading faithful references. Require at least 12 faithful teacher glosses and at least three teacher-only faithful glosses before treating the new images as a candidate supplemental training signal. Keep both targets for the comparison; the gate does not warrant replacing the archive. Reader familiarity and teacher computation remain part of the report.

## Preparation and paired reading

The 512 requests produced 12,050 rendered teacher cells. One 170-cell answer reached the token limit, leaving 511 complete usable pairs. The archived teacher service reported 215.8 seconds across requests for these targets. The full local run took 227.0 seconds. The 24-passage blind reading accepted sixteen archive glosses and sixteen teacher glosses. Six pairs gained fidelity under the teacher and six lost it. The teacher sometimes introduced a historically wrong identity. Keep the original targets and treat the eligible teacher images as additional supervision. The pilot contains no student-quality result. [Counts, source hashes and frozen readings](evidence/paired_teacher_glosses_20261119.json)

The paired arrays are in `artifacts/paired_teacher_glosses_v1/images.npz`. Each row points back to a training source and includes offsets for source, archival and teacher image streams. The `usable` mask excludes the capped answer. Original teacher responses and timings remain in `responses.jsonl`. Reproduce preparation with `PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/prepare_teacher_glosses.py`.
