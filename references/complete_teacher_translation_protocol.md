# Learning a teacher's full translation from pictured sentences

The previous independent visual writers translated zero of 144 new requests. A quantized bilingual teacher could translate most of a separate article panel, and the held evaluations showed a large difference between judgments made with and without an identified reference. Test whether changing the supervision on exactly the same pictured training sources improves autonomous preservation of meaning.

Reserve 24 bilingual passages from four unused literary works and one passage from each of 24 previously unused TED talks. Exclude document overlaps with the book training and prior development or evaluation panels, and exact normalized overlaps with the earlier training corpora. Reserve both directions. Use the previously reserved 32 FLORES articles as a secondary domain transfer panel. Omit all previous fitted diagnostics, role replacements and exact previously assessed sources from teacher sampling. Draw 1,024 distinct actual training requests in each combination of book or talk and English or Chinese target. Record the corpus target and generate an independent Qwen3-8B Q4 translation from the source alone. A stopped, renderable teacher target of at most 96 glyphs must pass both a reference-informed and a reference-blind assessment. Grade a masked sample separately using bilingual reading from a different model family. Report grader, rendering, selection and single-card costs. Preserve failed and ambiguous cases.

Within each domain and target language select the largest subset with exactly equal total output-glyph counts between teacher and corpus supervision. Both student arms start from the released prefix-recovery writer. On the retained examples the only change is the complete target image. Both arms rehearse previously learned book, talk and role examples in the same batches. Match student parameter count, source order, optimizer updates, supervised glyph exposures and stop exposures. Optimize complete sequence cross entropy, including every intermediate glyph and the terminal action. Run twelve passes at a learning rate decaying from 0.00016 to 0.00004. Select a trained epoch using the macro-average stopped normalized glyph similarity on the established disjoint development set. Keep the unchanged starting writer as a third comparator.

Evaluate raw generated images in both directions on all 96 new natural requests. Grade complete meanings in two masked orders, with reference and shuffled foil calibration and text Qwen on the same sources. Independently inspect a blinded sample using the source and full candidate answers. Retain the actual input and output PNGs and reproduce the illustrated pixels from the released weights. Report all 118 role families (eight transformations per family), a training-only probe, matched-cost receipts and independent free writing. Promotion requires a positive document-bootstrap lower 95% bound for fresh semantic gain over both the matched control and the start, plus at least 110 of 118 complete canonical role families. If semantics fails even on the fitted sample, distinguish failure to fit selected teacher outputs from failure to generalize before committing more compute. Each student has one RTX 4090 D, under 12 GiB allocated and one device-hour. Offline teacher and evaluator costs are additional.
