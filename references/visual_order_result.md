# Reading the requested order of two historical fields

A pictured question can ask for the same gloss and the same attested glyph in opposite orders. On a new set of four English and Chinese formulations, the visual order classifier raised completely correct pairs from **138/608 to 332/608**. A pair counted only when both requests on the same page returned the two exact source values in their respective orders and stopped. The 608 pairs cover 152 entries and 145 families withheld from fitting. All 4,256 outputs of the ordered reader replayed exactly on CPU. **Why intervene on order?** First, two copies of the matched recurrent reader continued for 640 updates on the same 5,112 training requests. Cycling through eight wordings raised fully correct pairs on an earlier wording panel from 66 to 120. The repeated-wording and varied-wording readers each completed zero English pairs. Yet on 288/304 English pairs the varied reader selected both right fields twice. The claim of improvement in both languages failed. On previously reported paraphrases, exact single answers rose from 367 to 375 overall, with English declining from 235 to 191 and Chinese rising from 132 to 184. All 3,040 repeat-control images and 3,039 varied-control images reproduced on CPU. **What changes in the deployed reader?** The recurrent model still selects source rows and decides when to stop. For two distinct selected rows, the added component matches literal parts of their pictured labels to nonoverlapping stretches of the pictured query. It reads one- to five-cell subsequences around the mentions, replaces headword occurrences with a shared marker, and predicts whether the earlier mention should be printed first. The headword and stage names do not enter as Unicode symbols. Its 32,768 hashed feature bins have 2,694 nonzero trained weights plus one bias. The 40,896 training images reduce to 88 observed feature–label combinations after normalization. The base model has 94,866 parameters. If both mentions cannot be located it preserves the recurrent choice. Supplied page boundaries, literal label overlap and coverage of the instruction language matter. This system returns existing fields and can preserve a specimen outside the available fonts. [Protocol](origin_visual_order_protocol.md) · [Fit](../models/origin_instructions/visual_order/fit.json)

**Transfer and limits.** On the further wording panel, the gain was 31.9 percentage points (post-fit 95% paired family-bootstrap interval 29.1–34.7). The gains were 2.6 points in English (1.0–4.6) and 61.2 in Chinese (56.3–66.0). This resamples families within four fixed formulations. On direct mention order the reader reached 267/304 pairs; when the last-output field appeared first in the question it reached 65/304. Reordering could still fix 118 pairs, all English. A further 158 lack the correct selected values on at least one request. The registered directional and retention criteria passed. On the original familiar wording it preserved 607/608 answers. On the first wording panel, which motivated the change, it reached 483/608 pairs, exactly the selection ceiling there. These scores measure constrained instruction following. Composing an explanation of an unsupplied origin remains open.
