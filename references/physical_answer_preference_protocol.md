# Training the likelihood of a complete visual answer

On 3,056 pairs of previously unused passages with identical immediate neighbors, the published writer favored the correct joint assignment of supplied expressions 1,956 times. Both individual answers were preferred in only 270 pairs. Test whether training directly against such competing physical trajectories improves complete free writing.

Use the registered training corpus and the published guided writer as initialization in both arms. Pair training windows once using the established identical-neighbor, different-source, different-answer rule. The reference loss is the mean negative log probability of the 48 given physical nibbles and the final stop, using the glyph actually rendered from each reference code for subsequent visual feedback. Keep the ink motor and landmarks frozen. Train the complete visual context reader and recurrent writer together for eight passes over all 71,439 training examples, batch size 192, matched seed 720, AdamW at 0.0002, decay 0.01, clip gradients to one, BF16 autocast and no reader dropout. The control uses the reference loss. In the treatment add 0.08 times the mean over all minibatch rows of softplus((L(foil)-L(source))/2) at paired rows, with zero additional loss at unpaired rows. Use the same other-source foil for a training context throughout. Evaluate only final weights. The student generates with the same physical beam and learns when to stop. Supplied foils are used only in training and diagnostic scoring.

Compare the published writer, joint reference-only fit and joint preference fit on the official development set, the bucket 36–39 confirmation just examined, and an unused bucket 40–43 confirmation. Exclude contexts from all earlier splits and deduplicate within the new set. Reuse strict whole-output physical scores, both-correct pairs, source-group bootstrap differences, unseen and absent answers, known-shape fraction and the local count baseline. On the final confirmation evaluate the supplied trajectory preferences as well. A preference-trained replacement needs a positive lower 95% paired improvement over each comparator on both official and unused groups, at least 99% known shapes, and no reduction in complete both-correct pairs versus the published writer on unused groups. If the reference-only fit improves reliably, report its effect independently. Do not select a new reference-only deployment on this same comparison. Preserve the historical reader and the sealed source. Keep the 8 GiB GPU, 6 GiB host RSS and 35-minute per-job limits and a single locked GPU. If physical preference improves only on supplied alternatives, treat the remaining difference as an independent generation problem. Separate any change from continued joint training from the effect of preference supervision.
