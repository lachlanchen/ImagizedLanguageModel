# Training an image-stream transducer on whole pictured phrases

The diversity study found that relaxing the insertion allowance increased output length but raised glyph error by approximately twelve percentage points. The next comparison changes the training signal for the inserted content. Fit a common monotone editor with either the log probability of one minimum-edit path or the logarithm of the sum over all compatible edit paths. Keep the physical draw distribution, state factorization, starting checkpoint, minibatches, update schedule, decoder and resource ceiling identical. Each emitted bitmap supplies 48 physical measurements to the next recurrent step. The renderer writes those measurements into their corresponding pixels. The likelihood describes these measurements. Free scores compare all 1,024 pixels of each emitted cell.

Use the first 16,384 pairs in frozen training shard A with at most 24 classical and 32 modern glyphs. Mix one quotation per four translations, train for three passes and retain the final weights. Full lattice scoring is quadratic in source and reference length. Report this length restriction, the actual grid sizes, training time and memory. Keep the 192-glyph inference limit, require source consumption before stopping, and use greedy edit actions followed by the same four-way within-glyph beam. An attempted emission beyond the limit is a failed stop.

Before fitting, reserve two disjoint chapter pools of 1,024 fresh classical sentences apiece, using the original training-chapter exclusion, deterministic row priorities and physical source deduplication against all previous rosters. Evaluation has no modern-reference length cutoff. Include unsupported requests in exact-answer denominators. Compare with the data-matched local edit rule and the frozen original editor on the same inputs. Recount saved pixels, complete changed translations, copied plus source-absent Han in longer answers, stopping, quotation and source exchange. Measure exact inserted spans under recorded edit histories and inspect first-in-order actual successes and failures. Test whether a change in who acts on whom changes the generated modern answer with a separately fixed role-swap probe.

An improvement requires positive lower 95% chapter-bootstrap bounds on glyph edit-rate improvement over fixed alignment and local edits on both cohorts, at least one complete mixed changed answer longer than eight glyphs per cohort, at least 99% exact quotations of all requests and improved or equal complete inserted-span identity. A failure retains the released default, and calls for a diagnosis using the final weights and actual outputs. One serial GPU owner, two host threads, 8 GiB allocated GPU memory, 6 GiB process RSS, and 35 minutes per fitting or scoring phase. Compare measured cost at matched examples and updates.
