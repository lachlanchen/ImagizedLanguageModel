# Choosing the first answer glyph from the whole page

The selected bilingual writer misses the first acceptable glyph in 573 of its 627 failed English answers on held-out BiPaR passages. On 494 of 748 English questions the intended action at the first point of disagreement between the two answers already scores above the rival action. A third action wins 536 of these 748 unrestricted choices. The same written inventory covers every non-whitespace English question cell. These observations motivate additional supervision on the first output distribution. [Reply audit](evidence/bilingual_novel_20261124/error_audit.json) · [Action audit](evidence/bilingual_novel_20261124/conditional.json)

For each book example let \(p_t(a)\) be the sum of the model’s generation mass and its copy mass for all positions bearing the physical glyph \(a\). Keep the existing per-glyph negative log likelihood, source-address supervision, decoder, training examples, replay, checkpoint initialization and resource ceiling. Add one complete-distribution negative log likelihood per novel question with unit coefficient. In the candidate use the first answer position. In the matched control use a later position, chosen deterministically per question from the remaining glyph actions including the stop. This changes the relative training weight of the first decision with a single transformer forward pass for each batch. On a failure at the first glyph, later correct decisions conditional on a supplied gold prefix cannot recover the exact freely emitted answer. The prefix and margin implications were checked in Lean. Compare the two new fits with ordinary aligned training. Freeze the bilingual release and the 64-page serving comparison. Retain the 374-passage bilingual book and 227-passage regular-print XQuAD scores as development comparisons. Evaluate subject changes, true stops, historical cards, source-absent content and full measured costs separately. Before claiming broader transfer, reserve fresh book passages by title or work, with two disjoint answers per page, outside this selection material. Require more four-answer pages and fewer wrong first glyphs than both controls on independent pages, alongside CMRC and historical retention, before replacing the bilingual writer. If the first-action score improves without a gain in complete answers, inspect the first subsequent failure under free generation before further training. The proposed reweighting has no measured result yet.
