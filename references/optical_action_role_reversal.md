# Can the two finished writers distinguish who acted on whom?

The independent writers correctly assigned most pairs of complete book translations, including pairs with equal source lengths. These books permit lexical cues. Before scoring a controlled role reversal, fix eight pairs of bilingual personal names, six directed transitive predicates and both affirmative and negative polarity, in both translation directions. Within each pair exchange the agent and patient while retaining the exact same multiset of source glyphs and of target glyphs. Verify those equalities, discard any full side present in either training corpus and use every eligible case. Count strict positive, negative and tied log-likelihood assignment margins, split by direction and verb, separately from free writing. Preserve all rendered test forms and raw margins. These constructed sentences measure sensitivity to role order with supplied alternatives. They cannot certify general grammar or credit a generated book translation.
