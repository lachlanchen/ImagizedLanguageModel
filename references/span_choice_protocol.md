# Choosing answer strips from Chinese page images

A role-conditioned reader answered both questions on both versions of 79 of 128 exchanged-name passages in both sentence orders. It answered 119 of 256 ordinary Chinese comprehension questions directly, below the generic reader's 187. Can supervision from ordinary human questions recover that loss while preserving the exchange improvement?

Four frozen readers propose physical source strips of up to 48 cells. A learned network weights the generic, factual, participant-likelihood and participant-margin proposals from contextual pixel features, relative span scores, confidence and agreement. Equal bitmap strings pool their weight. Training maximizes the probability of any exact human reference or named participant. Cases with no correct proposal contribute zero gradient and remain in reported accuracy denominators. The control permutes training supervision across rows. The proposal oracle gives a ceiling for this experiment. The Lean proofs give a sufficient mass condition and an unavoidable coverage error.

Use CMRC 2018 and DRCD training paragraphs plus paired DuIE training examples. Reserve source paragraphs and paired subjects from the training splits for model selection. Select the epoch by the equally weighted mean of natural and exchange exact-object accuracy there, taking the earliest tie. Freeze both fits before test scoring. Use a second sample from the CMRC development and DRCD test sets after excluding earlier factual and broad-test passages. Report upstream pretraining exposure as unknown. Neither corpus tags, strings, gold roles nor answers enter the selector at inference. Control the rendered face for exact scoring.

The primary rule requires fresh human direct accuracy at least equal to the generic reader, at least 79/128 complete original passage exchanges and 37/48 reordered clause boards correct in both emitted orders, and at least 34/48 complete human factual answers in both orders. Report both original broad QA and historical substitutions as retention diagnostics. Keep the preceding models available for either result. A pass supports a bounded source-grounded reader.
