# Free image-to-image translation with a causal visual writer

The exact phrase lattice cannot reproduce 993 of 1,024 recorded glosses. None of six independent proposals rescued the twelve failed reviewed passages with acceptable references. Train a new clause writer which can emit a source image, a frequently observed modern image, a drawn landmark sequence or an end decision at any decoder step. Encode both the pictured source and the previous output through the same visual network. Frequency exemplars consist of the 2,048 most common complete physical bitmaps in training targets. Disclose this visual inventory, the learned stop, the frozen motor and all parameters. At inference the decoder receives its actual emitted pixels after every step.

Use all 55,872 aligned training passages, eight shuffled passes, batch 96, AdamW 0.001 with gradient norm 1. The recurrent reader and writer have width 192. Supervise source attention using the deterministic monotone edit alignment only during training. Use physical copy when the aligned source cell is identical, exemplar selection on other common shapes, and free drawing for rarer shapes. Select a mode and then its most likely position, exemplar or four-pixel beam output. Freeze final weights unconditionally. Cap free outputs at 96 glyphs and count cap exits separately. One 4090 D, less than 10 GiB allocated GPU memory and a 24 GiB host reserve. No other ILM training may overlap.

Compare original source images, reversed source images and the previously released phrase composer on 1,024 different source passages of 5–24 glyphs from chapters outside both earlier aligned splits, the earlier confusable review and the new 1,024-case support study. Use a stable hash of chapter and ordinal with at most eight rows per chapter. Report full bitmap distance, changed exact glosses, output route and stops, and a blinded reading in 18 chapter-distinct passages with negative markers, speech markers and event verbs. These markers define sampling strata only. Accept a new default only if it improves physical error over the composer and more of its complete translations are judged faithful. Preserve every outcome and the preceding checkpoints. Weak free writing in the presence of strong teacher-forced prediction calls for measuring the first divergent output and error recovery.
