# Reading a candidate answer before printing it

The first independent writer added a price to the first held Chinese card. Its greedy decoder sometimes continued past a complete description or stopped midway through a name. Freeze the writer and train an inverse reader on the printed human descriptions in the same training split. Estimate which facts its convolutional reader can recover from an entire candidate. Select a stopped answer among width-eight likelihood beams using this evidence and the source meanings independently inferred from the input pixels. Include a same-beam likelihood-only control. Retain the original greedy outputs and scores.

Use 4,000 updates of 96 human descriptions, a fixed seed, a 10-minute training and 5 GiB allocation ceiling on GPU0. Keep eight live prefixes and up to eight completed sentences, using length exponent 0.7 and maximum 400 glyphs. Rank completed candidates by mean letter log likelihood plus 0.7 times the sum of the requested and unrequested Bernoulli log probabilities (each sum normalized by the requested count). Source and answer classifications use learned visual features. No test facts, reference text, regular expressions, or text templates may enter generation or candidate selection.

Use one final listener and fixed search settings. Report all unique held records with both Chinese and English inputs, the matched byte reader, identical beam-only and greedy controls, and the family-polarity interventions. Score the previously recorded exact detector and a separately labelled rating/price alias sensitivity measure applied identically to humans and models. Since these same records revealed the initial failure, any improvements are paired exploratory evidence. Inspect a systematic sample of Chinese-card answers directly for grammar, missing facts, contradictions and added claims. If both factual and linguistic results improve, retain the optional search path and report its cost. Otherwise preserve the failed experiment.

With the writer, listener and search settings frozen, also apply them once to all 1,484 distinct official development records. These records have not influenced fitting or model choice. Alternate Chinese and English field rows inside each card in canonical field order, beginning in Chinese. Report both exact criteria for beam-only and readback. The mixed setting is a transfer test of known row surfaces, with novel combinations of fields and scripts.

To separate readback from length renormalization, replay exactly the eight Chinese-card candidates for the 1,847 original records. Require reproduction of the previously frozen beam and readback strings. Compare the same mean log likelihood with the listener weight at zero and 0.7. Measure how often any of the eight already passes each fixed external test. These target-aware upper bounds are diagnostic and never select released answers.
