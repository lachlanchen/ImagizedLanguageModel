# Earlier glyph actor retains limited answers on the compact cohort

The frozen earlier actor gives **2/32 complete exact QA answers** and
**1/16 both-correct passage pairs** on the same physical inputs used by both
failed compact models. Both compact final checkpoints give zero on these
metrics. The earlier actor produces three fully readable normal QA outputs;
117 of its 120 endpoint outputs have uncertain whole-response readings.
No readable coherent complete book continuation is established. This is a
small development control, not a usable image language model.

| Model | Complete exact QA | Both questions correct | Credited complete book continuations |

| Compact V1, final | 0/32 | 0/16 | 0/12 |
| Wider compact writer, final | 0/32 | 0/16 | 0/12 |

The earlier actor retains 7,522,463,984 inherited visual/contextual parameters
and its existing neural glyph bridge. Its observer, adaptation data and
deterministic writer differ from the compact foundations and noisy flow
writers. The comparison does not isolate parameter count or establish an
efficiency advantage. The previous 16/32 clear-answer result used another
cohort and metric and remains separate.

## Frozen inputs, review and strict scoring

The [registered protocol](glyph_tail_compact_cohort_protocol.md) fixes the
18,000-update checkpoint, all 120 endpoint input PNGs, 128-glyph cap and
unchanged actual-pixel feedback. No new optimizer update is made. The
adaptation-data audit finds zero question-ID, normalized original-passage or
training-PNG overlap with the current cohort. Inherited pretraining and all
earlier book/copy ancestors remain outside that certification.

One Codex model reviewer inspected all ten opaque boards and the twelve
visible book prefixes before opening the mapping or scoring references.
Three complete readings were confirmed at exact nearest-neighbour enlargement.
The [frozen readings](evidence/glyph_tail_compact_learning_20261111/readings.json)
have SHA-256 `89286e717c3aad8a8045d54bf9534f7c0df8f466cfe6f3c508329587f8f76730`.
There was prior cohort familiarity and no independent human adjudication.
All 120 outputs are nonblank. Unresolved glyphs and repeated rows prevent
whole readings on 117 pages; recognizable prefixes do not supply complete
answers. All cases remain in their original denominators.

The two exact stopped answers are **169平方公里** and
**湖南省长沙县中南部**, for different questions about the same passage.
The third readable output is **大广弦、台湾月琴、台湾品仔**. It omits
the canonical answer's parenthetical alias **（台湾笛）**, so the unchanged
strict scorer rejects the complete exact match. This is not a finding that
the shorter answer is factually false. No new semantic adjudication or
reference-dependent transcription repair is introduced.

Both exact-answer rate and both-correct-pair rate are 6.25%, with pointwise
95% passage-group bootstrap intervals of **0–18.75%**. The normal-minus-erased
differences have the same intervals because both erased QA conditions score
zero. The intervals include zero; one successful passage pair is limited
evidence. Constant control observations produce degenerate intervals, without
establishing zero population success. Twelve book prefixes from two works
do not support a book-level interval.

The 44 normal inputs have eight predicted stops, 36 capped streams and no
empty responses. All twelve normal book streams hit the cap and have
uncertain whole readings, limiting the coherence judgment. Of 32
passage-erased QA streams, five stop and none gives a complete exact answer.
All 32 question-erased QA streams reach the cap and none succeeds.

80.20 seconds loading the actor and 1,484.92 seconds generating the 120
streams. Peak allocated GPU memory is **17.786 GiB**. No pressure wait is
recorded. These are frozen-inference costs, excluding prior adaptation,
corpus preparation and inherited pretraining.

row while the cohort wraps rows; exact tensors and display pixels, rather
than PNG layout bytes, are compared. Required input/checkpoint opens are
verified, with no observed forbidden reference/font/corpus access or IP
connection. The replay takes 84.89 seconds, including 68.85 seconds loading
and 15.12 seconds generating, with 17.757 GiB peak allocation. This verifies
one standalone invocation, without qualifying language. Both workers have

## Next measured intervention

The completed CPU audit verifies all 3,176 original adaptation PNG hashes.
Both cohorts use 1,024-pixel-wide input pages, but earlier training heights
range from 160 to 384 pixels, with a median of 288. Current normal QA heights
range from 448 to 992, with a median of 704. Earlier target answers range
from one to sixteen glyphs, with a median of six. Current canonical answers
range from three to 118, with a median of 16.5. Earlier prompts use a folded
proportional raster; the compact cohort uses fixed glyph cells, including
individual Latin/digit cells. These are observed distribution differences,
not a demonstrated explanation of the result.

Before another fit, register one frozen input-presentation comparison on all
32 normal QA cases and their erased-input controls. Reuse the earlier input
renderer, preserve the full original passage/question content and keep
weights, output path, cap and scoring fixed. Do not truncate to the earlier
answer limit, crop around a reference answer, reveal reference lengths or
select only the successful pair. Measure whether readable question-dependent
capability transfers better under that input presentation. See the
[bounded next decision](glyph_tail_input_presentation_decision.md).

The full goal remains independent image-input/image-output language on one
4090, followed by historical and unencoded forms and a quality-matched
resource comparison. The completed paper revision reports both compact
