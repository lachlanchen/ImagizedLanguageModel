# Writing from a change in the pictured passage

The frozen supplied-name reader answered both members of only 1/120 Winograd contrast pairs. Six pairs had identical local observations. In the other 114 a visible change was available. The present trial trains a general next-glyph writer. Its visual input is an ordered page of 32-pixel cells. It writes an arbitrary-length answer using stored training shapes or source pixels. It receives no list of answers, candidate spans, property schema or reference resolver at deployment.

Fit a causal optical writer on Chinese and English GOLEM fiction, pictured Alpaca instruction responses and AI2 WinoGrande-XL. Collapse only typographic whitespace for rendering. Keep complete answers, sampling windows from long records. Four printed cells form a causal block, with a within-block causal readout. Copy competes with output from a physical glyph bank. Teach the writer by next-glyph likelihood. The control and intervention share their optical warm start. For the intervention, add a preference for the correct full written answer over the other answer to each of two otherwise related pages, training the weaker of the two preferences. Match all pages, foil evaluations, base losses, schedules and updates for the independent-loss control. Fit a matched byte-input comparison. Keep source observations and self-generated history distinct.

The planned warm fit uses 8,000 batches per representation and the three subsequent fits use 2,000 batches apiece. Use 256-cell windows, 32 sequences in a warm batch, and one RTX 4090 D with a 20 GiB allocation ceiling. Use the final scheduled fits. Record timing and device energy separately for all arms. Checkpoint optimizer and schedule for exact resumption when possible. Report failures and keep interim artifacts private.

Keep original WinoGrande validation separate. Reserve a deterministic fraction of training prefix families for intact opposite-answer pairs. Exclude training windows from WinoGrande that share an eight-word passage fragment after candidate anonymization with either held set or external Winograd passages. Check new replies in free generation. Report candidate likelihood rankings separately. Compare both directions together, cue erasure or crossed contexts, nearest available name, individual accuracy, output spelling and stopping. Do not interpret a successful candidate ranking as a written answer.

Evaluate ordinary open writing with no candidates on held fiction and English and Chinese instruction prompts. Compare actual target likelihood with swapped same-language prompts, and inspect fixed free-running answers and book continuations. Literal agreement can miss valid alternatives, so preserve the original outputs. The null finding is actionable: a gain limited to forced rankings will require changing free writing, and a gain limited to the training families will require better contextual supervision. The same model must supply both the experimental measure and the depicted output.
