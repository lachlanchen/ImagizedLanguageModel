The previous Chinese writer reached both correct prefixes through the first divergence on one of 128 paired pages. It failed every pair of complete answers. The present comparison changes the training objective. Retain the original model, frozen visual prior, optimizer, initial reader weights, random order and four full passes over 11,865 Chinese exam questions. The authenticated matched-question writer is the equal-exposure full-likelihood control. Confirm the new draw-order digest against its receipt.

For each training question choose the first following sibling in cyclic source order with distinct question pixels, identical passage pixels and a different stopped target. Find the first position at which the two answer images differ, including stop as a possible difference. Let H be the mass of the common target prefix under the actual question and G the mass of that prefix including its own next glyph. Optimize the original mean per-cell stopped-answer loss plus the sum of log(H/G) on eligible rows divided by the size of the entire batch. The extra loss has unit weight. The empty prefix has mass one. Compute censored prefix masses by summing compatible copy and spelling paths. Compare their conditional ratios with sequential forced observation on real training inputs, including nonempty prefixes and stopping.
