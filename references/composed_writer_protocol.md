# Composing a natural Chinese answer in pixels

The released passage reader transfers contiguous evidence. The 571 held-out DuReader references include 177 contiguous answers, 94 rearrangements and 300 requiring content ink absent from both inputs. A perfect selector with access to all 2,692 training-answer bitmaps could spell 560. This comparison trains a recurrent span-and-image writer and tests whether it can select the right new glyphs and end a complete answer. [Corpus and physical limits](composed_answer_bitmap_support.md)

Fit one writer on all 2,565 pictured training examples for 18 shuffled epochs, batch size 32, seed 19401. Use only the final checkpoint. No development-based update, early stop or checkpoint selection. GPU 0 alone, at most 30 minutes for the full fit and inference and 8 GiB of PyTorch allocated memory. The frozen released reader supplies two pictured boundary score channels. Its parameters and training history belong to the system cost. Training memory comprises only physical tiles present in training answers. Record the seed, updates, loss, memory, weights, wall time and all held-out outputs locally. DuReader permits noncommercial research or teaching; retain its content locally.

Teach three actions from an initially empty stream. Copy the longest available input span, write a single missing tile or stop. Each copied run stays inside one input image. Sum the probability for all starts of the same longest run; supervise the end conditioned on the first such start. The recurrent decoder reads the appearance of its own last emitted segment. During supervised fitting it sees the reference segment history. The bitmap choice is scored through a shared shape encoder. The open renderer learns a signed-logit correction to the selected training bitmap with independent binary pixel losses. Train both literal heads wherever the reference requires writing. Record any difference from whole-image selection.

Evaluate the unchanged released passage reader, the new writer with input copying and stopping alone, the same weights with finite bitmap writing enabled and with the open renderer enabled. Each new writer has up to 64 output cells with an independent stop decision. Rotate the questions by one case with original passages and evaluate the open writer against the original references as a sensitivity control. These swapped questions may concern a different passage. Report all three reference groups, strict stopped exact answers, edit distance, lengths, nonempty/stopped outputs and overlap and exact positions for ink absent from the input. Report out-of-memory and out-of-training-set shapes literally as counts of novel bitmaps. Their presence alone gives no language credit.

A positive first writing result requires at least 15 complete, stopped references from the 300 input-absent cases, more than the exchanged-question condition, with at least as many exact total answers as the released reader. Independently inspect answer relevance and support for four preselected cases per reference group (lowest SHA256 of the concatenation of “composed-reader-19401/”, archive member and question ID). Hide arm labels during the reading. To claim meaningful writing require at least three of the four open-writer answers in the input-absent group to be responsive and fully supported. Report failures and semantic reading uncertainty. Compare whether any open-renderer change improves over the finite writer. A miss retains the released reader and requires localizing the error in routing, spelling, grounding or stopping before increasing training scale. General book or historical-script writing and the whole-system efficiency goal require separate evidence.
