# Writing two answers from a shared passage

After learning from 191,007 pictured questions the previous writer still repeated the whole passage on both original author probes. Direct glyph-set overlap solved 231 of 285 pairs in a follow-up recognition test. The new comparison uses 7,108 training pairs and 1,042 held-out pairs of natural traditional Chinese questions about the same passage. The selected human answer sets are disjoint and every question-to-answer overlap comparison ties or favours the sibling. [Prior model and corpus](grounded_glyph_writer_result.md)

Initialize both arms with the final matched 628,104-parameter writer and the same 7,715 training-answer shapes. Keep complete question and passage images, the decoder and greedy termination unchanged. For each pair enumerate both questions against both canonical answer images, including the stop. Score an answer by its mean log likelihood per target. Both arms minimize minus the mean score of the two correct assignments and replay natural DuReader answers with weight 0.3. Compute exactly the same four scores in the control arm. In the contrast arm, add half the mean softplus of the sibling score minus the correct score divided by 0.35. Use eight passes, 24 pairs per batch and 12 replay records per step. Use identical shuffled examples and replay order, gradient-norm limit one, AdamW, 60-step warmup and cosine decay from 0.00045 to 0.0001. Use the final weights only. Each fit has a 30-minute and 12 GiB allocation ceiling on GPU 0.

Write from a blank start for each distinct held-out question, with a maximum of 64 cells and a separate stop decision. Credit any annotated bitmap alternative exactly reproduced and stopped. The primary reading outcome is the number of pairs for which the model generates both requested answers. Compare the two adapted arms and their parent. Recount archived single-question images from the existing released span reader as a task-specific benchmark. Also evaluate a single white cell in place of the question, individual and joint candidate preferences, early endings and extra or wrong content. Use a paired bootstrap over source passages, 10,000 draws. Report distinct questions and repeated pair memberships.

A contrast-specific reading benefit requires a positive lower 95% passage-clustered bound against likelihood-only training for freely written pairs. An improvement limited to provided-answer scores counts as recognition. Keep the 571-case natural DuReader composition outcome and the 300 input-absent subgroup as a separate transfer test; retain the existing 15-of-300 minimum for a writing claim. Preserve the released reader unless a candidate demonstrates a practical advantage. If paired recognition improves without corresponding generation, locate the divergence between generation and candidate scoring on stored outputs before changing the network. Do not use held-out answer choices to pick an autonomous response. All weights, source documents and raw benchmark outputs stay in local research storage.
