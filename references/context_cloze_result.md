# What four neighbouring word images can predict

The image model supplied **that** between **strong enough** and **he could**, a combination of four flanking words absent from its sampled training windows. A same-exposure count predictor chose **to**. Across 50,012 held passage positions, however, the count predictor was more accurate. The neural writer recovered 4,863 words with both sides visible, 3,111 with only its left neighbours and 1,010 after exchanging the neighbourhood for one from a different story. All 50,012 interior word positions in 147 reserved English stories count, including 17,408 whose target lies outside the 2,048 writable prototypes. The neural network and count backoff use the same 256,000 passage draws. Their paired accuracy difference was −3.70 percentage points (95% story-bootstrap interval −4.07 to −3.34). Among 49,635 four-flank combinations absent from those draws, the neural writer recovered 4,808 centres and backoff recovered 6,608. The two predictors made different errors. The neural model alone was correct 1,768 times, and backoff alone was correct 3,620 times. These figures motivate a fresh test of learned interpolation; choosing between predictions with knowledge of the reference would give an oracle score. ## Does the pictured question help?

We fitted relative left and right image features and directly classified physically missing answer words against the full fitting inventory. The conditional endpoint passed. Among 115 stored but source-absent answer words, the passage-trained model placed 16 first when given the other answer words. Its parent placed five first, the QA-only arm 12, and the empirical missing-word prior zero. Mean reciprocal rank was 0.219 against 0.116 for the parent and 0.052 for the prior. The paired differences were +0.103 (95% interval +0.045 to +0.163) and +0.167 (+0.111 to +0.227), respectively. Passage training added +0.046 (+0.012 to +0.083) relative to identical QA supervision alone. Exchanging the question and history reduced MRR to 0.115; exchanging the passage gave 0.199, with an interval for the change spanning zero. Hiding all the surrounding answer words gave 0.164. [All ranks and paired comparisons](evidence/context_cloze_v1/results.json)

Once source copying and spelling also competed, the visual mixture emitted just 3/115 stored absent words, against 1/115 for the parent. In free generation it matched 484 of 2,234 original answers, 32 after question/history exchange, and 272 after passage exchange. The parent matched 479 on the original questions. Both matched **one of 265** difficult references. That answer, **he was altogether mistaken**, lost credit under both input exchanges. The new writer also produced **She Austen is now** for **she is the author of many books**. Difficult word F1 improved by 0.14 percentage points (95% interval −0.78 to +1.00). The autonomous criterion failed. The test uses one human reference per question and one fitting seed. ## What happens when the writer reads its own output?

For the first held prefix, **Nina, the**, the model wrote **song of the song of the song of**. Across 147 fixed eight-word tests it matched 50/1,176 words, versus 19 after prefix exchange and 70 with true two-word history restored at each position. It matched zero complete reference continuations. The 147 prompts reached only 36 distinct two-word states after two emissions. A deterministic local writer continuing from those states can match at most 36 of the 147 remaining literal six-word suffixes. The actual writer matched zero. Lean establishes the exact empirical ceiling under a state-only decoding assumption. It does not require a reference to be the only linguistically valid continuation. ## Computation and next comparison

Each of the four fits saw 128,000 QA positions from 45,505 distinct turns. The passage arms also saw 230,157 distinct centres in 256,000 draws from 1,181,413 eligible fitting-passage positions. On one RTX 4090 D, the passage-assisted image fit used 33.1 seconds, 3.81 kJ and 366 MB peak allocated GPU memory. It updated 322,833 parameters of 896,983. The visual and byte checkpoints occupy 4,712,036 and 4,700,606 bytes. The complete recorded ancestral preparation and fitting totals are 35.44 kJ for images and 34.04 kJ for bytes. These are GPU measurements and omit offline corpus assembly and host power. No matched-quality advantage over token language models has been established. [Fit records](../models/context_cloze) · [Image-path checks](evidence/context_cloze_v1/image_paths.json)

The forward backoff predictor recovered 3,294 words the neural predictor missed, while the neural model alone recovered 845. A useful next experiment is a causal visual writer that retains the passage prefix beyond its last two outputs and learns when to use longer matching sequences of ink. Compare with count backoff and a neural-only writer on source-disjoint books, including Chinese texts. Keep the paired byte control, free continuation and pictured-question evaluations. The measured English cloze gain alone cannot establish general language or historical Chinese reading.

The windowed task connects to distributed neural language modelling and masked prediction of rendered text. Our lexical output route carries a finite bank of full-word images. Its representation and coverage differ from patch reconstruction.
