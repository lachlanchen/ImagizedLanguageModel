# Learning to compose a short title from a pictured article

Adding ink planes without depth labels discards order. Two layers A,B and B,A have exactly the same sum. If both orders have equal probability, recovering their order from the sum alone has probability at most one half. A reversible reshape preserves order but supplies no new information. Store an ordered visual memory and change how a language decoder accesses it.

A binary pixel trained by squared error has conditional mean E[Y|X] as its optimum. If several next characters are plausible their average need not resemble any one of them. Independent pixel likelihood has a related problem: its joint mode may combine strokes from different plausible characters. This is a mechanism that can produce illegible writing. The frozen experiments alone cannot establish it as the unique cause of failure.

Test a small recurrent decoder on the [pictured article-title corpus](evidence/visual_title_composition_20261118.json). Train it to continue along a source span, move attention to another source position, draw a physical tile, or stop. The choice of a new source position depends on the full pictured memory and emitted history. Sum the probability over all source positions with the same physical tile. When a training glyph occurs several times, retain the posterior over its possible source positions. Continuing shifts this posterior by one position. A source-absent training target supervises the drawing head, whose 1,024 binary outputs can represent a new bitmap. The training route uses equality of ink; no learned glyph inventory or font renderer is used at output. The fixed title instruction limits this experiment to a single task.

This extends the pointer and generator construction of [See et al.](https://aclanthology.org/P17-1099/) to physical pictures. [PIXAR](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.874/) had already demonstrated autoregressive language modelling in pixel space and studied noisy output. The experimental question is how much correct image composition survives free decoding on a single device.

Use all 2,362 training articles, twelve shuffled passes, seed 417, AdamW at 0.002, a 64-dimensional state, batch size 16 and a 32-cell inference limit. Keep every final output on the 834 development articles. Compare normal decoding with a contiguous decoder using the same trained scores and stopping rule, and with intact decoding on a cyclically exchanged passage. Score full physical title and natural stop. Report each of the 724 contiguous, 46 distributed and 64 missing-glyph targets separately, along with the 34 titles containing a previously unseen training glyph. Count drawing and stopping decisions. Obtain paired 95% intervals by article. The composition criterion is at least five exact distributed titles, a positive lower confidence bound against the contiguous control on that group, and at least one exact missing-glyph title. Failure locates the missing capability; preserve all arms and the final weights. Use one GPU, at most two CPU threads, 8 GiB device allocation, 4 GiB host process RSS and 25 minutes. Do not consult development targets during fitting or change the thresholds after observing results.
