# From historical glyph collection to independent visual reading

The project asks whether a consumer GPU can train a model that reads a pictured question and book page, then writes the answer as an image. A user can type a modern prompt and have it rasterized, or supply the original ink of a symbol without a digital text encoding. The longer-term test is a page of a word-origin book: an informative modern answer in Chinese or English, with the right historical specimens and evidence for the account.

| Work requested | Available evidence and scope |
|---|---|
| Read Hanziyuan results, log errors, skip earlier downloads, keep original forms | The web UI, single-character CLI and resumable bulk CLI exist. The retained collection covers 9,055 queried headwords. A recorded absence survives a rebuild. The separate normalized library preserves alternate entries and every verified SVG from this snapshot. |
| Use public language resources and the supplied reference books | Training and evaluation use questions with human answers, including narratives and Chinese exams. The private book index covers eleven volumes. Page rendering and catalogue data access are separate; page-level annotations linking specimens to historical claims remain a further task. |
| Train and run an independent image-in/image-out model on one 4090 | Released weights take aligned glyph images, attend to source and generated history, write pixels and stop without a text teacher. Previous continuation-only training failed its language test. Later readers produced some complete answers, and balanced historical training generalized specimen selection to new families under familiar questions. |
| Use mathematics to guide the model, quantify generalization and costs | Lean proofs and paired interventions distinguish representability from selection. Unchanged-output predictions have a sharp ceiling on two distinct period requests. A separate printed-label baseline and paraphrase test limit any interpretation as semantic understanding. |
| Study MiniMind and compare with mature models | The exact upstream checkout, official full-SFT weights, real training-row traces and a matched 416-question comparison are available. Earlier quantized Qwen comparisons and current two-order semantic grading expose a substantial general-reading gap. |
| Keep an illustrated scientific manuscript, source and multilingual documentation | The Nature-style reading PDF, cumulative TeX/PDF, original output images and editable experimental figures are in the publication tree. The root README and language editions cover English, simplified and traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Arabic, French and Spanish. The Nutstore delivery contains the reading edition; additional scientific assets stay in the repository. |

The strongest result is narrow and reproducible. The balanced reader answered both requests correctly in 508 of 512 paired interventions on unseen historical families. On a separate paraphrase test it scored 261/512, below a direct label matcher. A question asking for an explanation of 言 produced one glyph. General reading, explaining an origin from independently scanned pages, generating unseen stroke outlines, reliable free continuation and better training efficiency at the same quality as a strong LLM remain research goals. The available measurements establish neither broad Qwen-8B parity nor a 10×, 100× or 1,000× capability advantage.
