# Reading the same Chinese question across typefaces

The distilled image writer answered 72 of 192 questions in regular Chinese print and zero with the same inputs in Kai. Replacing either input alone recovered at most one of the 72 previous successes. Test whether a shared visual mapping can restore reading while keeping each original source image available to the decoder.

Start both writers from the terminal interval-distilled model. Retain its 26,381 examples and add one alternate printing of each eligible question in its 6,110 paired training rows. Cycle Noto Bold, UKai and Noto Serif by passage. Use the source typeface for the answer. Exclude missing glyphs before constructing either arm. All other rows retain their original pixels. Use the existing single-epoch replay optimizer, seed 241204, same ordering and updates. The comparison arm reads the new images through its existing frozen visual interface. The candidate additionally learns a glyph correspondence from the four training fonts, using only identities present in those paired training materials and the writer's existing bitmap inventory. Train the correspondence model for 1,200 batches of 192 with translated images, using cosine scores against learned visual prototypes. Accept a mapping only when the top cosine is at least 0.65 with a margin of 0.06. Preserve out-of-inventory physical IDs for copying and stopping. Limit each GPU fit to 20 minutes and 20 GiB on GPU 0. Report all incremental costs.

Exclude the entire HanaMin family from learning and selection. Evaluate final weights on every eligible pair of the 98 CMRC development passages in regular print, bold, UKai and HanaMin, including question erasure on HanaMin. Score independently in either the canonical or incoming style at each cell, and report strict incoming and strict canonical outputs. Compare to both the unadapted parent and matched alternate-typeface replay. Report exact held glyph identity and abstention on development inscriptions. Recount complete historical replies, the known 言 orders, 359 earlier human answers and 571 open questions. Preserve all previous checkpoints. To select the candidate for cross-font use require more complete held-family pairs than both controls, at least 11/98 regular pairs, both orders of 言 and at least 190/192 familiar historical replies. Report any regressions. Holding out a font family in the current experiment establishes no guarantee about pretraining provenance. If the gate fails, locate the failure separately in visual identification, conservative abstention and downstream response generation.
