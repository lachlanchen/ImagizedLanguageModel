Train eight epochs using the same seeded batches, then apply a fixed probability threshold of one half. Only passage and question images enter inference. Preserve the exact direct extract and pointing controls, count complete question pairs, and report both source-absent cohorts. Require at least 90% of the direct reader's correct pairs on each natural source and at least 90% of the genuine writer's correct held-out familiar captions. Report unfamiliar wording separately.
