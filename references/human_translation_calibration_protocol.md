# Can local graders recognise a professionally annotated meaning error?

A model-based bilingual audit gave different decisions after swapping answers. On corpus-derived pairs, at least one ballot changed for 51/77 pairs of supplied translations and 36/77 pairs of free visual answers. The corresponding counts were 3/51 and 5/51 for constructed examples. These are family-level counts across four requests. An uncertified pair can reflect an ambiguous source, a poor translation or a grading error.

Use the Chinese-to-English WMT 2020 professional MQM annotations. Aggregate the annotations for identical source and translation after removing the error-span delimiters. Select every segment with two distinct translations of the same sentence where at least three named raters unanimously annotated one translation as error-free and every rater independently marked at least one major accuracy error on the other. A rater may contribute multiple error tags. Choose one fixed pair per segment by SHA-256 order, with decisions made before consulting either model. Preserve the segment, document, exact text hashes, rater identities and categories. Grade only the sentence provided to the model. The original raters could use document context. These cases favour clear differences and cover news translated into English.

Apply both local models using exactly the corpus-qualification prompt, once in each candidate order. Also assess each translation alone to test whether removing the competitor improves assessment. For each format report acceptance of the human-approved translation, rejection of the major error and joint correctness. Report position sensitivity and how often the two judges agree with one another while both miss the human result. Use a paired document bootstrap for the joint-rate difference between independent and comparative grading. A positive lower 95% bound would support an independent-candidate grading trial on fresh bilingual corpus examples. Otherwise preserve the comparative protocol and use the measured error rates to qualify interpretation. Neither outcome alters preceding labels, validates Chinese targets or qualifies the proposed student training cohort.

The source repository is pinned at 29acd6999aaea3586378c9dcb58d8cc4820cede3. The original human study used professional translators with access to document context. [MQM annotations](https://github.com/google/wmt-mqm-human-evaluation/tree/29acd6999aaea3586378c9dcb58d8cc4820cede3/newstest2020/zhen) · [Freitag and colleagues, 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.87/)

On the same fixed panel, also measure the deployed visual writer directly on CPU. Retain every pictured Chinese source that the existing input repertoire and 96-cell interface can express. Archive its freely stopped English output and the physical source. For the subset with two complete representable English candidates, compare mean stopped glyph log probability under the original source and under the next source in fixed segment order. Recompute the phrase draft from each source, giving a whole-system source intervention. Count all exclusions, ties, exact outputs and paired wins and reversals. These annotations apply to the two pre-existing translations. They do not automatically credit the writer's independently generated answer.
