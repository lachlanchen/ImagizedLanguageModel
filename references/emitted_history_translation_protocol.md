# Can a visual writer recover the intended sentence after reading its own ink?

The independent glyph writer produced five complete translations on the preceding 192 book requests. This comparison changes the history on which it learns to continue. Both arms start from the same guided visual writer and use all training pairs in both directions. No pretrained translator participates in either forward pass. The frozen parent writes a short prefix for each training source. Its length is at most one quarter, one half or three quarters of the reference length (cycling by training row), capped at 32 glyphs. A stop shortens the observed prefix. Compare its physical glyphs with every prefix of the reference by unit-cost edit distance. Choose the smallest distance, then the boundary nearest the observed length, then the earlier boundary. The suffix beyond that boundary, including stop, is shared by the two arms.

Both arms receive ordinary full-reference supervision plus equally weighted mean suffix likelihood. The control reads the true prefix at the boundary and the candidate reads the parent's actual pixels before learning the same suffix. A parent pixel must match a training palette entry exactly before caching. Report the fraction of different histories. The parent is frozen during collection. Four epochs, common length-bucketed batches, seed and learning-rate schedule, gradient norm limit one. Cap each fit at one hour and 12 GiB allocated on GPU 0. Use final weights.

Reserve the first four eligible distinct works in the registration script, 24 disjoint pairs per work, before any training. Score both directions. Archive every input, complete free response, stop and CPU replay. The primary judge sees the source and anonymous responses in two orders. Count complete meanings accepted in both orders with a real stop. Require a positive lower paired work-bootstrap bound against the matched control, no decline in either direction, and more credits than a same-direction source exchange. Report the starting writer, local Qwen3-8B and reference controls on the same inputs. A failure calls for inspecting source-conditioned proposition learning before allocating more epochs to this intervention.

Training on states induced by a learner has a basis in imitation learning. This fixed-parent experiment tests a particular edit-aligned suffix surrogate. Its success depends on whether the remaining reference suffix is still meaningful after the observed errors. [Ross, Gordon and Bagnell](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html)
