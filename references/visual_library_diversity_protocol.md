# Learning from more distinct pictured sentences at a fixed writing budget

The previous distilled writer learned its training wording and received three credits on 96 new translations in a masked source-only reading. Test whether a wider distribution of real bilingual propositions improves unassisted meaning transfer. Use the already reserved 28,342 aligned pairs from 21 literary works. The separate 240 development pairs and 480 test pairs come from two and four other works. Treat corpus translations as fallible.

Both arms start from the same prefix-recovery writer. Train on both directions of every library exposure plus the same legacy rehearsal stream. The broad arm sees every training pair once per pass. Construct the repetition control with a fixed seeded, bounded random reweighting on each work's bipartite graph of English and Chinese lengths. Each pair begins with weight one. Alternately increase and decrease weights around a cycle until one reaches zero or eight. Choose the two possible update signs in proportion to the distance to the opposite boundary. Preserve the entire histogram of both languages' lengths within each work. The control reuses each retained pair according to its final weight. Match target length and domain in each training slot, and account for both input and output glyph totals. The joint distribution of sentence lengths can change. Fix four passes, the same per-pass batch schedule, and the same learning rate decaying from 0.00016 to 0.00004. Use final weights. Record intervening development behavior without selecting a checkpoint. Cap each fit at one device-hour and 12 GiB allocated on GPU0.

Evaluate both models and the unchanged starting model by independently writing in both directions on all 480 reserved pairs. Count exact stopped images and stopped normalized edit similarity. Assess whole meanings on the first 12 pairs in each reserved work in the stored pretraining order (96 requests). Compare quantized text Qwen3-8B on precisely those sources. Score source-only and reference-informed candidate readings in both orders, with blinded candidate labels, a true reference and an unrelated foil. Report the two assessments separately, per-work counts and paired work bootstrap intervals. Count all eight stopped role-preserving answers for each of the 118 canonical grammatical families. Promotion requires positive lower 95% bounds on source-only meaning gains over both the narrow and starting writer, agreement on the sign under reference-informed grading, and at least 110 complete role families. Report failures and reference mistakes. Preserve original input and output images. Four held works give limited precision. If breadth alone fails, locate whether conditioning on the individual pictured source changes the writer's distribution before another increase in training volume.
