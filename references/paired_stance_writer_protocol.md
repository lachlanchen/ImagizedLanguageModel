# Writing a position and its supporting quotation from opposed evidence

The ordered question writer completed zero of 36 natural stance responses before adaptation. Each question in this comparison has a human affirmative and negative answer in different pictured passages. Each reference-assisted window contains the human quotation literally. The two prescribed prefixes are 肯定。 and 否定。. Both passages share the same pictured instruction and question. The output comprises the position, the unaltered human quotation and a learned stop.

Of the 949 available training pairs, hold out 144 for validation and fit to 805. Group identical normalized questions, full evidence windows and shared normalized 32-character runs across windows before partitioning. Keep the 18 official development pairs intact. All 144 validation records occur in the inherited answer training corpus. The official 18 share no source record with that corpus. The validation split measures transfer of the new supervision to other questions. The official sample provides a small independent estimate. All figures concern writing given source evidence.

Initialize both arms from the same ordered 694,152-parameter visual writer. Keep the ink encoder and writing keys fixed. Optimize the other weights, including passage reading, recurrent state, attention and stopping. Both writers receive every human quotation and both passages for every question every epoch. In the reassigned control, reverse the two stance prefixes for a pseudorandom half of the pairs in each epoch and complement the assignments the following epoch. Every control passage receives each prefix eight times while its human quotation remains correct. The matched arm always receives the annotated position.

For each response minimize the mean negative log probability of the first three bitmaps plus the mean over its quotation and stop. Add one quarter of the sequence-normalized image loss on 16 mixed English and Chinese whole dictionary responses per update to measure retention of an existing skill. Use 24 question pairs per batch, 16 epochs, matched row order and AdamW with 30 updates of warm-up and cosine decay from 0.0004 to 0.00008. Use only the final checkpoint, with two CPU threads and a single RTX 4090 D. Each arm has an eight GiB allocated memory ceiling and a 20-minute fit ceiling. Report incremental costs separately from inherited training.

Generate up to 64 glyphs from an empty answer, with the stop decision also evaluated after the last cell. Save every image and stop flag. Score exact three-glyph stance prefixes, both passages correct, complete stopped human quotations after either well-formed stance, and complete stopped answers. Report correct first quoted glyphs, literal source-span quotation, and erased-question and erased-passage results. Count the passage-marker baseline and the first and midpoint containing sentence with terminal punctuation as simple controls for the reference-assisted window selection. Evaluate preserved dictionary prompts, ordinary DRCD and off-task DuReader composition.

Credit evidence-specific stance learning if matched training exceeds both the marker and reassigned controls on both-passages-correct validation with paired question-bootstrap 95% intervals above zero, and both comparisons have a positive difference on the official cohort. Report exact whole-image answers separately, requiring a positive gain over the control and at least one such answer on official data before crediting improvement in this task's complete writing. If these criteria fail, inspect saved output errors and select a change tied to the observed failure. A correct fixed stance prefix or copied quotation alone cannot establish free writing.
