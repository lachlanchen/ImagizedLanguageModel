# What a source-only visual translator learned

The classical source 冬，十月，吴少诚引兵还蔡州。 produced 冬季，十月，吴少诚领兵返回蔡州。 A phrase composer learned these changes from 32,768 pictured source–gloss pairs. Neither full sentence appeared in its training set. On 如期以火山令卒。 the same model produced 如期用火山令世。 It retained the official title and lost the verb of dying. Both outputs are ordinary predictions from the released image-to-image runtime. The larger checkpoint generated 冬季，公孙归父到齐国。 from 冬，公孙归父如齐。 Its two changed exact targets were absent from training. The paired 95% chapter-bootstrap interval for its change in glyph error over the smaller checkpoint was −0.32 to +0.19 percentage points. An extra 23,104 parallel examples did not establish a consistent improvement. The 24-case readings use one study-involved model reader, with identical outputs deduplicated, method identity concealed and readings frozen before unmasking. Exact reference agreement penalizes valid paraphrases. A fluent translation also has to preserve names, roles, actions and qualifiers. ## What constrains learning

Permuting the glyph layers of a volume leaves their unweighted sum unchanged. For a stream of distinct glyphs, many different sentences therefore have exactly the same sum image. Once this order is discarded, additional training cannot recover it uniquely. Reversible tiling or a position-dependent encoder retains the information. The source-order intervention here changed edit error by 3.41 percentage points on the same complete passages. The neural writer used that information and still produced frequent filler. Its supervised local decisions do not ensure that the combined sentence states the original event.

The composer chooses a segmentation of source ink and joins attested visual target phrases. The score adds log conditional phrase probability and a clipped log probability ratio at each join. This ratio measures whether two adjacent output images occur together more often than their frequencies alone would predict. It supplies local fluency, while the bilingual counts supply source correspondence. It cannot decide by itself whether a clause preserves negation or refers to the right person. Phrase translation, fertility-based neural translation and prior pixel-generative models motivate these comparisons. The empirical contribution here is the same-source control and full physical output with a measured semantic reading. [Koehn, Och and Marcu](https://aclanthology.org/N03-1017/) · [Gu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.02281) · [Tai et al., PIXAR](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.874/)

The phrase composer accepts segmented 32 × 32 source images, emits observed shapes and copies unfamiliar source ink. This supports closed-inventory composition and open copying. The open neural writer can draw outside that inventory, but its ordinary outputs on the first cohort all coincided with known bitmaps. Neither result establishes free-form question answering, handwriting invariance, independent book writing or a compute advantage at matched language quality.

## Data and replication

The neural fits used eight passes over identical shuffled rows and the same frozen motor. Aligning the training archive changed 46 of their targets. The difficult 64-pair calibration set identifies concrete mismatches but is unrepresentative. Of the two substitutions in 5,194 held-out rows, blind grading favored both. The neural evaluation cohort contained neither substitution. The first two evaluation cohorts share 105 chapters with each other and have disjoint training chapters. The final size test excludes the alignment review. The next experiment should locate persistent errors in names, negation and predicate–argument relations. Keep the source and the phrase inventory fixed and measure whether a faithful complete clause survives among several proposed image streams before training a reranker. If it is often missing, change the proposals or supervision. If it survives, train an image-conditioned clause scorer on distinct chapters and compare the resulting full answers with this composer's original pixels. A held-out reading of whole answers is necessary to advance the semantic claim.
