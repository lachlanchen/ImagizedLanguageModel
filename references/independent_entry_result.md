# Learning to read a differently worded dictionary question

Asked what 明 originally meant and how it appeared in bronze inscriptions, the writer gave *bright* and Hanziyuan B10488 in one sentence image. A separately written request for 编 began with 老师您好. The same writer answered a neighbouring entry, giving *lǎo* and the oracle form of 老. Removing 老师 restored the correct headword and historical image but still gave *biān* in place of *to knit*. The greeting influenced which entry was selected in this example. The factual error remained after the greeting changed. ## Does additional training explain the gain?

The shared writer learned on 89,200 examples per epoch, including 44,600 additional phrasings. Its predecessor had received fewer updates. A subsequent control therefore matched all 713,600 target presentations, their exact batch positions, the initial weights and all 5,576 optimizer steps, while repeating the original questions. The additional wording raised whole-answer accuracy on the independent author from 18/96 to 66/96. The paired difference was 50.0 percentage points, with a 95% bootstrap interval of 42.7–56.3 points when resampling headwords within the six stage combinations. The interval describes this sample of cards and this author. Changing the candidate-specific update reduced the score to 52 under matched data; lower training loss did not predict the better autonomous writer. The post hoc replay was registered before its fit. [Replay definition](evidence/independent_entry_20261119/replay_protocol.json) · [Recount and interval](evidence/independent_entry_20261119/replay_recount.json)

The two primary fits ran on one RTX 4090 D for 30.30 and 30.60 seconds after 6.41 seconds of shared tensor preparation. Peak allocations were 155.82 and 160.91 MiB. These figures exclude the earlier checkpoint, corpus construction and evaluation. Local Qwen3-8B Q8 generated the test questions in 96 independent calls (57.12 seconds summed request durations). It received the headword and intended request but no answer values, images, examples or previous outputs. Each authored request was read for intent before scoring. Four formulations conflated original meaning with its expression in a particular historical form. Retaining all 96 gives the primary result. Among the other 92, the shared writer scored 62, and it scored 37/48 on direct requests versus 29/48 on requests commissioned with distractors. Independent model authorship provides a controlled wording change. Interpreting general human questions remains a separate requirement.
