# Does the second entry interfere with historical-form selection?

The trained stream writer opens every standard answer with the correct headword but supplies the other entry’s form for the requested stage 568 times. Freeze the final paired checkpoint and evaluate all 1,136 standard questions under four card layouts. Reproduce the original saved streams exactly. Exchange the order of the two intact records, present only the requested record, or present only its distractor. Keep the question, decoding rule, expected answer and historical rasters fixed. The two cropped conditions use the known record identity and therefore measure the effect of supplying the location. Record whole-answer and terminal-form accuracy, gained and lost exact responses and identical images. Run one inference owner for at most five minutes on GPU 0. If isolating the correct source recovers writing, test learned selection of visible records on untouched families in a subsequent comparison. Otherwise examine factual-field selection and spelling within the isolated record.
