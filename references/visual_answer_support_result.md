# What must an image writer be able to express?

A writer can learn the correct proposition and still lack a way to emit its reference image. Conversely, a writer that can spell every needed character may choose entirely wrong words. We measured these two constraints before adding instruction data to the visual language model.

## Learning the words around a fact

The factual reader experiment found 94 failed English statements whose surrounding text must change even when the writer receives the correct fact. We checked every one of the 101,364 reference wordings available to the original writer for the corresponding training signal. Each comparison used the ordered bitmap sequence. A fact had to match at exactly one offset to contribute to the surrounding-text count. Overlapping matches were included. [English failure examples](fact_span_ranking_result.md) · [Counts by training source](evidence/visual_answer_support_v1/results.json)

In 43,779 of 53,011 English statements, the fact appears once and the answer includes other text. Those answers provide 2,258,954 target glyphs outside the fact. In 33,576 statements there is writing on both sides. Another 9,060 omit the literal fact, 136 use it more than once, and 36 consist only of the fact. Treating every English answer as one literal value in one slot would exclude real human phrasings. All 12,101 Chinese exam training answers equal the supplied value. The separate Chinese catalogue has 3,911 wordings with context around a unique value. Chinese exam extraction therefore gives little direct supervision for composing an explanation.

Lean characterizes the English repair diagnostic exactly. A single replacement can yield the annotated reference at its designated fact location if and only if the emitted image already contains its required left and right contexts at the two ends and they do not overlap. The proof constructs the intervening substring for the reverse implication. The exhaustive small-alphabet check independently enumerates replacements, including insertions and adjacent boundary cases. The bound allows access to the reference and a chosen occurrence. [Checked proof](../formal/span_list/VisualAnswerSupport.lean) · [Executable test](../tests/test_fact_ranking.py)

## Complete instructions and an output budget

We examined all 115,831 local English and Chinese Alpaca and English Dolly records. There were 115,803 nonempty responses with 30,559,591 reference cells in total. This is an inventory measurement. None of these records contributed a gradient in the span-scoring experiment. Dolly contains human-written instruction/response records. The English Alpaca release uses instruction-generated supervision. Here the Chinese file is identified by its local content digest. [Dolly dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/databricks/databricks-dolly-15k) · [Stanford Alpaca](https://github.com/tatsu-lab/stanford_alpaca) · [Input and result digests](evidence/visual_answer_support_v1/results.json)

In the deployed writer each decision emits a single bitmap or stops. A complete response with a budget of 256 decisions can use at most 255 glyph cells. At every position it can spell from its output inventory or copy visible input. Lean proves both necessary conditions for all finite generation traces of this form. We gave the coverage calculation access to the **entire** instruction and context for copying. The deployed factual writer sees a narrower selected region. Missing font coverage in any required input or reference is counted as unsupported. Each Unicode scalar occupies one cell with this renderer; line breaks become whitespace. Page layout, image differences between fonts, factual adequacy and word order are outside this calculation. [Formal support bound](../formal/span_list/VisualAnswerSupport.lean) · [Measurement code](../scripts/measure_visual_answer_support.py)

We fitted a second catalogue by collecting distinct renderable output bitmaps from 92,585 nonempty training responses. An 80/20 deterministic split grouped identical normalized instructions across files. No held response supplied a shape. The catalogue adds 2,015 drawings to the original 4,331 and occupies 812,544 raw bytes including two reserved entries. Near-duplicates or translations with different instruction strings may cross the split. This split measures glyph coverage and is unsuitable as proof of semantic independence. [Training-only catalogue construction and held results](evidence/visual_answer_support_v1/training-inventory.json)

They leave the selection of the right glyph sequence entirely to the model. Regenerate the catalogue and counts with `python -m scripts.measure_visual_answer_support` followed by `python -m scripts.measure_expanded_ink_inventory`. ## Next writer comparison

Keep the trained span reader and existing writer as frozen end-to-end baselines. Give two new writers the same pictured questions, facts, full reference answers, open-input copy operation, fitted training-only inventory and output budget. Score every full answer in both arms, including ones that rephrase the fact or omit it. Train the comparison writer with an auxiliary objective predicting the ordered text on **both** sides of a uniquely aligned fact from the question and its pictured evidence. Learn when and where to place the fact, and retain unrestricted drawing for the remaining cases. The matched control receives extra full-answer supervision on the same examples and the same number of scored positions. Count the cost of both objectives. Fixed typed field labels from the restaurant task are unavailable on natural questions. [Prior limited role substitution result](variable_ink_result.md)

Reserve previously unseen human passages, changed-source pairs and long human instruction answers before either fit. Use the completed fourth cohort for development only. Remove held passages, questions and references from **all** training stages. Compare with the correct evidence supplied and with the reader's own choice. A forward step must improve autonomous whole-answer results on independent English and Chinese questions against both controls while preserving correct responses to changed evidence. Account for every long answer, including those outside a fixed compute budget, and separately measure conversational, book and historical transfer. A smaller conditional language loss or improved optical coverage alone cannot promote a writer. Existing pixel language models provide architectural comparators for the longer-term general generation test.
