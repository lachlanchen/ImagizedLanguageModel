# Transfer between conversational and editorial translation

The fine visual translator earned four semantic credits on 64 conversational sentences and none on 16 contrasts. Test the effect of greater diversity in bilingual supervision. Starting at its selected stride-one checkpoint, continue twice. Mix 25,000 ordinary bilingual pairs with up to 25,000 new short News Commentary v18 pairs per pass. For the control replace the news by the same number of further conversational draws. Keep the existing optical display inventory, both directions, optimizer schedule and four passes. Report actual target exposures because news sentences can be longer. Use GPU 0 serially, two CPU threads, 18 GiB CUDA allocation and three hours per fit. Preserve the fixed final checkpoint of each arm. Compare freely stopped outputs on new article groups and on conversational sources, including conversational families outside the first semantic evaluation. Use normalized bilingual sentence deduplication, article-title group splitting and exclusion of all exact normalized matches to the original corpus in either language. Report excluded long and unsupported rows. Grade sixteen new conversational families and sixteen new article groups in both directions in two orders, with a calibrated local 8B judge, the starting writer, both continued writers, a local quantized text comparator, a reference and a foil. A broader semantic improvement requires a gain over matched replay in both domains; improvements in letter overlap alone do not qualify. If this comparison fails, retain the domain-specific measurements and prioritize supervision of relations rather than extending this run.
