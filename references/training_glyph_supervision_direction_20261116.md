# Establish visual content supervision before another language fit

The original actor learns conditional glyph associations without reliably
answering questions or continuing books. Later correct-history QA writing
improves, while first images and autonomous answers fail. Training books also
fail with correct preceding images. Neither accumulated feedback alone nor
blank probability alone explains this gap. The latest model contains 7.553B
parameters, including 7.522B inherited parameters. Its comparison trains 5.006M
continuation/query/writer parameters with the native prompt/body frozen. This
attempts to transfer language through a new visual interface; it is not a
small foundation trained to understand language from scratch.

The [isolated reader result](isolated_glyph_reader_result_20261116.md) rejects
another unsafe assumption: a general vision teacher can confidently misidentify
clean source glyphs. Optimizing that feedback would not establish language
learning. Both earlier geometric rewards remain rejected. No qualified
whole-answer reward exists, and the RLOO helper is not an executed learning result.

## Next bounded investigation

Audit the saved request/image path and installed model preprocessing using
source and retained metadata. The independent 402-request hash recount already
passes. Inspect remaining internal transformations without new neural calls;
do not resume either interrupted reader or tune its prompt on development
answers. This distinguishes a transport/resolution defect from an unsupported
recognition assumption.

Next test whether authenticated **training-only** source strings can label
every actual corpus cell without omission, source-text mismatch or conflicting
physical identities. Prospectively bind original group splits, font, renderer,
raw hashes and complete records. Different canonical characters in an identical
mask make context-free identification impossible; report such aliases rather
than merging them. Leave missing characters unsupported. Add no development
reference or reserved confirmation image to the label bank. This proposed
coverage check has not run and admits no model fit.

If the image path is sound and labels are complete, a compact supervised visual
verifier is a candidate training component. Its image features would learn
from authenticated training glyphs and bound corruptions, with abstention.
Unicode labels are permitted **during training**; the deployed ILM still emits
physical pixels without a lexical output head, font lookup or external reader.
A small verifier could share the single-GPU budget, but actual footprint and
robustness must be measured. A classifier does not itself solve visual language.
The failed Hamming prototype bank and page OCR are preserved controls.

Freeze any verifier's architecture, labels, corruption law, optimization and
abstention before training/calibration. Old development outputs must not become
fitting or threshold-selection data. Qualify it on the complete preserved
cohort, retaining uncertainty, wrong entities, duplicates, whitespace and caps.
A failed qualification stops that candidate; source coverage alone never
licenses student optimization.

Only a qualified fixed signal permits a separately registered fresh-clone actor
preflight: actual emitted-pixel rollouts, complete-action probability replay,
differing complete-answer rewards, finite context/writer gradients, real
update/reset and measured single-GPU cost. Retain supervised image likelihood
as an anchor. Uniformly unreadable samples or equal rewards supply no usable
policy-gradient evidence. Books need their own continuation criterion; canonical
QA matching cannot grade every legitimate continuation.

The eventual intervention remains complete prompt-to-answer learning on the
model's own image history. A verifier, copying, lower loss or readable isolated
glyphs cannot complete that objective. Preserve complete answers, paired
questions, erasures, learned stopping, long books and untouched confirmation.
No new student preflight, full fit or verifier training is registered here.
