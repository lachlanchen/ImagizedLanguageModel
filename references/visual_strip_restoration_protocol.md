# Source-conditioned restoration of pictured sentences

The previous independent writer recovered seven of 192 book sentences even when given the correct unordered strips. We test whether learning position and content together improves free bilingual writing. A second task measures order recovery with the exact strip inventory supplied.

Both writers initialize from the same released optical-strip body and learn on the same 268,342 paired passages for four further epochs in both directions. The encoder reads 32-pixel glyph cells. The decoder attends in both directions on a canvas of visual strips. Each writer predicts its canvas length from the source. Both predict the same selected positions. One sees masks at every selected position. The other sometimes sees the selected strips displaced within the same sentence. One third of training rows begin fully masked. One quarter train with the entire sequence selected for displacement in the second arm. The remaining rows mix masking and within-sentence permutation. Both include a source-only length loss. Each fit uses one GPU, at most 20 GiB allocated and three hours. Both retain the original physical glyph motor.

For free writing, predict a length of one to 96 strips from the source alone. Start with all positions masked. Make six confidence-based parallel refinement passes. Use its own predicted length for termination and record overflow at 128 physical glyphs. Compare displaced training, masking alone, the fixed causal parent and exchanging the source of the displaced writer. The primary outcome is complete meaning in the existing reference-hidden, dual-order local judge. Require a positive lower paired book-level 95% interval against masking alone, neither language direction worse, and more correct than both parent and source exchange. Report all outcomes for four unseen works with 24 pairs per work, plus transfer to the previously frozen OPUS panel. Controls include the actual reference and a mismatched answer. Cases and model fits are fixed before scoring. Model-based judgments have limited independence.

Separately supply the correct unordered strips, sort their visual addresses then shuffle with a fixed seed, and use maximum-weight one-to-one assignment under the predicted position scores. Report exact recovery on the book panel and the existing role reversal set including both orders for same-bag pairs. Exact supplied-content reconstruction measures order conditional on content. It cannot establish free translation. If free meaning fails, retain both fits and use observed length, duplication and assisted-order errors to choose the next intervention.
