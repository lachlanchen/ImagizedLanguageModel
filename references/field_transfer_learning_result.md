## Where the model uses the source

Both systems began with the same behaviour. Each fitted 802,240 parameters on four complete passes over 28,342 bilingual training pairs from 21 books. Training used 2,040 updates and 7,343,256 target glyph or stop exposures in exactly the same order. The regression and prediction fits took 24.5 and 21.6 seconds on one RTX 4090 D and each used less than 3.91 GiB peak allocated memory. Prior training, teacher preparation, evaluation and host costs are additional. Each restored system has 10,481,570 parameters plus 604,480 stored code scalars. The assessment set excludes prior training/assessment optical matches in either direction. The four works themselves were studied before. The free answer starts with a pictured input and returns neural pixels. The source encoder and both auxiliary readers, optical choices, stop vector and stroke generator remained frozen. ## Reading the same passage with a pretrained decoder

On exactly the same pictured requests, a second, source-only assessment gave 28 meanings for the prediction-trained optical field supplied to a pretrained Marian decoder and 4 for the independent writer. The paired four-work interval for the difference was +4.17 to +24.48 percentage points. For regression the counts were 13 and 4. Both hybrid improvements involved the same pictured passages and model judge, with a different set of competing answers from the primary assessment. The hybrid receives boundary states and attention mass. The independent writer has an additional image encoder and writes its own glyphs. The difference concerns these complete interfaces. General language competence, freeform historical writing and a matched-quality speedup remain open. [Secondary assessment](evidence/field_to_glyph_v1/readouts.json)

## Supervision of the first visible target stroke pattern

On 240 paired development passages a separate teacher probe pooled the probabilities of the top 32 target pieces by their first newly visible glyph at verified boundaries. All other mass was left unresolved. Literal matching aligned 3,181/3,191 English and 1,422/3,197 Chinese decisions. Mean resolved mass was 84.8% and 75.9% respectively on those aligned positions. On glyph decisions alone, the correct glyph was provably the teacher's most probable choice on 2,447/2,941 English and 540/1,344 Chinese positions, even if all unresolved mass belonged to the strongest rival. The student's corresponding counts were 2,202 and 410. These are conditional reference-prefix observations with a text-source teacher. Chinese coverage failed the prespecified one-half rule. Normalization of earlier punctuation, including full-width commas, breaks literal prefix equality even when the next Han glyph is unambiguous. Preserve the literal result and test the original target codec's surface offsets before using this supervision.
