# Learn to write from previously unseen source shapes on news and speech

The new source path covers unfamiliar ink, yet the 1.82-million-parameter writer was fitted only on its original glyph repertoire. Test whether human-reviewed translations from other years teach it to use the additional visual information. Use only Chinese-to-English WMT 2021, 2022, 2023 and TED professional annotation files. Select one complete translation per source with at least one no-error rating and no conflicting annotation on that wording. Prefer the greatest number of distinct approving raters, breaking ties by hash. Check length and output repertoire. Strip only the annotation marks. Exclude normalized source and target surfaces from the entire WMT 2020 Chinese news annotation file. Deduplicate normalized source and target surfaces across all four corpora. Assign complete documents to a 20% held group by a deterministic hash of source-document identity. Treat the 2020 calibration panel as previously inspected. Previous model pretraining may overlap public material.

Compare the existing broad writer with two matched fine-tunes. Both see the same real source positions and phrase hints from contiguous covered runs. On source shapes outside the original Chinese inventory, show the physical pooled ink to one arm and the mean Chinese inventory observation to the other at both training and inference. The decoder emits English glyphs with its original motor and stopping rule. Fit all writer parameters using stopped whole-answer likelihood for eight passes through all eligible training examples. Freeze the original marker and phrase dictionary. Use 16 fresh examples and 16 English-directed rehearsal examples per update with the same ordered schedule, initialization and AdamW parameters. Learning rates are 0.00010 for four epochs, then 0.00005 for four. Clip gradient norm at one. Hold back the candidate fits unless at least 500 distinct training sentences, 100 unfamiliar-ink training sentences and 30 unseen-document sentences with unfamiliar ink are available. Use one GPU with 16 GiB peak allocation and 30 minutes per arm. The rejected OPUS contrast batch contributes no new supervision.

Evaluate the first 96 hashed held sources within each of the familiar and unfamiliar groups, or all available if fewer. Save every complete source, reference and all independently generated PNGs, with exact replays. Grade the independent answers in isolation with the previously calibrated primary assessor. On identical sentences compare the ink-trained writer with the hidden-ink-trained writer and its own erased-input replay. Require a positive lower bound for both paired document-bootstrap differences on unfamiliar sources and retention of known-source complete meaning relative to the parent before attributing a transferable semantic advantage to this change. For the common effect of reviewed training, report each arm against the parent separately. Include a professionally no-error reference and a displaced reference on every case to measure grader behaviour. A single-model credit, training loss and extra admitted source characters each measure different properties.
