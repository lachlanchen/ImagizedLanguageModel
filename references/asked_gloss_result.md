# Locating the word a pictured question asks about

In 執大象 the new model drew **hold** when asked for the main verb and **image** when asked for the object. The whole passage and the entire question enter as pictures; the location is inferred. The 1,345,457-parameter system combines a frozen classical Chinese contextual gloss writer with a new question reader. Its output motor draws a stopped sequence in one English print face. [Run the weights](../models/asked_gloss/README.md) · [Original figure](../publication/ilm-nature/figures/Figure_120_asked_gloss.png)

## Learning a location from the answer

A fixed writer assigns each possible source cell a probability for the full reference image sequence, including its end. The question reader learns how much probability to place on each cell. Summing their products yields a differentiable answer likelihood. In Lean, transferring a positive amount of focus mass from one cell to another improves this quantity exactly when the second cell makes the reference more likely, for two competing locations with common-denominator probabilities. Equal emissions leave the location unidentifiable from that answer alone. We compare the resulting reader with a reader given the true cell during training and a control with exchanged answer images. [Proof](../formal/span_list/LatentGlossFocus.lean) · [Model and optimization](../scripts/train_asked_gloss.py)

Six works provided 19,067 unique annotated clauses. Evaluation used 522 clauses from four other works in Kyoto's pinned dev/test files and 17 excerpts from the *Dao De Jing*. The relevant two Buddhist texts are identified by the actual documents in the pinned files. Each selected clause has distinct references for its predicate and object. We removed shared target windows from evaluation and froze acceptable external readings before scoring. The initial answer-supervised reader answered both questions and located both words in 225/539 passages for the held simple Chinese prompt; location supervision also gave 225 and exchanged answers gave zero. A count baseline that received the question's role identity and backed off over neighbouring ink gave 230. Quoting the target character and taking its first occurrence gave 359, and supplying the exact position gave 363. The count system with true positions gave 414. ## When the order changes the request

“Translate the main verb in this line and ignore the direct object in this line” asks the opposite question after swapping the two grammatical terms. These two strings have identical multisets of three-glyph windows, including both ends. Each registered Chinese pair satisfies the same condition. A pooled encoder of these windows maps the two questions to the same representation in exact arithmetic. Lean verifies the permutation argument. The matched pooled model changed its focus probabilities by at most 3.0 × 10⁻⁷ on CPU. [Formal invariant](../formal/span_list/PooledQuestionOrder.lean) · [Training and held strings](../scripts/asked_gloss_questions.py)

We trained a bidirectional recurrent question encoder on the same clauses, phrasings, images and updates as the pooled control. The stringent comparison requires **eight correct position-and-image answers on the same clause**. Each language asks about both roles under both orders of the two instructions. Neither the pooled model nor the recurrent exchanged-answer control completed any of the 539 clauses. With free writing, the aligned recurrent model completed 234 clauses on all four Chinese contrasts, 232 on all four English contrasts and 230 across both languages. The single-phrase count baseline is given a role identity and does not parse the contrastive instructions. [All answers and locations](evidence/asked_gloss_v1/order_result.json)

Given an unfamiliar or unfinished free response, an optional decoder scores all 2,193 distinct training gloss images from the recurrent writer, including termination. It kept 2,614 of the 3,119 source-position streams unchanged, replaced 505 and recovered 44 accepted target-position readings, with zero observed losses. Cropped examples show a correction to *male* and an incorrect choice of *turn* for *female*. A valid spelling does not ensure the right contextual interpretation. The gloss bank contains physical sequences, takes 10,758 bytes, and excludes novel gloss strings. ## Which language claims survive?

Four subsequent everyday prompt pairs omitted the trained grammatical terms. The free model answered and grounded both questions on **0, 33, 0 and 2 of 539** clauses. Only 13 clauses placed the object before the verb; the model completed one for the positive-first Chinese contrast. There were no opposite-role matched pairs with three identical central glyphs under the registered pairing. The experiment supports order-sensitive task interpretation on the tested phrasings. It leaves general paraphrase understanding, long-range syntactic inference, unprompted historical explanation and matched-quality efficiency unresolved. An accepted gloss on the external work is an editorial judgement; for example, the related word *wise* earned no credit where our registered alternatives glossed the worthy. [Everyday question probes](evidence/asked_gloss_v1/paraphrases.json) · [Independent reference list](evidence/asked_gloss_v1/daodejing.tsv)

The aligned ordered reader took 24.97 seconds, 2.35 kJ and 234,447,360 bytes peak PyTorch allocation for 1,788 optimizer updates on a single RTX 4090 D. The initial image likelihood cache took 5.19 seconds. The prior writer fit cost 38.75 seconds plus 0.41 seconds for its drawing network. These timings exclude preparation, controls and evaluation. The first-stage energy counter was unavailable; there is no complete end-to-end energy measurement. The next comparison should reserve independently authored lexical questions, including everyday descriptions, object-first clauses and negative questions, before adding training paraphrases. Evaluate new works and sources of questions separately. Genuine improvements must survive the existing local count and role-identity controls and transfer to open wording.

A CPU replay used the public two-image inference function separately for all eight ordered requests on every held clause. All 4,312 source positions, stop decisions and image streams matched the stored evaluation. The attested decoder also matched on all 168 requests spanning every Laozi clause and the first clause from each of the other four works. The comparison includes wrong answers.
