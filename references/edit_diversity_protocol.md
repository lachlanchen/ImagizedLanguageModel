# Does independent sentence coverage improve visual translation?

The previous whole-glyph experiment recovered some ordered context while complete modern Chinese translations remained rare. A conditional distribution can fit frequent local edits and still choose the wrong expression for a new sentence. Repeating a passage estimates its own gradient more precisely under stochastic batching. Sampling a different passage also changes the empirical joint distribution of source and answer. The predicted benefit is lower edit distance on previously unseen chapters. Experiments with text language models have found small loss differences through several epochs of repetition, making a null outcome plausible here. [Earlier visual result](edit_contrast_result.md) · [Muennighoff et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16264)

Use the same released 2.03M-parameter model, 32-pixel-square glyphs, likelihood, trainable recurrent editor/action/drawing heads, optimizer, seed and 1,920 updates. Partition 192,000 unique physical sources into four 48,000-sentence blocks A through D. Compare training on A four times with training on A, B, C and D once each. Both runs receive 192,000 translations and 48,000 quotation examples. Report actual glyph exposures and time. One seed and a fixed font limit inference.

Draw from the original training chapters of the pinned NiuTrans corpus. Exclude prior training and evaluation sources by both string and physical bitmap. Assign chapters excluded from all training to two evaluation pools by hash parity, retaining the first 2,048 distinct sources by seeded row priority in each. Training limits are 96 source and 128 target glyphs. Evaluation applies only the source limit. Unsupported requests remain in exact-answer denominators. Freeze lists before fitting. The initial attempt to exclude even previously evaluated chapters yielded only 85 eligible discovery records. Before training or scoring, eligibility was broadened to include chapters used in previous evaluations. New sentences remain physically disjoint from those evaluations, and the two new evaluation sets remain chapter-disjoint from one another. Compare each arm with a local three-glyph frequency editor given its own complete training material, including the original 48,000 pairs.

Require positive lower 95% paired chapter-bootstrap bounds for edit-rate improvement over repetition, its data-matched local rule and copy on both evaluation sets, at least one completely correct changed answer longer than eight glyphs that combines copied and source-absent Han, and 99% exact quotations on all requests. Report novel full answers separately. Inspect saved freely generated pixels and edits, and draw accuracy under supplied correct history. If the decision fails, retain the parent and inspect the first divergent free choice before proposing another training change. One 4090, one serial owner, two CPU threads, at most 8 GiB allocated VRAM, 6 GiB process RSS and 35 minutes per fit/evaluation phase.
