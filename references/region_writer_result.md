# Can a visual writer carry a fact into a new sentence?

A 913,127-parameter model trained from scratch wrote six complete English reference sentences and 33 complete Chinese directory answers on 96 unseen document or entity families per language. Each counted answer ended autonomously and matched the reference glyphs bit for bit. Chinese catalogue replies usually placed a new value in a familiar construction. The English writer occasionally expressed an unseen construction correctly and often damaged the sentence around a correct value. On a separate calibrated English semantic check, none of its 16 replies earned credit. The experiment establishes limited composition on aligned glyph streams. Reliable general reading remains open. ## One image interface, two training conditions

The input supplies a pictured question and region boundaries. A reader learns which regions contain the annotated fact. A two-layer recurrent writer conditions on its own prior glyphs and attends to the question and source pixels. At each step it mixes probabilities over the training bitmap bank with probabilities of copying visible bitmaps. Both distributions act on the same physical glyph space. The region condition uses the trained reader’s own selection during fitting and generation. Its matched control reads every region. A third evaluation supplies annotated evidence to the fitted region writer. No text model, transcription, answer candidates or human region labels are required by the released inference path. The 32 × 32 cells and their order are supplied. The writer can copy an unfamiliar visible glyph or emit one of its learned bitmap choices. It lacks a stroke generator for wholly new glyphs. [Model](../ilm/visual_lm/region_writer.py) · [Inference](../scripts/write_region_writer.py)

Training used 53,011 English human statements paired with natural SQuAD questions and paragraphs, 32,333 human descriptions for 12,520 named restaurant records, and 3,919 human replies to Chinese catalogue questions. Restaurant prompts were constructed. Each writer saw all 89,263 human formulations in the same order over four passes, totalling 357,052 answer presentations and 9,912 updates. Development reserved 96 distinct families per source. Full training targets with identical normalized reference wording were excluded. The selector fitted in 70.49 s and 8.09 kJ. The direct and selected writers fitted separately in 163.87 s and 23.96 kJ, and 148.10 s and 18.62 kJ, respectively. Peak PyTorch allocations were 193, 556 and 477 MiB, respectively, on one RTX 4090 D. Times and energies cover the device fitting stages. On pages where **every** accepted reference was absent as both normalized wording and contiguous glyph bitmaps in every supplied region and the question, the region writer reproduced 6/94 English statements and 32/92 Chinese replies. The full-page control scored 4/94 and 35/92. Masking the annotated value revealed a training frame for all 33 exact Chinese cases and none of the six exact English cases. The latter finding permits reuse of shorter phrases and syntax. An earlier English story reader followed by a dedicated statement writer achieved 8/96 on these same English pages. Paired bootstrap 95% intervals for the change from direct to selected were +2.1 to +21.9 percentage points for English fact presence and −2.1 to +6.3 for exact English answers. None of the restaurant descriptions matched a complete human wording. A local Qwen3-8B text judge assessed blinded natural replies alongside the human reference and a changed-fact foil in both presentation orders. For 16 English questions it accepted all 16 references and rejected all 16 foils in both orders, then credited 1/16 direct and 0/16 region-writer replies. Its Chinese calibration failed on three genuine replies. An additional local Q4 Qwen3-VL-8B image comparison covered eight questions per domain. Both that model and the selected ILM scored 3/8, 7/8 and 2/8 on the English value, Chinese value and restaurant field checks. Execution and batching differed. These 24 examples establish neither equivalent language quality nor a matched-quality training advantage. ## A mathematical test of the next change

For a fixed image and generated prefix, let the spelling and copy distributions be $s$ and $c$. At mixture weight $g$, the margin of target glyph $a$ against rival $b$ is $(c_a-c_b)+g[(s_a-s_b)-(c_a-c_b)]$. Each rival imposes a one-dimensional linear constraint. Intersecting all constraints with the unit interval answers exactly whether *any* mixing weight could make an accepted next glyph win. Include every acceptable reference still consistent with the prefix and the stop action. The Lean proof covers strict and weak inequalities. Compute the intersection using the exact binary ratios of the two measured component scores, then replay the deployed choice. [Lean proof](../formal/span_list/OpticalRouteMargin.lean) · [Implementation](../scripts/probe_optical_route.py)

The region writer first departed from all recorded human wordings on 90 English statements. All 90 replayed winning choices agreed with the saved emissions. Only 24 of those positions admit a mixing weight that makes a permissible next glyph win. On the other 66 it cannot even tie. For Chinese the corresponding feasible count is 14/63 and for restaurants it is 27/96. Independently rounding both distributions to eight decimal places gives the same decisions. This is a statement about the first deviation from recorded answers. An unrecorded paraphrase can be meaningful, and one repaired step does not guarantee a good sentence. [Exact probability audit](evidence/region_writer_v1/route-native.json) · [Rounded comparison](evidence/region_writer_v1/route.json)

The next comparison should train the two component distributions to represent who did what to whom at the first wrong content word, retaining whole-answer supervision. Compare equal-exposure training with and without role-sensitive alternatives on training-only passages. Reserve new families before fitting, and require a generated clause with the correct subject, action and requested value on both the original page and an independently edited version. Preserve ordinary book, typeface and historical probes and an independently calibrated semantic reading. If grounding improves while complete propositions do not, retain the previous specialists and inspect which roles still collapse. Increasing fitting time alone cannot repair a fixed pair of distributions at these measured prefixes.

The experiment draws on two distinct lines of prior work. PIXAR demonstrated autoregressive generation of rendered language in pixel space. QA2D supplied the human transformations from a question and short answer into a full statement. Here an additional controlled measurement asks whether a complete pictured answer follows an edited fact and whether the two learned glyph distributions could have written its observed wording.
