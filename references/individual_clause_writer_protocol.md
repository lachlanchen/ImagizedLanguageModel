# Writing one factual clause at a time

The earlier writer gave the correct name higher probability on 52 of 96 single-question story requests and passed no four-way story contrast. The next writer receives a pictured passage and one pictured question and emits an entire Chinese clause with its own stop. Two requests can be served together, and their two physical output strips can be read in sequence. All wording and names come from the writer. This approach supplies the request order as part of the interface.

Use the earlier aligned writer as the common parent. Each edited training scene contributes the same two questions in two languages, two page versions, and two exposures per individual input. In the aligned fit both targets are the requested action assigned to the correct participant. In the independent fit one exposure names the other participant while preserving the action. Both see identical ordered two-question replay, human questions about novels with teacher-written answer clauses, and historical image requests. Keep the same seed, updates, batch order and target marginal distributions within scene, language and action. Reuse verified local teacher targets for the first 1,024 book pairs; ask the same local teacher to finish the remaining eligible training pairs. Remove unwritable new book clauses from both arms. Run under the existing 20-GiB, 1,200-second optimization ceilings.

Twelve new paired stories, four per role, time and correction category, were written and fixed before either fit. Evaluate old stories, new stories, the 128 held-out credits, human novel questions and historical prompts. Measure each freely generated clause separately, requiring a stop and only the correct participant. A complete edited-page contrast requires both roles to pass under both page versions in the same language. Also read the two emitted clauses together on one prespecified question order per new story (forward on even-indexed stories, reverse on odd-indexed), both worlds and both languages. Credit fully supported statements, screen arm identities, and compare the unmodified parent and local text Qwen3-8B. Report the earlier two-question decoding route as a separate measurement. For the book comparison evaluate the first 96 eligible validation passages separately from the other 253. Passing the narrow credit task alone does not qualify this writer for general narrative use. Transfer requires more complete new-story contrasts than both controls and more supported full statements in the blinded sample. Preserve the original free responses if these conditions fail.

The paper revision will report the isolated-action diagnosis and the measured outcome of clause training, showing original free output pixels from the first reserved story. Keep benchmark outcomes, deterministic ordering and human-language understanding distinct.
