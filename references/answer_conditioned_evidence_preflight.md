# Learning from what the writer can say

On the new stories, the frozen writer produced no complete reference verbatim under any candidate. A semantic judge nevertheless credited a response for some candidate on nine of sixteen stories. Before a new fit, test a learning signal that integrates the actual likelihood of complete human wordings over all candidate locations. The prior is the signed reader's selection distribution. For candidate s, its likelihood is the sum over distinct accepted physical wordings of the product of conditional next-image probabilities, including stop. A zero-probability copy or unavailable drawing route contributes zero. The logarithmic selector gradient equals prior minus the normalized product of prior and likelihood.

Use the official 232 training stories of FairytaleQA only. Retain local questions with an identified complete source section within the existing geometry. Remove held articles, duplicate physical questions, held question/page images and disallowed complete answer images. Report data counts, sources and all exclusions. Choose at most one question per training story in hash order for a 64-question numerical preflight. Enumerate all 32 candidates, all human wordings and all output cells. Compare batched probabilities with individual scalar steps, including mixture routes and stop. Check the prior-minus-posterior derivative with autograd and verify a descent update on selector logits. Report the signal on questions where no accepted answer strip occurs in the list. This is a training-signal test. A separate matched full fit on disjoint material is necessary to establish learned answer quality.
