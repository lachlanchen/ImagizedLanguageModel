# Decision after the complete white-target comparison

The [completed comparison](white_target_physical_result.md) rejects the registered
white-target objective. All 41,298 retained updates, five snapshots, the CPU audit,
124 conditional cases and 33 opaque boards/2,080 frozen readings are complete.
Readability passes in every group, but false ink is 12/252 against a maximum of
eleven and false blanks are 54 against a maximum of 37. All seventeen additional
false blanks occur in books relative to the unchanged nonempty-ranking pixels.

This actor is terminal and ineligible for the registered autonomous language
comparison. Preserve the failed gate, final and intermediate checkpoints,
uncertain readings, original controls and all 115 bound sources. Do not create
its post-gate execution binding, repeat its fit or qualification, select an
intermediate checkpoint, alter thresholds or repair a frozen transcription.
No complete-language score exists for this actor; an unexecuted trial is not a
zero score.

## What this changes

The white-target term reduces wrong ink at legitimate spaces from 25 to twelve,
while worsening nonempty erasure from 37 to 54. Exact source bitmaps decrease
from 4,609 to 4,543. Development-book source-mask likelihood improves while
bitmap agreement and blank errors worsen. This rejects the tested objective
as a sufficient repair. It does not isolate whether probability calibration,
conditional uncertainty, context adaptation or the visual interface dominates
the remaining error.

Individual readable forms have repeatedly improved without demonstrating
complete language. The original complete continuation trial remains the last
autonomous language result: 7/32 strict proportional answers, 0/16 both-correct
pairs and no credited coherent complete book continuation. Correct-history
book failures also prevent an explanation based only on accumulated feedback
errors. These findings leave the full visual-language objective open.

The next work must connect language intent to the complete writing that is
actually emitted. Another blank/nonblank margin or an unchanged fit using correct
history is not the selected next intervention. Preserve the completed probability
and writer controls rather than repeating them.

## Qualification required before a successor fit

The [whole-answer direction](whole_answer_learning_direction_20261116.md)
provides actual-pixel sampling and differentiable trajectory replay, with
supervised learning retained as an anchor. Both tested reward judges failed
their fixed calibration. Their policy fits remain inadmissible. A fixed image
preference loss is another candidate, but the prepared machine-derived labels,
complete rendering, body support and actual-output quality are unqualified.
Neither candidate is a demonstrated replacement algorithm.

The next admitted work is **training-only qualification of a complete-answer
learning signal**, not a successor full fit. A proposal must identify the
observed failure it addresses, a fixed correctness signal and a prospective
rejection rule. Keep transcribing the generated image separate from judging
its answer: the transcriber must not receive the question or reference, and
ambiguous writing must remain uncertain. Qualify any semantic judge on actual
outputs, including readable wrong entities/dates, repeated or missing forms,
spaces, caps and stopping. A failed judge stops that proposal. Do not tune an
old failed judge to the frozen development readings or use those outputs as
fitting data.

Only a qualified signal permits a separately registered training-only body,
gradient and resource preflight on a fresh clone. Require actual emitted-pixel
feedback, complete-action probability replay, finite gradients, reset trial
updates and bounded sampling cost. Equal rewards or uniformly unreadable samples
do not supply evidence of a usable language update. Offline pair preferences
still require trustworthy labels and qualified complete image support; their
lengths cannot be truncated into apparent successful stopping.

No successor model call, optimizer update or full fit is authorized by this
decision alone. Keep the complete corpus and unchanged control, prospective
checkpoints, complete answers, paired questions, all erasures, natural stopping,
long books and earlier successes in any later comparison. Untouched confirmation,
historical forms, cold execution and whole-model matched-quality cost remain
full-goal requirements.

## Publication

Update the Nature-style manuscript and native editable figures from the actual
12/252 and 54/12,597 failures, all seven readability groups and original pixels.
Preserve the current PDF baselines, previous negative figures, both frozen
control reviews and failed unfrozen entries. Do not illustrate the result with
newly generated successful writing. Publication follows the measured verdict;
it does not qualify language or replace the missing complete-answer experiment.
