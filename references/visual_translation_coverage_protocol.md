# Testing transfer across Chinese sentence constructions

A visual writer reproduced thirteen complete translations when a numeral changed in a familiar sentence. A one-cell lookup and substitution rule reproduced all eighteen. Both failed on complete passages from unfamiliar chapters. The next comparison asks whether distributing the same supervised exposure budget over more distinct source sentences yields a measurable gain on new wording. [Previous result and analogy control](screened_visual_targets_result.md)

## Data and contrasts

The candidate training roster contains 1,024 pictured-source candidates from 1,024 chapters of the existing 55,872-row training partition. Exactly 256 fall in each source-length range, 4–12, 13–20, 21–30 and 31–60 cells. A nested narrow roster contains 32 per range. One chapter per row increases exposure to different writing. Chapter count alone cannot establish how many distinct grammatical constructions are present. Local Qwen3-8B receives only the classical source to draft a translation. Keep the original book target, raw draft and any revision distinct. Review the drafts against the complete sources, correct unsupported names, numbers and events, and record all exclusions. Apply one review rubric to both rosters.

The new natural test contains 192 passages from 161 further chapters, 64 in each of the three available length ranges up to 64 cells. No eligible 65–96-cell passage remained after excluding earlier cohorts. Six readings from distinct chapters per available length range give eighteen whole-meaning judgments. Eight additional passages have a uniquely aligned numeral followed by a matching unit in the book translation. For each, replace one numeral in a copy of the input and evaluate both answers. Some changes intentionally create counterfactual dates or quantities. Credit faithful reporting of the pictured change. Do not credit an answer that fixes historical or astronomical facts by reversing the edit. The paired book gloss can import details from neighbouring sentences. Credit meaning against the supplied fragment and document uncertain readings.

The delexicalized number test replaces runs of Chinese numerals with the same placeholder for a training-overlap check. This check concerns exact writing around a number. Its scope is narrower than a test of an entirely unseen syntactic class. The actual learner and nearest-example control consume ordered images. Check every complete held-out input and target in physical glyph space against training inputs and targets. [Preparation and held-out records](evidence/visual_translation_coverage_20261119)

## Observed preparation quality

All 1,024 source-only drafts completed and rendered in the study font. The eight recorded batch durations sum to 445 seconds of teacher wall time; this excludes an interrupted partial batch. Physical comparison found zero complete overlaps between the new test sources or targets and the supervised archive or the new drafts. All eight quantity frames are absent from the original training sources after erasing Chinese numeral spans. The closest new training candidate lies at least nine glyph edits from each of those eight changed inputs. An editorial model then reviewed a fixed 32-draft sample balanced across four lengths and the nested versus additional candidates. Fourteen were retained, ten revised and eight excluded. The editor saw the classical source and draft. Uncertain fragments, an uninterpreted numerical heading and changes to historical detail accounted for exclusions or revisions. The remaining 992 drafts are candidates for review. ## Matched comparison

Compare the nested and broad reviewed sets from the same ordered-copy parent. Replay the common book corpus as in Figure 67 and add the same number of supplemental exposures per length range in each arm. Only the number of distinct reviewed examples and their consequent repetition frequencies should differ. Freeze the final roster, grade rubric, optimizer schedule, exposed cell counts and resource ceiling before fitting. Different sentence lengths or correction rates may leave residual differences in target cells; record them. Keep the parent, phrase composer and one-cell visual analogy as controls. Evaluate teacher and student separately. Count whole translations, both sides of each quantity change, stops, glyph edits and inference cost. Screened-target coverage has to improve whole meanings over the narrow arm and composer and yield more correct changed pairs than the matching narrow arm before attributing improved transfer to broader exposure. If transfer fails, preserve both models and their output. Use paired readings to decide whether content selection, word order or the writing mechanism limits transfer before increasing scale.

## Relation to recent work

MIXAR trained multilingual autoregressive pixel models up to 0.5B parameters and reports gains from rewarding their free generations. Chu and colleagues report that purely visual models on controlled reasoning tasks benefit from writing intermediate steps in successive frames. Both results motivate scoring what a model independently emits. Our numeral study also identifies a confound for such a reward: a single substitution can earn a perfect whole-image score while the same mechanism abstains on every fresh passage. A future sequence reward should therefore be tested on unseen sentence frames and credit paraphrases that preserve meaning. The present comparison changes data coverage first so any effect remains interpretable. [MIXAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11575) · [Pixel is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22820)
