# Can one image-stream writer answer questions about passages and historical entries?

The question contrast writer sometimes returns different text for two questions on the same pictured record. Both example answers about the scripts in a handout were wrong. We test whether this same network can learn to write an answer containing a requested lexical fact and a particular historical bitmap while preserving modern passage answering. Previous specialized entry readers used an explicit card parser. This writer sees the raw stream of glyph images. Its output consists of pixels copied from the input or shapes in the same fixed 7,715-image writing memory.

Initialize both arms from the contrast-trained passage writer. Use 8,920 original entry questions on 1,115 training cards and 44,600 additional pictured phrasings for the same answers. Fit five complete passes in identical order, 96 entry examples per step. The matched arm sees the corresponding question and the control shuffles questions within each card anew each epoch, preserving wording frequency, card, answers and schedule. Both receive identical rehearsal of 12 natural DuReader and 12 contrast-cohort DRCD questions per step, with each natural corpus weighted 0.4. Minimize negative mean per-answer glyph log likelihood including stop. Use AdamW, gradient clipping at one, 50 warmup updates and cosine decay from 0.00045 to 0.00012. Keep only final checkpoints; 30 minutes and 12 GiB per fit on GPU 0.

Generate complete answers on 142 held-out cards containing 284 families and 568 historical shapes excluded from dictionary training. Check that the requested shape is physically present in the evidence and absent from the fixed drawing memory. Report exact answer and stop, exact historical form immediately before final punctuation, groups with all questions correct, a white-cell question, the held-out paraphrase and all 96 previously independently authored prompts. Include a separate English question frame for each of the 1,136 targets. It asks for the same pictured Chinese response. The extra training questions are in Chinese. Rerun the 1,042 same-passage pairs and 571 natural writing questions for each arm with their original references.

For useful cross-task transfer, look for a positive card-clustered lower 95% bound over scrambling on the whole canonical answer, success on the independent requests, and retention of at least the initial 21 correct DRCD pairs and 16 exact DuReader answers. Report all measures independently if only some improve. Preserve the earlier 15/300 minimum for a claim about independently writing missing natural content. Previously published specialist performance remains a comparison rather than a training target. Mark copying a new source image separately from inventing one. A separate demonstration uses the first available genuine oracle and bronze source exemplars of 言, whose training entry contained different stages. Keep the headword's prior exposure explicit and preserve failed outputs.
