# Transferring a Chinese reader into continuous glyph space

The small participant reader learned held-out grammatical questions but tied its exchanged-label control on new situations. This separate intervention gives a pixel reader access to contextual knowledge acquired by a pretrained Chinese question-answering encoder. Inheritance and incremental fit cost must be reported separately.

Use the pinned CKIP BERT Chinese QA checkpoint. During training only, use its vocabulary to associate visible single-character forms with input vectors. Render all supported normalized single characters in two typefaces, Noto Sans CJK SC Regular and Noto Serif CJK SC Regular. Merge exact duplicate images and average conflicting targets. Learn a dense function from raw image pixels to the continuous encoder input, using a fixed Gaussian projection of multiscale ink images, rectification and ridge regression. No question, answer, split, name or test score selects the training glyphs, map width, checkpoint or ridge parameter. Fix seed 17039, width 24576 and ridge 0.0003. An unchanged contextual stack receives continuous vectors and two sequence delimiters. Discard the lexical lookup and tokenizer from the visual runtime. It returns the original image cells in the highest-scoring legal source span, with no list of answer options. This is extractive reading and inherits text pretraining. It cannot answer a question requiring information or glyphs absent from its source.

Compare the resulting reader with CKIP's native text path, the published small writer and a visually decoupled response control that uses the wrong question from the same scene. Evaluate all 96 original explicit-role questions and 64 independently phrased new ones, in the fitted sans font and an untrained bold face. For the 16 new situations require at least eight complete four-answer scenes in both fonts and more individual answers than the calibrated writer. Measure the complete free answers on the Chinese XWinograd split, and record whether the corresponding source answer can be copied. Keep failures. A positive result earns an image reader with inherited knowledge. Open writing, zero-shot arbitrary historical reading and total training efficiency remain separate requirements. Limit the map fit to one GPU, 18 GiB allocated and 600 seconds. Evaluate the final regression solution only.
