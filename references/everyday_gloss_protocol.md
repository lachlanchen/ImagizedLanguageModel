# Transfer of pictured classroom questions

A reader trained to distinguish explicit grammatical roles failed four previously tested conversational question pairs. This comparison changes the distribution of pictured requests during fitting. The visual architecture, emission cache, 19,067 classical clauses, twelve passes, optimizer, seed, free English image writer and stop rule follow the preceding ordered reader. One treatment adds 24 manually authored conversational pairs to the 16 existing pairs. One control exchanges the answer images independently by clause and epoch. The previously released reader is the matched narrow-prompt baseline.

The primary outcome is the number of passages with the correct source cell and complete acceptable English images for **all four independently sourced classroom questions**. The new 32 excerpts from the Zhuangzi and Han Feizi are selected for syntax coverage. Exclude full-passage visual duplicates from prior evaluation or training and any passage whose target writer window occurs in the writer fit. Report all exclusions. Acceptable glosses are fixed editorial readings, not an exhaustive bilingual dictionary. Report previous held books separately, transfers to the four earlier everyday question pairs, retention on the original order contrasts, fronted objects, and the supplied-locations upper bound. Keep errors as well as successful image pairs. A new-source gain over both controls supports bounded transfer. A low supplied-location ceiling directs the next experiment to writing contextually appropriate meanings.

English classroom wording comes from the Austin Peay State University handout on subjects and predicates and the San José State University handout on transitive verbs. The two Chinese classroom prompts excerpt the action and recipient tests from Thinka's lesson on sentence constituents. The two languages each contribute one source pair; question-source diversity is limited. The train-only prompts were composed for this experiment. The published stems are independent of that authorship. [Austin Peay](https://www.apsu.edu/writingcenter/writing-resources/Subjects-and-Predicates-Handout-2025.pdf) · [San José State](https://pdp.sjsu.edu/writingcenter/docs/updated-handout-pdf/Verbs-%20Transitive%20and%20Intransitive.pdf) · [Thinka](https://www.thinka.ai/zh-HK/小學/中國語文/主語、謂語、賓語)

The fit is limited to 900 seconds and 8 GiB allocated VRAM per arm on one RTX 4090 D. No checkpoint selection or glossary-constrained decoding is used. The Lean counting identity measures precisely which complete gains a prompt intervention can produce with the same writer.
