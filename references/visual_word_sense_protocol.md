# Writing the meaning of a marked character in running prose

The Kyoto Classical Chinese dependency treebank assigns an English gloss to annotated words in their sentence context. For example, 言 can be glossed as *speak* or *speech*. Our input is an image of the marked character with up to eight printed characters on either side. The desired output is a voluntarily terminated image of the gloss. This task places the source and output in different scripts. The previous compositor generated a small set of connectives and primarily copied source spans. A context-blind headword dictionary can give at most one correct reference answer on two passages with the same headword and different glosses. [Lean proof](../formal/span_list/SameInkMeaning.lean) · [Previous model](grounded_ink_writing_result.md)

**Sources and exclusion.** Hold out the entirety of KR1h0004 and KR4a0001. Use the other six works from the locally pinned Kyoto UD 2.16 training file. Keep lowercase alphabetic or hyphenated glosses up to 24 cells, single Han headwords, at least one neighbouring character and font coverage in both training faces. Remove identical printed windows that occur across splits or have conflicting labels, then retain one occurrence per reference image window. The final split has 247,456 training instances, 6,987 in the first evaluation work and 7,173 in the second. Freeze disjoint pairs having the same marked character but different attested glosses, each seen at least three times for that head in training. There are 566 and 403 pairs. Every window in the held works also receives an unrestricted response. Pair identities and acceptable alternative glosses are fixed before fitting. Report original reference matches separately from the small list of alternatives. Remaining valid paraphrases can score zero. The annotated choice of headword is given. [Split and source checksum](evidence/visual_word_senses_20261124/split.json) · [Pairs](evidence/visual_word_senses_20261124/pairs.json) · [Alternative readings](evidence/visual_word_senses_20261124/aliases.json)

**Learning comparison.** Train a contextual encoder and recurrent bitmap writer from random initialization. Use the same learned drawing network for both arms, trained solely to reconstruct the distinct physical training output cells from discriminating pixel coordinates. Require exact recovery for these cells before training language. On each decoding step the writer predicts one joint pattern of the selected pixels or stops. Its drawing network expands each chosen pattern to 1,024 pixel logits. The previous pattern feeds the recurrence. A training-only head predicts equality classes of complete gloss images to aid learning. In eight passes over the full training set, alternate regular and bold source images, keep the regular output typeface and use identical inputs, initialization, optimizer, batch order and compute. In the control, permute the entire response among training examples with the same central bitmap each epoch. Both arms see identical counts of every input and output. The frozen two-region editor also receives the first 32 paired contrasts per book as a baseline, with the marked center supplied to both region inputs and the pictured instruction 寫出英文詞義. Compute the training-modal reference per central image for every held instance.

**Readout.** Archive all generated image streams and ending decisions. Score strict image equality, exact or predeclared synonymous output, incomplete spells, unseen gloss strings, head-only prediction and both-correct paired contexts. Resample whole headwords 10,000 times for paired intervals against shuffled supervision. The predeclared contextual criterion requires a positive paired lower bound in both works and at least one complete contrast for 言 in either work. Report the first complete and incomplete paired examples in frozen order and the first successful 言 pair, when available. A positive result licenses a claim about lexical sense in marked passages. Unrestricted instruction following, other scripts, comparative efficiency at matched quality and free explanations need independent measurement. If the criterion fails, distinguish fluent glosses for the wrong sense from malformed spellings before changing the model. Each fit has a 25-minute, 6 GiB allocation ceiling on one RTX 4090 D.
