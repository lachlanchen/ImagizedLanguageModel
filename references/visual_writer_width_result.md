# More parameters at the same visual supervision

Increasing the book writer from 1,688,480 to 14,895,840 parameters yielded zero strict semantic credits on 64 prospectively reserved translation requests, the same as the small control. The quantized text Qwen3-8B comparator earned 49. The two visual models each earned one credit on 96 fitted requests. The difficulty extends to expressing the meaning of sentences already present in the training corpus.

The thin and wide writers restored the same starting function. The optical reader uses 2 × 2 pooling of 32 × 32 glyphs, ordered four-cell windows and three encoder layers. A two-layer decoder attends to the source and earlier emitted glyphs, then selects an image from the output inventory or a stop. Expanding four attention heads to twelve retains 40 coordinates per head. Copied incoming weights sum to their old values across each replica group. The old and expanded initial functions agreed within 0.000044 in teacher-forced logits and produced the same 64 stopped streams in a CPU preflight. A discarded Adam step verified that replicas acquire different gradients. [Architecture and preflight](../ilm/visual_lm/wide_translation.py) · [Preflight measurements](evidence/prefix_recovery_v1/wide-preflight.json)

The training comparison matched the small reference-prefix fit in examples, order, target weights, 5,574 updates, optimizer settings and free-development selection rule. The 14.90M model used three 1,858-step passes, peaked at 3.04 GiB allocated on one RTX 4090 D and took 144.3 seconds across the passes. It scored 49,519,224 target positions, just as the previously measured control did in 110.7 seconds and 1.58 GiB. Wider pass three and narrow pass one were selected on development. The original function, corpus and optical inventory contain prior training and preparation costs. The new panel contains two directions of one sentence from each of 32 additional documents. Automated two-order grading with stopped answers produced zero successes at both widths across all 32 documents. The sample-based paired bootstrap is degenerate in this case and cannot establish population equivalence. Each of the 1,184 repeated small-control images (144 known natural requests, 944 role requests and 96 fitted natural requests) reproduced the earlier packed pixels. The text comparator and reference calibrations on the repeat panel were independently graded against a changed candidate set and differ slightly from the earlier Qwen counts. Wide role-family accuracy fell from the small control's 103/118 to 100/118. The registered criterion required at least 110 plus a positive lower confidence bound for fresh semantic improvement. ## Next language experiment

A reference-blind check of the 96 *training* requests evaluated previously generated teacher translations, corpus targets and shuffled foils in two masked orders. The judge accepted 82 teacher translations and 47 corpus translations twice and accepted zero foils. Of the teacher outputs, 93 fit the deployed repertoire and length limit, 80 were writable, stopped and accepted in the reference-blind test, and 56 also passed the original reference-informed assessment. The informed assessment gave 58 teacher credits and 94 corpus credits. Both reviews used a Qwen-family judge, so their agreement is a screening signal with shared model-family bias. The corpus sample was length matched to evaluation and is unsuitable for estimating a population-wide acceptance rate. The 96 previously examined cases remain a diagnostic set. [Per-target result and calibration votes](evidence/wide_translation_v1/teacher-targets.json)

Test whether training the independent visual writer to express a competent bilingual teacher's complete translations improves free natural writing. Quantized Qwen can generate such targets offline. First measure its direct translations on a representative *training-only* sample in both directions, check rendering and training/reference discrepancies, and assess semantic fidelity without telling the reviewer which side is the reference. Keep all held article and book translations excluded from supervision. Compare visual students starting at the same checkpoint on the same source images, with complete human targets or teacher targets under a matched supervised-glyph budget. Preserve the unreduced natural language task and all raw replies. Pass requires a gain on previously unseen documents plus retention of the controlled role families. Use actual held-output improvement to decide whether longer training is warranted. Sequence-level distillation has previously been studied for neural translation.
