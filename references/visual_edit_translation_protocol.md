# Per-glyph copying and drawing in classical-to-modern Chinese

The frozen whole-answer system could never reproduce 47,944 of 53,585 pictured next segments in two books. A source interval and an eight-cell writer cannot compose a longer answer that adds new glyphs. Test that missing capability on modern renderings of classical Chinese. Both source and answer are ordered physical images. Human translations in the NiuTrans collection are paired automatically at sentence level and can admit other valid wordings. [Corpus and alignment procedure](https://github.com/NiuTrans/Classical-Modern)

Train on 48,000 distinct renderable source sentences from the pinned NiuTrans archive. Split by chapter before sampling. Limit sources to 96 cells, restrict training targets to 128, and keep evaluation target lengths unrestricted. Reserve two disjoint rosters of 2,048 questions each, including unrenderable requests as failures. Deduplicate pictured sources across rosters. Keep corpus and answer strings outside inference.

Supervise a monotone edit transducer using minimum-edit training alignments. Its cursor can copy a source image, draw a replacement, insert a drawing, discard a source cell or stop. A bidirectional source encoder sees the complete source and a pictured instruction. A causal decoder reads the most recently emitted ink on every step. Source copying retains the entire 32-by-32 bitmap. Drawing predicts twelve four-pixel groups from a learned language state and the previously predicted groups, then decodes the 48 measurements to an image. No answer inventory or font is available during generation. Train a physical renderer on training shapes. Include requests to reproduce the original alongside requests to translate. Use eight fixed passes. Release decisions use the final weights.

Compare whole stopped answers, exact long answers containing absent Han, physical character edit distance, and the same source with a quotation request. Include a direct copy and a training-only local edit model. Repeat on the second roster if any real longer mixed answer succeeds. To qualify the new translator require a complete longer mixed answer with an absent Han in both rosters, a positive lower chapter-bootstrap bound for edit-distance improvement over both controls and at least 99% complete quotation. Where a criterion fails, retain the experimental weights and measure the location of the loss. Keep the pre-existing passage-QA and historical interfaces independent during this comparison. The diagnostic goal is composition on real recorded wording. General translation quality also requires semantic evaluation. Use one RTX 4090 D, two host threads, 8 GiB allocated device memory and 6 GiB host RSS per phase. Preserve 24 GiB available host RAM, 48 GiB free disk and a 35-minute ceiling per phase.
