# Can a generative teacher improve the first content decision?

The compact direct writer first selected the wrong visible word in 628 of 697 difficult development replies. Evaluate the shared Qwen3-4B Q4_K_M text teacher on all 83 turns for which each of four human answers uses absent multiword phrasing and on 128 other eligible development turns chosen by a fixed hash. Give it the complete passage, question and previous exchange. Ask for a brief answer and one exact supporting passage quote. Decode greedily with a fixed prompt. Evaluate the original, the prior within-story question/history exchange and the prior between-story passage exchange. Retain malformed, unfinished and ungrounded outputs in the denominator. Admission requires at least three any-reference exact answers and official F1 of at least 0.35 on the difficult turns, a drop of at least 0.08 F1 for each exchange, and a real contiguous passage quote on at least half of difficult original turns. If admission fails, retain the teacher result and diagnose whether its answers or evidence fail before using its labels.

On admission, obtain teacher responses only for 3,072 fitting turns. Select 1,536 multiword primary answers whose complete normalized phrase is absent from the three inputs, then fill by uniform hash from the remaining training turns. Fit on teacher responses only when the generated answer satisfies the student geometry and palette, the quote identifies an unambiguous passage interval, and the answer has normalized word F1 of at least 0.35 against the training human answer. Preserve all attempted cases and rejection reasons. The teacher sees no gold answer in its request. No held response may be added to training.

For each representation (glyph image and ASCII byte), start both student arms from the corresponding released cold writer. Run 1,500 AdamW updates at 0.0002 and seed 420121. Each batch contains 16 randomly drawn original human turns and 16 samples from the accepted fitting cases, with identical case identities in both arms. The human control uses the original answers and rationales in both halves. The treatment uses teacher answers and quotes in the second half. Add to both losses a 0.35-weighted negative log of the total pointer mass on matching source-word edits within the relevant supporting passage interval, wherever such matches exist. Retain the existing routing, spelling, endpoint and pointer losses and greedy image feedback. Cap each fit at 20 minutes and 12 GiB allocated. If no usable teacher cases survive, stop before student fitting. Charge the teacher separately and include its label-generation energy in incremental cost.

Evaluate original parents and final students on a new set of complete official development stories of 321–448 words, with printable source, question and supplied history, using up to 32 question words and 48 history words. Exclude stories overlapping any earlier eligible fitting or held passage (canonical whitespace, case and punctuation). Score all eligible turns regardless of answer length, all four original answers, both source exchanges and the subset where every human response needs an absent multiword phrase. Publish story-bootstrap intervals for the difference between the teacher and human continuation arms. A transfer claim requires an increase in official answer F1 with a positive lower paired 95% bound on fresh stories. A claim of novel composition also requires additional exact previously absent answers whose outputs change under both exchanges. Keep previous development qualification separate. If either test fails, retain the measured outcome and distinguish failure of label quality, coverage, copying and content selection. One consumer GPU, teacher offline during student training and no teacher at student inference.
