# Can a glyph writer follow a change in the facts?

The preceding visual writer sometimes repeated the rehearsal assignments after the passage explicitly said that the musicians exchanged instruments. Its direct and clause-based versions each gave four complete statements on 48 authored requests. This trial trains the direct causal writer on pictured passages with two possible assignments of the same participants. At inference it receives an intact passage and a printed question. It generates every answer glyph and the stop itself.

The aligned and independent-label fits share an initialization, minibatch order, pictured pages, question orders, names, relation words, targets, optimizer and number of updates. Every paired input appears twice. Both exposures use the correct answer in the aligned condition. In the control one exposure retains the correct answer and the other assigns the counterpart page's answer. Human annotated DuIE pages provide genuine role assignments and the comparison exchanges the two names. An additional collection uses actions suggested by a local model from training novels in fictional accounts of role, time and negation changes. Both fits include the same genuine book-question and historical-form replay.

Hold out subjects from the DuIE development split before selecting training passages. The twelve separately written test stories have different surface wording and two versions each. Keep the page, question order and participant pair together when computing uncertainty. Evaluate both orders in Chinese and English. Publish exact image matches, ordered names with an actual stop, and completed contrasts requiring both versions and both orders. Examine full statements on all twelve stories with system identities hidden on a predetermined subset. Compare against a text Qwen3-8B reader and the preceding writer on its normal input. Recheck the 48 older requests and the historical-form task. Report successes in this controlled domain separately from general visual language. Consider the intervention transferable only if the aligned writer improves complete edited-page contrasts over both controls on held DuIE and separately worded stories, and improves supported full statements on the independent stories. A failure warrants a score-level localization between page interpretation and free generation.

The manual reading sample uses both versions and both question languages for every authored story. Present the first question order for even-numbered stories (zero based) and the reversed order for odd-numbered stories. Credit a stopped response only when it communicates both supported assignments in the requested sequence. Agreement on the two names alone is insufficient.

For losses on complete stopped answers, let rows index pictured passages and columns index answer assignments. Subtracting the aligned empirical loss from the independent-label loss yields the sum of the two within-page margins. A page-blind predictor has zero gain. The corresponding binary squared-loss gain is twice the label scale times the change in the predicted score. Lean checks both identities in `formal/span_list/SourceEditSupervision.lean`. These comparisons apply at fixed weights. Free generation and transfer require separate measurements.

The manuscript revision will show both supervision conditions, full edited-page performance, original output pixels and at least one failure. Update the abstract, main results and methods after evaluation.
