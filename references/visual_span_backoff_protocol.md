# Complementarity of visual spelling and passage copying

On 1,858 official restorations requiring a glyph absent from the inputs, the paired neural writer recovered 31 and the two-neighbor physical count recovered 196. Compare a learned choice between their complete response images with each component alone. Use the same fitted writer, same training-only physical counts, same official masks and stop decisions. This follow-up is motivated by already observed official aggregate counts.

Represent both proposals as streams of raw 32-by-32 bitmap cells. The count estimate uses visible left and right neighbors of the gap and backs off to a single neighbor or the marginal training span. Fit one 15-coefficient logistic gate on the 3,348 internal validation cases. Its training targets are the choice between the two proposals when exactly one equals the stopped human target. Its inputs comprise an intercept, neural stop, three backoff-level indicators, length and single-glyph indicator for each proposal, log source occurrence count, maximum number of matching adjacent flanks in the source and a claim occurrence flag for each proposal. No reference, label or reference-visibility flag enters the gate at inference. Selecting the count proposal terminates its finite visual stream. Keep the neural writer's own stop flag when selecting its output.

Evaluate once on all unchanged official masks. Require a positive paired premise-bootstrap lower bound against each writing component on overall complete images before crediting an improved selector. Separately report subset scores, per-length totals, the union of correct proposals, newly lost responses and storage costs. The gate uses internal validation labels, so that split is calibration data for this follow-up. A positive result measures constrained missing-span writing, with open generation and comparison to alternative algorithms still separate.
