# Learning who acts on whom in pictured Chinese

The preceding writer reproduced the correct name on 21 of 96 explicit person questions. Even after both name locations were supplied, it preferred the requested person in only 53 cases. This comparison trains a residual source-span scorer on two questions about different participants in the same pictured sentence. A frozen writer still decides whether to copy or compose. Its weights, glyph memory and copy scores provide the common starting point. Both arms receive the same pictures, initialization, rehearsal examples, schedules and optimization. In the control, the two extra role targets exchange places with probability one half independently at each presentation.

Every epoch includes all 872 official training predicates with a subject and object from Chinese GSD, in their original sentences. A second set crosses 24 human transitive verbs with six sentence structures, name exchanges, two script renderings and three pairs of pictured questions. Both the agent and the patient can occur first. These constructed events and the treebank serve different purposes. Natural DRCD training questions with a source answer rehearse ordinary reading. For each role question minimize negative log likelihood of the correct complete source span plus the softplus of the rival minus target score. Rehearsal maximizes annotated span likelihood. Train ten epochs with two CPU threads and one GPU, at most 900 seconds and 6 GiB allocated per arm. Use the last weights.

Free answer images and their stops determine all scores. Report original held-out GSD dev and test, including predicates absent from the GSD training split. Separately evaluate eight held-out event verbs in two voices, 24 previously inspected explicit situations and 16 newly written scenes with new names, independent wording, exchanged names and two different questions per scene. The fresh scenes are investigator-written. Fixed name order yields at most half of individual answers and cannot solve a whole scene. Archive both controls, all outputs and the first fresh scene in fixed order. Replay the frozen parent's stored answers on all overlapping tasks. Report whole DRCD, natural person questions, reordered composition, historical writing and open DuReader answers.

Require positive lower bounds for the matched advantage over randomized targets on both test GSD paired answers and fresh individual answers, with a 95% bootstrap grouped by sentence or scene. Require at least two complete fresh scenes, and retention of at least 95% of the preceding exact counts for natural person questions, familiar composition and historical descriptions. If any fails, keep the earlier general-purpose checkpoint as reference. Separate real language transfer from accurate writing in a known pattern. No comparison here measures total pretraining cost parity.
