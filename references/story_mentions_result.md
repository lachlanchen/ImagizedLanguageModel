# Whose price is it?

A Chinese paragraph introduces a garden and a tower, then uses *the former* and *the latter* to give their admission prices. The previous writer gave the garden's 12 yuan in both answer images. With a learned reference reader and the same property writer, the two answers read **门票是12元的。** and **门票是35元的。** Both complete output strips reproduced byte for byte on CPU from the archived question and name-panel PNGs. The example was authored to investigate a known error. ## What the natural reference tests establish

The two training languages use the same 32-pixel cell interface. From GOLEM's official fiction splits, 40 of 44 training works contributed 4,049 eligible marked pronouns. Five other works supplied 415 development examples and five more supplied 630 test examples. The free antecedent locator scored 43/200 in Chinese and 157/430 in English. With supplied name images and mixed-corpus training, scores reached 100/200 and 236/430, respectively. Only 11/62 Chinese and 19/71 English query pairs with the same nearest occurrence gave both correct choices. The story splits informed subsequent model changes. For the mixed fit we added 1,154 English GAP and 1,026 Chinese CLUE cases. English Wikipedia article groups and Chinese groups sharing normalized 32-character substrings were isolated from the reserved and previously evaluated official material. On official GAP the mixed visual reader gained 117 net decisions over its matched story-only control. Resampling whole articles gives a 95% interval of +5.52 to +10.45 percentage points. The official screen had been consulted in selecting training data. On the fresh screen the interval was −0.33 to +11.07 points. On official CLUE the model accepted 54 of 111 true links and rejected 125 of 193 false links. The official and fresh Chinese screens supplied zero non-overlapping opposite-label pairs under the same-name, same-pronoun, small-edit criterion. ## Why ordinary pronouns are still difficult

A separate test retained 120 adjacent pairs from the Winograd Schema Collection. Within a pair, the pictured pronoun, two candidate spellings and output vocabulary stay fixed. The correct name reverses with an edit elsewhere in the sentence. The frozen reader got 117/240 individual choices right and changed its answer only four times. The nearest candidate got 120/240. Only one pair had both learned answers correct. This distinguishes recovery of a taught construction from reading a contextual change. The source collection is attributed to Ernest Davis and its contributing authors. [Winograd Schema Collection](https://cs.nyu.edu/~davise/papers/WinogradSchemas/WSCollection.html) · [Paired PNG decisions and eligibility](evidence/story_mentions_v1/winograd-pairs.json)

The occurrence scorer has three width-five convolutions with dilations 1, 3 and 7. Its radius is 22 collapsed cells around each scored name and each query cell. For six pairs every relevant local window, candidate distance and copied spelling was identical across variants. No deterministic decision from those observations can solve both. In the other 114 pairs the observable features differed. Only one of these was solved in both directions. Increasing context length alone would remove a hard limit for six pairs. The other failures require learning how a visible change changes the relation. The next experiment should train that distinction directly and compare it with independent classification on the same examples and draws. ## Architecture and cost

The occurrence scorer collapses each matched name to a shared visual marker. The system retains the original pixels and equality classes outside that scorer. Summing exponentiated scores over repeated occurrences yields the name mass. The strongest occurrence supplies the copied strip. The learned order adjustment uses up to eight marked glyphs plus six following glyphs, with zeros at names and padding. On eligible examples it changes the log-odds of the two names ordered by their most recent previous mentions. A separate output adjusts the odds of naming anyone. The original general reference reader supplies the null score. Lean checks that the occurrence scores and grouped masses commute with an injective change of name under preserved matching structure. This property can hold even for an inaccurate scorer.

The trained visual cast reader has 53,451 parameters. The order adjustment adds 27,866. The existing property reader and writer supply 486,442, giving 567,759 in the composed system. The two new deployed fits took 10.77 and 3.67 seconds, consuming 1.043 and 0.338 kJ of device energy on one RTX 4090 D. Including the preceding property components yields roughly 72 seconds and 7.1 kJ of accumulated fitting. This excludes preprocessing, auxiliary controls, evaluation and host energy. The evidence does not establish unrestricted visual language generation, style-invariant reading or a general efficiency gain over LLMs. [Release and reproduction](../models/story_mentions/README.md)
