# Where an image-native reader finds the answer

A compact reader learned to use the order of pictured context to locate English evidence. It still struggles to express the requested relation in a complete answer. The frozen whole-span reader and human-answer writer remain the general baseline. The signed reader is released as an English evidence-selection result. [Protocol](directional_reading_protocol.md) · [Inference and weights](../models/directional_reading/README.md)

## Reading, writing and meaning

Each test cohort contains two distinct questions on each of 96 pages. The comparison uses official monolingual MLQA English and Chinese test documents and a selected English DROP validation cohort. All arms received the same images. Each triplet below gives the earlier reader, the unsigned control and the reader with a signed difference between right and left context.

| On 192 questions per cohort | MLQA English | MLQA Chinese | DROP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Intermediate strip equals an accepted answer | 30 / 37 / 43 | 14 / 13 / 14 | 6 / 8 / 9 |
| Stopped reply contains the requested fact | 31 / 40 / 47 | 47 / 64 / 70 | 41 / 43 / 43 |
| Stopped reply equals a complete reference | 0 / 0 / 0 | 14 / 13 / 13 | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| An accepted answer appears in a source region | 192 | 189 | 107 |

The signed reader gained seven and lost one exact English selection relative to its matched control. The paired page-bootstrap 95% interval for the gain was 0.52 to 6.25 percentage points. Swapping just the signed channels at inference changed 132 English selections and reduced exact selections from 43 to 21. Swapping the unsigned pools changes no feature by construction. Exchanging the entire question changed 172 of the signed reader's 192 English selections. On a fixed subset of 16 questions per cohort, a Qwen3 8B Q4 text judge read transcriptions of the question, passage and replies, with anonymized candidates in two opposite orders. Both decisions had to accept the reply and each optical model had to stop. The earlier reader earned 1, 3 and 1 credits, the unsigned reader 2, 4 and 0, the signed reader 2, 6 and 0, and a Qwen3-VL 8B Q4 vision comparator 10, 15 and 8. Controls using the human answer and another question's answer passed the predefined calibration in all cohorts. This is an automatic judgment on 48 questions. Timed inference used 311 J for the signed pipeline and 14,399 J for Qwen on the same pictured content. These measurements establish costs at different accuracy levels. ## What actually limits the answer

The exact selection in English improved even though the number of Chinese exact selections stayed at 14. Of the 192 Chinese strips, 57 strictly exceeded a reference and 16 contained a proper fragment. For 105, neither string contained the other. On the 189 Chinese questions with visible reference ink, supplying that ink to the unchanged writer raised complete exact replies from 13 to 181. In English it raised stopped replies containing an accepted fact from 47 to 141 of 192. No English reply matched a short reference exactly under either input condition. The first credited English answer in the fixed review order is “Dickinson wrote the poem.” The first credited Chinese reply is “26.50%”. A separate English output states the correct occupation twice. An exact short-answer mismatch alone cannot establish a semantic error. [Extent audit](evidence/directional_reading_v1/extent.json) · [Fixed-writer intervention](evidence/directional_reading_v1/supplied.json) · [Original output strips](evidence/directional_reading_v1/examples.json)

We enumerated the 32 physically available candidates on every question and verified membership of each actual selection. On 131 English questions some candidate was an accepted answer, but the final reader selected one on only 43. In Chinese the corresponding figures were 77 and 14, and in DROP 63 and nine. An oracle restricted to exactly these candidates could therefore recover at most 88, 63 and 54 more correct evidence images. The remaining 61, 115 and 129 require a different candidate or a generated answer. This bound concerns the evidence interface. A writer can still produce words that its reader missed. Lean constructs a witness attaining the finite-list ceiling and proves the decomposition of observed errors. [Candidate census](evidence/directional_reading_v1/candidates.json) · [Checked bound](../formal/span_list/OpticalCandidateCeiling.lean)

Prior development questions showed 12 English and 58 Chinese complete answers out of 192 for the signed reader, compared with 12 and 50 for the earlier reader. The signed system matched none of 128 held long-form replies and none of 68 conversational replies. It did not produce any of the requested specimens on 130 questions per language. It continued zero of 128 English and 24 Chinese book excerpts even under an eight-cell prefix criterion. The local comparison fails the bilingual complete-language promotion rule. ## Data, computation and reproducibility

Both fits covered 39,057 authentic questions four times, with 4,708 updates per arm and equal trainable parameter counts of 25,026. A frozen optical reader, whole-span ranker and image writer bring the deployed total to 1,077,322 parameters. The signed fit used 44.3 seconds and 7.28 kJ of GPU energy; the unsigned fit used 44.0 seconds and 7.22 kJ. These are marginal fitting costs of the new layers. The training loss summed accepted span locations before final reranking. The signed loss fell from 3.3675 to 3.0649 nats over the four epochs. The held side exchange tests a prediction of the Lean representation theorem. [Direction theorem](../formal/span_list/DirectionalReading.lean) · [Training trajectory](evidence/directional_reading_v1/trajectory.json) · [Fit and source hashes](../models/directional_reading/signed/fit.json)

A second audit compared the held question, page and target image streams with the two parent supervised pools. No complete training question or page matched, and no complete supervised writer target matched a held answer. Sampled 64-cell fragments at stride 32 were shared for two English MLQA and four DROP questions. The original denominators include them. Checked CPU inference on both released modern examples and both fitted arms reproduced GPU evidence and answer ink bit for bit. The two separately credited bilingual examples also reproduced on CPU, including English typed-input replay. ## Next comparison

Test whether judging a candidate against competing candidates for the same pictured question improves selection. Keep the trained candidate generator and writer fixed in that comparison. Match training exposure and capacity against an independently scored candidate control. Check the answerable-candidate ceiling, the effect of switching questions on the same page and the meaning of both free answers. New untouched document families must decide promotion. Overlong evidence alone cannot explain the 105 unrelated Chinese strips. English also needs independent assessment of formulation after correct evidence. The ultimate criteria remain grounded composition, historical reading, free book continuation and whole-system efficiency at matched quality.
