# Can a visual question encoder learn the requested fact and script in English?

When the visible-headword locator selected all 1,136 English records correctly, the existing writer produced 249 exact answers. Another 631 answered a different lexical field or historical stage for that same word. This comparison changes the visual interpretation of the question while keeping the passage encoder, glyph writer, drawing memory and stopping parameters fixed.

Start both fits from the same final matched general-entry writer. Optimize only the question convolutions and their layer norms, query projection and pooling. On each of twelve passes, picture one English question for every one of the 8,920 real training targets. Each group of four requests shares a headword, construction and choice of synonyms. Four variants request a fact and script directly; four distinguish requested from already known information. The control shuffles complete questions within each four-intent headword group every epoch. Both arms use identical source rasters, targets, question frequencies, sampled order and rehearsal. Give each arm approximately half full two-entry cards and half single requested records during training. Inference uses either the whole card or the previous exact-image locator. Fit with batches of 64, 24 Chinese entry and 12 examples from each natural corpus per update. Use per-image-sequence mean log likelihood including stop, weights 1/0.4/0.4/0.4, AdamW with warmup and cosine decay, a 900-second and 8-GiB ceiling on GPU 0 for each fit.

Generate autonomous complete answers for all 142 held-out two-entry cards (284 headwords, 568 unseen source rasters). Reuse the same English questions, and separately test an unseen construction in which all four requests about each headword have **identical multisets of pictured glyphs**. Also test the 96 newly authored English requests from a separate local Qwen3-8B with no answers or training prompts, the previously frozen 96 Chinese requests, Chinese standard questions and erased questions. Keep all 96 independent English requests in the primary denominator. A pre-fit intent review specifies a secondary subset and reasons. Reserve the original 1,383 DRCD single questions/1,042 pairs and 571 DuReader questions including 300 requiring new content. Reference comparison uses full physical pixels and learned stop. Report recognition of the requested historical form and all four complete answers per headword separately.

For a transferable English reading gain, require positive lower 95% card-clustered bounds over both shuffling and initialization on the independently worded and equal-multiset questions. Require retention at least at the parent's independent Chinese complete-answer, DRCD both-correct and DuReader complete-answer counts before promoting across tasks. Preserve the unmodified parent regardless. Examine errors as full sentences that may answer other questions on the same card. Report resource costs of the adaptation separately from parent training and test-author inference. Next decisions depend on whether independent phrasing, controlled role exchanges or natural composition remain deficient.
