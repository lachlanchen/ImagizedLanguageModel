# Learning which source glyphs answer a pictured question

The preceding writer frequently copied a passage fragment beginning with the wrong glyph. The next comparison adds a complete source-run action to the frozen inverse writer. The new head sees the original question-aligned visual states, exchanges context between neighbouring source positions, and scores every contiguous run of one to 64 glyphs. A learned binary decision chooses between copying a complete run and invoking the original free writer. The decoder supplies no list of possible answers. At training time we maximize the total conditional probability of the annotated source intervals. Targets without a supported run teach the model to use the free writer. The model takes printed or historical cells in the same physical representation. This copy action alone cannot draw a novel shape.

Use eight epochs and one final checkpoint per arm. Present every DRCD training question every epoch, alternating annotated evidence sentences and whole passages. Form all different-question, disjoint-answer pairs whose answers share a sentence. Choose the shortest common sentence with ties by reading order. Include every pair once and repeat the human who-pairs three further times per epoch. Keep both questions and both targets for every pair. Randomly exchange the two questions with probability one half in the control's extra paired examples. Both arms receive identical correct single-question and OCNLI rehearsal targets, initial weights and updates. During rehearsal include original human judgments and real missing phrases. Limit each fit to 1,200 seconds and 8 GiB allocated per arm on GPU 0. Archive complete stopped outputs.

The primary test supplies the identical annotated sentence to both questions and requires two different correct free responses. Score all shared development pairs and the prespecified 27 who-pairs separately. Compare the frozen parent and the specialized deployed passage reader under identical evidence. Separately test all 387 who-questions with a sentence chosen solely by pictured glyph overlap, all 131 pairs, whole DRCD, historical name swaps, unfamiliar wording, OCNLI and unrestricted answer writing. For an advantage attributable to paired supervision, require a positive lower 95% passage-bootstrap bound versus randomization on the all-pairs and person-pairs primary outcomes. Treat annotation-free inference, new glyph copying, free writing and whole-model compute as separate claims. If narrow gains fail to transfer, report the failing conditions from complete archived answers.
