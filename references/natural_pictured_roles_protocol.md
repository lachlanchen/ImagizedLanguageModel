# Recover roles when surface word order changes

The visual strip reader is correct on 84/84 affirmative English actives and 0/84 matched passives. Fit a separate visual reader to human-authored clauses with annotated grammatical relations. The test asks which of two marked noun heads corresponds to the active subject of the marked verb. In an ordinary transitive active clause the labels are its subject and object. For a passive we require both a passive subject and an explicit by-agent. These syntactic counterparts can name experiencers or other participants besides intentional agents.

Use the immutable UD English EWT and GUM 2.15 train/dev/test splits. Reserve the official tests before training; exclude duplicate normalized sentence text across splits. Include clauses with exactly one suitable subject and one object or agent and a verb head. Gather the whole sentence up to 224 glyph cells, or the contiguous window containing all three marked heads. Exclude heads that cannot fit or overlap and ambiguous argument attachments. Save train, development and test provenance and file hashes. Development and test retain their natural distributions. Also retain the 42 held active/passive template pairs as a separately disclosed transfer test. They motivated the change and remain distinct from the official test.

Read aligned 32-pixel grayscale glyphs and marks for the predicate and two candidates. Both candidate marks are identical. Predict the active-subject counterpart, return both selected and complementary pictured words as source crops. Train a small source encoder with balanced draws from active and explicit-agent passive training clauses. Fix the random seed, ten epochs of 100 batches of 64 clauses, AdamW, 0.0007 peak learning rate, 12 GiB allocated and 20 minutes. Compare with an identically initialized and trained model that sees marks, layout and candidate ink but no surrounding ink. Report exact first-participant choice and both fields, by voice and corpus. Compare against the leftmost candidate baseline. Success requires better balanced accuracy than both controls on each dataset, and an improvement in passive accuracy without an active accuracy loss over ten percentage points against the leftmost rule. Also report source-order-matched subsets. This is supervised role reading with supplied candidate and predicate spans. It supplies no evidence of unrestricted translation or automatic span discovery.
